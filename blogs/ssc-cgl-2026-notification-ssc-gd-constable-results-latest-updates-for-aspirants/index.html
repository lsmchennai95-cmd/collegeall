SSC CGL 2026 Notification & SSC GD Constable: Your Essential Guide to Latest Updates

For millions of aspirants across India, the Staff Selection Commission (SSC) exams represent a golden ticket to a stable and respected career in government service. Two of the most sought-after examinations are the Combined Graduate Level (CGL) and the General Duty (GD) Constable. As we navigate through May 2026, there's significant buzz around the upcoming SSC CGL 2026 notification and the recent developments concerning SSC GD Constable results and the ongoing 2026 cycle. Let's delve into the latest information to keep you thoroughly prepared and informed.

SSC CGL 2026: What Aspirants Need to Know

The SSC CGL examination is an annual event for recruiting candidates into various Group 'B' and Group 'C' posts within central government departments and ministries. Aspirants eagerly await the official notification, which is the gateway to these coveted positions.

✅ Notification Release: The SSC CGL 2026 notification is widely anticipated to be released in May or June 2026.

✅ Application Period: Online application forms are expected to become available concurrently with the notification in May/June 2026, continuing into June.

✅ Tier 1 Exam Dates: The Tier 1 examination is tentatively scheduled to be held in August-September 2026.

✅ Expected Vacancies: This year, over 15,000 vacancies are expected to be announced, offering a substantial opportunity for graduates.

These posts include roles in departments like Income Tax, Customs, CBI, CBDT & CBEC, and various other ministries. The detailed notification, once released on the official website ssc.gov.in, will provide comprehensive information on vacancies, eligibility criteria, exam patterns, and important dates.

Eligibility and Application Highlights

1. Educational Qualification
For most SSC CGL 2026 posts, a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university is the primary requirement. However, specific posts like Statistical Investigator Grade-II and Junior Statistical Officer might have additional subject-specific requirements.
2. Application Fee
The application fee is generally Rs. 100/-, payable online through various digital payment methods or via SBI Challan.
3. How to Apply
Candidates will need to register on the SSC's official website (ssc.gov.in) and then proceed to fill out the online application form, upload necessary documents, and pay the fee.

SSC GD Constable: Latest Result & Ongoing 2026 Exam Cycle

The SSC GD Constable examination attracts a huge number of applicants aiming to join the Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), SSF, Assam Rifles, and Narcotics Control Bureau.

SSC GD Constable 2024 Result Declared

The Staff Selection Commission declared the final results for the SSC GD Constable 2024 exam on December 13, 2024. Previously, the Computer-Based Examination (CBE) results for 2024 were released on July 10, 2024. A total of 351,176 candidates qualified in the CBT exam, comprising 311,736 male and 39,440 female candidates.

Candidates who cleared the CBT for the 2024 cycle are now eligible for the subsequent stages: the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST), which are conducted by CRPF.

How to Check SSC GD 2024 Results:

1. Visit the official SSC website: ssc.gov.in.

2. Click on the 'Result' tab on the homepage.

3. Navigate to the 'GD' section and click on the relevant result link.

4. Download the result PDF and search for your roll number.

Individual marks and scorecards for the 2024 exam were released on July 10, 2024, on the old SSC website (ssc.nic.in) and were available until July 24, 2024.

SSC GD Constable 2026: The Road Ahead

For those looking towards the next opportunity, the SSC GD Constable 2026 recruitment cycle is already underway:

✅ Notification Release: The SSC GD Notification 2026 was released on December 1, 2025.

✅ Application Period: The application window was from December 1 to December 31, 2025.

✅ Exam Dates: The Computer-Based Test (CBT) for the SSC GD 2026 exam is being conducted from April 27 to May 30, 2026.

✅ Vacancies: The 2026 notification announced 25,487 Constable positions.

SSC GD Selection Process 2026

The selection process for SSC GD Constable positions involves multiple stages to ensure the recruitment of suitable candidates.

1. Computer-Based Test (CBT)
An online objective-type examination covering General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Knowledge & General Awareness, Elementary Mathematics, and English/Hindi. It consists of 80 multiple-choice questions for 160 marks, with a duration of 60 minutes and negative marking.
2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST)
Candidates who qualify the CBT proceed to physical tests, which include fulfilling height/weight requirements and performing a race within specified time limits.
3. Medical Examination
A detailed medical examination to assess the candidate's overall health and fitness for the Constable position.
4. Document Verification (DV)
Verification of original documents and certificates.
5. Final Merit List
The final merit list is prepared based on performance in the CBT, subject to qualifying all subsequent physical and medical stages.

Staying Ahead: Important Tips for Both Exams

Whether you're targeting SSC CGL 2026 or awaiting results/participating in SSC GD 2026, consistent preparation and staying updated are key.

✅ Regularly visit the official SSC website (ssc.gov.in) for the latest notifications and updates.

✅ Familiarize yourself with the detailed syllabus and exam pattern for your target examination.

✅ Practice with previous year's question papers to understand the exam's difficulty level and common question types.

✅ Maintain physical fitness, especially for the SSC GD Constable exam, as physical tests are a crucial stage.

✅ Start your preparation early and follow a structured study plan.

The Staff Selection Commission continues to be a major recruiter for government positions. By staying informed about the SSC CGL 2026 notification and tracking the progress of the SSC GD Constable recruitment, aspirants can significantly boost their chances of success. Good luck with your preparations!
