SSC CGL 2026: Your Gateway to a Central Government Career – Apply Now!

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially released the much-awaited notification for the Combined Graduate Level (CGL) Examination 2026, opening doors to over 12,000 coveted Group B and C posts across various Central Government ministries and departments. This is a golden opportunity for graduates aspiring to secure a stable and rewarding government job. The application window is now open, and candidates are encouraged to apply well before the deadline to avoid last-minute rushes.

The SSC CGL examination is one of the most sought-after national-level recruitment exams, attracting lakhs of applicants annually. The 2026 cycle brings with it a substantial number of vacancies and some crucial changes that every aspirant must be aware of. Read on to get all the essential details for your SSC CGL 2026 application.

Key Dates for SSC CGL 2026 Application

Mark your calendars! Staying informed about the important dates is crucial for a smooth application process. Here’s a quick overview of the key timelines for SSC CGL 2026:

Event Date
SSC CGL 2026 Notification Release May 21, 2026
Online Application Start Date May 21, 2026
Online Application Last Date June 22, 2026
Last Date for Online Fee Payment June 23, 2026
Application Correction Window June 29 – July 1, 2026
Tier-I Exam (CBE) Tentative Dates August – September 2026
Tier-II Exam Tentative Dates December 2026

SSC CGL 2026 Vacancies: A Closer Look

This year, the Staff Selection Commission aims to fill approximately 12,256 tentative vacancies through the SSC CGL 2026 recruitment cycle. These openings are for a wide range of posts including Assistant Section Officer, Inspector, Auditor, Accountant, Junior Statistical Officer, Tax Assistant, and many other Group B and Group C positions in central government departments, ministries, and offices across India.

✅ Total Vacancies: Approximately 12,256

✅ Posts: Assistant Section Officer, Inspector, Auditor, Accountant, Junior Statistical Officer, Tax Assistant, and more.

✅ Opportunity: A significant chance for graduates to secure central government employment.

Eligibility Criteria: Are You Eligible?

Before you proceed with your application, it's essential to ensure you meet the SSC CGL 2026 eligibility criteria. These generally cover nationality, educational qualification, and age limit.

1. Nationality / Citizenship
Applicants must be a citizen of India. Subjects of Nepal or Bhutan, or Tibetan refugees (who came to India before January 1, 1962, to settle permanently) are also eligible. Additionally, persons of Indian origin who have migrated from specific countries (like Pakistan, Burma, Sri Lanka, East African countries, etc.) with the intention of permanently settling in India can also apply, provided they possess the necessary documentation issued by the Government of India.
2. Educational Qualification
A Bachelor's degree from a recognized university is the fundamental requirement for most posts. However, some specific posts have additional educational requirements:
  • Junior Statistical Officer (JSO): A Bachelor's degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects.
  • Assistant Audit Officer (AAO) / Assistant Accounts Officer (AAO): A Bachelor's degree from any recognized university. Desirable qualifications include Chartered Accountant, Cost & Management Accountant, Company Secretary, Master's in Commerce, Master's in Business Studies, Master's in Business Administration (Finance), or Master's in Business Economics.
  • Statistical Investigator Grade II: A Bachelor's degree in any subject with Statistics as one of the subjects.
It's important to note that for Statistical Investigator, eligibility now also includes graduates in AI, Data Science, and IT.
3. Age Limit
The age limit generally ranges from 18 to 32 years, but it can vary depending on the specific post you are applying for. Age relaxation is provided to candidates belonging to reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC/PwBD/ESM) as per government norms. The age will be calculated as of August 01, 2026.

How to Apply for SSC CGL 2026

The application process for SSC CGL 2026 is entirely online. Follow these steps carefully to ensure a successful application:

Step 1: One-Time Registration (OTR)
If you are a new user, you must first complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) on the official SSC website, ssc.gov.in, or through the mySSC app. This involves providing basic details and verifying your identity.
Step 2: Fill the Application Form
Log in with your OTR credentials and proceed to fill out the SSC CGL 2026 online application form. Carefully enter your personal, category, exam center preferences, and educational details.
Step 3: Upload Documents
Upload your photograph and signature as per the guidelines. A significant change this year is the requirement for a live photo capture during the application submission, rather than uploading a pre-saved image.
Step 4: Pay Application Fee
Pay the application fee of Rs. 100/- online. Women candidates and candidates belonging to SC, ST, Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), and Ex-Servicemen (ESM) categories are exempted from fee payment. The fee can be paid through BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or by using Visa/Mastercard/Maestro/RuPay Debit cards. The last date for online fee payment is June 23, 2026.
Step 5: Submit and Print
Review all the details carefully before final submission. Once submitted, download and print the confirmation page for your records.

Important Changes and Updates for SSC CGL 2026

The SSC CGL 2026 examination introduces a few significant changes that aspirants must be aware of to adjust their preparation strategies.

✅ Sectional Timing: Both Tier 1 and Tier 2 examinations will now feature fixed sectional timing.

✅ New Paper for AAO: A brand-new Paper-III has been added to Tier 2, specifically for candidates applying for Assistant Audit Officer (AAO) posts.

✅ Expanded Eligibility for Statistical Investigator: The eligibility for Statistical Investigator now includes graduates with degrees in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Data Science, and Information Technology (IT).

✅ Live Photo Capture: Instead of uploading a pre-existing photograph, candidates are now required to capture a live photo while submitting the application form.

✅ AAO Post Cadre Split: AAO posts are now categorized into Central and State Cadre, with specific language requirements for the State Cadre.

These changes highlight the Commission's efforts to streamline the examination process and adapt to evolving requirements. Aspirants are advised to go through the detailed official notification for a complete understanding of the updated rules and regulations.

Don't miss out on this incredible opportunity to build a career in central government services. Prepare diligently, apply on time, and take the first step towards a brighter future with SSC CGL 2026!
