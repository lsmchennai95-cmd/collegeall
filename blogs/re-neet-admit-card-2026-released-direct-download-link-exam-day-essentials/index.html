RE-NEET Admit Card 2026 OUT: Your Gateway to the Re-Examination on June 21st!

The moment aspirants have been eagerly waiting for has arrived! The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially released the RE-NEET UG 2026 Admit Cards. This is a critical update for all candidates appearing in the re-examination, which is now scheduled for **June 21, 2026**. With the exam just around the corner, downloading your admit card promptly is the first crucial step towards securing your dream medical seat.

The RE-NEET UG 2026 re-examination will be conducted on June 21, 2026, between 2 PM and 5:15 PM. The admit cards were released on June 14, 2026, providing candidates ample time to download and review their details.

RE-NEET 2026 Admit Cards are LIVE!

The National Testing Agency (NTA) activated the RE-NEET Admit Card 2026 download link on June 14, 2026. This re-examination has been organized following the cancellation of the earlier NEET UG exam held on May 3, 2026, due to certain allegations. Candidates who are eligible and registered for this re-test must download their fresh admit cards, as the previous ones will not be valid.

The admit card is a mandatory document for entry into the examination hall and contains vital information regarding your exam centre, reporting time, and personal details.

How to Download Your RE-NEET 2026 Admit Card

Downloading your admit card is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to get your hall ticket from the official NTA website:

1. Visit the Official NTA NEET Website
Open your web browser and navigate to the official NEET NTA portal: neet.nta.nic.in. Ensure you are only using the official website to prevent any security risks.
2. Locate the Admit Card Link
On the homepage, look for the "RE-NEET Admit Card 2026" or "Admit Card for NEET (UG) – 2026 Re-Examination" link in the 'Candidate Activity' section or under 'Latest News'. Click on it.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you need to enter your NEET Application Number, Password (or Date of Birth), and the Security Pin (captcha) displayed on the screen. Ensure these details are entered accurately.
4. Submit and Download
After entering the details, click on the "Submit" or "Login" button. Your RE-NEET UG 2026 Admit Card will appear on the screen. Carefully review all the information before downloading.
5. Print Your Admit Card
Download the admit card in PDF format and take at least two to three clear printouts. It is highly recommended to keep multiple copies for future reference and safety.

Crucial Details to Verify on Your Admit Card

Once downloaded, meticulously check all the information printed on your RE-NEET Admit Card 2026. Any discrepancy should be reported immediately to the NTA helpdesk.

✅ Your Name, Photograph, and Signature

✅ Roll Number and Application Number

✅ Date of Birth and Category (if applicable)

✅ Examination Date (June 21, 2026), Time (2 PM to 5:15 PM), and Reporting Time

✅ Exam Centre Name and Address

✅ Specific Exam Day Instructions and Barcode

Essential Documents for Exam Day

Beyond the admit card, ensure you have these documents ready for the examination day:

✅ Printed copy of your RE-NEET UG 2026 Admit Card

✅ A valid photo identity proof (e.g., Aadhar Card, Passport, PAN Card, Driving License, Voter ID)

✅ One passport-size photograph (same as uploaded in the application form) for pasting on the attendance sheet

✅ PwD certificate (if applicable)

Troubleshooting: What if You Face Issues?

Sometimes, candidates might encounter difficulties during the admit card download process or find errors. Here’s what you can do:

1. Login Failures
Double-check your application number, password/date of birth, and security pin for any typos. Ensure your keyboard's caps lock is not accidentally on.
2. Website Overload
During peak hours, the official website might experience heavy traffic. If the page isn't loading, try after some time, use an alternate browser, or try the alternate download links provided by NTA if available.
3. Discrepancies on Admit Card
If you find any error in your name, photograph, signature, or exam center details, immediately contact the NTA Helpdesk. You can reach them at 011-40759000 or email neetug2026@nta.ac.in.

Remember, the new admit card issued for the re-exam is the only valid document for the June 21 test. The admit card used for the cancelled May examination will not be accepted.

Best of luck to all the aspirants appearing for the RE-NEET UG 2026 examination. Stay calm, stay focused, and give your best shot!
