NEET UG Counselling 2026: Your Essential Guide to Medical Admissions

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET UG) is the gateway to a dream career in medicine and dentistry in India. For thousands of aspiring doctors, the NEET UG Counselling 2026 is the crucial final step in securing admission to MBBS, BDS, and BSc Nursing courses across the country. This year, the counselling process holds particular significance following the re-examination conducted on June 21, 2026, after the initial May 3 exam was cancelled due to alleged paper leaks. Consequently, the NEET 2026 results are now expected by July 20, 2026, which will directly influence the counselling timeline.

Key Counselling Authorities

The NEET UG counselling process is bifurcated into two main categories, each managed by a distinct authority:

✅ Medical Counselling Committee (MCC): The MCC, on behalf of the Directorate General of Health Services (DGHS), conducts counselling for 15% All India Quota (AIQ) seats in government medical and dental colleges. It also manages 100% of seats in Central Universities, Deemed Universities, AIIMS, JIPMER, ESIC, and AFMC institutions.

✅ State Counselling Authorities: The remaining 85% of state quota seats in government medical colleges and all seats in private/self-financed colleges within a state are handled by the respective state counselling authorities. Candidates must apply separately through state portals for these seats.

While the official detailed schedule is awaited after the NEET UG 2026 results, here's an expected timeline based on recent updates and past trends. It's crucial for candidates to monitor the official MCC website (mcc.nic.in) and respective state counselling portals for precise dates and notifications.

Step-by-Step NEET UG Counselling Process

The counselling process involves several critical stages that candidates must meticulously follow to secure their desired seat.

1. Registration
Candidates must first register online on the official MCC counselling portal (mcc.nic.in) for AIQ seats and on respective state portals for state quota seats. This involves entering NEET roll number, personal details, creating a password, and paying the non-refundable registration fee and refundable security deposit.
2. Choice Filling & Locking
After registration, candidates can select their preferred colleges and courses from the available options. It's crucial to arrange these choices in order of preference. Once satisfied, candidates must lock their choices before the deadline. Unlocked choices might not be considered for allotment.
3. Seat Allotment
The seat allotment is based on various factors including NEET UG rank, category, choices filled, and seat availability. Results are published round-wise on the official portals.
4. Downloading Allotment Letter
Successful candidates can download their provisional seat allotment letter from the counselling portal.
5. Reporting to Allotted College & Document Verification
Candidates must report physically to their allotted college within the specified timeframe with all necessary original documents for verification. Admission is confirmed upon successful document verification and payment of the admission fee.
6. Subsequent Rounds
The counselling typically proceeds in multiple rounds (Round 1, Round 2, Mop-Up/Round 3, and Stray Vacancy Round) to fill vacant seats. Fresh registrations might be allowed in certain subsequent rounds.

Documents Required for NEET UG Counselling 2026

Candidates must keep the following documents ready for the counselling and admission process. It is advisable to carry both original documents and multiple sets of photocopies.

✅ NEET UG 2026 Admit Card

✅ NEET Scorecard/Rank Letter

✅ Class 10th Certificate (for Date of Birth proof)

✅ Class 12th Mark Sheet and Passing Certificate

✅ Identity Proof (Aadhaar card, Passport, PAN Card, Driving License)

✅ Passport-size Photographs (matching those on the NEET application form)

✅ Provisional Seat Allotment Letter

✅ Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS), if applicable

✅ PwD Certificate (if applicable)

✅ Domicile/State Residence Certificate (for state quota counselling)

✅ Migration Certificate & Transfer Certificate

✅ Medical Fitness Certificate

Seat Matrix and Reservation Policy

As per the latest updates, approximately 1,29,805 MBBS seats are available across 824 medical colleges in India for NEET UG 2026 aspirants. The Government of India's reservation policy is followed for AIQ seats, and states have their own reservation policies for state quota seats.

✅ SC: 15%

✅ ST: 7.5%

✅ OBC-NCL: 27%

✅ EWS: 10%

✅ PwD: 5% (horizontal reservation across all categories)

Important Tips for NEET UG Counselling 2026

✅ Stay Updated: Regularly check official MCC and state counselling websites for the latest notifications and schedules.

✅ Understand the Process: Familiarize yourself with the entire counselling flow, including exit rules for different rounds.

✅ Prioritize Choices Wisely: Research colleges and courses thoroughly. Your preference order during choice filling is crucial.

✅ Keep Documents Ready: Ensure all required documents are organized and ready well in advance to avoid last-minute stress.

✅ Seek Guidance: Don't hesitate to seek expert advice if you have doubts about the process or choice filling.

Conclusion

The NEET UG Counselling 2026 is a detailed and multi-stage process, essential for all qualified candidates seeking admission to medical and dental courses. With the re-exam results expected by July 20, 2026, candidates must remain vigilant for the official counselling schedule. By understanding the process, preparing necessary documents, and staying informed, aspirants can confidently navigate their path to a successful medical career. Remember, careful planning and timely action are your best allies in this competitive journey.
