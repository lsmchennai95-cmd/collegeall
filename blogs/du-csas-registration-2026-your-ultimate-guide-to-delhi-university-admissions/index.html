Navigating DU CSAS Registration 2026: Your Essential Guide to Delhi University Admissions

Dreaming of joining the vibrant academic community at Delhi University (DU)? The pathway to undergraduate admissions at this prestigious institution is through the Common Seat Allocation System (CSAS). Designed to streamline admissions based on Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) scores, the CSAS portal is now live for the 2026-27 academic session, marking a crucial period for lakhs of aspiring students. While the system aims for transparency, understanding its multi-stage process is key to securing your desired seat. This comprehensive guide will demystify the DU CSAS registration, providing you with all the essential information to navigate your application successfully.

What is the DU CSAS Portal?

The DU CSAS (Common Seat Allocation System) Portal is the official online platform that governs all undergraduate admissions at Delhi University. It's a centralized counselling and seat allotment system where eligible candidates, having appeared for CUET UG 2026, register their details, select their preferred courses and colleges, and ultimately secure admission based on merit and preferences.

Who Can Apply for DU CSAS 2026?

To be eligible for admission through the DU CSAS portal, candidates must meet specific criteria:

✅ Candidates must have appeared for the CUET UG 2026 examination.

✅ Applicants must have passed Class 12 or an equivalent examination from a recognized board.

✅ It is mandatory to meet the programme-specific eligibility criteria prescribed by Delhi University in its UG Bulletin of Information 2026-27.

✅ Candidates must have appeared in CUET UG 2026 in those subjects in which they have passed Class 12.

The Three Phases of DU CSAS Registration 2026

The DU CSAS admission process is meticulously structured into three main phases, each requiring careful attention and timely action:

1. Phase I: Applying to the University of Delhi (Registration)
This initial phase involves registering on the CSAS portal using your CUET UG 2026 application number. Candidates are required to submit their personal and academic details. Crucially, many details like the candidate's name, category, and date of birth will be automatically fetched from the CUET UG 2026 portal. Applicants must ensure all information is accurate and upload the necessary documents.
2. Phase II: Filling Preferences for Programs and Colleges
Once registered, candidates proceed to fill their preferences for programs (courses) and colleges. This is a strategic stage where you should select your preferred courses and colleges in order of priority. Delhi University advises candidates to enter as many preferences as possible to enhance their chances of securing a seat. The university will also verify that your CUET subjects match the subjects you studied in Class 12 for program eligibility.
3. Phase III: Seat Allocation-cum-Admission
In the final phase, seats are allocated based on your CUET UG 2026 scores, your category, eligibility criteria for the chosen programs, and the order of preferences you submitted. After a seat is allocated, candidates will have the option to 'Accept' the allotted seat or opt for an 'Upgrade' (if available) to be considered for higher preferences in subsequent rounds. This phase also includes document verification by the colleges and the final payment of fees.

Essential Documents Required for Registration

Before you begin your CSAS registration, ensure you have the following documents ready to avoid any last-minute hurdles:

✅ CUET UG 2026 Application Number.

✅ Valid Registered Mobile Number and Email ID.

✅ Class 10th Marksheet and Certificate.

✅ Class 12th Marksheet.

✅ Category Certificate (SC/ST/OBC-NCL/EWS/PwBD), if applicable, issued in the prescribed format and within the validity period.

✅ ECA/Sports Certificates (if applying under these supernumerary quotas).

✅ Recent Passport-size Photograph and Signature.

DU CSAS Application Fees 2026

A one-time, non-refundable application fee is required to complete your CSAS registration. The fee structure for the 2026-27 academic session is as follows:

Category CSAS (UG)-2026 Application Fee
UR/OBC-NCL/EWS ₹ 250.00
SC/ST/PwBD ₹ 100.00
Additional fee for BFA/B.Sc(PE,HE&S)/BA(H) Music ₹ 400.00 (per program)
Additional fee for ECA and Sports Quota ₹ 100.00 (per quota)

Applications with incomplete fee payments will not be considered for seat allocation.

Tips for a Successful DU Admission Journey

The competition for DU seats is intense, with over 71,000 seats available across 79 programs in more than 70 colleges. To maximize your chances, consider these tips:

✅ Thoroughly read the UG Bulletin of Information 2026-27 for detailed eligibility criteria and program specifics.

✅ Carefully select and prioritize your college and program combinations in Phase II. Strategic preference filling based on your CUET scores and desired courses is crucial.

✅ Keep all your documents ready and ensure they are accurate before uploading them.

✅ Regularly check the official DU admission portal, your dashboard, and registered email for all communications and updates.

✅ Avoid last-minute submissions to prevent technical glitches.

The DU CSAS process, while comprehensive, is designed to ensure a fair and merit-based admission. By staying informed and meticulously following each step, you can significantly enhance your prospects of joining one of India's most prestigious universities. Good luck with your application!
