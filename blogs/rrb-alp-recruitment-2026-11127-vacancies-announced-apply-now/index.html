RRB ALP Recruitment 2026: Your Gateway to a Rewarding Railway Career!

Exciting news for railway job aspirants! The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has officially released the Centralized Employment Notice (CEN 01/2026) for the recruitment of Assistant Loco Pilots (ALP). This highly anticipated notification brings a significant opportunity with 11,127 vacancies announced across various railway zones in India. The application window is currently open, presenting a golden chance to join the prestigious Indian Railways.

Key Highlights of RRB ALP 2026 Recruitment

✅ Official Notification Released: CEN 01/2026 was published on May 14, 2026.

✅ Total Vacancies: A substantial 11,127 Assistant Loco Pilot posts available.

✅ Application Window: Online applications commenced on May 15, 2026, and will close on June 14, 2026.

✅ Revised Selection Process: A significant change has been introduced, giving 50% weightage to both CBT 2 Part A and CBAT for the final merit list.

What is an Assistant Loco Pilot (ALP)?

An Assistant Loco Pilot plays a crucial role in the Indian Railways, assisting the Loco Pilot in operating and managing the train. The responsibilities include monitoring equipment, ensuring safety throughout the journey, and handling minor technical tasks to support smooth train operations.

Important Dates to Remember

Application Timeline

The online application process for RRB ALP Recruitment 2026 (CEN 01/2026) has critical dates that aspiring candidates must keep in mind to avoid missing out on this opportunity.

  • Notification Release Date: May 14, 2026
  • Online Application Start Date: May 15, 2026
  • Online Application Last Date: June 14, 2026

Eligibility Criteria for RRB ALP 2026

To be considered for the Assistant Loco Pilot post, candidates must fulfill specific eligibility requirements concerning age, educational qualifications, and medical standards.

1. Age Limit (as on July 1, 2026)
Candidates must be between 18 and 30 years of age. Age relaxations are applicable for candidates belonging to reserved categories (OBC-NCL, SC, ST, Ex-servicemen, etc.) as per government norms.
2. Educational Qualification
Applicants need to possess one of the following qualifications:
  • Matriculation / SSLC plus ITI (from NCVT/SCVT) in relevant trades (e.g., Fitter, Electrician, Instrument Mechanic, etc.) OR
  • Matriculation / SSLC plus Course Completed Act Apprenticeship OR
  • 3-year Diploma in Mechanical / Electrical / Electronics / Automobile Engineering.
  • A Degree in Engineering in the above disciplines is also acceptable in place of a Diploma.
It's crucial to have these qualifications by the last date for online application submission.
3. Nationality
Candidates must be a citizen of India, a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, a Tibetan refugee who came to India before January 1, 1962, or a person of Indian origin migrated from specific East African or Asian countries with the intention of permanently settling in India.
4. Medical Standards
Candidates must meet strict A-1 medical standards, including 6/6 distance vision without glasses (with a fogging test not accepting +2D), 0.6 near vision without glasses, and must pass tests for Colour Vision, Binocular Vision, Field of Vision, Night Vision, and Mesopic Vision.

The Rigorous RRB ALP Selection Process 2026

The selection process for RRB ALP 2026 is comprehensive, involving multiple stages designed to assess candidates' technical knowledge, aptitude, and physical fitness.

1. First Stage CBT (CBT 1)
This is a qualifying examination to shortlist candidates for CBT 2. It consists of 75 objective-type questions to be completed in 60 minutes. Marks from CBT 1 are not counted towards the final merit list. The revised syllabus for CBT 1 now focuses on Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, and General Science.
2. Second Stage CBT (CBT 2)
CBT 2 is divided into two parts: Part A and Part B.
  • Part A: Comprises 100 questions from Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, Basic Science & Engineering, and General Awareness & Current Affairs. The marks obtained in Part A hold 50% weightage in the final merit list.
  • Part B: This part is qualifying in nature (75 questions) and assesses knowledge in relevant trade subjects as per ITI/Diploma/Engineering discipline.
3. Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT)
Only candidates who clear CBT 2 are eligible for CBAT. This stage is crucial for ALP posts and evaluates candidates on aspects like concentration, mental alertness, observation, decision-making, and psychological fitness. A minimum of 42 marks in each test battery is required to qualify. Crucially, CBAT now carries 50% weightage in the final merit list, a significant change from previous recruitments.
4. Document Verification (DV)
Candidates who qualify through all CBT stages and CBAT will undergo document verification to confirm the authenticity of their submitted credentials.
5. Medical Examination (ME)
The final stage involves a thorough medical examination to ensure candidates meet the strict medical standards prescribed by Indian Railways for the ALP position.

Major Change Alert: The final merit list for RRB ALP 2026 will be calculated based on 50% marks from CBT 2 Part A and 50% marks from the Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT). This revised weightage emphasizes the importance of the aptitude test significantly.

How to Apply for RRB ALP Recruitment 2026

The application process is entirely online. Follow these steps to submit your application successfully:

1. Visit the Official Website
Go to the official RRB central recruitment portal at www.rrbapply.gov.in or your respective regional RRB website.
2. Register Online
Click on the link for "CEN 01/2026 – Assistant Loco Pilot Recruitment" and register with your basic details, including name, date of birth, and contact information.
3. Fill the Application Form
Log in using your registration credentials and meticulously fill out the complete application form with all personal and educational details.
4. Upload Documents
Upload your passport-sized photograph, signature, and other required documents as specified in the notification.
5. Pay Application Fee
Pay the applicable application fee online based on your category.
6. Submit and Print
Review your application carefully, submit it, and take a printout of the confirmation page for your records. Remember, candidates can apply to only one RRB to avoid disqualification.

Salary and Career Prospects

Selected Assistant Loco Pilots will receive a competitive salary, with a pay scale typically ranging from INR 19,900 to INR 35,000. Beyond the initial salary, Indian Railways offers excellent career growth opportunities through promotions and departmental examinations, allowing ALPs to advance to higher positions like Loco Pilot, Goods Driver, and Passenger Driver.

Conclusion

The RRB ALP Recruitment 2026 presents a significant opportunity for eligible candidates to build a stable and fulfilling career with the Indian Railways. With 11,127 vacancies and a transparent, multi-stage selection process, now is the time to intensify your preparation. Make sure to apply before the deadline of June 14, 2026, and embark on your journey towards becoming an Assistant Loco Pilot. Good luck!
