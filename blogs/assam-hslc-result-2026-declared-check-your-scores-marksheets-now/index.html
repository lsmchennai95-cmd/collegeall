Assam HSLC Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Future Endeavors!

The wait is finally over for over 4.38 lakh students across Assam! The Assam State School Education Board (ASSEB), formerly known as the Secondary Education Board of Assam (SEBA), has officially declared the much-anticipated High School Leaving Certificate (HSLC) Examination Results for 2026 today, April 10, 2026, at 10:30 AM.

This momentous announcement marks a crucial milestone for thousands of students who appeared for the Class 10 examinations held between February 10 and February 27, 2026. Your hard work and dedication throughout the academic year culminate in this result, opening doors to your chosen academic and professional pathways.

How to Check Your Assam HSLC Result 2026

Accessing your Assam HSLC (Class 10) Result 2026 is a straightforward process. The ASSEB has made the results available across multiple official and alternative platforms to ensure smooth and quick access for all students. Here’s a step-by-step guide:

1. Visit Official Websites
Head over to any of the official result portals:
  • sebaonline.org
  • resultsassam.nic.in
  • asseb.in
  • results.shiksha
  • assamresult.in
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the prominently displayed link that reads "Assam HSLC Result 2026" or "SEBA Class 10 Result 2026".
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be prompted to enter your Roll Number and Number exactly as they appear on your HSLC Admit Card.
4. View and Download Your Marksheet
After entering your details, click on the "Submit" or "Get HSLC Results" button. Your Assam HSLC Result 2026 and provisional marksheet will be displayed on the screen. Download and print a copy for your records and future reference.

Alternative Methods to Check Results

To ease the pressure on official websites due to high traffic, ASSEB has also facilitated checking results through other platforms:

✅ Mobile App: Download the 'ASSEB Results 2026 Mobile App' from the Google Play Store.

✅ DigiLocker: Students can access their scorecards through the DigiLocker website (digilocker.gov.in) or app.

✅ UMANG Application: The UMANG app also provides an option to check your results.

✅ Times of India Result Portal: An alternative platform to avoid heavy traffic on official websites.

Your digital marksheet will contain vital information, including your name, roll number, examination name, subject-wise marks, total marks, and qualifying status. Ensure all details are correct.

Assam HSLC 2026: Key Statistics at a Glance

This year, a significant number of students participated in the HSLC examinations. Here's a quick look at the key figures for 2026 and a comparison with the previous year (2025):

Category Assam HSLC 2026 Assam HSLC 2025
Total Students Appeared 4,38,565 4,22,737
Male Candidates 1,90,243 1,87,752
Female Candidates 2,48,322 2,34,985
Overall Pass Percentage *To be announced with detailed results* 63.98%
Male Pass Percentage (2025) - 67.59%
Female Pass Percentage (2025) - 61.09%

The board will release a comprehensive analysis, including the overall pass percentage, gender-wise performance, district-wise results, and the much-awaited toppers' list, during an official press conference shortly after the results are made live.

Post-Result Scenarios: What's Next?

For many students, the HSLC result dictates their academic path forward. Here are some immediate considerations:

1. Class 11 Admissions
Successful candidates will now proceed to Class 11 admissions, choosing between Science, Commerce, or Arts streams based on their scores and interests.
2. Re-checking or Re-evaluation
If you are unsatisfied with your marks in a particular subject, you can apply for re-checking or photocopy with re-checking of answer scripts. Applications can be made online via www.asseb.in or www.sebaonline.org for a nominal fee (around Rs. 350/-).
3. Supplementary Exams
The schedule for supplementary examinations will be announced by the board after the main results, providing another opportunity for students who could not clear certain subjects.
4. Explore Other Options
Beyond traditional streams, students can also consider diploma courses, skill-based programs, or vocational training to align with their career aspirations.

Congratulations to all the students on your Assam HSLC Result 2026! This achievement is a stepping stone. Plan your next steps wisely and embark on a fulfilling academic journey.
