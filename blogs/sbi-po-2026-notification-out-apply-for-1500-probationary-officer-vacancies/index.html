Unlocking Your Banking Dream: SBI PO 2026 Notification Released for 1500 Vacancies!

The wait is over for aspiring banking professionals! The State Bank of India (SBI) has officially released the much-anticipated SBI PO Notification 2026, announcing 1500 vacancies for Probationary Officers. This is a golden opportunity to join India's largest public sector bank. The online application window is now open, so read on for all essential details to kickstart your application journey!

Key Highlights of SBI PO 2026 Notification

✅ Increased Vacancies: A total of 1500 Probationary Officer posts are available.

✅ Revised Exam Pattern: Changes in Mains descriptive paper marks and total Mains marks.

✅ Enhanced Career Progression: Annual CTC at Mumbai Centre increased to Rs. 21.97 Lakh.

✅ More Attempts: Increased maximum permissible attempts for General/EWS and OBC/PwBD categories.

✅ Mandatory Aadhaar Verification: Streamlined security measure for applicants.

Eligibility Criteria for SBI PO 2026

1. Age (as on April 1, 2026)

Candidates must be a minimum of 21 years and a maximum of 30 years old. This means applicants should have been born not earlier than April 2, 1996, and not later than April 1, 2005 (both dates inclusive). Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per government norms.

2. Educational Qualification (as on September 30, 2026)

A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university or any equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government is mandatory. Final year/semester students are also eligible to apply provisionally, provided they can produce proof of having passed their graduation examination on or before September 30, 2026, if called for an interview.

3. Nationality

Applicants must be an Indian Citizen, a Subject of Nepal or Bhutan, a Tibetan Refugee (who came to India before January 1, 1962), or a Person of Indian Origin (PIO) who has migrated from specific countries with the intention of permanent settlement in India.

SBI PO 2026 Selection Process

A Comprehensive Three-Phase Selection Journey

The SBI PO 2026 selection process evaluates candidates through three distinct phases:

1. Preliminary Examination (Phase I)
An online objective test, primarily a qualifying stage. Marks from Prelims are not included in the final merit list. It covers English Language, Quantitative Aptitude, and Reasoning Ability.
2. Main Examination (Phase II)
Consists of an Objective Test and a Descriptive Test. The objective section assesses Reasoning & Computer Aptitude, Data Analysis & Interpretation, General/Economy/Banking Awareness, and English Language. The Descriptive Test focuses on English Language skills (Letter Writing & Essay). Mains marks are crucial for final selection.
3. Psychometric Test, Group Exercise & Interview (Phase III)
Shortlisted candidates from Mains proceed to this phase, including a Psychometric Test, Group Exercise (20 marks), and Personal Interview (30 marks). Final selection is based on combined performance in Mains and Phase III.

Key Changes in SBI PO 2026 Exam Pattern

Significant changes have been introduced in the SBI PO 2026 exam pattern. Notably, the Descriptive Paper in the Mains exam now carries 30 marks (reduced from 50), leading to a revised total for the Mains exam of 230 marks (down from 250). Furthermore, the maximum permissible attempts have been increased for certain categories: General/EWS candidates can now attempt the exam 6 times (previously 4), and OBC/PwBD candidates have 9 attempts (up from 7). There remains no restriction on the number of attempts for SC/ST candidates.

How to Apply for SBI PO 2026

Your Step-by-Step Guide to a Successful Application

The application process for SBI PO 2026 is entirely online. Follow these steps for a smooth submission:

1. Visit the Official SBI Careers Portal
Navigate to the official SBI website at sbi.bank.in or sbi.co.in/web/careers. Click on the "Careers" section.
2. Locate the Recruitment Notification
Under "Current Openings," find and click on the "Recruitment of Probationary Officers (PO) 2026" link. Carefully read the entire official notification PDF (CRPD/PO/2026-27/09).
3. Register and Create Login Credentials
Click on the "Apply Online" link. If you are a new user, register by providing your basic details like name, contact number, and email ID to create your login credentials.
4. Fill Out the Application Form
Log in with your generated credentials and accurately fill in all required personal, educational, and category-related information. Ensure Aadhaar verification is completed as it is now mandatory.
5. Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of your recent passport-sized photograph, signature, left thumb impression, and a handwritten declaration, along with any other prescribed certificates, in the specified format and size.
6. Pay the Application Fee
Pay the requisite application fee through the available online payment modes. Please refer to the official notification for the exact fee structure for your category.
7. Review and Submit
Before final submission, thoroughly review all the details filled in the application form to avoid any errors. Once satisfied, submit the application form.
8. Print Confirmation
Download and save the confirmation page. It is highly recommended to take a printout of the submitted application form and the e-receipt of the application fee for your records and future reference.

Stay updated with the official SBI website for any further announcements and begin your journey towards becoming a Probationary Officer with the State Bank of India!
