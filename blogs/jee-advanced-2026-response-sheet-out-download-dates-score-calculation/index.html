JEE Advanced 2026 Response Sheets Released: Your Gateway to Score Calculation and IIT Admissions!

The highly anticipated JEE Advanced 2026 examination, conducted on May 17, 2026, has reached another crucial milestone! The Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Roorkee, the organizing institute for this year's exam, officially released the JEE Advanced 2026 response sheets on May 21, 2026. This is a pivotal moment for all candidates as it allows them to review their marked answers and begin estimating their performance before the official results are declared. If you appeared for the exam, it's time to download your response sheet and take the first step towards understanding your potential for admission into the prestigious IITs.

What is the JEE Advanced Response Sheet?

Understanding Your Exam Performance

The JEE Advanced response sheet is an official document that digitally records all the answers you selected for each question in both Paper 1 and Paper 2 of the examination. It serves as a transparent record of your attempts, allowing you to accurately cross-verify your responses against the official answer key, which will be released soon. This document is indispensable for calculating your probable score and getting an early assessment of your performance.

Key Dates for JEE Advanced 2026: Don't Miss Out!

Staying informed about the official schedule is crucial for every JEE Advanced aspirant. Here's a quick overview of the important upcoming dates:

Event Date & Time
JEE Advanced 2026 Exam Date May 17, 2026 (Sunday)
JEE Advanced 2026 Response Sheet Release May 21, 2026 (5 PM IST)
JEE Advanced 2026 Provisional Answer Key Release May 25, 2026 (10 AM IST)
Objection Window Against Provisional Answer Key May 25 - May 26, 2026 (Till 5 PM IST)
JEE Advanced 2026 Final Answer Key & Result Declaration June 1, 2026 (10 AM IST)

How to Download Your JEE Advanced 2026 Response Sheet

Accessing your response sheet is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to download your official document:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official JEE Advanced 2026 website: jeeadv.ac.in.
2. Locate the Candidate Portal
Look for the "JEE Advanced response sheet PDF link" or the candidate login portal on the homepage.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will need to enter your JEE Advanced registration number, date of birth, and mobile number (or password, depending on the portal) to log in.
4. Download Your Response Sheet
Upon successful login, your response sheet will be displayed on the screen. Download it in PDF format and save it for future reference. It's advisable to take a printout as well.

Understanding the Answer Key & Challenging Responses

The provisional answer key for JEE Advanced 2026 will be released on May 25, 2026. This key contains the correct answers to all questions in both papers. You can compare your recorded responses with this provisional key to estimate your score. Should you find any discrepancies in the provisional answer key, IIT Roorkee provides a window from May 25 to May 26, 2026, to raise objections. A fee of INR 500 per question is typically charged for challenging the answer key. After reviewing all objections, the final answer key will be published along with the JEE Advanced 2026 results on June 1, 2026.

Calculating Your Probable Score

Once you have both your response sheet and the provisional answer key, calculating your estimated score is simple:

✅ Download your response sheet and the official provisional answer key.

✅ Match your marked responses with the correct options in the answer key.

✅ Add marks for every correct answer according to the JEE Advanced marking scheme.

✅ Deduct marks for incorrect answers where negative marking is applicable.

✅ Unattempted questions will not carry any marks (neither added nor deducted).

✅ Sum up the scores from both Paper 1 and Paper 2 to get your estimated total score.

What's Next? JoSAA Counselling & IIT Admissions

The release of the response sheet and subsequently the answer key marks critical steps toward securing your dream IIT seat. Your estimated score will give you an idea of your potential All India Rank (AIR). Once the final results are out on June 1, 2026, the Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) counselling process will commence. This process is crucial for seat allocation in IITs and other participating institutions. Start researching your preferred IITs and branches based on your probable score and last year's cutoffs to be well-prepared for counselling.

We wish all JEE Advanced 2026 aspirants the very best as they navigate through this crucial phase of their academic journey!
