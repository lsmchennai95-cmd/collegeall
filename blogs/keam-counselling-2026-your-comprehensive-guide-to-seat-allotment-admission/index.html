KEAM Counselling 2026: Your Essential Guide to Seat Allotment & Admission

The much-anticipated KEAM 2026 counselling process is officially underway, bringing thousands of aspiring engineering, architecture, and pharmacy students closer to their dream institutions in Kerala. The Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE), Kerala, has initiated the Centralised Allotment Process (CAP), and candidates who have successfully secured a rank in the KEAM 2026 rank list can now participate in the crucial option entry phase. This comprehensive guide provides all the vital information you need to navigate the KEAM 2026 counselling, from key dates to the step-by-step allotment procedure and essential documents.

Breaking News: KEAM 2026 counselling registration and option entry commenced on June 30, 2026, with the last date to submit options set for July 7, 2026! The trial KEAM 2026 seat allotment will be released on July 8, 2026, followed by the first official seat allotment on the same day.

Important Dates for KEAM 2026 Counselling

Staying updated with the timeline is crucial for a smooth counselling experience. Here are the key dates to mark on your calendar:

✅ KEAM 2026 Rank List Published: June 27, 2026

✅ KEAM 2026 Counselling Registration & Option Entry Begins: June 30, 2026

✅ Last Date for KEAM Option Entry 2026: July 7, 2026

✅ Trial KEAM 2026 Seat Allotment Release: July 8, 2026

✅ First KEAM 2026 Seat Allotment Expected: July 8, 2026

✅ Provisional Category List Published: June 30, 2026

Understanding the KEAM 2026 Counselling Process (CAP)

The Centralised Allotment Process (CAP) for KEAM 2026 is conducted entirely online by the CEE, Kerala, to ensure transparency and efficiency. Here's a breakdown of the key steps involved:

1. Counselling Registration
Candidates included in the KEAM 2026 rank list must register for counselling using their application number and password on the official CEE Kerala website (cee.kerala.gov.in).
2. Option Entry (Choice Filling)
This is a critical step where candidates log in to their portal and fill in their preferred choices of courses and colleges. You can choose from a vast list of course-college combinations. Remember, you can edit, rearrange, and delete your choices until the specified deadline. The order of preference is paramount as seat allotments are based on rank, category, and your submitted preferences.
3. Trial Allotment
A trial allotment is conducted to give candidates an idea of potential seat allocations based on their current options. This allows you to re-evaluate and modify your choices before the final allotment rounds.
4. Seat Allotment
After the option entry closes, CEE Kerala processes all submissions, and the KEAM seat allotment results are released on the official website. Candidates can check their allotment using their application number and password. If allotted a seat, you must download the KEAM allotment memo, which details the allotted college, course, and the fee to be remitted.
5. Fee Payment and Reporting to College
Candidates who are allotted a seat and wish to confirm it must pay the prescribed fee online within the given deadline. Failure to remit the fee and join the college/course will result in the loss of the allotted seat and all existing options in that stream for subsequent phases. Following fee payment, candidates must report to the allotted institution with all necessary documents for admission and verification.

KEAM 2026 Eligibility Criteria at a Glance

To participate in KEAM 2026 counselling, candidates must meet specific eligibility requirements related to age, nationality, and educational qualifications:

✅ Nationality: Only Indian citizens are eligible. Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) and Overseas Citizens of India (OCI) are also treated at par with Indian citizens, though they are not eligible for reserved category benefits.

✅ Age Limit: Candidates must have completed 17 years of age as of December 31, 2026. There is generally no upper age limit for Engineering, Architecture, and B.Pharm courses.

✅ Academic Qualification for B.Tech: Must have passed the 10+2 examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects and Chemistry (or Computer Science/Biotechnology) as an optional subject, with a minimum of 45% aggregate marks in these subjects.

✅ Academic Qualification for B.Pharm: Must have passed the 10+2 examination with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology/Mathematics with at least 50% marks in these subjects combined.

✅ Academic Qualification for B.Arch: Must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (50% aggregate) or a 10+3 Diploma with Mathematics (50% aggregate), and must qualify with minimum eligibility marks in NATA 2026.

Documents Required for Verification

When reporting to your allotted college, ensure you have the following original documents and their self-attested copies:

Essential Documents
  • KEAM 2026 Admit Card
  • KEAM 2026 Result/Scorecard
  • Allotment Memo issued by CEE
  • Fee Receipt issued by the bank (proof of fee payment)
  • Proof of Date of Birth (e.g., Class 10th certificate)
  • 10+2th Standard Mark Sheet and Pass Certificate
  • Transfer Certificate from the last institution attended
  • Physical Fitness Certificate
  • Migration Certificate (if applicable)
  • Eligibility Certificate (for candidates who passed a qualifying exam other than CEE Kerala)
  • Any other documents specified in the allotment Memo/Notification
  • Originals of all documents and certificates uploaded with the online application

KEAM 2026 Allotment Rounds & AICTE Compliance

The KEAM 2026 CAP is expected to consist of three regular allotment rounds, possibly followed by a mop-up round to fill any remaining vacant seats. CEE Kerala is meticulously structuring the admission timeline to adhere to the deadlines set by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), with all counselling phases and final college admissions anticipated to conclude by the end of July or the first week of August 2026.

The release of the Provisional Category List on June 30, 2026, is another crucial update, detailing candidates eligible for Communal Reservation, Special Reservation, and various fee concessions. Candidates are strongly advised to check their status on the official portal.

Stay Updated!

The KEAM 2026 counselling is a dynamic process. It is highly recommended that all candidates regularly visit the official website of the Commissioner for Entrance Examinations (CEE) Kerala, cee.kerala.gov.in, for the latest announcements, notifications, and updates regarding subsequent allotment rounds, cutoff scores, and admission instructions. Don't miss out on important deadlines that could affect your admission.
