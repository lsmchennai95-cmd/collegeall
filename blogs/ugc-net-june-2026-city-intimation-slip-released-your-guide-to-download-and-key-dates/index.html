UGC NET June 2026 City Intimation Slip Released: A Comprehensive Guide

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially released the Advance City Intimation Slip for the UGC NET June 2026 examination, a crucial step for all aspiring Assistant Professors and Junior Research Fellows. This slip is designed to inform candidates about their allotted examination city well in advance, enabling them to make necessary travel and accommodation arrangements without last-minute hassle. If you've applied for the UGC NET June 2026 session, it's time to check your exam city!

What is the UGC NET City Intimation Slip?

The City Intimation Slip is an advance notification from the NTA regarding the city where your examination centre will be located. It is released prior to the admit card to give candidates ample time to plan their logistics. It's vital to understand that this slip is NOT your Admit Card. You will still need to download your official Admit Card separately, closer to the exam date, which will contain your exact exam centre details, reporting time, and other important instructions.

✅ It provides advance information about your examination city.

✅ Helps in planning travel and accommodation arrangements.

✅ It is NOT the Admit Card.

✅ Contains only the name of the city, not the specific centre.

Key Dates for UGC NET June 2026

Staying informed about important dates is crucial for smooth preparation and exam day experience. Here’s a quick overview of the essential timelines for the UGC NET June 2026 examination:

Event Date
UGC NET June 2026 City Intimation Slip Release June 10, 2026
UGC NET June 2026 Exam Dates June 22, 23, 24, 25, 29, and 30, 2026
UGC NET June 2026 Admit Card Release By June 15, 2026
Number of Subjects (June 2026 Exam) 87 subjects
Exam Mode Computer-Based Test (CBT)

**Please Note:** While the June 2026 exam is conducted in CBT mode, there was a UGC NET June 2024 exam which was conducted in OMR (Pen and Paper) mode on June 18, 2024, for 83 subjects. Candidates should ensure they are referring to the correct examination cycle.

How to Download Your UGC NET June 2026 City Intimation Slip

Downloading your city intimation slip is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to access your examination city details:

1. Visit the Official NTA UGC NET Website
Open your web browser and navigate to the official website: ugcnet.nta.nic.in.
2. Locate the City Intimation Slip Link
On the homepage, look for a link related to "UGC NET June 2026 City Intimation Slip" or "Advance City Intimation of UGC-NET June 2026." Click on it.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you need to enter your Application Number, Date of Birth, and the Security Pin (Captcha) as displayed on the screen.
4. Submit and View Your Slip
After entering the details, click on the "Submit" button. Your UGC NET June 2026 City Intimation Slip will be displayed on your screen.
5. Download and Print
Carefully review the details on the slip. Download it and take a printout for your reference. It is advisable to keep a physical copy for your records.

Important Instructions for Candidates

✅ The City Intimation Slip is for advance information only; it is not the Admit Card for entry to the examination center.

✅ The official Admit Card for UGC NET June 2026 will be released separately by June 15, 2026, on the same official website.

✅ Always carry a valid photo ID along with your Admit Card on the day of the examination.

✅ Regularly visit the official NTA websites (ugcnet.nta.nic.in and www.nta.ac.in) for the latest updates and announcements.
