COMEDK UGET Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Engineering Dreams in Karnataka

The wait is finally over for lakhs of aspiring engineers! The Consortium of Medical, Engineering, and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK) has officially announced the COMEDK UGET 2026 results. This is a pivotal moment for all candidates who appeared for the undergraduate entrance test, as their performance in this examination will pave the way for admissions into prestigious engineering colleges across Karnataka.

COMEDK UGET 2026 Results: Key Announcement

The COMEDK UGET 2026 results were declared on May 29, 2026. Initially anticipated for 4 PM, the rank cards and scorecards were made available for download at 6 PM on the official website, comedk.org. This announcement marks a crucial step in the admission cycle for undergraduate engineering and architecture courses in private unaided institutions affiliated with the consortium. Around 1.3 lakh candidates appeared for the COMEDK UGET 2026 exam, vying for nearly 20,000 seats across over 190 engineering colleges.

How to Check Your COMEDK UGET 2026 Result

Accessing your COMEDK UGET 2026 rank card is a straightforward process. Candidates need to have their application number and password ready to log in to the official portal.

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official COMEDK website: www.comedk.org.
2. Locate the Result Link
Look for the 'COMEDK UGET Result 2026' or 'Rank Card' link on the homepage. This link is usually prominently displayed.
3. Enter Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your application number and password (or date of birth, as specified).
4. View and Download Your Scorecard
Once logged in, your COMEDK UGET 2026 rank card will be displayed. It will contain details such as your subject-wise scores, total marks, percentile, and All India Rank (AIR). Download and save the PDF for future reference and the upcoming counselling process.

Important Dates at a Glance

Staying updated with key dates is crucial for a smooth admission process. Here’s a summary of the important COMEDK UGET 2026 dates:

Event Date (2026)
COMEDK UGET 2026 Exam Date May 9
Provisional Answer Key Release May 16
Objection Window Close May 18
Final Answer Key Release May 23
COMEDK UGET 2026 Result Declaration May 29 (6:00 PM)
Counselling Registration Start (Expected) Mid-June

What's Next? COMEDK UGET 2026 Counselling

The declaration of results is just the beginning. Qualified candidates will now proceed to the centralized online counselling process. The COMEDK UGET 2026 counselling is anticipated to commence in June 2026. This stage is critical for securing admission to your preferred engineering college and branch.

✅ Counselling Registration: Candidates must register online through the official COMEDK website for counselling.

✅ Document Verification: A crucial part of counselling involves the verification of necessary documents. Ensure you have all required certificates ready.

✅ Choice Filling: Based on your rank, you will be able to fill in your preferred colleges and courses. Researching cutoff trends and college placements is highly recommended.

✅ Seat Allotment: Admission will be purely merit-based, determined by your COMEDK rank, choices filled, and the availability of seats.

With a significant number of candidates competing for limited seats, strategic planning for counselling, including understanding cutoffs and college preferences, will be vital for a successful admission outcome. Keep an eye on the official COMEDK website for the detailed counselling schedule and guidelines.

Understanding Your COMEDK Rank and Cutoff

Your COMEDK UGET rank is crucial for admissions. Factors influencing the cutoff include the number of candidates who appeared, the difficulty level of the exam, and the availability of seats in various colleges. Historically, popular branches like Computer Science Engineering (CSE), Artificial Intelligence (AI), Data Science, and Information Science Engineering (ISE) tend to have higher competition and stricter cutoffs, especially in top Bangalore colleges known for their strong placement records. A score of 65-70+ is generally considered necessary for a rank below 20,000, while 100-120 marks are often required for CSE in top institutions.

We wish all the candidates the very best for their future endeavors. Stay tuned to the official COMEDK website for further updates on the counselling process.
