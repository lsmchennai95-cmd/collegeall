KTET February 2026 Results Are Out! Your Gateway to a Teaching Career in Kerala Awaits

The much-anticipated results for the Kerala Teacher Eligibility Test (KTET) February 2026 session have been officially released by the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan. Aspirants who appeared for the examination on February 21 and 23, 2026, can now check their scores and determine their eligibility to pursue teaching careers in Lower Primary, Upper Primary, High School, and Language/Specialist categories across Kerala's government and aided schools. This is a crucial step for thousands of candidates towards achieving their professional aspirations.

Key Highlights of KTET February 2026 Results

✅ Results Declared: KTET February 2026 results officially released on May 26, 2026.

✅ Official Website: Access your scorecard at ktet.kerala.gov.in.

✅ Mandatory Credentials: You'll need your Registration Number, Date of Birth, and Exam Category to check results.

✅ Lifetime Validity: Qualified candidates receive a KTET certificate valid for a lifetime.

The Kerala Teacher Eligibility Test is a vital examination designed to assess the quality and aptitude of teacher candidates. Successfully qualifying this test opens doors to numerous teaching opportunities within the state. The announcement of the February 2026 results brings a conclusion to the waiting period for many hopeful educators.

How to Check Your KTET February 2026 Result Online

Checking your KTET 2026 result is a straightforward process. Follow these detailed steps to view and download your scorecard from the official portal:

1. Visit the Official KTET Website
Navigate to the official Kerala Teacher Eligibility Test (K-TET) Pareeksha Bhavan website: ktet.kerala.gov.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the link titled "KTET FEBRUARY 2026 RESULT" or "K-TET December 2025 Result". Click on this link.
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be redirected to a new login window. Here, you need to select your exam Category (1, 2, 3, or 4) from the dropdown menu, and then enter your Register Number and Date of Birth in the respective fields.
4. View Your Scorecard
After entering the required details, click on the "Check Results" or "Submit" button. Your KTET February 2026 scorecard will be displayed on your screen.
5. Download and Print
Download the result sheet as a PDF and take a printout for future reference. This document is crucial for upcoming job applications and verification processes.

Understanding Your KTET Scorecard

The KTET scorecard is a comprehensive document that provides essential information regarding your performance in the examination. Once downloaded, you will find the following details:

✅ Candidate's Full Name

✅ Hall Ticket / Registration Number

✅ Category Appeared For (Category 1, 2, 3, or 4)

✅ Subject-Wise Marks

✅ Total Marks Secured

✅ Qualifying Status (Qualified / Not Qualified)

✅ Date of the Examination

It is imperative to keep your scorecard safe as it will be required at every stage of the teacher recruitment process.

Qualifying Marks and Certificate Validity

To be declared qualified in the KTET February 2026 examination, candidates must meet the prescribed minimum qualifying marks. For general category candidates, a score of 90 out of 150 marks (60%) is required. Candidates belonging to SC, ST, OBC, and PH categories receive a relaxation, needing 82 out of 150 marks (55%) to qualify.

A significant advantage for qualified candidates is the lifetime validity of the KTET certificate. This means once you qualify, your eligibility remains valid, eliminating the need to re-appear for the exam in the future.

What's Next After Qualifying KTET?

For those who have successfully qualified the KTET February 2026 examination, congratulations! This achievement makes you eligible to apply for teaching positions in various government and aided schools across Kerala, as per the eligibility criteria set by the state's education authorities. Keep an eye on official notifications for recruitment drives and further processes.
