UPSC CSE Prelims Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Mains!

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially announced the much-awaited Civil Services Preliminary Examination (CSE) 2026 results! Aspirants across the nation who appeared for the screening test on May 24, 2026, can now check their qualifying status. This is a pivotal moment for thousands of candidates vying for a prestigious career in the Civil Services.

The results, released on June 15, 2026, mark the successful completion of the first stage of the rigorous Civil Services Examination. A total of 13,343 candidates have provisionally qualified for the Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026, which is a testament to their hard work and dedication. Additionally, 1,046 candidates have been shortlisted for the Indian Forest Service (IFoS) Main Examination.

The UPSC Civil Services Mains Examination 2026 is scheduled to commence from August 21, 2026.

How to Download Your UPSC CSE Prelims Result 2026 PDF

The UPSC Prelims Result 2026 has been released in a PDF format, containing the roll numbers of all qualified candidates. Follow these simple steps to check your result:

1. Visit the Official UPSC Website
Navigate to the Union Public Service Commission's official website: upsc.gov.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the "What's New" or "Examination" or "Written Results" section. Click on the link titled "UPSC Prelims Result 2026" or "Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination, 2026 Result".
3. Download the Result PDF
A PDF file containing the roll numbers of the qualified candidates will open in a new tab. Download this PDF to your device.
4. Search for Your Roll Number
Open the downloaded PDF. Use the 'Ctrl+F' (or 'Find' function on mobile) shortcut to search for your roll number quickly. If your roll number appears in the list, congratulations, you have qualified for the Mains Examination!
5. Save for Future Reference
It is advisable to save the result PDF for your records and future reference.

What's Next? Crucial Steps for Qualified Candidates

Qualifying the Prelims is just the first hurdle. The journey to becoming a civil servant continues with the Mains Examination. Here are the immediate next steps for successful candidates:

✅ Detailed Application Form (DAF-I) Submission: All shortlisted candidates MUST fill out the online Detailed Application Form-I (DAF-I) for the Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026. This mandatory process will be available on the UPSC online portal (upsconline.nic.in) between June 19 and June 28, 2026.

✅ Fee Payment: Candidates are required to submit the admission fees for the Mains examination, which is Rs. 200 (if applicable).

✅ Mains Preparation: The Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026 is scheduled to begin on August 21, 2026. Utilize this period for intensive preparation for the nine descriptive papers.

✅ Admit Card for Mains: E-Admit Cards for the Mains Examination will be released subsequently, only for those who complete the DAF-I process successfully.

Important Information Regarding Marks and Cut-offs

Individual Marks and Cut-off Scores
The marks, cut-off marks, and final answer keys for the Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2026 will be uploaded on the Commission's website only after the completion of the entire Civil Services Examination 2026, including the interview stage, and after the final results are declared.
Preliminary Exam is Qualifying in Nature
It is crucial to remember that the CSE Prelims is a qualifying examination, and its scores are not included in the final merit list for selection. The Mains Examination and the Personality Test (Interview) determine the final ranking.

Heartfelt congratulations to all the candidates who have successfully cleared the UPSC CSE Prelims 2026! For those who did not make it this time, remember that persistence and a refined strategy are key. The journey of Civil Services is challenging, but with dedication, success is within reach.
