Indian Army Agniveer Admit Card 2026: Your Essential Guide to Download and Exam Readiness

The much-anticipated Indian Army Agniveer Admit Card 2026 has officially been released, marking a critical step for thousands of aspirants aiming to join the prestigious Indian Army under the Agnipath scheme. If you've applied for Agniveer General Duty (GD), Technical, Tradesman, or other categories, it's time to download your hall ticket and gear up for the Common Entrance Exam (CEE).

Key Dates for Indian Army Agniveer CEE 2026

Staying informed about the official dates is paramount for a smooth examination experience. The Indian Army has outlined a clear schedule for the Agniveer CEE 2026.

Event Date
Agniveer Admit Card Release Date May 15, 2026 (for most categories)
Agniveer GD/Tradesman Admit Card Release Date May 18, 2026
Common Entrance Exam (CEE) Dates June 1 to June 15, 2026
Application Start Date February 13, 2026
Application End Date April 1, 2026 / April 10, 2026

The Indian Army Agniveer Common Entrance Exam (CEE) 2026 is scheduled to be conducted between June 1 and June 15, 2026, across various centers nationwide. The admit cards for most categories were released on May 15, 2026, with those for Agniveer General Duty (GD) and Tradesman posts being released on May 18, 2026.

How to Download Your Indian Army Agniveer Admit Card 2026

Downloading your admit card is a straightforward process. Follow these steps carefully to ensure you have your essential document ready for the examination:

1. Visit the Official Website
Go to the official Indian Army recruitment website: joinindianarmy.nic.in.
2. Enter Captcha Code
Upon landing on the homepage, you may be prompted to enter a Captcha code to proceed. Do so and click "Enter Website".
3. Locate Admit Card Link
Look for the "Admit Card" or "Important Links" section, or navigate to the "Agnipath" tab. Click on the link specifically for "Admit Card for Common Entrance Exam for Agniveer, JCOs/OR 2026-27" or similar.
4. Log In with Your Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your JIA Roll Number / Registration Number / Registered Email ID and your Password.
5. Submit and Download
After entering your details, you might need to enter another Captcha. Click "Submit" or "Login." Your Agniveer Admit Card 2026 will appear on the screen. Download the PDF and take 2-3 colored printouts for the exam day.

Essential Documents for the CEE Examination

To avoid any last-minute issues, ensure you have all the necessary documents ready to carry to your examination center.

✅ Printed Agniveer Admit Card 2026 (multiple copies recommended).

✅ Valid Photo Identity Proof (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID Card, Driving Licence, or Passport).

✅ Recent Passport-size Photographs (several copies).

✅ A good quality Ballpoint Pen.

Details to Verify on Your Admit Card

Once you download your admit card, it is crucial to thoroughly check all the printed information. Any discrepancy should be reported immediately to the concerned authorities.

✅ Candidate's Name

✅ Father's/Mother's Name

✅ Date of Birth

✅ Roll Number and Registration Number

✅ Examination Name and Category

✅ Exam Date and Time Slot

✅ Examination Centre Address

✅ Your Signature and Photograph

✅ Important Examination Instructions

Important Advisory for Agniveer Aspirants

The Indian Army issues strict guidelines and advice to candidates to ensure fairness and transparency in the recruitment process.

Clarify Doubts
If you have any doubts regarding the recruitment process or your application, reach out to the nearest Army Recruitment Office (ARO) for clarification.
Medical Fitness
Undergo treatment for any minor medical ailments well in advance to avoid potential rejection during the medical examination.
Document Readiness
Ensure all your original certificates and mark sheets are in order and any discrepancies are corrected before the rally. This includes educational certificates, domicile, caste, character certificates, and valid ID proofs.
Stay Updated
Regularly visit the official website, joinindianarmy.nic.in, for the latest updates and announcements regarding the Agniveer recruitment drive.

The Agniveer recruitment process is a golden opportunity to serve the nation. By diligently following these instructions and preparing thoroughly, you can significantly enhance your chances of success. Good luck!
