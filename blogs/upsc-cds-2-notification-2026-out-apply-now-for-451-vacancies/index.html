UPSC CDS 2 Notification 2026 Out: Your Gateway to a Distinguished Career in the Armed Forces!

Aspiring to serve the nation with honor and valor? The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the much-anticipated Combined Defence Services (CDS) 2 Notification 2026, opening doors for eligible graduates to join India's prestigious defence academies. This is your chance to embark on a fulfilling journey as a commissioned officer in the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA), and Officers Training Academy (OTA). The notification, released on May 20, 2026, invites applications for a total of 451 vacancies.

The online application window for CDS 2 2026 commenced on May 20, 2026, and will close on June 9, 2026. Make sure to complete your application well before the deadline!

Key Dates to Remember for CDS 2 2026

Staying informed about crucial dates is vital for a smooth application process and effective preparation. Here's a quick overview of the important dates for UPSC CDS 2 2026:

✅ Notification Release Date: May 20, 2026

✅ Online Application Start Date: May 20, 2026

✅ Last Date to Apply Online: June 9, 2026 (until 6:00 PM)

✅ Last Date to Pay Application Fee: June 9, 2026

✅ Written Examination Date: September 13, 2026

UPSC CDS 2 Vacancy 2026: An Overview

A total of 451 vacancies have been announced for CDS 2 2026 across various academies. This provides a significant opportunity for eligible candidates to secure a position in their desired defence wing. The detailed breakdown of vacancies is as follows:

Academy Name Vacancies
Indian Military Academy (IMA) 100
Indian Naval Academy (INA) 26
Air Force Academy (AFA) 32
Officers Training Academy (OTA) (Men) 275
Officers Training Academy (OTA) (Women) 18
Total 451

Eligibility Criteria: Are You Ready?

Before applying, it's crucial to understand the eligibility criteria set by UPSC. These include nationality, age limit, educational qualification, and physical standards.

1. Nationality
Candidates must be a citizen of India, or a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, or a Tibetan refugee who came to India before January 1, 1962, for permanent settlement.
2. Age Limit (as of July 1, 2027, for IMA/INA/AFA courses and October 1, 2027, for OTA courses)
  • Indian Military Academy (IMA): Unmarried male candidates born not earlier than July 2, 2003, and not later than July 1, 2008.
  • Indian Naval Academy (INA): Unmarried male candidates born not earlier than July 2, 2003, and not later than July 1, 2008.
  • Air Force Academy (AFA): Candidates aged 20 to 24 years (born between July 2, 2002, and July 1, 2006). Age relaxation up to 26 years for candidates holding a valid Commercial Pilot Licence issued by DGCA (India).
  • Officers Training Academy (OTA) (SSC Course for Men): Unmarried male candidates born not earlier than July 2, 2001, and not later than July 1, 2007.
  • Officers Training Academy (OTA) (SSC Women Non-Technical Course): Unmarried women, issueless widows who have not remarried, and issueless divorcees (in possession of divorce documents) who have not remarried, born not earlier than January 2, 2000, and not later than January 1, 2006.
3. Educational Qualification
  • For IMA and OTA: A Bachelor's degree from a recognized University or equivalent.
  • For INA: A Bachelor's degree in Engineering from a recognized University/Institution.
  • For AFA: A Bachelor's degree from a recognized University (with Physics and Mathematics at 10+2 level) or a Bachelor of Engineering.

Candidates in their final year of graduation can also apply, but must submit the passing degree certificate at the time of course commencement.

How to Apply for UPSC CDS 2 2026

The application process for CDS 2 2026 is entirely online. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you through:

Step 1: Visit the Official UPSC Website
Navigate to upsc.gov.in.
Step 2: One-Time Registration (OTR)
If you are a new user, complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) process. This involves providing basic details like name, gender, date of birth, father's/mother's name, mobile number, and email ID.
Step 3: Log In and Fill the Application Form
Log in using your OTR credentials. Click on the "CDS 2 2026 Notification" or application link and proceed to fill in your personal, educational, and communication details.
Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of your photograph, signature, and identity proof in the prescribed format (jpg/jpeg).
Step 5: Pay the Application Fee
A non-refundable application fee of ₹200 is applicable for General Male candidates. Female, SC, and ST candidates are exempt from paying the fee. You can pay online through various modes.
Step 6: Review and Submit
Carefully review all the details entered before final submission. No corrections or alterations are allowed after submission. Take a printout of the final submitted form for future reference.

UPSC CDS 2 Exam Pattern 2026

The CDS examination comprises a written test followed by a Services Selection Board (SSB) interview and a medical examination. Understanding the exam pattern is crucial for effective preparation.

Written Examination

The written exam consists of objective-type questions and is bilingual (Hindi and English for General Knowledge and Elementary Mathematics). Each paper has a duration of 2 hours.

For IMA, INA, and AFA
Candidates will appear for three papers:
  • English (100 Marks)
  • General Knowledge (100 Marks)
  • Elementary Mathematics (100 Marks)
The total marks for these academies is 300.
For OTA
Candidates will appear for two papers:
  • English (100 Marks)
  • General Knowledge (100 Marks)
Mathematics is excluded for OTA candidates. The total marks for OTA is 200.

Marking Scheme

✅ Each question carries four options, with only one correct answer.

✅ For every incorrect answer, 1/3rd of the marks assigned to that question will be deducted.

✅ Marking two or more options for a single question also results in a 1/3rd mark deduction.

✅ There is no negative marking for unattempted questions.

SSB Interview and Medical Examination

Candidates who qualify the written examination will be called for the Services Selection Board (SSB) Interview, which assesses their intelligence and personality. This stage involves a two-stage selection process:

Stage 1
Officer Intelligence Rating (OIR) Test and Picture Perception Description Test (PP & DT).
Stage 2
Interview, Group Testing Officer (GTO) Tasks, Psychology Tests (Situation Reaction Test, Self Description Test, Word Association Test, Thematic Apperception Test), and Conference.

The SSB interview carries 300 marks for IMA, INA, and AFA, and 200 marks for OTA. This is followed by a medical examination to ensure candidates meet the required physical and medical standards.
