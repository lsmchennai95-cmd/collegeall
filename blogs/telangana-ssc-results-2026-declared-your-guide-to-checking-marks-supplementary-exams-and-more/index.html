Telangana SSC Results 2026 Declared: All You Need to Know!

The wait is officially over for lakhs of students across Telangana! The much-anticipated Telangana State Secondary School Certificate (TS SSC) Results for 2026 have been announced. This year, the Directorate of Government Examinations, Telangana, declared the Class 10 results on April 29, 2026, at 2 PM. K Keshava Rao, the esteemed Minister to the Government of Telangana, officially released the results, marking a crucial milestone for over five lakh students.

Congratulations to all the successful candidates! For those who need to improve their scores or clear pending subjects, vital information regarding supplementary exams and re-verification is also available.

Key Highlights of TS SSC Results 2026

✅ Declaration Date: April 29, 2026, at 2 PM.

✅ Overall Pass Percentage: An impressive 95.15%.

✅ Students Appeared: A total of 5,26,166 candidates.

✅ Students Passed: 4,97,312 students successfully cleared the examination.

✅ Gender Performance: Girls outperformed boys with a pass percentage of 96.26% compared to 94.07%.

✅ Top Performing District: Mulugu district led with a 99.30% pass rate.

How to Check Your TS SSC Results 2026

Accessing your marks memo is straightforward. Students can check their results through various official and trusted third-party portals. Make sure to have your Hall Ticket Number ready.

1. Via Official Websites
Visit any of the following official websites:
  • bse.telangana.gov.in
  • results.bsetelangana.org
  • school.edu.telangana.gov.in
  • www.results.shiksha
On the homepage, click on the "TS SSC Result 2026" link. Enter your Hall Ticket Number and click submit. Your provisional marks memo will be displayed.
2. Through Third-Party Portals
Reputable education portals like Manabadi and NDTV Education also host the results. Simply visit their respective result pages, find the Telangana SSC 2026 link, and enter your details.
3. Using MeeSeva WhatsApp Service and DigiLocker
You can get your results via the MeeSeva WhatsApp service by sending "Hi" to 8096958096 and following the instructions. DigiLocker also provides a government-verified digital marks memo, which is legally valid for admissions.

Remember, the online marks memo is provisional. Students are required to collect their original mark sheets from their respective schools.

Recounting and Re-verification of Answer Scripts

For students who believe there might be a discrepancy in their marks, the Telangana Board provides an option for recounting and re-verification.

Application Period & Fees
The application window for recounting and re-verification is from April 30 to May 14, 2026. The fee for recounting is ₹500 per subject, while re-verification costs ₹1,000 per subject. Applications must be submitted online, either directly by the candidate or through their respective headmaster, along with the hall ticket and TS SSC marks memo. It's important to note that there is no provision for revaluation.

TS SSC Advanced Supplementary Examinations 2026

Students who were unable to clear their subjects in the main examinations have a valuable second chance with the Advanced Supplementary Examinations.

Key Dates and Eligibility
The supplementary exams are scheduled to be conducted from June 5 to June 12, 2026, between 9:30 AM and 12:30 PM. Students who failed in the March 2026 exams, or those who failed in exams since 2015 under the CCE pattern, are eligible to appear.
Application Process for Supplementary Exams
Applications for the supplementary exams must be remitted through school heads, with payments made online via the Cyber Treasury. The last date for fee payment is May 14, 2026. A late fee of ₹50 will be applicable for submissions up to two days before the respective subject exam. Hall tickets for these exams will be available on the official BSE Telangana portal.

These supplementary exams offer a critical opportunity for students to avoid losing an academic year and to pursue their higher education without interruption.

We wish all students the very best for their results and future endeavors!
