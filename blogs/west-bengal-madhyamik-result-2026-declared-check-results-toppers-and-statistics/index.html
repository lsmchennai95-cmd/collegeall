West Bengal Madhyamik Result 2026 Declared: Your Guide to Scores, Toppers & Statistics

The wait is finally over for lakhs of students across West Bengal! The West Bengal Board of Secondary Education (WBBSE) officially declared the Madhyamik (Class 10) Examination Results for 2026 on May 8, 2026. This comprehensive guide provides all the essential information you need, from checking your results to understanding the key statistics and celebrating the toppers.

Key Highlights of Madhyamik Result 2026

✅ Results declared on May 8, 2026, at 9:30 AM via press conference.

✅ Online result links activated from 10:15 AM on official websites.

✅ Overall pass percentage stands at 86.83% for regular candidates.

✅ Over 9.7 lakh students appeared for the examination.

✅ Abhirup Bhadra secured Rank 1 with an impressive 698 marks (99.71%).

How to Check Your Madhyamik Result 2026 Online

Accessing your West Bengal Madhyamik Result 2026 is a straightforward process. Keep your admit card handy for your roll number and date of birth.

1. Visit Official Websites
Navigate to any of the official websites:
  • wbresults.nic.in
  • wbbse.wb.gov.in
  • wbbsedata.com
Due to high traffic, if one website is slow, try another or consider alternative methods.
2. Locate the Result Link
Click on the "WB Board 10th Result 2026" or "Madhyamik Result 2026" link available on the homepage.
3. Enter Your Credentials
On the new page, enter your Roll Number and Date of Birth in the specified format (DD/MM/YYYY).
4. View and Download Your Result
Click "Submit" to view your provisional marksheet. You can then download and save it for future reference. It is advisable to take a printout.

Alternative Methods to Check Results

✅ Via SMS: Send a text message in the format "WB 10[roll number]" to 56070 or 56263.

✅ Through DigiLocker: Log in with your Aadhaar number or mobile number, navigate to the Education and Learning section, and search for the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education.

Performance Overview: Statistics and Trends

The 2026 Madhyamik results showcase a strong academic performance across the state.

The overall pass percentage for regular candidates in Madhyamik 2026 is 86.83%, a slight improvement from last year's 86.5%.

Gender-wise Performance

While some reports indicated girls outshining boys, the detailed statistics reveal nuanced performance figures.

Category Number Appeared Number Passed Pass Percentage
Female Candidates 491,880 415,847 84.54%
Male Candidates 409,844 367,123 89.57%

District-wise Performance

Kalimpong emerged as the top-performing district, setting a high benchmark for academic excellence.

District/Region Approx. Pass Percentage / Observation
Kalimpong 95.10%
Kolkata 92.31%
Jalpaiguri 75–79% (comparatively lower)

Meet the Madhyamik Toppers 2026

The West Bengal Board announced the names of students who excelled in the examinations, achieving top ranks.

Rank 1: Abhirup Bhadra
Hailing from Uttar Dinajpur, Abhirup Bhadra secured the top position with an outstanding score of 698 out of 700, achieving 99.71%.
Rank 2: Priyatosh Mukherjee
Priyatosh Mukherjee from Birbhum secured the second rank with 696 marks, translating to 99.43%.
Rank 3: Joint Achievers
The third rank was jointly shared by three talented candidates, including Ankan Kumar Jana and Mainak Mandal, among others. A total of 131 students from 19 districts made it to the top 10 merit list, showcasing widespread talent.

Important Information Post-Result

Passing Criteria
To successfully clear the Madhyamik examination, students must secure a minimum of 34% overall marks and pass separately in each subject.
Collection of Original Marksheets
While online results are provisional, original marksheets and certificates will be distributed to schools from the respective board camp offices. Students should contact their schools in the coming days to collect their official documents.
Re-evaluation and Scrutiny
Students who are not satisfied with their scores can apply for re-evaluation or scrutiny of their theory papers. The re-evaluation results for Madhyamik 2026 are tentatively expected in June 2026.

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared the West Bengal Madhyamik Examination 2026! We wish you all the best for your future academic pursuits.
