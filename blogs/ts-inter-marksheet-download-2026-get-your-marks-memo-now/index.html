TS Inter Marksheet Download 2026: Your Essential Guide to Getting Your Marks Memo Online

The wait is over! Telangana Intermediate Public Examination (IPE) 2026 results are officially out. Find out how to quickly and easily download your provisional marks memo for both 1st and 2nd year, along with crucial information you need to know.

The Telangana Board of Intermediate Education (TSBIE) has officially announced the results for the Intermediate Public Examinations (IPE) March 2026. Students across Telangana can now access their 1st and 2nd year marks memos online. This highly anticipated announcement, made on April 12, 2026, at 11 AM, brings clarity for nearly 9.97 lakh students who appeared for the exams this year.

Downloading your marks memo is a straightforward process, but it's important to use the correct official channels and have your details ready. This provisional marksheet is crucial for immediate academic pursuits, while original certificates will be issued by your respective junior colleges later.

Key Highlights of TS Inter Results 2026

✅ Results Declared: April 12, 2026, at 11:00 AM.

✅ Total Students Appeared: Approximately 9.97 lakh students.

✅ 1st Year Pass Percentage: 66.20%.

✅ 2nd Year Pass Percentage: 70.58%.

✅ Official Websites: tgbie.cgg.gov.in, results.cgg.gov.in, tgbienew.cgg.gov.in.

✅ Credentials Needed: Hall Ticket Number, and Date of Birth (if prompted).

How to Download Your TS Inter Marks Memo 2026

Follow these detailed steps to access and download your Telangana Intermediate marks memo online:

1. Visit Official TSBIE Portals
Navigate to any of the official Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education (TSBIE) websites:
  • tgbie.cgg.gov.in
  • results.cgg.gov.in
  • tgbienew.cgg.gov.in
  • You may also find links on trusted third-party sites like Manabadi or IndiaResults, but always prioritize official sources.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for links related to "TS Inter 1st Year Results 2026" or "TS Inter 2nd Year Results 2026," or "IPE March 2026 Marks Memo." Select the appropriate link for your year and stream (General or Vocational).
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login window. Here, you must accurately enter your Hall Ticket Number. In some cases, your Date of Birth might also be required. Make sure to double-check the entered details to avoid errors.
4. View and Download Your Marks Memo
After entering the required information, click on the "Get Memo" or "Submit" button. Your TS Inter 2026 marks memo will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the details, including your name, subject-wise marks, total score, and qualifying status.
5. Print for Future Reference
It is highly recommended to download the marks memo as a PDF and take a color printout. This digital copy serves as a provisional document until you collect your official original certificate from your respective junior college.

Accessing Your Marks Memo via DigiLocker

For added convenience and security, students can also access their TS Inter 2026 marks memo through the DigiLocker app or website.

Steps to use DigiLocker:

1. Open the DigiLocker app or visit its official website.

2. Log in using your registered mobile number, Aadhaar, or username.

3. Navigate to the 'Education' or 'Issued Documents' section.

4. Select 'Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education (TSBIE)' from the list.

5. Enter your roll number and other required details.

6. Click on 'Get Document' to access your result.

7. Download and save the digital marks memo for future reference.

Important Details on Your Marks Memo

Your provisional marks memo will contain essential information, including:

✅ Student's Name

✅ Roll Number / Hall Ticket Number

✅ Parent's Name

✅ Subject-wise Marks

✅ Total Marks Obtained

✅ Grade

✅ Qualifying Status (Pass/Fail)

Remember, the online marks memo is provisional. The original marks certificate must be collected from your respective junior college once it is issued by the board.

What if You Don't Pass or are Dissatisfied with Your Results?

If you haven't achieved the minimum passing marks (35% in each subject and 350 out of 1000 aggregate, or 25% for visually/hearing impaired students) or are unhappy with your scores, there are options available:

Supplementary Exams
Students who did not pass can apply for the Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examination (IPASE) 2026. These exams are expected to be held in May 2026. The TSBIE will release a separate notification with dates and application procedures.
Revaluation/Rechecking
If you believe there was an error in the evaluation of your papers, you can apply for revaluation or rechecking through the official TSBIE website. The application window for this is also expected to open in May 2026.
