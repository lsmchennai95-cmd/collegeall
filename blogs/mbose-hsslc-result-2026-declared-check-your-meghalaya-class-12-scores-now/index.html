MBOSE HSSLC Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Future Endeavors!

The wait is finally over for thousands of students across Meghalaya! The Meghalaya Board of School Education (MBOSE) has officially declared the Higher Secondary School Leaving Certificate (HSSLC) Class 12 Results for 2026. This significant announcement marks a pivotal moment in the academic journey of countless students, opening doors to higher education and diverse career paths.

The results were announced on May 4, 2026, at 11 AM, encompassing all streams: Arts, Science, Commerce, and Vocational. Students who appeared for the MBOSE HSSLC exams, conducted from February 18 to March 13, 2026, can now access their scorecards online.

Key Highlights of MBOSE HSSLC Result 2026

✅ **Result Declared:** May 4, 2026, at 11 AM.

✅ **Streams Covered:** Arts, Science, Commerce, and Vocational.

✅ **Official Websites:** www.mbose.in, www.megresults.nic.in, www.mboseresults.in, and www.results.shiksha.

✅ **Exams Conducted:** February 18 to March 13, 2026.

How to Check Your MBOSE HSSLC Result 2026 Online

Accessing your MBOSE HSSLC scorecard is a straightforward process. Follow these steps carefully to view and download your results:

1. Visit the Official Websites
Navigate to any of the official MBOSE result portals: www.mbose.in, www.megresults.nic.in, or www.results.shiksha.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the "HSSLC Result 2026" link. You might find separate links for different streams (Arts, Science, Commerce, Vocational). Click on the link corresponding to your stream.
3. Enter Your Roll Number
A new window will open prompting you to enter your MBOSE Roll Number. Ensure you enter it correctly as per your admit card. You may also need to enter a captcha code for verification.
4. View and Download Your Result
After submitting your details, your detailed MBOSE HSSLC Result 2026, including subject-wise marks, will be displayed on the screen. It is highly recommended to download the scorecard and take a printout for future reference.
Check Your Results via SMS

For students facing internet connectivity issues, MBOSE also provides an option to receive results via SMS. Here's how:

For Science Stream:
Type **MBOSE12S ** and send to **56263**.
For Arts Stream:
Type **MBOSE12A ** and send to **56263**.
For Commerce Stream:
Type **MBOSE12C ** and send to **56263**.
Accessing Your Official Marksheet via DigiLocker

In addition to online portals, students can also access their official digital marksheets through the DigiLocker platform. This is a convenient and secure way to store your academic documents.

How to use DigiLocker:

1. Visit digilocker.gov.in or download the DigiLocker mobile app.

2. Log in or register using your Aadhaar-linked mobile number.

3. Search for 'Meghalaya Board of School Education' in the partner section.

4. Enter your roll number and access your result documents.

MBOSE HSSLC 2026 Stream-wise Performance Overview

The Meghalaya Board has also released the stream-wise pass percentages, showcasing the collective effort of students and educators across the state. The Vocational stream demonstrated an outstanding performance this year.

Stream Pass Percentage (2026)
Vocational 95.23%
Arts 88.63%
Commerce 87.49%
Science 83.70%

The board also announced the toppers list for each stream, recognizing the exceptional academic achievements of students.

What's Next? Re-evaluation and Supplementary Exams

For students who may not be satisfied with their scores, or those who could not clear certain subjects, MBOSE provides options for re-evaluation and supplementary examinations.

Re-evaluation:
Students can apply for re-evaluation if they believe there was an error in the marking of their papers. The application forms for re-evaluation will be available a few days after the result declaration, requiring a minimal fee.
Supplementary Exams:
Students who did not qualify in one or two subjects have the opportunity to appear for supplementary exams to save their academic year. The registration for MBOSE HSSLC supplementary exams is expected to begin in June 2026, with exams likely to be conducted in June 2026 and results released in August 2026.
Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared the MBOSE HSSLC Examination 2026! Your hard work and dedication have paid off. For those exploring further options, remember that every challenge is an opportunity to grow.
