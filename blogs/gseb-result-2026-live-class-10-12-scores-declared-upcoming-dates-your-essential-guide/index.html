GSEB Result 2026 LIVE: Class 10 & 12 Scores Declared & Upcoming Dates – Your Essential Guide

The wait is finally over for lakhs of students across Gujarat! The Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB) has officially declared the Class 12 (HSC) results for all streams today, May 4, 2026. Students who appeared for the examinations can now access their scores. The Class 10 (SSC) results are also set to be announced very soon, bringing anticipation to an end for many.

This comprehensive guide provides you with all the crucial information regarding the GSEB Results 2026, including how to check your scores, key statistics, passing criteria, and details about supplementary examinations.

GSEB HSC Result 2026: Declared Today!

The Gujarat Board has announced the GSEB Class 12 results for Science, General, Vocational, U.B., and Sanskrit Madhyama streams, along with GUJCET 2026, today, May 4, 2026, at 10 AM.

✅ Declaration Date: May 4, 2026 (10:00 AM)

✅ Streams Covered: Science, General, Vocational, U.B., Sanskrit Madhyama

✅ GUJCET 2026 Result: Also declared alongside HSC results

Key Statistics for GSEB HSC 2026

The overall performance in the HSC examinations shows significant trends:

  • The Science stream recorded a pass percentage of 84.33%.
  • The General stream achieved a pass percentage of 92.17%.
  • This year's General stream pass rate is slightly lower than last year's 93.7%.
  • Girls have slightly outperformed boys in the Science stream, with a pass rate of 84.65% compared to boys' 84.04%.

GSEB SSC Result 2026: Anticipated Release

Students eagerly awaiting their Class 10 (SSC) results can mark their calendars! The GSEB Class 10 board results are expected to be announced on May 6, 2026, at 8:00 AM.

✅ Expected Declaration Date: May 6, 2026 (08:00 AM)

✅ Official Website: gseb.org

How to Check Your GSEB Result 2026

Accessing your Gujarat Board results is straightforward. You will need your six-digit seat number (roll number) to view your provisional mark sheet.

1. Online via Official Website (gseb.org)

The primary method to check your result is through the official GSEB website. Follow these steps:

  1. Visit the official website: gseb.org.
  2. Click on the "GSEB HSC Result Link 2026" or "GSEB SSC Result Link 2026" on the homepage (depending on your class).
  3. Enter your six-digit seat number in the designated field.
  4. Click on the 'Submit' or 'Go' button.
  5. Your GSEB result 2026 will be displayed on the screen.
  6. Download and take a printout of your provisional marksheet for future reference.

Be aware that the website may experience high traffic immediately after the results are declared.

2. Via WhatsApp

GSEB offers a convenient WhatsApp service to receive your results:

  1. Save the number +91 63573 00971 to your contacts.
  2. Open WhatsApp and start a chat with this number.
  3. Type your six-digit seat number and send it.
  4. You will receive your result details as an automated reply.
3. Through DigiLocker

DigiLocker is another official platform to access your results:

  1. Go to the DigiLocker website: results.digilocker.gov.in.
  2. Find and click on the relevant GSEB HSC/SSC Result Link 2026.
  3. Provide your six-digit seat number and submit.
  4. Your Gujarat Board result will appear on the screen.

Understanding GSEB Passing Criteria & Grading System 2026

To successfully pass the GSEB Class 10 and 12 examinations, students must meet specific passing criteria.

Passing Criteria

Students need to secure a minimum of 33% marks in each subject and also an aggregate of 33% overall to be declared as pass.

Additionally, candidates must obtain a minimum of Grade 'D' in all subjects.

For subjects with practical components (especially in HSC Science), students must pass both the theory and practical examinations separately with at least 33% marks.

GSEB Grading System (General Guideline)

The GSEB employs a nine-level grading system. Below is a general overview:

Students scoring below 33% (or grades E1/E2) in any subject will be considered for supplementary exams.

What if You Don't Pass? Supplementary Exams 2026

For students who, unfortunately, do not achieve the minimum passing marks in one or two subjects, the GSEB provides an opportunity to appear for supplementary (or compartment) examinations.

✅ Eligibility: Students failing in a maximum of one or two subjects can apply.

✅ Application: Registration forms for supplementary exams are typically released in May, after the main results.

✅ Exam Dates: Supplementary exams are generally conducted in June or July 2026.

✅ Admit Card: Students will receive a separate admit card for the supplementary exams.

✅ Fees: A nominal fee is charged per subject, with exemptions for disabled and female students.

The supplementary exams are crucial for students to save their academic year and continue their education without a significant delay. The time table for these exams will be released on the official website.
