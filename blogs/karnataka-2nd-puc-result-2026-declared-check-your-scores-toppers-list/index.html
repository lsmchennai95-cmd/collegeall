Karnataka 2nd PUC Result 2026: Your Scores Are Out!

A comprehensive guide to checking your results, understanding the statistics, and what's next.

The eagerly anticipated Karnataka 2nd PUC Result 2026 has been officially declared by the Karnataka School Examination and Assessment Board (KSEAB) on April 9, 2026, at 3 PM. This marks a crucial milestone for over 7 lakh students who appeared for the Class 12 examinations across the state. The results, initially slated for an earlier release, were deferred due to the model code of conduct during elections.

Key Highlights of 2nd PUC Result 2026

✅ **Declaration Date:** April 9, 2026, at 3 PM.

✅ **Overall Pass Percentage:** A significant 86.48% (a notable increase from 73.45% in 2025).

✅ **Girls Outperform Boys:** Female students achieved a pass percentage of 88.70%, while male students recorded 83.65%.

✅ **Rural Excellence:** Rural areas outperformed urban areas with a pass percentage of 87.62% compared to 85.92%.

✅ **Science Stream Leads:** Science recorded the highest pass percentage at 91.69%.

✅ **Top Performing District:** Udupi emerged as the district with the highest pass percentage at 93.90%.

This year's results demonstrate a remarkable improvement in the overall pass percentage, showcasing the dedication of students and educators alike. The increase by approximately 13% from the previous year is a testament to the efforts put in.

How to Check Your 2nd PUC Result 2026

Students can access their results through multiple official platforms. Ensure you have your registration number handy for quick access.

1. Online via Official Websites

The primary portals for checking your results are:

  • karresults.nic.in
  • kseab.karnataka.gov.in

Steps:

  1. Visit either of the official websites.
  2. Click on the "Karnataka 2nd PUC Result 2026" or "PUC II Exam 1 Result 2026" link available on the homepage.
  3. Enter your registration number.
  4. Select your subject stream (Arts/Commerce/Science) if prompted.
  5. Click on the "Submit" button.
  6. Your subject-wise marks and qualifying status will be displayed. Download and save a copy of your provisional marks card for future reference.
2. Through DigiLocker

You can also access your digital marksheet via DigiLocker.

  1. Download the DigiLocker app or visit digilocker.gov.in.
  2. Log in using your registered mobile number or Aadhaar linked credentials.
  3. Go to the 'Education and Learning' section.
  4. Select 'Karnataka School Examination and Assessment Board (KSEAB)'.
  5. Click on 'Class 12 / 2nd PUC marksheet'.
  6. Enter your registration number and required details.
  7. Your digital marks card will appear; download or save it.
3. Via SMS and UMANG App

Results can also be checked through SMS and the UMANG (Unified Mobile Application for New-age Governance) app.

  • SMS: Type KAR12REGISTRATION NUMBER and send it to 56263.
  • UMANG App: Download the app, log in, search for KSEAB or Karnataka PUC Result, and enter your details.

What's Next? Higher Education & Supplementary Exams
Higher Education

With the 2nd PUC results declared, students can now proceed with their higher education plans, including admissions to various undergraduate programs. It is advisable to download and keep a copy of your marks memo for admission and verification purposes.

2nd PUC Exam 2 (Supplementary Exams)

For students who wish to improve their scores or were unable to clear certain subjects, the Karnataka Secondary Education Examination Board has announced that PUC 2 Exam-2 for 2026 will be conducted from April 30 to May 13, 2026. This provides a valuable opportunity to reappear and achieve desired results.

Students can apply for revaluation or supplementary exams as per the board rules if they are not satisfied with their marks.

Minimum Passing Criteria

To pass the Karnataka 2nd PUC exams, students must secure at least 33 percent overall, with a minimum of 30 percent marks in each subject.

We extend our heartfelt congratulations to all the successful candidates and wish them the very best for their future endeavors. For those who need to improve, the upcoming supplementary exams offer another chance to shine.
