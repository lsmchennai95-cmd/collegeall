Intermediate Results 2026: Your Essential Guide to Key Announcement Dates

The academic year 2026 is a pivotal time for countless students and aspiring professionals awaiting crucial "intermediate results." From state board examinations to professional certifications and college admissions, these announcements mark significant milestones. Staying informed about the release dates is key to planning your next steps. This comprehensive guide rounds up the latest information on intermediate results expected in 2026.

Telangana (TS) Intermediate Results 2026

One of the most anticipated announcements in India is the Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education (TGBIE) Intermediate Results for both 1st and 2nd year students. With examinations concluding in March, the board has been working diligently to process the results.

✅ TS Inter Result Date: April 12, 2026

✅ Expected Time: 11 AM

✅ Official Websites: tgbie.cgg.gov.in, tgbienew.cgg.gov.in, tgbieht.cgg.gov.in, results.cgg.gov.in

✅ Total Students: Approximately 10 lakh students appeared for the exams

Students can expect the TS Intermediate 1st and 2nd Year Results 2026 to be released on April 12, 2026, with an expected announcement time around 11 AM, following past trends. The board will likely hold a press conference to announce result statistics before activating the result links online. Over 9.9 lakh students eagerly await these results, which are crucial for their higher education admissions.

How to Check TS Inter Results 2026
  1. Visit the official TGBIE website (tgbie.cgg.gov.in, tgbienew.cgg.gov.in, results.cgg.gov.in).
  2. Click on the respective link for TS Inter 1st Year Result 2026 or TS Inter 2nd Year Result 2026.
  3. Enter your hall ticket number and any other required credentials.
  4. Your result will be displayed on the screen.
  5. Download and print your provisional marks memo for future reference.

Results can also be checked via SMS and DigiLocker. For SMS, general students can type "TSGEN2" and vocational students "TSVOC2" to receive their results.

Andhra Pradesh (AP) Intermediate Results 2026

Students in Andhra Pradesh are also keenly awaiting their intermediate results. The Board of Intermediate Education Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP) is set to announce the AP Inter 1st and 2nd Year Results 2026.

✅ AP Inter Result Date: Expected by the last week of April 2026, with some sources indicating the second week of April.

✅ Official Website: bie.ap.gov.in

✅ Alternative Portals: Manabadi, results.shiksha, schools9.com

The AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026 are likely to be announced in the second week of April, similar to last year's pattern, where results were declared on April 12th. Students can access their results using their Hall Ticket Number once the links are activated.

CPA Exam Score Release Dates 2026

For aspiring Certified Public Accountants, knowing the CPA Exam score release dates is crucial for career planning. The AICPA has released a detailed schedule for 2026, differentiating between Core and Discipline sections.

The Core sections (FAR, AUD, REG) offer continuous testing in 2026, leading to faster score reporting, typically within 1-2 weeks of taking the exam. Discipline sections (BAR, ISC, TCP) are administered quarterly, with specific release dates.

College Admissions Decisions 2025-2026

High school students applying to colleges for the Class of 2030 have already received, or are eagerly awaiting, various intermediate results in the form of admissions decisions.

Early Decision & Early Action

Most Early Decision and Early Action results for the 2025-2026 admissions cycle were released in early to mid-December 2025. Exact dates varied by institution. These early notifications allow students to finalize their college plans sooner.

Regular Decision & Ivy Day 2026

Regular Decision notifications are primarily released between mid-March and early April. A significant event is Ivy Day 2026, when all eight Ivy League schools typically release their Regular Decision notifications simultaneously. For 2026, Ivy Day was confirmed as March 26, 2026, with decisions expected around 7 PM ET.

Many selective universities have already begun releasing or are preparing to release their Regular Decision results throughout March, with most expected by late March to early April.

Key Intermediate Result Dates at a Glance

To help you keep track, here's a summary of the key dates mentioned:

Examination/Event Expected/Confirmed Result Date 2026 Notes
TS Inter 1st & 2nd Year Results April 12, 2026 (11 AM) Check on tgbie.cgg.gov.in and other official portals.
AP Inter 1st & 2nd Year Results Expected Second Week/Last Week of April 2026 Check on bie.ap.gov.in and other education portals.
CPA Core Exams (e.g., FAR, AUD, REG) Rolling releases, 1-2 weeks after exam Continuous testing, check AICPA schedule for specific cutoffs.
CPA Discipline Exams (BAR, ISC, TCP) March 13, June 16, September 11, December 15 (for quarterly testing) Administered in the first month of each quarter.

Whether you're awaiting academic scores or professional certifications, these intermediate results are critical stepping stones. Keep your hall ticket numbers or application credentials ready, and regularly check the official websites for the most accurate and up-to-date information. Good luck to all awaiting their results!
