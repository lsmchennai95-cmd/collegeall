Assam HSLC Result 2026: Official Date Confirmed for April 10!

The wait is almost over for thousands of students who appeared for the Assam High School Leaving Certificate (HSLC) Examinations 2026! The Board of Secondary Education, Assam (SEBA), now functioning under the Assam State School Education Board (ASSEB) Division – I, is all set to declare the much-anticipated Class 10 results. Students and parents across the state have been eagerly awaiting this announcement, and we have the confirmed details you need to know.

According to official confirmations, including an announcement by Assam's Chief Minister Himanta Biswa Sarma, the Assam HSLC Result 2026 will be released on April 10, 2026.

Key Details of Assam HSLC Result 2026 Announcement

The 2026 HSLC examinations were a significant event for nearly 4.38 lakh students across 1046 examination centres in Assam. The theoretical exams were conducted from February 10 to February 27, 2026, with practical examinations held earlier in January 2026. Now, the focus shifts to the result declaration, which is expected to happen between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM on April 10.

✅ **Result Declaration Date:** April 10, 2026

✅ **Expected Time:** Between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM

✅ **Official Websites:** sebaonline.org, asseb.in, resultsassam.nic.in, results.shiksha

✅ **Total Students Appeared:** 4,38,565 (1,90,243 male, 2,48,322 female)

How to Check Your Assam HSLC Result 2026

Checking your HSLC result will be a straightforward process. Students will need their Roll Number to access their provisional mark sheets. Here’s a detailed guide:

1. Visit Official Websites
Navigate to any of the official websites: www.sebaonline.org, www.asseb.in, www.resultsassam.nic.in, or www.results.shiksha.
2. Locate the Result Link
Look for the link specifically labeled "Assam HSLC Result 2026" or "SEBA HSLC Result 2026". This link will become active on April 10, 2026, at the time of the announcement.
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be prompted to enter your Roll Number (e.g., B26 + Roll) and Number as it appears on your admit card. Ensure you enter the details accurately to avoid any issues.
4. View and Download Your Result
After submitting your details, your Assam (SEBA) 10th Result & Marksheet 2026 will be displayed on the screen. It is highly recommended to download and take a printout of this provisional marksheet for your immediate reference and future admission purposes.

Alternative Ways to Access Results

Beyond the official websites, SEBA also provides other convenient methods to check your HSLC results:

Mobile Apps
You can download the 'ASSEB Results 2026 Mobile App' from the Google Play Store for free to check your scores.
DigiLocker and UMANG App
The results will also be available through the Digilocker and UMANG applications, offering a secure and official way to access your digital marksheet.

Important Information Post-Result Declaration

Once the results are out, students should be aware of a few key points:

  • The online marksheet is provisional. Students must collect their original mark sheets and certificates from their respective schools after the official release.
  • Carefully verify all the details mentioned on your scorecard, including your name, roll number, subject-wise marks, total marks, division/grade, and result status. In case of any discrepancies, immediately contact the Board of Secondary Education, Assam (SEBA).
  • Along with the individual results, the Assam HSLC Toppers List 2026, including names, roll numbers, districts, and schools, will also be released.

Re-checking and Photocopy of Answer Scripts

For students who wish to apply for re-checking or obtain a photocopy of their answer scripts, the option will be available. You will need to visit www.asseb.in or www.sebaonline.org, click on the relevant link, and pay a nominal online fee of approximately Rs. 350/-.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When will the Assam HSLC Result 2026 be declared?
The Assam HSLC Result 2026 will be declared on April 10, 2026.
Q2: What time will the results be announced?
The results are expected to be announced between 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM on April 10, 2026.
Q3: Which websites can I use to check my HSLC result?
You can check your results on sebaonline.org, asseb.in, resultsassam.nic.in, and results.shiksha.
Q4: What details are required to check the result online?
You will need your Roll Number as mentioned on your admit card to access your result.
Q5: Is the online marksheet the final one?
No, the online marksheet is provisional. Students must collect their original mark sheets from their respective schools.
