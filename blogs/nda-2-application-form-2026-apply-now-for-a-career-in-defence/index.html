NDA 2 Application Form 2026: Your Gateway to a Distinguished Defence Career

Aspiring young men and women looking to serve the nation with pride and honour, listen up! The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially released the National Defence Academy (NDA) & Naval Academy (NA) Examination (II), 2026 application form. This is your golden opportunity to embark on a prestigious career in the Indian Army, Navy, or Air Force.

The application window opened on May 20, 2026, and will close promptly on June 9, 2026, at 6:00 PM. Don't miss this crucial deadline! The written examination for NDA 2 2026 is scheduled for September 13, 2026.

Key Highlight: A total of 394 vacancies have been announced for admission to the Army, Navy, and Air Force wings of the NDA for the 158th Course and for the 120th Indian Naval Academy Course (INAC), commencing from July 1, 2027.

Important Dates for NDA 2 2026

Keeping track of key dates is essential for a smooth application process. Here's a quick overview:

Event Date
UPSC NDA 2 Notification Release May 20, 2026
Application Start Date May 20, 2026
Application End Date June 9, 2026 (until 6:00 PM)
NDA 2 Exam Date September 13, 2026
Course Commencement July 1, 2027 (for 158th NDA & 120th INAC)

Are You Eligible? NDA 2 2026 Eligibility Criteria

Before you proceed with your application, it's vital to ensure you meet the prescribed eligibility standards set by UPSC.

1. Nationality and Marital Status
Candidates must be Indian citizens, subjects of Bhutan or Nepal, or specific categories of migrants. Only unmarried male and female candidates are eligible to apply.
2. Age Limit
For NDA 2 2026, candidates must have been born not earlier than January 2, 2008, and not later than January 1, 2011. This means you should be between 16.5 and 19.5 years of age on the date the course commences.
3. Educational Qualifications

The academic requirements vary slightly depending on your chosen wing:

  • For Army Wing of NDA: You must have passed the 12th Class or its equivalent examination from a recognized Board or University.
  • For Air Force and Naval Wings of NDA and for the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at the Indian Naval Academy: You must have passed the 12th Class or equivalent with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics.

Important Note: Candidates who are currently appearing in Class 12 (or equivalent) are also eligible to apply, provided they furnish proof of passing the examination before the commencement of the course (July 1, 2027).

How to Apply for NDA 2 2026: Step-by-Step Guide

The application process is entirely online and involves a few crucial steps on the UPSC's official website (upsconline.nic.in or upsc.gov.in). We've broken it down to make it easier for you:

✅ Step 1: One-Time Registration (OTR) / Account Creation

If you're a new user, you'll need to create an account by providing your email ID, mobile number, and basic personal details. Existing users can log in directly.

✅ Step 2: Access the Application Form

Once registered, navigate to the "Latest Notification" tab and click on the "NDA & NA (II) 2026" link.

✅ Step 3: Fill Out the Application Form

Carefully fill in all required personal, educational, and communication details. Ensure all information is accurate to avoid rejection.

✅ Step 4: Select Preferences

Choose your preferred service wings (Army, Navy, Air Force, INA) in order of preference.

✅ Step 5: Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of your recent photograph (white background, 20 KB to 300 KB, JPG format), signature, and a valid photo ID proof (Aadhar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Passport, etc., in PDF format). Ensure images meet the specified dimensions.

✅ Step 6: Pay the Application Fee

A fee of INR 100 is applicable for General/OBC/EWS candidates. Female candidates and those belonging to SC/ST categories are exempted from paying the fee. Pay through the secure online channels provided.

✅ Step 7: Submit and Print

Review your application thoroughly before final submission. After submission, print a copy of the confirmation page for your records.

The NDA Examination Process

The NDA selection process is rigorous, designed to identify the best talent for the Indian Armed Forces. It typically involves three stages: a Written Examination, a Services Selection Board (SSB) Interview, and a Medical Examination.

Written Examination
The written exam for NDA 2 2026 will be held on September 13, 2026. It consists of two papers: Mathematics (morning session) and a General Ability Test (afternoon session), each 2.5 hours in duration. The syllabus focuses on conceptual learning, analytical ability, and practical aspects of knowledge, aligning with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020.
Services Selection Board (SSB) Interview
Candidates who clear the written examination will be called for a 5-day SSB Interview, which assesses personality, intelligence, and suitability for a career in the armed forces.
Medical Examination
A comprehensive medical examination is the final stage to ensure candidates meet the strict physical and medical standards required for defence services.

This is your chance to join an elite force and serve the nation with dedication. Ensure you meet all eligibility criteria and submit your NDA 2 Application Form 2026 well before the deadline. Good luck to all aspirants!
