RRB JE CBT 1 Result 2026 Released: Your Gateway to a Railway Career!

The wait is finally over for thousands of aspiring Junior Engineers! The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has officially declared the results for the Computer-Based Test (CBT) 1 of the Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) recruitment under Centralized Employment Notice (CEN) 05/2025. The RRB JE CBT 1 Result 2026 was released on May 13, 2026, bringing a crucial update for candidates who appeared for the examination held on February 19, 20, and 25, 2026.

This announcement marks a significant milestone in the recruitment drive for 2585 coveted vacancies across various railway zones. All candidates who successfully cleared CBT 1 are now provisionally shortlisted for the next stage: CBT 2.

How to Check Your RRB JE CBT 1 Result 2026

Checking your result is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to access your qualifying status and scorecard:

1. Visit Your Regional RRB Website
Navigate to the official website of the Railway Recruitment Board for the region you applied to (e.g., rrbcdg.gov.in, rrbmumbai.gov.in, etc.).
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for a link titled "Result of CBT 1 for JE/DMS/CMA (CEN 05/2025)" or similar under the 'News & Announcements' or 'Results' section.
3. Download the Merit List PDF
Click on the result link to open the zone-wise merit list PDF. This PDF contains the roll numbers of all shortlisted candidates.
4. Search for Your Roll Number
Use the 'Ctrl+F' function (or the search option on your device) to quickly find your roll number within the PDF.
5. Access Your Scorecard
For a detailed breakdown of your marks, normalized score, and qualifying status, click on the "Scorecard Login" link on the RRB website. You'll need your Registration Number and Date of Birth to log in.
The RRB JE Scorecard 2026 link has been activated across all railway zones and will remain active for a limited period. It is highly recommended to download and save your scorecard for future reference.

Understanding RRB JE CBT 1 Cut-Off Marks 2026

Alongside the results, the Railway Recruitment Board has also released the zone-wise and category-wise cut-off marks for CBT 1. These cut-off marks represent the minimum scores required for candidates to qualify for CBT 2. The cut-off is determined by several factors, including the difficulty level of the exam, the total number of vacancies, and the number of candidates who appeared.

While the actual cut-offs vary by zone and category, the minimum qualifying marks are set as follows:

✅ General (UR) / Economically Weaker Section (EWS): 40%

✅ Other Backward Class (OBC-NCL) / Scheduled Caste (SC): 30%

✅ Scheduled Tribe (ST): 25%

It's important to note that actual cut-offs are generally higher than these minimum qualifying marks.

What's Next? Preparing for RRB JE CBT 2

Congratulations to all candidates who have successfully cleared CBT 1! This is a significant step, but the journey to becoming a Junior Engineer continues. The next crucial stage is the Computer-Based Test (CBT) 2.

CBT 2 Examination
The RRB JE CBT 2 Exam 2026 is expected to be conducted in June 2026. This stage is more competitive and will test your in-depth knowledge of engineering disciplines relevant to the post. Your performance in CBT 2 will be the primary factor for the final merit list.
Document Verification (DV)
Candidates who qualify in CBT 2 will be called for Document Verification. It is crucial to keep all your original educational certificates (especially engineering degrees/diplomas) and category certificates ready.
Medical Examination
The final stage involves a medical examination to ensure candidates meet the specified medical standards (A-2 or B-2, depending on the job).

Key Highlights and Important Dates

✅ **RRB JE Notification (CEN 05/2025):** October 28, 2025

✅ **Application Period:** October 31 to December 10, 2025

✅ **CBT 1 Exam Dates:** February 19, 20, and 25, 2026

✅ **Answer Key Released:** March 5, 2026

✅ **CBT 1 Result Declaration:** May 13, 2026

✅ **Expected CBT 2 Exam Date:** June 2026

✅ **Total Vacancies:** 2585 Junior Engineer, DMS, and CMA posts.

The journey to a fulfilling career with the Indian Railways requires dedication and consistent effort. With the CBT 1 results out, it's time to intensify your preparation for CBT 2. Stay updated with your respective regional RRB websites for all official announcements regarding CBT 2 dates, admit cards, and further selection processes. Good luck to all the qualified candidates!
