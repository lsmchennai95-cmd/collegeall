NEET UG 2026: Exam Conducted Amidst Paper Leak Allegations & What It Means for Aspirants

The National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test (Undergraduate), or NEET UG 2026, a crucial gateway for aspiring medical students in India, was successfully conducted on May 3, 2026. However, the examination has recently come under intense scrutiny due to widespread allegations of a paper leak, triggering a high-level investigation. This development has cast a shadow of uncertainty over the future of numerous candidates.

NEET UG 2026 at a Glance: Key Dates

The NEET UG 2026 examination cycle has seen several important dates, from application commencement to the actual exam day. Candidates navigated a structured timeline set by the National Testing Agency (NTA).

Event Date(s)
Registration Start Date February 8, 2026
Last Date to Apply March 11, 2026 (Extended)
Application Correction Window March 12 – March 14, 2026
Admit Card Release April 26, 2026
NEET UG 2026 Exam Date May 3, 2026 (Sunday)
Provisional Answer Key & OMR Release Expected June 2026
Result Declaration Expected June 2026

The Alleged Paper Leak Controversy

The most pressing news surrounding NEET UG 2026 is the deepening suspicion of a paper leak. Allegations have emerged that a "guess paper" containing questions closely matching the actual exam circulated prior to May 3, 2026.

What are the Allegations?
The Rajasthan Police Special Operations Group (SOG) is currently investigating claims that materials, including a "guess paper" with approximately 410 questions, were circulating among aspirants weeks before the exam. Reportedly, around 120 Chemistry questions from this "guess paper" showed a significant match with the actual NEET UG 2026 paper. Some reports even suggest around 140 matching questions from a paper containing over 300 questions.
NTA's Response and Investigation
The National Testing Agency (NTA), the body responsible for conducting NEET UG, stated it received inputs regarding "malpractice" on May 7, 2026, and subsequently escalated the matter to central agencies for independent verification on May 8, 2026. The NTA has affirmed its commitment to cooperating fully with the investigating agencies, providing all necessary examination-related data and technical assistance.
Will There Be a Re-NEET 2026?
As of May 11, 2026, the NTA has not announced any re-examination (Re-NEET 2026). The agency has urged students not to believe rumors and to await the conclusion of the ongoing investigation. The NTA maintains that the exam was conducted with stringent security measures, including GPS-tracked vehicles, AI-based CCTV supervision, biometric verification, and 5G jammers. Any decision regarding cancellation or re-conduction will depend on the findings of the central agencies.

This incident, unfortunately, echoes the NEET UG 2024 paper leak scandal, which led to widespread protests and a CBI investigation, highlighting persistent concerns regarding the integrity of such high-stakes examinations.

Understanding the NEET UG 2026 Exam Pattern

Despite the current controversy, understanding the exam's structure remains crucial for aspirants. The NEET UG 2026 exam adhered to the established pattern:

✅ Mode: Offline (Pen and Paper – OMR based).

✅ Duration: 3 hours (180 minutes). PwD candidates were allotted 3 hours and 20 minutes (200 minutes).

✅ Total Questions: 180 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs).

✅ Subjects: Physics (45 questions), Chemistry (45 questions), Botany (45 questions), and Zoology (45 questions).

✅ Total Marks: 720 marks (4 marks for each correct answer).

✅ Marking Scheme: +4 for every correct answer, -1 for every incorrect answer, and 0 for unattempted questions.

✅ Languages: The question paper was available in 13 languages, chosen by candidates during the application process.

NEET UG 2026 Syllabus: No Major Changes

For those preparing for NEET, the syllabus is a foundational aspect. The NEET UG 2026 syllabus, officially released by the National Medical Commission (NMC) on December 23, 2025, largely remained consistent with the previous year's structure.

✅ The syllabus is based on the NCERT curriculum for Classes 11 and 12, covering Physics, Chemistry, and Biology.

✅ There have been no major changes to the NEET 2026 syllabus compared to NEET 2025. Any revisions, such as the removal of certain topics, occurred in earlier cycles and remain applicable.

✅ Students were advised to primarily focus on NCERT textbooks, as a significant portion of the questions are directly sourced from them.

What's Next for NEET UG 2026 Aspirants?

With the exam concluded and an investigation underway, candidates are eagerly awaiting the next steps.

✅ Answer Key & OMR Sheet: The provisional answer key along with candidates' OMR response sheets are expected to be released in June 2026, allowing candidates to challenge any discrepancies.

✅ Results: The NEET UG 2026 results are anticipated to be declared by mid-June 2026.

✅ Counselling: Following the results, the counselling process for admissions to MBBS, BDS, AYUSH, and other allied courses will commence, likely in July 2026.

Stay Informed & Focused

In these uncertain times, aspirants are advised to remain calm, avoid rumors, and rely solely on official announcements from the National Testing Agency (NTA) and relevant government bodies for accurate information regarding the investigation and subsequent processes.
