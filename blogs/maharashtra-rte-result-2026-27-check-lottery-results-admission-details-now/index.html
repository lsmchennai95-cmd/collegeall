Maharashtra RTE Result 2026-27: Your Comprehensive Guide to Admission

The wait is over for thousands of parents across Maharashtra! The much-anticipated results for the Right to Education (RTE) 25% quota admissions for the academic year 2026-27 have been released. This blog post provides all the essential information you need to check the results, understand the admission process, and secure your child's seat.

RTE Maharashtra 2026-27 Results Are Out!

The School Education and Sports Department, Government of Maharashtra, officially released the Maharashtra RTE Result 2026 today, April 10, 2026. The computerized lottery for admission under the 25% quota was successfully conducted on April 6, 2026, at Vidya Nirbhaya in Pune. Parents can now check their child's allotment status and download the crucial allotment letter from the official website.

Important Update: A staggering 2,89,609 applications were received for the RTE 25% quota admissions for the 2026-27 academic year, highlighting the immense competition for the available 1,14,826 seats across 8,701 schools. This means nearly double the number of applicants vied for a spot!

How to Check Your Maharashtra RTE Result 2026

Checking the RTE lottery result is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to find out your child's admission status:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official RTE Maharashtra admission portal: student.maharashtra.gov.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for links such as "RTE 25% Admission 2026-27," "Application wise details / RTE Lottery Result," "Maharashtra RTE Lottery Result 2026," or "RTE Merit List 2026." Click on the relevant link.
3. Enter Your Application Details
You will be prompted to enter your unique Application Number and the displayed Captcha code.
4. View and Download Result
Click on the 'Submit' button. Your Maharashtra RTE Result 2026, along with the allotment letter (if selected), will be displayed on the screen. Download and take a printout of this allotment letter for your records and the subsequent admission process.

Selected candidates will also receive SMS alerts on their registered mobile numbers from April 10, 2026, onwards.

Key Dates for RTE Maharashtra Admission 2026-27

It's crucial to adhere to the admission timeline to ensure your child's seat is secured. Here are the important dates:

Event Date
RTE Application Commencement February 17, 2026
RTE Application Last Date (Extended) March 31, 2026
RTE Lottery Draw Conducted April 6, 2026
RTE Results/Allotment Letter Release April 10, 2026
Admission Window for Selected Candidates April 10 to April 20, 2026
Waiting List Generation April 10, 2026

Documents Required for RTE Admission

If your child has been allotted a seat, prepare the following essential documents for verification at the allotted school:

✅ Printed RTE Allotment Letter

✅ Printed Application Form

✅ Child's Aadhaar Card

✅ Birth Certificate

✅ Income Certificate (for Economically Weaker Sections, below ₹1 lakh p.a.)

✅ Caste Certificate (for SC/ST/VJ-NT/OBC/SBC, if applicable)

✅ Disability Certificate (for Divyang, minimum 40% disability, if applicable)

✅ Medical Certificate (if applicable for HIV/COVID affected)

✅ Proof of Orphan status (if applicable)

✅ Passport-size photographs of the child

✅ Residence Proof (Ration Card / Electricity Bill / Rental Agreement, etc.)

Remember to carry original documents along with photocopies for verification. Failure to submit correct and complete documents within the stipulated time may lead to the cancellation of the allotted seat.

What After Checking the Result?

Once you have checked the result and downloaded the allotment letter, the next steps are crucial to secure your child's admission:

1. Report to the Allotted School
Selected candidates must report to their allotted school between April 10 and April 20, 2026.
2. Document Verification
The school authorities will verify all the submitted documents. Ensure you have both original and photocopies ready.
3. Complete Admission Formalities
After successful document verification, complete all necessary admission formalities as guided by the school.

If you fail to take admission within the specified window (April 10-20, 2026), the allotted seat may be cancelled.

Important Changes and Guidelines for 2026-27

This year's RTE admission process in Maharashtra has seen some significant updates:

✅ Relaxed Distance Restriction: The previous rule limiting school selection within a one-kilometre radius has been removed. Applicants can now select schools within 1 km, 1 to 3 km, and beyond 3 km categories.

✅ Transparency: The lottery draw was conducted using a centralized digital system and was live-streamed, ensuring fairness and transparency in the selection process.

✅ Strict Document Policy: Authorities emphasized that any mistakes, missing details, or unconfirmed applications can lead to cancellation of admission. Only passbooks issued by nationalized banks are accepted as valid proof of residence.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: What is the official website to check the RTE Maharashtra Result 2026?
The official website to check the RTE Maharashtra Result 2026 is student.maharashtra.gov.in.
Q2: When were the RTE Maharashtra results declared?
The RTE Maharashtra Result 2026 for the 2026-27 academic year was officially released on April 10, 2026. The lottery draw took place on April 6, 2026.
Q3: What if my child is on the waiting list?
A waiting list up to the number of available RTE seats was generated on April 10, 2026. If vacancies arise after the initial admissions, students on the waiting list will be contacted sequentially by the National Informatics Centre (NIC).
Q4: What documents are crucial for admission after selection?
Key documents include the printed allotment letter, application form, child's Aadhaar Card, Birth Certificate, Income/Caste/Disability Certificates (if applicable), and Residence Proof.
Q5: What is the last date to complete the admission process?
Selected candidates must complete their admission formalities at the allotted school between April 10 and April 20, 2026.

We wish all parents and students the very best in their RTE admission journey. Stay vigilant, verify all details, and complete the process within the given timelines to secure a bright future for your child.
