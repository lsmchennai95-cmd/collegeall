AP Inter Results 2026: 1st & 2nd Year Scores Declared Today!

The moment of truth has arrived for over 10.57 lakh students across Andhra Pradesh! The Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP) has officially declared the AP Inter Results 2026 for both 1st and 2nd year students today, April 15, 2026. The eagerly awaited results were announced at 10:31 AM IST, bringing an end to the anticipation following the Intermediate Public Examinations (IPE) held in February and March.

This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about checking your results, understanding the key statistics, and planning your next steps.

Key Highlights of AP Inter Results 2026

✅ Result Declared Today: April 15, 2026, at 10:31 AM IST.

✅ Exams Conducted: February 23 to March 24, 2026 (1st Year) and February 24 to March 23, 2026 (2nd Year).

✅ Total Students: Over 10.57 lakh candidates for both 1st and 2nd year.

✅ Official Websites: resultsbie.ap.gov.in and bie.ap.gov.in.

✅ Alternative Access: WhatsApp (9552300009), SMS, and DigiLocker services available.

How to Check Your AP Inter Results 2026

Students can easily access their provisional marks memo through multiple platforms. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Via Official Websites

The primary method to check your results is through the official BIEAP portals:

Step 1: Visit any of the official result websites: resultsbie.ap.gov.in or bie.ap.gov.in.

Step 2: Look for the link that says "AP Inter 1st Year Results 2026" or "AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026."

Step 3: Enter your Hall Ticket Number/Roll Number in the designated field.

Step 4: You may also need to enter your Date of Birth (in DD/MM/YYYY format) if prompted.

Step 5: Click on 'Submit' or 'Get Result'.

Step 6: Your AP Inter Result 2026 and marks memo will be displayed on the screen. Download and take a printout for future reference.

2. Through Manabadi Portal

Manabadi.co.in is another popular and reliable platform to check AP Inter results.

Step 1: Go to manabadi.co.in.

Step 2: Click on the "AP Inter Results 2026" link.

Step 3: Select your year (1st or 2nd) and enter your Hall Ticket Number and Date of Birth.

Step 4: Submit to view your result.

3. Via WhatsApp (Mana Mitra Service)

For added convenience, BIEAP has enabled result checking through WhatsApp.

Step 1: Send a “Hi” message to 9552300009.

Step 2: Follow the prompts to receive your marks memo directly on your mobile device.

4. DigiLocker & SMS Services

The results are also accessible via DigiLocker, which will host digital certificates, and through SMS services to ensure uninterrupted access during peak traffic.

AP Inter Results 2025: A Glimpse at Previous Year's Performance

Understanding the trends from the previous year can provide valuable context. In 2025, the AP Inter results recorded significant pass percentages, with girls outperforming boys.

Category AP Inter 1st Year Pass % (2025) AP Inter 2nd Year Pass % (2025)
Overall General Stream 70% 83%
Overall Vocational Stream 62% 77%
Girls (General Stream) 75% 86%
Boys (General Stream) 66% 80%

Source: BIEAP, 2025 Results

In 2025, Krishna district emerged as the top-performing district, achieving a remarkable 93% pass rate in the second year and 85% in the first year. Guntur and NTR districts also showcased strong academic performance.

What's Next After the Results?

For students who have successfully cleared their examinations, it's time to plan for the future. For those who may not have achieved the desired results, several options are available.

✅ For Passed Students: Download your provisional marks memo. This is crucial for admissions into undergraduate courses and other professional programs.

✅ Supplementary Exams (IPASE): Students who fail to secure the minimum passing marks are eligible to appear for supplementary exams, typically conducted in May or June.

✅ Recounting & Revaluation: Those dissatisfied with their scores can apply for recounting or revaluation after the results are announced. The dates for these processes will be released by BIEAP shortly.

✅ Higher Education: 2nd-year students will now focus on applying for various undergraduate courses, while 1st-year students will proceed to their final year of intermediate education.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. When were the AP Inter Exams 2026 conducted?

The Andhra Pradesh Intermediate First Year (General & Vocational) Exams 2026 were held from February 23, 2026, till March 24, 2026. The Second Year exams were conducted from February 24, 2026, till March 23, 2026.

2. Can I check my AP Inter Results 2026 name-wise?

Yes, some portals like results.shiksha allow checking results name-wise. You typically need to enter the first few characters of your name and then locate your result from the displayed list.

3. What details are required to check AP Inter Results 2026?

You will primarily need your Hall Ticket Number/Roll Number. In some cases, your Date of Birth might also be required.

4. Is the online marks memo valid for admissions?

Yes, the digital marks memo released online is considered provisional but valid for admission purposes until original documents are issued by the board.

Conclusion

The declaration of AP Inter Results 2026 marks a significant milestone in the academic journey of thousands of students. We wish all students the very best for their future endeavors. Remember to stay calm, check your results through official channels, and explore the various options available to you.
