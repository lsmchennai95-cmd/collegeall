AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Higher Education!

The wait is over! The Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP) has officially released the Intermediate 2nd Year Results for 2026, marking a pivotal moment for thousands of students across the state.

Key Highlights of AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026

✅ Results declared on April 15, 2026, at 10:30 AM.

✅ Overall pass percentage for 2nd Year stands at an impressive 81%.

✅ Girls outshined boys with an 85% pass rate in the 2nd Year general stream.

✅ Access your results via official websites, SMS, WhatsApp, and DigiLocker.

✅ This year recorded one of the strongest academic performances in the last 12 years.

How to Check Your AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026

Accessing your results is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to view and download your provisional marks memo:

1. Visit Official Websites
Navigate to any of the official BIEAP result portals:
  • bie.ap.gov.in
  • resultsbie.ap.gov.in
  • manabadi.co.in
  • results.shiksha
  • schools9.com
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, click on the link specifically for "AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026" or "IPE 2026 Results - Second Year".
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be prompted to enter your Hall Ticket Number and Date of Birth (in DD/MM/YYYY format).
4. View and Download Your Result
After submitting, your AP Inter 2nd Year Result and Marks Memo will be displayed on the screen. Download and print a copy for your records and future admissions.

Beyond the Web: Alternate Ways to Get Your Scores

Facing heavy website traffic? BIEAP offers convenient alternative methods to check your results:

✅ Via SMS: For General stream, type APGEN<space>REGISTRATION NO. and send it to 56263. For Vocational 2nd Year, type APVOC2<space>Registration Number and send to 56263.

✅ Via WhatsApp (Mana Mitra): Send a "Hi" message to 9552300009 to receive your marks memo directly on your registered mobile number.

✅ Via DigiLocker: Results are also made available on the DigiLocker platform for easy access and secure storage.

Understanding Your Marks Memo

Your online marks memo is a provisional document displaying subject-wise marks, overall total score, division or grade, and your pass/fail status. This provisional memo is crucial for applying to undergraduate courses and other higher education programs. The original marks sheets will be issued by the board at a later date.

Important Note on Passing Marks: To successfully clear the AP Inter examinations, students must secure a minimum of 35% marks in both the theory and practical components of each subject.

Performance Analysis: A Look at the Trends

The AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026 showcase a strong academic performance, with an 81% overall pass percentage, marking it as one of the highest in the past 12 years. Girls have consistently outperformed boys, with an impressive 85% pass rate for girls compared to 76% for boys in the general stream this year.

Let's look at the pass percentage trends over the last few years for a broader perspective:

Year General 1st Year (%) General 2nd Year (%) Vocational 1st Year (%) Vocational 2nd Year (%)
2020 59% 59% 41% 50%
2022 54% 61% 45% 55%
2023 61% 72% 49% 62%
2024 67% 78% 60% 71%
2025 70% 83% 62% 77%
2026 77% 81% 61% 74%

Source: BIEAP Official Statistics

As evident from the table, the general stream for the 2nd year has shown a significant upward trend, peaking at 83% in 2025 and maintaining a strong 81% in 2026. Government Junior Colleges (GJCs) also showed a notable improvement, with a 68% pass percentage for 2nd-year students, the second-highest in a decade.

What's Next? Supplementary Exams and Re-evaluation

For students who, unfortunately, did not achieve the minimum passing marks in one or two subjects, the Board provides a crucial second chance through the Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examinations (IPASE).

1. Supplementary Exams (IPASE 2026)

These exams are typically held in May or June. For reference, the 2024 supplementary exams for 2nd year were conducted from May 24 to June 1, in the afternoon shift (2:30 PM to 5:30 PM).

Students must carry their hall tickets to the examination center. A separate timetable for vocational courses is usually released.

2. Recounting and Re-verification

Students who are dissatisfied with their marks can apply for recounting or re-verification of their answer sheets. The application dates for recounting and re-verification will be announced shortly after the results. For example, in 2024, the application period was from April 13 to April 24, and for supplementary results, from June 20 to June 24, with a fee of Rs. 1000 per subject for re-verification.

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared their AP Inter 2nd Year examinations! This achievement is a testament to your hard work and dedication. For those who need to appear for supplementary exams or apply for re-evaluation, remember that this is another opportunity to succeed. Best of luck with your future academic endeavors!
