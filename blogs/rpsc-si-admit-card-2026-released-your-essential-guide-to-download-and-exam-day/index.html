RPSC SI Admit Card 2026 Released: Your Essential Guide to Download and Exam Day

The wait is over for aspiring Sub-Inspectors in Rajasthan! The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has officially released the admit cards for the SI Combined Competitive Examination 2025 (Advt. No. 05/2025), scheduled for April 5 and 6, 2026. This is a crucial step for thousands of candidates vying for 1,015 Sub-Inspector and Platoon Commander posts across the state. If you're among the registered candidates, it’s time to download your admit card and finalize your exam preparations.

Key Highlights of RPSC SI Admit Card 2026

✅ **Admit Card Release Date:** April 2, 2026

✅ **Examination Dates:** April 5 & 6, 2026

✅ **Total Vacancies:** 1,015 Sub-Inspector posts

✅ **Official Websites:** rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in and sso.rajasthan.gov.in

✅ **Mandatory Document:** Admit card is essential for exam entry

Your RPSC SI Admit Card is your gateway to the examination hall. Ensure you download it well in advance to avoid any last-minute technical glitches!

How to Download Your RPSC SI Admit Card 2026

Accessing your admit card is a straightforward process. Follow these detailed steps to download your hall ticket:

1. Visit the Official RPSC Website
Navigate to the Rajasthan Public Service Commission's official website: rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in. Alternatively, you can also use the SSO portal at sso.rajasthan.gov.in.
2. Locate the Admit Card Link
On the homepage, look for the "RPSC SI Admit Card 2026" or "Admit Card for Sub Inspector Combined Competitive Examination 2025" link under the "News & Events" or "Important Links" section.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a new login page. Here, you need to enter your Application Number/SSO ID and your Date of Birth. Make sure to enter the details accurately.
4. Submit and View Your Admit Card
After entering your details, click on the "Submit" or "Download Admit Card" button. Your RPSC SI Admit Card 2026 will then be displayed on your screen.
5. Download and Print
Carefully review all the details on your admit card. Download the PDF file and take at least two to three clear printouts. These hard copies are mandatory for entry into the examination hall.

What to Carry to the Exam Hall

To ensure a smooth examination experience, candidates must bring the following to the exam center:

✅ A printed copy of your RPSC SI Admit Card 2026

✅ A valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Driving License, or Passport)

✅ Two recent passport-size photographs (preferably the same as uploaded in the application form)

✅ A blue or black ballpoint pen for OMR-based exams

Remember, no candidate will be allowed to enter the examination hall without their admit card and a valid photo ID.

RPSC SI Exam Pattern and Selection Process

The RPSC SI selection process is multi-faceted, designed to evaluate candidates on various parameters. It primarily consists of:

1. Written Examination
This is the first stage, comprising two papers. On April 5, candidates will appear for General Hindi (11:00 AM to 1:00 PM), and on April 6, for General Knowledge & General Science (3:00 PM to 5:00 PM). Candidates need to achieve a minimum of 36% in each subject and 40% overall to pass.
2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST)
Successful candidates from the written exam will proceed to these physical tests to assess their fitness and endurance.
3. Interview
The final stage of the selection process involves an interview, where candidates' personality, general awareness, and suitability for the role are assessed.
Verify Details on Your Admit Card

Upon downloading, it is imperative to cross-check all the details mentioned on your RPSC SI Admit Card 2026. Ensure the following information is accurate:

✅ Candidate's Name and Photograph

✅ Roll Number and Registration Number

✅ Exam Date and Shift Timings

✅ Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time

✅ Exam Centre Name and Full Address

✅ Important Exam Day Guidelines

In case of any discrepancy, immediately contact the RPSC authorities for rectification.

Beyond RPSC: Other SI Recruitments in 2026

While the RPSC SI exams are currently taking center stage, other significant Sub-Inspector recruitment drives have also been in motion or are anticipated:

  • **UP Police SI:** The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) conducted its written examinations for SI posts on March 14 and 15, 2026, with admit cards released on March 11 and 12, 2026, respectively.
  • **SSB SI Recruitment:** The Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) has released a notification for 51 SI vacancies. The application period was from March 21 to April 20, 2026, with a selection process involving PET, PST, Written Exam, Document Verification, and Medical Examination.
  • **Assam Police SI:** Assam Police also announced recruitment for 102 SI posts, with the application deadline on February 22, 2026.
  • **Punjab Police SI:** The Punjab Police SI recruitment notification for 2026 is awaited, but candidates can expect admit cards to be released about a week before the exam.

With the RPSC SI examinations just around the corner, candidates are advised to dedicate their time to final revisions and stay calm. Ensure you have all necessary documents ready and reach your exam center well in advance. We wish all the candidates the very best of luck!
