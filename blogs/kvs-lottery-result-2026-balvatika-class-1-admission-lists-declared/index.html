KVS Lottery Result 2026: Balvatika & Class 1 Admission Lists OUT!

Your comprehensive guide to Kendriya Vidyalaya admissions for the 2026-27 academic session.

The eagerly awaited Kendriya Vidyalaya Sangathan (KVS) lottery results for Balvatika and Class 1 admissions for the academic session 2026-27 have been officially declared! Thousands of parents across India have been on tenterhooks, and the moment to check your child's admission status has arrived. This detailed guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from checking the results to the crucial next steps for securing a coveted spot in a Kendriya Vidyalaya.

The KVS admission process is renowned for its transparency, primarily relying on a computer-generated lottery system to ensure fairness, especially given the high demand for limited seats in many urban Kendriya Vidyalayas.

KVS Lottery Results 2026: Key Dates to Remember

Mark your calendars! The provisional lists have been released in a phased manner, and subsequent lists are also scheduled:

1. Balvatika Provisional Lists Declared
The first provisional selection lists for Balvatika-1, Balvatika-2 (where applicable), and Balvatika-3 were released on April 8, 2026.
2. Class 1 Provisional Lists Released
The first provisional list for Class 1 admissions was declared on April 9, 2026.
3. Special Dates for Certain Regions
For Kendriya Vidyalayas in Assam, Kerala, and Puducherry, the declaration of both Balvatika and Class 1 lists was deferred to April 10, 2026, due to general elections.
4. Subsequent Provisional Lists
If seats remain vacant after the initial rounds, a second provisional list will be published on April 16, 2026, followed by a third provisional list on April 21, 2026.
How to Check Your Child's KVS Lottery Result 2026

Checking the results is a straightforward process. Follow these steps carefully to determine your child's admission status:

1. Visit the Official KVS Portals
Navigate to either the official KVS website, kvsangathan.nic.in, or the dedicated KVS admission portal, admission.kvs.gov.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for a prominent link titled "Balvatika Lottery Result 2026" or "Lottery Result 2026" for Class 1 admissions.
3. Select Your Details
You will be prompted to select the specific Balvatika class (Balvatika-1 or Balvatika-3) or Class 1, and then choose the Kendriya Vidyalaya your child applied to. In some cases, you might also need to select your State.
4. View and Download the Provisional List
The provisional selection list and waiting list will appear as a PDF document on your screen.
5. Search for Your Child's Name
Use the 'Ctrl + F' (or 'Cmd + F' on Mac) function to quickly search for your child's name or application number within the PDF. Remember to download and save a copy for your records.
What to Do After the Results: Document Verification

Congratulations if your child has been provisionally selected! The next crucial step is document verification. This process is mandatory to confirm the admission.

✅ Parents of selected candidates MUST complete document verification within the prescribed timeline.

✅ Final admission is granted ONLY after successful scrutiny of all submitted documents.

✅ Keep checking the official KVS portal regularly for updates on the document verification schedule and any additional instructions from the allotted Kendriya Vidyalaya.

Important Documents for KVS Admission

Ensure you have the following documents ready for verification:

Birth Certificate
This is mandatory, especially for Class 1 admissions, to prove your child's age.
Recent Photograph
A recent passport-size photograph of your child, typically in digital format (up to 200 KB).
Address Proof
A valid document proving your current residential address.
Aadhaar Card
Aadhaar card of both the child and parents.
Caste Certificate (if applicable)
If applying under SC/ST/OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) categories.
EWS/BPL Certificate (if applicable)
For admissions under the Economically Weaker Section or Below Poverty Line categories.
Service Certificate of Guardian (if applicable)
Essential for applicants under priority categories for government and defense personnel.
Disability Certificate (if applicable)
For Children With Special Needs (CwSN), which may also grant age relaxation.

Remember to carry original documents along with photocopies. Attestation may be required for some documents.

Understanding KVS Admission Criteria for 2026-27

The KVS admission process follows specific age criteria and a priority system:

Age Criteria (as of March 31, 2026)

Class Minimum Age Maximum Age
Balvatika 1 3 years 4 years
Balvatika 2 4 years 5 years
Balvatika 3 5 years 6 years
Class 1 6 years Less than 8 years

Note: Children born on April 1st are considered eligible for admission. A maximum age relaxation of two years is provided for differently-abled children.

Admission Priority System

✅ Seats are first allocated to candidates under the Right to Education (RTE) category.

✅ This is followed by applicants in Service Priority Categories I and II (children of central government employees, armed forces personnel, etc.).

✅ Subsequently, other eligible candidates are considered based on seat availability.

The KVS admission process for 2026-27 is a significant event for countless families. By staying informed, meticulously following the instructions, and preparing all necessary documents, you can smoothly navigate the process. Keep a close watch on the official KVS portals for any further announcements, especially regarding subsequent lists and offline admission opportunities.

Good luck to all the aspiring Kendriya Vidyalaya students!
