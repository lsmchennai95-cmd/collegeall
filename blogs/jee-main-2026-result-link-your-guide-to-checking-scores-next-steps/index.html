JEE Main 2026 Result Link: Your Essential Guide to Checking Scores & Future Steps

The Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main is one of India's most crucial engineering entrance exams. Aspirants nationwide eagerly await their results, which pave the way for admissions into prestigious NITs, IIITs, GFTIs, and eligibility for JEE Advanced. With the JEE Main 2026 Session 2 results just around the corner, here's everything you need to know to access your scores and plan your next move.

Key Dates and Important Announcements

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has diligently conducted the JEE Main 2026 examinations. Here’s a quick overview of the recent and upcoming result declarations:

Event Date Details
JEE Main 2026 Session 1 Result February 16, 2026 Scores for Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) released.
JEE Main 2026 Session 2 Provisional Answer Key & Response Sheet Around April 11-13, 2026 Candidates could challenge answers during this period.
JEE Main 2026 Session 2 Result (Paper 1) On or before April 20, 2026 Highly anticipated release for B.E./B.Tech aspirants.
JEE Main 2026 Session 2 Result (Paper 2) 1st week of May 2026 (Expected) For B.Arch./B.Planning courses.
Important Note: The NTA typically releases the final answer key shortly before or along with the result announcement. Keep an eye on the official website for the most up-to-date information!

How to Check Your JEE Main 2026 Result

Accessing your JEE Main scorecard is a straightforward process. Ensure you have your application number and password (or Date of Birth) ready. Follow these steps:

1. Visit the Official JEE Main Website
Navigate to the official JEE Main portal: jeemain.nta.nic.in. You might also find active links on nta.ac.in, examinationservices.nic.in, or ntaresults.nic.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for a prominent link like "Result of JEE (Main) 2026 Session 2" or "View Scorecard (JEE Main 2026)". Click on this link.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your Application Number and your Password (or Date of Birth, depending on the login prompt). You may also need to enter a security pin (CAPTCHA) as displayed on the screen.
4. View and Download Your Scorecard
After submitting your details, your JEE Main 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Carefully review all the information.
5. Save and Print for Future Reference
It is crucial to download the PDF of your scorecard and take several printouts. These will be essential for upcoming counselling rounds and admission processes.

What Your JEE Main Scorecard Contains

Your JEE Main scorecard is more than just a score; it's a comprehensive report of your performance. Here are the key details you will find:

✅ Candidate's Name, Application Number, and Roll Number

✅ Parent's Names, Date of Birth, Category, and Nationality

✅ State Code of Eligibility

✅ Subject-wise NTA Scores (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)

✅ Total NTA Score (Percentile)

✅ All India Rank (AIR)

✅ Qualifying Cut-off for JEE Advanced (for Paper 1)

Understanding Your JEE Main Percentile

The JEE Main results are declared in the form of percentile scores. This normalization process ensures fairness, accounting for varying difficulty levels across different shifts of the examination. Your percentile score indicates the percentage of candidates who have scored equal to or less than you in that particular session.

The NTA considers the higher of the two NTA scores obtained in Session 1 and Session 2 for compiling the final merit list and All India Ranks.

Next Steps After JEE Main Results: JEE Advanced & JoSAA Counselling

Once your JEE Main results are out, the journey continues for aspiring engineers:

1. JEE Advanced Qualification
The top 250,000 candidates who clear Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) in JEE Main across all categories will be eligible to apply for JEE Advanced 2026, the entrance exam for admission to the prestigious Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs).
2. JoSAA Counselling
Qualified candidates will participate in JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) counselling. This is a unified platform for admissions to IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs. Ensure you are aware of the counselling schedule and procedures.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When will the JEE Main 2026 Session 2 result be released?
The JEE Main 2026 Session 2 result for Paper 1 (B.E./B.Tech) is expected to be released by April 20, 2026.
Q2: What is the official website to check JEE Main results?
The primary official website is jeemain.nta.nic.in. Other sites where results might be available include nta.ac.in and examinationservices.nic.in.
Q3: What credentials are required to check the result?
You will need your JEE Main Application Number and Password (or Date of Birth) to log in.
Q4: What if I forget my Application Number or Password?
On the login page, there will be options like "Forgot Application Number" or "Forgot Password" to help you recover your credentials, usually through your registered email or mobile number.
Q5: What is considered a good percentile in JEE Main?
A percentile above 95 is generally considered good, often corresponding to eligibility for top NITs/IIITs and qualification for JEE Advanced. For example, 120 marks typically correlate to a 95-97.5+ percentile.

We wish all the JEE Main aspirants the very best for their results. Stay calm, follow the steps, and prepare for the exciting journey ahead!
