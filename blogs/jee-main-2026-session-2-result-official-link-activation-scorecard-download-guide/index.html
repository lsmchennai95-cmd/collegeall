JEE Main 2026 Session 2 Result: Official Link Activation & Your Essential Scorecard Guide

The wait is almost over for lakhs of engineering aspirants! The National Testing Agency (NTA) is on the verge of declaring the Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) Main 2026 Session 2 results. While the official link is anticipated to be activated very soon, this comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from checking your scorecard to understanding the next steps in your academic journey.

The JEE Main 2026 Session 2 results are expected to be declared by April 20, 2026, with a slight possibility of extension until April 23. Candidates are strongly advised to keep their login credentials ready and regularly visit the official NTA websites for the most up-to-date information.

Official Links for JEE Main 2026 Results

Once activated, candidates can access their JEE Main Session 2 scorecards on the following official websites managed by the National Testing Agency:

✅ jeemain.nta.nic.in

✅ nta.ac.in

✅ examinationservices.nic.in

✅ ntaresults.nic.in

How to Check Your JEE Main 2026 Session 2 Scorecard: A Step-by-Step Guide

Follow these simple steps to download your JEE Main 2026 Session 2 scorecard once the result link is live:

1. Visit the Official NTA Website
Open your web browser and navigate to jeemain.nta.nic.in or any of the alternative official result portals mentioned above.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for a prominent link that says "Result of JEE (Main) Session 2" or "View Scorecard for JEE Main 2026 Session 2". Click on it.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login window. Here, you need to input your JEE Main Application Number and your Password or Date of Birth.
4. Complete the Security Check
Enter the security code (CAPTCHA) displayed on the screen accurately.
5. View Your Scorecard
Click on the "Submit" button. Your JEE Main 2026 Session 2 scorecard will then appear on your screen.
6. Download and Print for Future Reference
Download the scorecard and take multiple printouts. This document is crucial for future admission and counselling processes.

Understanding Your JEE Main 2026 Scorecard

The JEE Main 2026 scorecard is more than just marks; it's your gateway to top engineering institutions. Ensure you meticulously check all the details mentioned on it:

✅ Candidate's Name, Application Number, and Roll Number

✅ Father's Name and Mother's Name

✅ Subject-wise NTA Percentile Scores (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics)

✅ Total NTA Score (Overall Percentile)

✅ All India Rank (AIR)

✅ Category Rank (if applicable)

✅ JEE Advanced 2026 Qualification Status

✅ State of Eligibility and Nationality

✅ Disability Status (if applicable)

It's important to remember that JEE Main results are declared in a percentile format, which accounts for varying difficulty levels across different examination shifts through a normalization process.

For candidates who appeared in both Session 1 and Session 2, the "Best of Two" rule will be applied, meaning your highest percentile score from either session will be considered for the final merit list and All India Rank.

What's Next After JEE Main 2026 Results?

Once your results are out, the next critical phase begins: counselling and admissions. The Joint Seat Allocation Authority (JoSAA) is responsible for conducting the centralized counselling process for admissions to NITs, IIITs, GFTIs, and IITs (for JEE Advanced qualified candidates).

1. JEE Advanced Eligibility
If you have qualified in JEE Main and your rank falls within the top 2,50,000 candidates (across all categories), you are eligible to register for JEE Advanced 2026.
2. JoSAA Counselling
All qualified candidates (for NITs, IIITs, GFTIs, and IITs) must register for JoSAA counselling online. This involves choice filling (selecting preferred institutes and branches), seat allotment in multiple rounds, seat acceptance, fee payment, and document verification.
3. CSAB Special Rounds
After all JoSAA rounds, if vacant seats remain in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, the Central Seat Allocation Board (CSAB) conducts special counselling rounds.
4. Prepare Your Documents
Ensure you have all necessary documents ready for counselling, including your JEE Main 2026 scorecard and admit card, Class 10th and 12th mark sheets, category certificates (if applicable), and identity proof.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When is the JEE Main 2026 Session 2 result expected?
The JEE Main 2026 Session 2 results are expected to be announced by April 20, 2026, or possibly with a slight delay until April 23.
Q2: What details are required to check the JEE Main result?
You will need your JEE Main Application Number and your Password or Date of Birth to log in and check your result.
Q3: What if I appeared in both Session 1 and Session 2?
The NTA considers your best percentile score out of the two sessions for preparing the final merit list and All India Rank.
Q4: What should I do after checking my JEE Main result?
Download and print your scorecard, check your eligibility for JEE Advanced, and prepare for JoSAA counselling. Keep all necessary documents ready.
