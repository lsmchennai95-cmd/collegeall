TS EAMCET Counselling 2026: Your Essential Roadmap to College Admissions

The wait is over for aspiring engineers, agriculturists, and pharmacists in Telangana! The Telangana Council of Higher Education (TGCHE) has officially commenced the TS EAMCET (now known as TG EAPCET) 2026 counselling process, marking the crucial phase for admissions into various undergraduate courses across the state. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every essential detail, from registration and fee payment to web options and seat allotment, ensuring you're well-prepared for a successful counselling journey.

Key Highlights of TS EAMCET 2026 Counselling

✅ **Official Start:** Counselling commenced on June 19, 2026.

✅ **Official Website:** All activities are conducted via tgeapcet.nic.in.

✅ **Phased Process:** Counselling will be conducted in three distinct phases: First, Second, and Final, followed by internal sliding and spot admissions.

✅ **Courses Covered:** Admissions are for Engineering, Agriculture, and Pharmacy programs.

✅ **Online Process:** The entire counselling is online, except for the mandatory in-person certificate verification.

Step-by-Step TS EAMCET 2026 Counselling Process

The counselling process involves several crucial steps. Follow them diligently to secure your desired seat:

1. Payment of Processing Fee & Online Registration
Visit the official website, tgeapcet.nic.in, click on the "PAYMENT OF PROCESSING FEE" link, and complete the registration. You'll need your TS EAMCET Hall Ticket Number, Rank, and Date of Birth. The processing fee is Rs 1,200 for OC/BC candidates and Rs 600 for SC/ST candidates. This can be paid online.
2. Slot Booking for Certificate Verification
After successful fee payment, proceed to book a slot for certificate verification at a designated Help Line Centre (HLC). Select your preferred date and time for verification.
3. Certificate Verification (In-Person)
Attend the certificate verification process at your chosen Help Line Centre on the allotted date and time. This is a mandatory offline step.
4. Web Option Entry
Once your certificates are verified, log in to the official portal to exercise your web options. Carefully select your preferred colleges and courses in order of priority. It is advisable to fill as many options as possible to maximize your chances of securing a seat.
5. Freezing and Mock Allotment
After selecting your options, you must 'freeze' them before the deadline. A mock seat allotment will be displayed to give you an idea of probable seat allocation based on your choices and rank. You can then modify your options if needed before the final freezing.
6. Provisional Seat Allotment
The provisional seat allotment result will be released on the specified date. Log in to download your Provisional Allotment Order.
7. Tuition Fee Payment & Self-Reporting
If you are allotted a seat, pay the prescribed tuition fee online and self-report through the official website within the given timeframe. Failing to do so will result in the cancellation of your allotted seat.
8. Physical Reporting at Allotted College
For the second phase, physical reporting at the allotted college with original documents and a hard copy of the allotment order and fee receipt is mandatory to confirm your admission.
Documents Required for Verification

Ensure you have the following original documents along with their photocopies ready for the certificate verification process:

✅ TS EAMCET 2026 Rank Card

✅ TS EAMCET 2026 Hall Ticket

✅ Aadhaar Card

✅ SSC (Class 10) Mark Sheet

✅ Intermediate (Class 11 & 12) Mark Sheets

✅ Transfer Certificate (TC)

✅ Income Certificate (if applicable, issued after January 1, 2026)

✅ Caste Certificate (if applicable, for SC/ST/BC candidates)

✅ Residence Certificate (for private candidates/local status) (if applicable)

Counselling Fee Details: Candidates must pay a processing fee to participate in the counselling. For OC/BC candidates, the fee is Rs 1,200, while SC/ST candidates need to pay Rs 600. The payment must be made online before slot booking.
Eligibility Criteria

To be eligible for TS EAMCET 2026 counselling, candidates must meet the following academic requirements in their Intermediate or equivalent examination:

✅ General Category (OC): A minimum of 45% marks in group subjects.

✅ Other Categories (SC/ST/BC): A minimum of 40% marks in group subjects.

The TS EAMCET 2026 counselling process is a critical gateway to your higher education in Telangana. By meticulously following the schedule, understanding each step, and preparing all necessary documents, you can significantly enhance your chances of securing a seat in your desired course and college. Keep a close watch on the official website, tgeapcet.nic.in, for any further announcements and updates. Good luck!
