TNPSC Group 4 Vacancy 2026: Your Gateway to Tamil Nadu Government Service!

Are you aspiring for a stable and respected career in the Tamil Nadu government? The Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC) Group 4 examination for 2026 is one of the most sought-after recruitment drives, attracting lakhs of candidates every year. This comprehensive guide will equip you with all the essential information regarding the TNPSC Group 4 Vacancy 2026, including important dates, eligibility criteria, key posts, exam patterns, and crucial preparation tips to help you secure your dream job.

Key Dates for TNPSC Group 4 2026

Mark your calendars! The TNPSC has released its Annual Planner for 2026, providing aspiring candidates with a clear roadmap for the upcoming Group 4 examination.

✅ Official Notification Release Date: October 6, 2026

✅ Online Application Period: Starts with notification release, expected to close around November 5, 2026

✅ TNPSC Group 4 Exam Date: December 20, 2026

The detailed notification will be released on the official TNPSC website (www.tnpsc.gov.in) and will contain specific information on the number of vacancies, application procedures, and more.

Anticipated Vacancy Details

While the precise number of vacancies for TNPSC Group 4 2026 will be officially announced with the notification on October 6, 2026, there are indications of a significant number of openings. This recruitment cycle is expected to fill various positions across different departments in the Tamil Nadu government.

Posts Covered Under TNPSC Group 4

The TNPSC Group 4 examination recruits candidates for a wide array of entry-level positions, including but not limited to:

  • Village Administrative Officer (VAO)
  • Junior Assistant
  • Typist
  • Steno-Typist (Grade 3)
  • Field Surveyor
  • Draftsman
  • Forest Guard
  • Forest Watcher
  • Junior Executive
  • Junior Revenue Inspector
  • Personal Clerk
  • Assistant
  • Bill Collector

These roles offer a promising career path with opportunities for growth within the Tamil Nadu state administration.

TNPSC Group 4 Eligibility Criteria 2026

Meeting the eligibility criteria is paramount for any aspiring candidate. The TNPSC Group 4 eligibility conditions encompass age limits, educational qualifications, and language proficiency. Candidates must fulfill these requirements as of July 1, 2026.

1. Age Limit (as on July 1, 2026)

The minimum age to apply for most Group 4 posts is 18 years. However, for specific posts like VAO and Forest posts, the minimum age is 21 years. The upper age limit varies based on the category and specific post, with relaxations for reserved categories.

Category Minimum Age Maximum Age (Most Posts) Maximum Age (VAO & Forest Posts)
General / OC 18 Years 32 Years 32 Years
BC / BCM / MBC / DNC 18 Years 42 Years 37-42 Years (Varies by post)
SC / SC(A) / ST 18 Years 42 Years 37-42 Years (Varies by post)
Differently Abled Persons 18 Years 10 years relaxation on upper age limit
Ex-servicemen 18 Years Up to 53 years (SC/ST/OBC), 48 years (Others)

Note: Higher qualifications may remove the maximum age limit for certain categories as per the official notification.

2. Educational Qualification

The fundamental educational requirement for most TNPSC Group 4 posts is a pass in the SSLC (10th Standard Public Examination) or its equivalent from a recognized board. It's crucial to note that simply holding a higher degree does not exempt candidates from the SSLC requirement.

Technical Qualifications for Specific Posts:

  • Typist & Steno-Typist: Candidates must possess a "Certificate Course in Computer on Office Automation" awarded by the Technical Education Department. However, those with degrees or diplomas in Computer Science, Applications, or Information Technology are typically exempted from this certificate.
  • Typewriting/Shorthand Certificates: Relevant higher/lower grade certificates in Typewriting and/or Shorthand are mandatory for Typist and Steno-Typist roles, respectively.
3. Language Proficiency

A strong understanding of the Tamil language is a prerequisite for TNPSC Group 4. Candidates are generally expected to have:

  • Passed SSLC or equivalent, HSC, or a Degree with Tamil as one of the subjects.
  • Studied their high school course in Tamil medium.
  • Passed the SSLC or equivalent in Tamil medium.
  • Alternatively, passed the Second Class Language Test (Full Test) in Tamil conducted by TNPSC.

If candidates do not upload the required documents proving Tamil proficiency, they must pass the Second Class Language Test in Tamil within two years from the date of appointment.

TNPSC Group 4 Exam Pattern 2026

The TNPSC Group 4 examination is a single-stage written test conducted in offline (OMR-based) mode.

✅ Exam Duration: 3 hours

✅ Total Questions: 200 Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

✅ Total Marks: 300 marks (each question carries 1.5 marks)

✅ Negative Marking: There is no negative marking.

✅ Minimum Qualifying Marks: 90 out of 300 (candidates must obtain 60/150 marks in Part A to be eligible for Part B assessment).

Selection Process

The selection process for TNPSC Group 4 typically involves the following stages:

  1. Written Examination: Candidates must clear the objective-type written test.
  2. Document Verification: Shortlisted candidates will undergo document verification to confirm their eligibility.
  3. Counselling: Based on merit and vacancies, candidates are called for counselling.
  4. Physical/Endurance Tests: Applicable for certain posts like Forest Guard and Forest Watcher.
Preparing for Success

With the exam date set for December 20, 2026, now is the opportune moment to intensify your preparation. Regularly visit the official TNPSC website (tnpsc.gov.in) for the latest updates, syllabus, and previous years' question papers.

Stay updated, prepare diligently, and seize this excellent opportunity to join the Tamil Nadu government service!
