Maharashtra SSC Result 2026 Declared Today: Your Complete Guide to Scores, Statistics, and Next Steps

The wait is finally over for over 16 lakh students as the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE) announces the SSC Class 10 results today, May 8, 2026, at 1 PM. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about checking your results, understanding the statistics, and planning your academic future.

Key Highlights of Maharashtra SSC Result 2026

✅ Result Declaration Date: Today, Friday, May 8, 2026

✅ Declaration Time: 1:00 PM (IST)

✅ Total Students Appeared: Over 16.15 lakh

✅ Official Websites: mahresult.nic.in, mahahsscboard.in, sscresult.mkcl.org, sscresult.mahahsscboard.in

✅ Credentials Required: Roll Number/Seat Number and Mother's Maiden Name

✅ Alternative Access: DigiLocker and SMS services are available

How to Check Your Maharashtra SSC Result 2026

Students can access their provisional marksheets through multiple online platforms. Ensure you have your admit card ready with your Roll Number or Seat Number and your Mother's Maiden Name for a smooth process.

1. Checking Online via Official Websites

Follow these steps to download your Maharashtra SSC Result 2026 from the official portals:

  • Visit any of the official websites: mahresult.nic.in, mahahsscboard.in, sscresult.mkcl.org, or sscresult.mahahsscboard.in.
  • Look for the "Maharashtra SSC Result 2026" or "SSC Result Maharashtra Board" link on the homepage and click on it.
  • Enter your Roll Number or Seat Number.
  • Carefully input your Mother's Maiden Name.
  • Submit the details to view your provisional marksheet.
  • Download and print your scorecard for future reference.
2. Accessing Results via DigiLocker

Digital marksheets are also available through the DigiLocker app, providing a convenient and secure way to store your academic documents.

  • Log in to your DigiLocker account using your Username and Password.
  • Sync your Aadhar Number on the 'Profile' page.
  • Click on 'Pull Partner Documents' in the left sidebar.
  • Select 'Maharashtra State Board of Secondary & Higher Secondary Education'.
  • Choose 'SSC Marksheet'.
  • Enter the required details, such as your Roll Number.
  • Click 'Get Document' to access your digital marksheet.
  • Download and save it for future use.
3. Checking Results via SMS

In case of heavy website traffic, students can opt for the SMS service to receive their results.

  • Type MHSSC (space) your Seat Number.
  • Send the SMS to 57766.
  • Your result will be sent to your registered mobile number.

What's Next After the Results?

After checking their provisional results, students have several options regarding their marksheets, re-evaluation, and supplementary examinations.

1. Collecting Original Marksheets

The online marksheet is provisional. Students must collect their original mark sheets and certificates from their respective schools a few days after the online declaration.

2. Application for Verification and Revaluation

Students who are not satisfied with their marks can apply for verification of marks or revaluation of their answer sheets.

  • Applications for verification are typically accepted online on the official website (verification.mh-ssc.ac.in) in May/June 2026.
  • The results of revaluation are usually released in June.
3. Supplementary Examinations

For students who do not pass the examinations in their first attempt, the MSBSHSE conducts supplementary exams to save an academic year.

  • The application forms and timetable for supplementary exams will be released in May 2026.
  • These exams are generally held in June-July 2026.
  • Results for the supplementary exams are usually declared in July/August 2026.
  • Students need to secure at least 35 marks out of 100 in all subjects to pass the SSC examinations.
