MPBSE 10th & 12th Result 2026 Declared: Direct Links & How to Check Your Scorecard

The wait is finally over for lakhs of students across Madhya Pradesh! The Madhya Pradesh Board of Secondary Education (MPBSE) has officially declared the Class 10th (High School) and Class 12th (Higher Secondary) Board Examination Results for 2026. This highly anticipated announcement marks a crucial milestone for students, paving the way for their future academic and career pursuits.

Breaking News: The MPBSE 10th and 12th Results for 2026 were declared on April 15, 2026, at 11 AM. Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav officially released the results.

The overall pass percentage for Class 10 stands at 73.42%, while Class 12 recorded an impressive 76.01% pass rate.

MPBSE 10th & 12th Result 2026: Key Highlights

Here's a quick overview of the essential information regarding the MP Board Results 2026:

✅ Result Declaration Date: April 15, 2026

✅ Result Time: 11:00 AM

✅ Declared By: Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav

✅ Official Websites: mpbse.nic.in, mpresults.nic.in, mpbse.mponline.gov.in, result.mponline.gov.in, digilocker.gov.in

✅ Details Required: Roll Number and Application Number

✅ Class 10 Pass Percentage: 73.42%

✅ Class 12 Pass Percentage: 76.01%

How to Check Your MP Board Result 2026 Online (Step-by-Step Guide)

Students can easily access their MPBSE Class 10th and 12th results online by following these simple steps. Ensure you have your admit card handy for your roll number and application number.

1. Visit the Official MPBSE Website
Open your web browser and navigate to one of the official result portals:
  • mpbse.nic.in
  • mpresults.nic.in
  • mpbse.mponline.gov.in
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the link related to "MP Board 10th Result 2026" or "MP Board 12th Result 2026". The link will typically be highlighted or prominently displayed.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your login details. Carefully input your Roll Number and Application Number as mentioned on your admit card.
4. Submit and View Your Result
After entering the details, click on the "Submit" button. Your MPBSE Class 10th or 12th result will be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print Your Scorecard
Review all the details on your provisional marksheet. It is highly recommended to download the result page and take a printout for future reference. This digital copy will serve as a provisional marksheet until you receive the original from your school.

Direct Links to Your MPBSE Scorecard

To facilitate easy access and manage high traffic, MPBSE results are available on multiple official and partner websites. Here are the direct links:

Important Details to Verify on Your Marksheet

Once you have downloaded your provisional marksheet, it's crucial to check all the details carefully. Any discrepancies should be reported to your school authorities immediately. Ensure the following information is correct:

✅ Student's Name

✅ Father's Name

✅ Mother's Name

✅ Roll Number

✅ Application Number

✅ Subject-wise Marks

✅ Total Marks

✅ Division/Grade

✅ Qualifying Status (Pass/Fail)

Understanding Your MPBSE Results: FAQs

1. What are the passing marks for MPBSE Class 10th and 12th exams 2026?
Students need to secure a minimum of 33% marks in each subject and in aggregate to pass the MP Board exams.
2. What if I fail in one or two subjects?
If a student fails in one or two subjects, they will have the opportunity to appear for supplementary examinations. The MPBSE conducts these exams to allow students to improve their performance without losing an academic year.
3. Can I apply for re-evaluation or re-checking?
Yes, students who are not satisfied with their marks can apply for re-evaluation or re-checking of their answer sheets. Details and application forms for this process are usually released shortly after the main results.
4. When were the MPBSE 10th and 12th exams held in 2026?
The MP Board Class 10th exams were conducted from February 13 to March 6, 2026. The Class 12th exams were held from February 7 to March 7, 2026.

Congratulations to all the students who have successfully cleared their MPBSE Class 10th and 12th examinations! Your hard work and dedication have paid off. For those who may not have achieved their desired results, remember that this is just one step in your academic journey, and there are always opportunities to improve and succeed.
