JAC 11th Result 2026 DECLARED: All You Need to Know!

The wait is finally over for lakhs of students in Jharkhand! The Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC) has officially announced the results for the Class 11th Annual Examination 2026. This much-anticipated declaration brings clarity for students as they prepare to step into the crucial Class 12th board year. Let's dive into all the essential details, from how to check your results to the impressive statistics and grading system.

The Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC) declared the Class 11th results 2026 on April 14, 2026.

How to Check Your JAC 11th Result 2026 Online

Accessing your JAC Class 11th scorecard is a straightforward process. Follow these simple steps to view and download your provisional marksheet:

1. Visit the Official JAC Results Portal
Navigate to the official websites of the Jharkhand Academic Council: jacresults.com or jac.jharkhand.gov.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, click on the prominent link that reads "Results of Class XI Examination - 2026" or "JAC 11th/XI Examination 2026".
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be prompted to enter your Roll Code and Roll Number, as provided on your admit card.
4. View Your Result
After entering the details, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button. Your detailed marksheet will then be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print
It is highly recommended to download a PDF copy or take a printout of your provisional marksheet for immediate reference and future academic procedures. Remember, this is a provisional mark sheet; the original hardcopy will be distributed by your respective schools in the coming weeks.

Impressive Performance: JAC 11th Result 2026 Statistics

The Jharkhand Academic Council has reported an outstanding performance for the Class 11th examination this year. The examinations were conducted from February 25 to February 28, 2026, using OMR sheets for five core subjects, with each subject comprising 40 multiple-choice questions and an additional 10 marks from internal assessments.

Notably, female students have slightly outperformed their male counterparts, maintaining a trend of strong academic performance. The results reflect a high level of participation and successful completion across the state.

Understanding the JAC Grading System

The JAC Class 11th results are presented using a grade-based system, which evaluates student performance beyond just raw marks. Here's a breakdown of the grading scale:

✅ A+ (Excellent): 80% and above

✅ A (Very Good): 60% to less than 80%

✅ B (Good): 45% to less than 60%

✅ C (Average): 33% to less than 45%

✅ D (Marginal/Fail): Below 33%

What Details Are on Your Marksheet?

Your online provisional marksheet will contain comprehensive information, including:

✅ Roll Code

✅ Roll Number

✅ Registration Number

✅ SL. No.

✅ Date of Birth

✅ School Name

✅ Student Name

✅ Father's Name

✅ Mother's Name

✅ Subject-wise Marks

✅ Total Marks

✅ Result Status (Promoted, Marginal, Incomplete)

✅ Percentage

It's crucial to carefully verify all these details. In case of any discrepancies, students must report the issue to their respective schools at the earliest for correction.

Next Steps for Students

With the Class 11th results now out, students can look forward to their promotion to Class 12. This result is a crucial academic checkpoint that sets the stage for the upcoming board examinations. The Jharkhand Academic Council is expected to expedite the evaluation process for Class 10 and 12 results, which are anticipated in late April or early May.

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared the JAC 11th Examination 2026! Your hard work and dedication have paid off, and we wish you the very best for your future academic endeavors.
