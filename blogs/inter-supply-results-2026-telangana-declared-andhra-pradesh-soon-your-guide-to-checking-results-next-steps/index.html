Inter Supply Results 2026: Navigating Your Path Forward

The anticipation is finally over for lakhs of students across Telangana, and the wait is almost through for those in Andhra Pradesh! The Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examinations (IPASE) 2026 results are crucial for many to continue their academic journey. Here's a comprehensive guide to understanding the latest announcements, checking your scores, and planning your next steps.

Telangana Inter Supply Results 2026 Declared!

Great news for students who appeared for the Telangana Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examinations (IPASE) 2026! The Telangana Board of Intermediate Education (TGBIE) officially declared the TS Inter Supply Results 2026 on June 11, 2026, at 11:30 AM. These examinations were held from May 13 to May 21, 2026, for theory papers, with practical exams conducted between May 22 and May 25, 2026.

Direct Link to Check TS Inter Supply Results 2026: Students can access their results directly on the official TGBIE websites: tsbie.cgg.gov.in and results.cgg.gov.in.

How to Check Your TS Inter Supply Results 2026

1. Visit the Official Websites
Navigate to either tsbie.cgg.gov.in or results.cgg.gov.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, click on the "IPASE Result 2026" or "TS Inter Supplementary Results 2026" link.
3. Select Your Details
Choose your examination year (1st Year or 2nd Year) and your stream (General or Vocational).
4. Enter Hall Ticket Number
Carefully enter your Hall Ticket Number in the designated field.
5. View and Download
Click "Submit" to view your marks memo. It is advisable to download and print a copy for future reference.

TS Inter Supply Result 2026: Key Statistics

✅ The overall pass percentage for TS Inter 1st Year General examinations stands at an impressive 68.71%.

✅ For the 2nd Year General examinations, the pass percentage is 47.70%.

✅ Girls have once again demonstrated exceptional performance, outperforming boys in both 1st and 2nd-year supplementary exams.

Andhra Pradesh Inter Supply Results 2026 Expected Soon!

For students in Andhra Pradesh, the wait for the AP Inter 2nd Year Supplementary Results 2026 is almost over. The Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP), is expected to release these results tentatively on June 15, 2026. The Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examinations (IPASE) 2026 in AP were conducted from May 21 to June 5, 2026. Notably, one exam originally scheduled for May 28 was postponed to June 5 due to the Bakrid public holiday.

Where to Check AP Inter Supply Results 2026: Once released, students can find their results on the official BIEAP website: resultsbie.ap.gov.in and bie.ap.gov.in.

Checking Your AP Inter Supply Results 2026

Similar to the TS Inter process, students will need their Hall Ticket Number and Date of Birth to access their AP Inter 2nd Year Supplementary Results 2026 online. The results will also be made available on DigiLocker, providing another convenient way to access your marks memo.

Important Information for All Intermediate Supplementary Examinees

Whether you've received your results or are eagerly awaiting them, here are some critical points to remember:

✅ The results published online are provisional. Students are advised to collect their original marks memos and certificates from their respective junior colleges.

✅ To be declared passed in the Intermediate examinations, students must secure a minimum of 35% marks in each subject and in aggregate.

✅ For any discrepancies or queries regarding the TS Inter Supply Results, students can contact the TGBIE helpline at 040-24603315.

What's Next After Results?

Receiving your supplementary results opens up new avenues for your academic journey. For those who successfully cleared the exams, this is a moment to celebrate and focus on future admissions, be it for higher education or vocational courses. The timely release of these results ensures that students can participate in upcoming admission processes like TG EAPCET and TG DOST without missing out on important deadlines.

For students who may not have achieved the desired outcome, it's important to remember that this is not the end of the road. Explore options for re-evaluation, recounting, or consider other educational pathways. Many institutions offer diverse programs and opportunities, and proper guidance can help you find the right fit.

We wish all students the very best as they embark on their next academic adventures!
