SSC Selection Post Phase 14 Vacancies 2026: Your Gateway to Government Service

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has once again opened doors to aspiring candidates seeking a career in various government departments with the release of the SSC Selection Post Phase 14 Notification 2026. This eagerly awaited recruitment drive presents a significant opportunity for individuals with diverse educational backgrounds, ranging from matriculation to graduation. With a substantial number of vacancies announced, it's a prime chance to secure a coveted position in the public sector.

The official notification, released on April 13, 2026, details the application process, eligibility criteria, and important dates for candidates keen on joining the central government machinery.

Key Highlights of SSC Selection Post Phase 14

✅ Total Vacancies: A massive 3003 posts are available across various departments.

✅ Application Window: Applications commenced on April 13, 2026, and conclude on May 4, 2026.

✅ Educational Levels: Opportunities for 10th pass, 12th pass, and graduate candidates.

✅ Exam Type: Computer-Based Examination (CBE) scheduled for June 2026.

This year's recruitment drive for SSC Selection Post Phase 14 is one of the biggest, offering more than 3000 vacancies for various Group B and Group C posts like Laboratory Assistant, Deputy Ranger, and Upper Division Clerk (UDC).

A total of 3003 vacancies have been announced under the SSC Selection Post Phase 14 Recruitment 2026. These positions are distributed across different categories, ensuring broad representation.

Eligibility Criteria: Are You Qualified?

To be considered for the SSC Selection Post Phase 14, candidates must meet specific eligibility criteria, including educational qualifications and age limits. These requirements vary depending on the level of the post (Matriculation, Higher Secondary, or Graduation & above).

1. Educational Qualification

The minimum educational qualification depends on the specific post you are applying for:

  • Matriculation Level: Candidates must have passed Class 10th from a recognized board.
  • Higher Secondary (10+2) Level: Candidates must have passed Class 12th from a recognized board.
  • Graduation & above Level: Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree in any stream from a recognized university.
2. Age Limit

The age limit is calculated as of January 1, 2026. The minimum age to apply is generally 18 years. The maximum age can range from 30 years to 35 or even 42 years, depending on the specific post. Candidates are advised to refer to the official notification for post-wise age relaxation details.

Application Process: How to Apply

The application process for SSC Selection Post Phase 14 is entirely online. It's crucial to follow the steps carefully to ensure a successful submission.

1. One-Time Registration (OTR)

If you haven't already, register yourself on the official SSC website (www.ssc.gov.in) by completing the One-Time Registration (OTR) process. This involves filling in basic details and creating login credentials.

2. Fill the Application Form

Log in with your registered ID and password and click on the "Selection Post Phase 14" application link. Carefully fill in all personal, academic, and communication details. Ensure all information matches your official documents.

3. Upload Documents

Upload a live photograph (without cap/glasses and with a plain background) and a scanned signature as per the prescribed specifications. Keep academic certificates, ID proof, and category certificates (if applicable) ready for reference.

4. Application Fee Payment

A fee of ₹100/- is applicable for General and OBC male candidates. Women candidates and those belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), and Ex-Servicemen (ESM) are exempted from fee payment. The fee can be paid online via BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Cards.

5. Review and Submit

Review your application thoroughly before final submission. It's advisable to download and print a copy of the submitted application form for future reference.

Important Note: Candidates applying for multiple posts must submit separate applications for each. Discrepancies or multiple applications for the same post may lead to disqualification.

Selection Process and Exam Pattern

The selection process for SSC Selection Post Phase 14 primarily involves a Computer-Based Examination (CBE). For certain posts, a skill test will also be conducted.

1. Computer-Based Examination (CBE)

There will be three separate Computer-Based Examinations (Objective Type Multiple Choice Questions) based on the minimum educational qualification required for the post (Matriculation, Higher Secondary, and Graduation & above levels).

The exam pattern is as follows:

Subject Number of Questions Maximum Marks
General Intelligence 25 50
General Awareness 25 50
Quantitative Aptitude 25 50
English Language 25 50
Total 100 200

The duration of the examination is 60 minutes. There is a negative marking of 0.50 marks for each incorrect answer.

2. Skill Test (If Applicable)

For certain posts, skill tests like Typing, Data Entry, or Computer Proficiency Tests will be conducted. These tests are qualifying in nature.

3. Document Verification (DV)

Candidates who qualify the CBE will be required to upload all necessary documents on the official website for verification. Failure to do so may lead to cancellation of their application.
