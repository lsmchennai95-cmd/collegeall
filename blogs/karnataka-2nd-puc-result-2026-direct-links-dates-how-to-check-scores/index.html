Karnataka 2nd PUC Result 2026: Your Ultimate Guide to Checking Scores!

The wait is almost over for thousands of students across Karnataka! The Karnataka School Examination and Assessment Board (KSEAB) is all set to release the much-anticipated 2nd PUC Result 2026. This result marks a pivotal moment in your academic journey, paving the way for higher education and future career prospects. We understand the anticipation and are here to provide you with all the essential information regarding the result links, declaration dates, and easy steps to access your scores.

The Karnataka 2nd PUC Result 2026 for Examination-1 is highly expected to be declared on April 7, 2026. Keep your registration details ready!

Where and How to Check Your 2nd PUC Result 2026

Once declared, students can quickly access their results through various official channels. Here's a detailed guide on how to check your Karnataka 2nd PUC Result 2026:

1. Visit the Official Websites
The primary portals for checking your results are:
  • karresults.nic.in
  • kseab.karnataka.gov.in
  • result.bspucpa.in (Alternative link)
  • result.proed.in (Alternative link)
Navigate to any of these websites on the result declaration day.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the link that says "Karnataka 2nd PUC Examination-1 Result 2026" or similar. Click on it to proceed.
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page where you need to enter your details. Ensure you have your:
  • Registration Number / SATS Number / Vmitra Registration Number / Enrollment Number
  • Date of Birth (as per your admit card)
Carefully input the required information.
4. Submit and View Your Scorecard
After entering your credentials, click on the "Submit" button. Your Karnataka 2nd PUC Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print for Future Reference
It is highly recommended to download your scorecard in PDF format and take a printout for your records. This provisional marksheet will be crucial for college admissions until the original marksheet is issued.

Alternative Methods to Access Your Results

✅ Via SMS: For those facing internet connectivity issues, the SMS facility is a reliable backup.
Type: KAR12 (Your Registration Number) and send it to 56263.
You will receive your 2nd PUC Result 2026 on the same mobile number.

✅ Through DigiLocker: DigiLocker has confirmed that the Karnataka Class 12 Results 2026 will be available on its platform.
Visit digilocker.gov.in or use the DigiLocker app for secure access to your digital marksheet.

Important Dates and Statistics

The 2nd PUC examinations for 2026 were conducted between February 28 and March 17, 2026. A total of 7,10,363 students registered for the exams this year across 5,174 colleges in the state.

What's Next? Post-Result Information

Once you have your results, it's natural to consider your next steps. Here are a few key points to keep in mind:

Re-evaluation and Scrutiny
If you are unsatisfied with your marks, the board will provide an opportunity to apply for re-evaluation or scrutiny of your answer scripts. Details regarding application procedures, fees, and deadlines will be released through official notifications by KSEAB.
Three Annual Examinations
This year, KSEAB has introduced a system of three annual examinations (Exam 1, 2, and 3) for 2nd PUC students, aiming to eliminate the concept of supplementary exams. This offers students more opportunities to improve their scores.
Free Bus Travel for Students
In a student-friendly initiative, the School Education and Literacy Minister announced free bus travel for students appearing for SSLC and PU examinations on Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC) and Bangalore Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC) buses upon producing valid hall tickets.

We wish all the Karnataka 2nd PUC students the very best for their results! Stay calm, follow the official channels, and celebrate your achievements.
