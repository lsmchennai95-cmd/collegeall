SSC GD Constable City Intimation 2026: Your Essential Guide to Exam City & Dates

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially released the much-anticipated City Intimation Slip for the Constable (General Duty) Examination 2026! This is a crucial update for all candidates who have applied for posts in the Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), SSF, Assam Rifles, and Sepoy in the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB). If you're among the thousands preparing for this significant recruitment drive, it's time to check your allotted exam city and dates to plan your logistics effectively. The exam city intimation slip was released on April 19, 2026, on the official SSC website.

What is the SSC GD City Intimation Slip?

The SSC GD City Intimation Slip is an advance notification provided by the Staff Selection Commission to inform candidates about their examination city, date, and shift timing. Its primary purpose is to assist candidates in making necessary travel and accommodation arrangements well before the actual exam. It is important to remember that this slip is NOT your admit card. The official admit card, containing the exact exam center address and reporting time, will be released a few days prior to your scheduled examination date.

✅ Released on April 19, 2026.

✅ Informs about exam city, date, and shift.

✅ Helps in advance travel and accommodation planning.

✅ Not valid for entry into the exam hall; it is NOT the admit card.

✅ Admit cards will be released 2-3 days before the exam.

The Computer-Based Test (CBT) for SSC GD Constable 2026 is scheduled to be held in multiple phases across various centers nationwide. Here's a quick overview of the important dates:

  • Week 1: April 27 – May 2, 2026

How to Download Your SSC GD City Intimation Slip 2026

Accessing your exam city details is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to download your SSC GD Constable City Intimation Slip:

1. Visit the Official SSC Website
Open your web browser and go to the official Staff Selection Commission website: www.ssc.gov.in.
2. Navigate to the City Intimation Link
On the homepage, look for the "Information regarding City of Examination – Constable (GD) Examination 2026" notice or a similar link related to the SSC GD Exam City Slip 2026. This is often found in the 'Latest News' section or under the 'Admit Card' tab.
3. Log In with Your Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your Registration Number (Username) and Password (or Date of Birth, as per your registration details).
4. Enter Captcha and Submit
Carefully enter the captcha code displayed on the screen and click on the 'Submit' button.
5. View and Download Your Slip
Your SSC GD Constable Exam City, Exam Date, and Shift details will be displayed on the screen. Review all the information carefully.
6. Print for Future Reference
Download the slip as a PDF and take a printout. It is advisable to keep both a digital and a physical copy for your records and exam day preparation.

Important Points to Remember

✅ Verify all details on your City Intimation Slip immediately. If there are any discrepancies, contact SSC authorities for correction.

✅ This slip is only for planning purposes. You will still need your official Admit Card for entry into the examination hall.

✅ Plan your travel and accommodation in advance to avoid last-minute stress.

✅ Stay updated with the official SSC website for further announcements regarding admit cards and exam day guidelines.

SSC GD Constable Exam Pattern (CBT)

Familiarizing yourself with the exam pattern is crucial for effective preparation. The Computer-Based Test (CBT) will consist of one objective-type paper with the following structure:

Subject Number of Questions Maximum Marks
General Intelligence and Reasoning 20 40
General Knowledge and General Awareness 20 40
Elementary Mathematics 20 40
English/Hindi 20 40
Total 80 160

Note: Each question carries 2 marks. There will be a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer. There will be no provision for re-evaluation or re-checking of scores.

The release of the SSC GD Constable City Intimation Slip is a significant step towards the examination. Ensure you download your slip promptly and make all necessary preparations. Good luck to all the aspirants!
