AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026: Your Complete Guide to Scores, Re-evaluation, and Supplementary Exams

The wait is over! Andhra Pradesh Intermediate 2nd Year Results for 2026 have been officially declared. Here’s everything you need to know about checking your scores, understanding the process, and planning your next steps.

Results Declared: A Moment of Truth!

The Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP) announced the much-anticipated Intermediate Public Examination (IPE) 2026 results for 2nd year students on April 15, 2026, at 10:30 AM. This declaration marks a pivotal moment for thousands of students across the state, opening doors to higher education and future career paths. Similar to previous years, such as 2025 and 2024, the results were released around mid-April, ensuring timely progression for students.

The overall pass percentage for AP Inter 2nd Year in 2026 stood at an impressive 81%. This reflects a strong academic performance, continuing the trend of improved outcomes in government and government-managed institutions.
How to Check Your AP Inter 2nd Year Results 2026

Accessing your results is now easier than ever, with multiple platforms available to ensure a smooth experience even during peak traffic. Students will need their Hall Ticket Number and Date of Birth to check their scores.

1. Official Websites
The primary platforms to check your results are the official BIEAP portals.
  • resultsbie.ap.gov.in
  • bie.ap.gov.in
Simply visit either website, click on the link for "IPE February/March 2026 Second Year Results," enter your hall ticket number and date of birth, and submit. Your marks memo will be displayed instantly.
2. Third-Party Portals
Several trusted third-party websites also host the results to distribute the load.
  • manabadi.co.in
  • results.shiksha
  • schools9.com
3. WhatsApp Service (Mana Mitra)
In a convenient initiative, BIEAP has enabled result access via WhatsApp. Students can simply send a "Hi" message to 9552300009 to receive their marks memo instantly on their registered mobile number.
4. SMS Service & DigiLocker
Results can also be accessed through SMS services and DigiLocker, providing additional reliable options.

Girls have consistently outperformed boys in the AP Inter exams, a trend observed across both 1st and 2nd years. Government Junior Colleges also demonstrated significant improvement in their pass percentages for 2026.

Understanding Passing Criteria

To successfully clear the AP Inter 2nd Year examinations, students must meet specific passing requirements:

✅ A minimum of 35% marks in each individual subject.

✅ This 35% criterion applies to both the theory and practical components of subjects.

✅ An overall aggregate of 35% across all subjects is also required.

For subjects with different total marks, the passing marks vary accordingly. For example, for a 100-mark paper, 35 marks are required, while for a 75-mark paper (like Mathematics or Geography), 26 marks are needed, and for a 60-mark paper (Science subjects like Physics), 21 marks are required to pass.

Post-Result Options: Re-evaluation, Recounting, and Supplementary Exams

If you are not satisfied with your results or couldn't clear certain subjects, BIEAP provides avenues for recourse and improvement.

1. Recounting (RC) & Re-verification (RV)

Students who believe there might be an error in their marks can apply for recounting or re-verification.

  • Recounting (RC): Involves re-totaling the marks to ensure there are no calculation errors.
  • Re-verification (RV): Includes a comprehensive re-evaluation of the answer sheet by a different examiner, along with providing a scanned copy of the evaluated script.

Application Dates for 2026: The application window for Recounting and Re-verification will be open from April 20 to April 27, 2026.

Fees: Recounting costs ₹260 per subject, while Scanned Copy-cum-Revaluation costs ₹1300 per subject.

How to Apply: Applications are submitted online through the official BIEAP portal (bie.ap.gov.in or bieap.apcfss.in) within the stipulated deadline.

2. Supplementary Exams (IPASE)

For students who did not meet the minimum passing criteria in one or more subjects, the Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examinations (IPASE) offer a second chance to qualify. These are also known as improvement exams for students wishing to improve their marks.

Exam Dates for 2026:

  • Theory Exams: May 21 to June 4, 2026.
  • Practical Exams: June 7 to June 11, 2026, at district headquarters.

Application Dates for 2026: The registration process for supplementary exams is expected to take place from April 20 to April 27, 2026.

Exam Timings: 1st-year exams are usually held from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM, and 2nd-year exams from 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM.

Results: Supplementary exam results are generally released in June 2026.

"Even a small improvement in marks can create big opportunities. Supplementary exams are a second chance, not a fallback. Treat them with full seriousness."
What's Next?

Once you have your official marks memo, it's crucial for admissions to undergraduate (UG) courses and other professional programs. The online marks memo is essential for immediate applications, with original mark sheets to be issued by the board later through your respective colleges.

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared their AP Inter 2nd Year exams! For those considering re-evaluation or supplementary exams, remember that these are valuable opportunities to achieve your academic goals. Stay positive and prepare well for your next steps!
