COMEDK UGET Result 2026 Declared Today at 6 PM: Direct Link to Download Scorecard & Check Rank

The wait is finally over for thousands of aspiring engineering students! The Consortium of Medical, Engineering and Dental Colleges of Karnataka (COMEDK) has officially declared the COMEDK UGET 2026 results today, May 29, 2026. Initially slated for 4 PM, the result declaration was delayed and is now live at 6 PM. Candidates can now access their scorecards and ranks directly from the official COMEDK website, comedk.org.

This is a pivotal moment for over 1.29 lakh students who appeared for the COMEDK UGET 2026 examination, held on May 9, 2026, vying for approximately 20,000 engineering seats across more than 150 participating colleges in Karnataka.

How to Download Your COMEDK UGET 2026 Scorecard and Check Rank

Accessing your COMEDK UGET 2026 rank card is a straightforward process. Follow the steps below to download your results and check your All India Rank (AIR):

1. Visit the Official COMEDK Website
Navigate to the official website of COMEDK: www.comedk.org.
2. Locate the Result/Login Link
On the homepage, look for the "COMEDK 2026 Rank Card" or "COMEDK Login" link.
3. Enter Your Credentials
Click on the link and enter your Application Number (or User ID) and Password in the designated fields.
4. Submit and View Result
After entering your details, click on the "Submit" or "Sign In" button. Your COMEDK UGET 2026 rank card will be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print
Carefully review all the details on your scorecard, including your subject-wise marks, total score, percentile, and All India Rank. Download the scorecard as a PDF and take a printout for future reference and the upcoming counselling process.

Important Details on Your COMEDK UGET Scorecard

Your COMEDK UGET 2026 scorecard is a crucial document. Ensure the following details are accurate:

✅ Applicant's Name and Registration Number

✅ Date of Birth and Category (if applicable)

✅ COMEDK Test Admission Ticket (TAT) Number

✅ Subject-wise Marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics

✅ Total Marks Scored out of 180

✅ Percentile Score and All India Rank (AIR)

✅ Qualifying Status

If you find any discrepancies on your scorecard, immediately contact the COMEDK helpdesk to get it rectified. Timely action is crucial to avoid issues during counselling.

What's Next? Understanding the COMEDK UGET 2026 Counselling Process

The declaration of results marks the beginning of the COMEDK UGET 2026 counselling process, which is conducted entirely online. Qualified candidates must participate in this centralized process to secure admission into engineering programs. Counselling is expected to commence from June 1, 2026.

Key Stages of COMEDK Counselling 2026:

1. Registration and Document Upload
Candidates must register on the official COMEDK counselling portal and upload scanned copies of mandatory documents, including their COMEDK UGET admit card, scorecard, Class 10th and 12th mark sheets, photo ID, and a recent photograph. A non-refundable counselling registration fee of approximately Rs. 2000/- is typically required.
2. Document Verification
The uploaded documents will undergo verification by the COMEDK team. Successful verification is essential to proceed to the next stages.
3. Choice Filling and Locking
Once documents are verified, candidates can fill in their preferred colleges and engineering branches in order of priority. It is advisable to list as many relevant options as possible to maximize your chances of securing a seat. Choices must be locked before the deadline.
4. Mock Allotment (Informational)
COMEDK usually conducts a mock allotment round to give candidates an idea of the colleges and branches they might get based on their preferences. This is purely for informational purposes and helps students refine their final choices.
5. Seat Allotment Rounds
Based on ranks, preferences, and seat availability, seats will be allotted in multiple rounds. Candidates will have options to accept and freeze their seat (Choice 1), accept and float for a better option in subsequent rounds (Choice 2), or reject the seat and re-enter for later rounds (Choice 3).
6. Fee Payment and College Reporting
Candidates who accept their allotted seat must pay the first-year tuition fee online through the COMEDK portal. After payment, an admission order will be generated, and candidates must report physically to the allotted college with all original documents within the stipulated time.

It's important to stay updated with the official COMEDK website for the detailed counselling schedule and notifications. Around 190 private engineering colleges participate in COMEDK UGET counselling, offering diverse engineering branches.

Understanding COMEDK UGET Rank and Tie-Breaking Rules

The COMEDK UGET rank is crucial for admissions. In case of a tie in the total percentile scores, COMEDK employs specific tie-breaking rules to determine the final rank:

✅ Higher Percentile score of Physics and Mathematics marks taken together.

✅ Higher Percentile score in Mathematics.

✅ Higher Percentile score in Chemistry.

✅ Age of the Candidate (elder candidate is ranked higher).

We wish all the candidates the very best for their results and future academic endeavors! Keep a close eye on the official COMEDK website for all counselling-related announcements.
