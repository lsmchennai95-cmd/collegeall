Rajasthan BSTC Pre D.El.Ed Result 2026 Out: Your Gateway to a Teaching Career!

The wait is over for lakhs of aspiring teachers in Rajasthan! The much-anticipated Rajasthan Basic School Teaching Course (BSTC), also known as Pre D.El.Ed, Result for 2026 has been officially declared. This is a crucial step towards securing admission into the two-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed) program, paving the way for a rewarding career in primary education across the state. Let's dive into how you can check your results, understand the next steps, and navigate the counselling process.

Result Declared: Your Scores Are Live!

Vardhman Mahaveer Open University (VMOU), Kota, the conducting body for the examination, announced the Rajasthan BSTC (Pre D.El.Ed) Result 2026 on June 10, 2026, at 3 PM. Candidates who appeared for the entrance examination can now access their scorecards and determine their eligibility for the next stage – the counselling process.

How to Check Your Rajasthan BSTC Pre D.El.Ed Result 2026

Checking your result is a straightforward process. Ensure you have your login credentials ready. Follow these simple steps to download your scorecard:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official portals for the Rajasthan Pre D.El.Ed examination: predeledraj2026.com or predeledraj2026.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the link specifically for "Rajasthan BSTC Pre D.El.Ed Result 2026" or "Result".
3. Enter Login Details
You will be prompted to enter your Roll Number and Date of Birth. In some cases, your application number might also be required.
4. View and Download Your Scorecard
After submitting your details, your Rajasthan BSTC Pre D.El.Ed 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Download it and take a printout for future reference.

Important Note: The scorecard will contain crucial details such as your name, category, marks obtained in each paper, total marks, overall percentage, and merit status. Verify all details carefully.

Key Examination Statistics for 2026

The Rajasthan BSTC Pre D.El.Ed 2026 examination saw a massive turnout, reflecting the high aspirations for teaching careers in the state. Here’s a quick overview of the key numbers:

✅ Exam Date: May 20, 2026

✅ Total Registered Candidates: 6,05,242

✅ Candidates Appeared: 4,97,178

✅ Total Seats Available: Approximately 25,970 across 376 colleges

✅ Exam Duration: 600 marks, covering Mental Ability, General Awareness of Rajasthan, Teaching Aptitude, and Language Ability.

The Next Big Step: Rajasthan BSTC Counselling 2026

For all qualified candidates, the journey doesn't end with the result. The next critical phase is the counselling process, which determines seat allotment in various D.El.Ed colleges across Rajasthan. The good news is, the Rajasthan BSTC Counselling 2026 has already commenced!

The counselling process involves several steps, including registration, choice filling of institutions, fee payment, and document verification. It is essential to adhere to the strict timelines announced by VMOU.

Rajasthan BSTC Counselling 2026: Important Dates

Here’s a detailed schedule for the counselling process to help you plan accordingly:

Event Date(s)
Counselling Registration & Amendment in Application (with fee of Rs. 200/-) June 11 - June 16, 2026
Last Date to Deposit Counselling Registration Fee (₹3,000/-) & Fill College Preferences June 11 - June 16, 2026
First Round Allotment List Release June 20, 2026
Online Payment of Admission Fee (₹13,555/-) by Selected Candidates June 20 - June 27, 2026
Reporting to Allotted Teacher Education Institution for Document Verification June 20 - June 29, 2026
Online Admission Confirmation by Institutions June 20 - June 29, 2026
Obtain Provisional Admission Slip by Self-Login June 20 - June 29, 2026
Commencement of Classes July 1, 2026
Last Date to Apply for Upward Movement (Upgradation) July 3 - July 4, 2026
Counselling Fee
A non-refundable counselling registration fee of ₹3,000 is required, payable through online modes like e-Mitra, Net Banking, Debit/Credit Cards, or UPI.

Understanding the Merit List and Expected Cut-off

Along with the results, VMOU also released the BSTC Merit List 2026, which ranks all qualified candidates based on their scores in the entrance exam. This merit list is crucial for the counselling and seat allotment process.

While the final cut-off marks are determined after the merit list and counselling rounds, historical trends provide an estimate:

✅ General Category: Typically, candidates scoring around 380-400 marks out of 600 or above have a good chance of securing admission.

✅ Reserved Categories: For SC/ST/OBC and other reserved categories, the cut-off generally ranges between 350 and 380 marks.

These figures are indicative, and the actual cut-offs may vary based on factors like the total number of applicants, their performance, and the availability of seats.

What’s Next for Aspirants?

With the results out and counselling underway, candidates are advised to keep a close watch on the official websites for any further announcements. Prepare your documents for verification, complete your counselling registration and choice filling within the stipulated time, and stay informed about the seat allotment process.

The Rajasthan BSTC Pre D.El.Ed 2026 result marks a significant milestone for thousands of students aiming to build a career in elementary education. Congratulations to all the successful candidates, and best of luck for your future endeavors!
