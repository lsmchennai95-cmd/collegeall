GSEB HSC 12th Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Future Endeavors!

The moment of truth is here! The Gujarat Secondary and Higher Secondary Education Board (GSEB) has officially declared the much-anticipated HSC (Class 12th) results for the 2026 examinations. Students across Science, General (Arts & Commerce), Vocational, and Sanskrit streams can now check their scores and embark on their next academic journey. This comprehensive guide provides all the essential information you need, from checking your results to understanding the detailed statistics.

GSEB HSC 12th Result 2026: Key Highlights

✅ **Result Declaration Date:** May 4, 2026.

✅ **Time of Release:** 10:00 AM.

✅ **Official Website:** gseb.org.

✅ **Streams Covered:** Science, General (Arts & Commerce), Vocational, and Sanskrit streams simultaneously.

✅ **Examination Period:** February 26 to March 18, 2026.

✅ **Credentials Required:** Six-digit seat number.

How to Check Your GSEB HSC 12th Result 2026

The GSEB has made it convenient for students to access their results through various platforms. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Online via Official Website (gseb.org)
  1. Visit the official GSEB website: gseb.org.
  2. Click on the "Result" tab or the specific "GSEB HSC Result 2026" link on the homepage.
  3. Enter your six-digit seat number in the designated field.
  4. Click on the 'Go' or 'Submit' button.
  5. Your GSEB HSC 12th Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen.
  6. Download and print your provisional scorecard for future reference.
2. Via WhatsApp

For added convenience, GSEB offers a WhatsApp service to receive your results directly.

  1. Save the official WhatsApp number: 6357300971.
  2. Open WhatsApp and send your six-digit seat number to this contact.
  3. You will receive your result directly via WhatsApp.
3. Via SMS

Students can also get their results via SMS:

  • For Science Stream: Type GJ12S [Seat Number] and send to 58888111.
  • For General Stream: Type GJ12G [Seat Number] and send to 58888111.

GSEB HSC 12th Result 2026 at a Glance: Key Statistics

The 2026 results showcase strong academic performance across Gujarat. Here's a detailed look at the pass percentages and other important statistics:

The overall pass percentage in the Science stream is 84.33%, while the General stream recorded an impressive 92.17%.

Stream-wise Pass Percentages (2026)

Stream Pass Percentage (2026) Pass Percentage (2025)
Science 84.33% 83.51%
General (Arts & Commerce) 92.17% 93.7%

While the Science stream saw a slight improvement compared to the previous year, the General stream experienced a marginal dip from 93.7% in 2025 to 92.17% in 2026.

Gender-wise Performance

Girls have once again outperformed boys in both the Science and General streams, maintaining a consistent trend observed in recent years.

Stream Girls' Pass Percentage (2026) Boys' Pass Percentage (2026)
Science 84.65% 84.04%

District-wise Performance

Among the districts, Morbi emerged as the top-performing district with a remarkable 94.85% pass rate, while Dahod recorded the lowest at 57.44%.

Understanding the Passing Criteria and Grading System

To successfully clear the GSEB HSC 12th examinations, students need to meet specific criteria:

Passing Marks

Students must secure a minimum of 33% marks in each subject (theory and practical components, where applicable) and also achieve an aggregate score of at least 33% across all subjects. For subjects with practicals, candidates must score at least 33% in both theory and practical work separately.

Grading System

The GSEB HSC employs a 7-point grading scale from A1 to D. An A1 grade is awarded for scores between 91-100 marks, signifying the highest achievement, while D is the lowest passing grade for scores at or below 33%.

Marks Range Grade
91-100 A1
81-90 A2
75-80 B1
62-74 B2
50-61 C1
33-49 C2
Below 33 D (Fail)

Note: This table represents a general grading structure and may have slight variations based on specific subject distributions.

What's Next After Results?

The declaration of results opens up several avenues for students. Here's what you should know:

✅ **Digital Marksheet:** Students can download and print their provisional marksheet for immediate use. The original mark sheets and certificates will be distributed by schools later.

✅ **Rechecking/Re-evaluation:** Students who are not satisfied with their marks can apply for rechecking or re-evaluation of their answer sheets. Details and application forms will be announced by GSEB shortly after the results.

✅ **Supplementary Exams:** For students who unfortunately did not clear one or two subjects, the board will conduct supplementary (compartmental) exams. The dates and application process for these exams will be announced separately.

✅ **GUJCET Results:** Along with the HSC results, the GUJCET 2026 scores were also released, which are crucial for admissions into engineering and pharmacy courses in Gujarat.

✅ **Future Admissions:** Students can now proceed with their applications for higher education based on their HSC results and GUJCET scores (if applicable).

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared the GSEB HSC 12th examinations! Your hard work and dedication have paid off. For those who may not have achieved their desired results, remember that this is just one step, and there are always opportunities for improvement and alternative paths to success. We wish all students the very best for their future endeavors!
