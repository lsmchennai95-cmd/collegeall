India Post GDS 2nd Merit List Cut Off 2026: Your Guide to Expected Trends & Latest Updates

The wait is almost over for thousands of aspirants eyeing a position as a Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) with India Post. Following the successful release of the first merit list and the subsequent document verification, all eyes are now on the highly anticipated India Post GDS 2nd Merit List 2026 and its cut-off scores.

If you weren't among the candidates shortlisted in the initial round, or if you're keen to understand the dynamics of the GDS recruitment process, this comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about the upcoming second merit list, expected cut-offs, and the next crucial steps.

Understanding the India Post GDS Selection Process

Unlike many other government recruitments, the India Post GDS selection process stands out for its unique, merit-based approach. There's no written examination or interview for these coveted posts of Branch Postmaster (BPM), Assistant Branch Postmaster (ABPM), and Dak Sevak.

✅ Purely Merit-Based: Selection is based solely on the marks obtained in your Class 10 Secondary School Examination.

✅ No Written Exam or Interview: Higher academic qualifications beyond Class 10 are not considered.

✅ Decimal Precision: Class 10 marks are converted to a percentage, computed accurately up to four decimal places for merit list preparation.

✅ Circle-wise Merit: Merit lists are prepared separately for each postal circle across the country.

The entire process is automated, ensuring fairness and transparency, with candidate performance in Class 10 being the sole determinant for shortlisting.

What is the GDS 2nd Merit List Cut-off?

The cut-off score for India Post GDS recruitment represents the minimum percentage of marks in Class 10 required for a candidate to be considered for selection in a particular postal circle and category. When the second merit list is released, its cut-off will indicate the lowest percentage secured by the last candidate selected in that round.

Factors Influencing the GDS Cut-off

Several variables play a crucial role in determining where the cut-off ultimately settles for each merit list:

✅ Number of Vacancies: A higher number of available positions in a specific circle can lead to a comparatively lower cut-off.

✅ Total Applicants: The overall pool of candidates applying for a particular circle directly impacts the competitiveness and thus, the cut-off.

✅ Category-wise Reservation: Separate cut-offs are determined for various categories such as UR, OBC, SC, ST, EWS, and PwD, with reserved categories generally having lower cut-offs.

✅ Performance of Applicants: The aggregate Class 10 percentages of the applicants in a given cycle significantly influence the cut-off.

Why the 2nd Merit List is Crucial for Aspirants

The release of the second merit list holds significant importance, especially for candidates who narrowly missed out on the first list. Here's why:

✅ Filling Unfilled Vacancies: The 2nd merit list is released to fill positions that remained vacant after the first round of document verification, often due to candidates not joining or failing the DV process.

✅ Second Chance for Selection: It offers another opportunity for candidates with slightly lower Class 10 marks to secure a place.

✅ Potentially Lower Cut-offs: The cut-off for the second list is typically slightly lower than the first, increasing selection chances for many.

✅ Multiple Lists: India Post has a history of releasing multiple merit lists (sometimes up to 6 or 7) if vacancies persist.

Expected Release Date of India Post GDS 2nd Merit List 2026

The Department of Posts is expected to release the India Post GDS 2nd Merit List 2026 in April 2026. Following the completion of document verification for the first merit list (which concluded around March 23), the department is preparing to announce the subsequent list. Candidates are advised to keep a close watch on the official website for real-time updates.

How to Check the India Post GDS 2nd Merit List 2026

Once released, checking your status on the GDS 2nd Merit List is a straightforward process:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official India Post GDS online engagement portal: www.indiapostgdsonline.gov.in.
2. Locate the "Shortlisted Candidates" Section
On the homepage, look for the "Shortlisted Candidates" or "Result" section.
3. Select Your Postal Circle
Click on your respective state/postal circle to view the results specific to your region.
4. Download the Merit List PDF
The 2nd merit list will be available in PDF format. Download it to your device.
5. Search for Your Details
Open the downloaded PDF and search for your registration number or name to check your selection status.

Expected Cut-off Trends and Previous Year Insights

While an exact cut-off for the 2nd merit list can only be determined upon its release, past trends and the factors mentioned earlier provide a good indication. Generally, the cut-offs for subsequent merit lists are slightly lower than the first. In the 2025 1st Merit List, general category cut-offs typically ranged between 84% and 94%, with highly competitive regions often reaching the upper end.

For North Indian circles like Haryana and Rajasthan, experts suggest the cut-off might remain above 95% even in the 2nd merit list due to a high volume of applicants with perfect scores.

What Happens After the 2nd Merit List? Document Verification (DV)

If your name appears on the India Post GDS 2nd Merit List, the next critical step is the Document Verification (DV) process. This stage is mandatory, and your candidature will be cancelled if you fail to complete it within the stipulated deadline.

1. Prepare Your Documents
Ensure you have all original educational certificates (Class 10 marksheet is paramount), valid identity proof, caste certificate (if applicable), and two sets of self-attested photocopies ready.
2. Attend DV at Allotted Division
Shortlisted candidates will be called for document verification at their allotted postal division within a specified timeframe (usually 15 days).
3. Final Appointment Letter
Upon successful document verification, candidates will be issued a final appointment letter.
4. Basic Training
Selected candidates typically undergo a period of basic training before commencing their duties as Gramin Dak Sevaks.

Stay calm and keep your documents organized and accessible. This is the final hurdle before securing your position.

Conclusion

The release of the India Post GDS 2nd Merit List 2026 is a significant event for many aspiring candidates. With thousands of vacancies for BPM, ABPM, and Dak Sevak roles across 23 postal circles, this round offers a fresh opportunity. Remember, selection is purely based on your Class 10 marks, making every percentage point count. Keep a vigilant eye on the official India Post GDS website for the latest updates and prepare your documents for the crucial verification stage. Good luck to all the candidates!
