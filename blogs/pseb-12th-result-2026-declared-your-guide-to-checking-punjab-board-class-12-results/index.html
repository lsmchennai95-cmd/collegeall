PSEB 12th Result 2026 Declared: All You Need to Know!

The wait is finally over for thousands of students across Punjab! The Punjab School Education Board (PSEB) has officially announced the Class 12 results for the academic session 2025-26. This much-anticipated declaration, made on May 13, 2026, brings with it a wave of excitement and relief for students who appeared for the senior secondary examinations.

The PSEB Class 12 examinations were successfully conducted from February 17 to April 4, 2026, across various centers in Punjab. A total of 2,65,417 students participated in these crucial exams, with 2,42,755 successfully clearing them. This year's overall pass percentage stands at an impressive 91.46%, reflecting a strong performance by the students.

How to Check Your PSEB 12th Result 2026

Accessing your PSEB Class 12 scorecard is straightforward. Students have multiple convenient options to check their results quickly and efficiently:

1. Via the Official PSEB Website

The primary method to check your result is through the official website of the Punjab School Education Board.

✅ Visit pseb.ac.in.

✅ Click on the 'PSEB 12th Result 2026' link available on the homepage.

✅ Enter your Roll Number as it appears on your admit card.

✅ Submit the details. Your result will be displayed on the screen.

✅ Download and take a printout of your provisional marksheet for future reference.

2. Through DigiLocker

DigiLocker is a secure platform to access your digital mark sheet.

✅ Visit results.digilocker.gov.in or open the DigiLocker app.

✅ Sign in using your registered mobile number and 6-digit access code.

✅ Navigate to the 'Results' section and click on 'Punjab Board XII Result 2026'.

✅ Enter the required credentials (e.g., roll number).

✅ Your digital marksheet will be displayed and can be downloaded.

3. Via SMS Service

In case of heavy website traffic or internet issues, the SMS facility provides a reliable alternative.

✅ Type: PB12<Roll No>

✅ Send to: 5676750

✅ Your result will be sent to your mobile number.

Remember to verify all details on your online marksheet, including your name, roll number, subject-wise marks, and qualifying status. The original mark sheets and certificates will be issued by your respective schools later.

PSEB 12th Result 2026: Key Statistics and Highlights

The 2026 results showcase several interesting trends and exceptional performances:

Overall Performance

✅ Total Students Appeared: 2,65,417

✅ Total Students Passed: 2,42,755

✅ Overall Pass Percentage: 91.46%

Gender-Wise Performance

Girls have once again outshined boys in the Class 12 examinations, continuing a consistent trend.

Category Pass Percentage (2026)
Girls 94.73%
Boys 88.52%
Transgender Candidates 100% (All 6 appeared candidates passed)

Stream-Wise Pass Percentage (2026)

The Commerce and Science streams recorded excellent pass rates this year.

Stream Pass Percentage (2026)
Commerce 98.78%
Science 98.47%
Humanities 87.74%
Vocational 87.80%

Top Performers

This year's merit list is adorned with exceptional talent. Sukhneet Kaur, with a perfect score of 500 out of 500, has emerged as a state topper from Government Adarsh Senior Secondary School, Boha in Mansa district. Suhani Chauhan and Divanshi are also among the top performers who achieved outstanding results.

Urban vs. Rural & School Type Performance

Rural Areas Slightly Edge Out Urban

Students from rural areas showed a marginally better performance with a pass percentage of 91.63%, compared to urban areas at 91.24%.

Non-Government Schools Lead

Among school categories, non-government schools registered the highest success rate at 92.68%, followed by government schools at 91.41%, and aided schools at 88.44%.

Previous Year's Trends (2025 vs. 2026)

A comparison with the previous academic session reveals a slight improvement in the overall pass percentage, showcasing a positive trend in student performance.

Year Students Appeared Students Passed Overall Pass Percentage
2025 2,65,388 2,41,506 91.00%
2026 2,65,417 2,42,755 91.46%

*Pass percentage for 2025 for specific streams: Science (98.52%), Commerce (96.83%), Humanities (87.58%), Vocational (90%).

What's Next? Re-evaluation and Supplementary Exams

Students who are not satisfied with their results will have the option to apply for re-evaluation of their marks. Furthermore, those who could not clear one or more subjects will have the opportunity to appear for supplementary (compartment) exams. To pass the exams, students must secure a minimum of 33% marks overall and separately in both theory and practical components for subjects having them. The dates and application procedures for re-evaluation and supplementary examinations will be announced by the board shortly.

The declaration of the PSEB 12th Result 2026 marks a significant milestone in the academic journey of these students. We congratulate all the successful candidates and wish them the very best for their future endeavors!
