Haryana Board of School Education: Navigating Recent Changes and Future Pathways (2024-2026)

The Haryana Board of School Education (HBSE), headquartered in Bhiwani, is at the forefront of shaping academic journeys for millions of students across Haryana. Established in 1969, HBSE, also known as BSEH, annually conducts public examinations at Middle, Matric (Secondary), and Senior Secondary (Academic & Vocational) levels. In recent times, the board has been proactive in implementing significant reforms and announcing crucial updates to align with national educational goals and improve the quality of education.

Key Highlights and Recent Announcements

The academic landscape under HBSE is dynamic, with several important developments impacting students and educators alike. Here's a look at the major updates:

✅ HBSE Class 12th Results 2024 Declared: Results were announced on April 30, 2024, with an impressive overall pass percentage of 85.31%. Girls notably outperformed boys.

✅ HBSE Class 10th Results 2024 Announced: The Class 10 results were declared on May 12, 2024, with a pass percentage of 95.22% for regular students.

✅ New Curriculum for Classes 9-12: A revamped curriculum, aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020), has been rolled out for the academic session 2024-25.

✅ Exam Dates for 2025 & 2026 Released: HBSE has already released tentative exam schedules for the upcoming Class 10 and 12 board examinations.

HBSE Board Results 2024: A Look Back

The year 2024 brought significant moments for HBSE students, with the declaration of Class 10th and 12th board results.

1. Class 12th Results (Declared April 30, 2024)
The HBSE 12th examination results for 2024 were declared on April 30, 2024, in a press conference. The overall pass percentage stood at 85.31%. Breaking down the statistics, boys achieved an 88.14% pass rate, while girls secured an 82.52% pass rate, indicating girls leading by 5.62%. A total of 2,13,504 candidates appeared for the Senior Secondary (Educational) regular examination, with 1,82,136 passing.

Students could check their results on the official website, bseh.org.in, by entering their credentials.

2. Class 10th Results (Declared May 12, 2024)
The HBSE Class 10th results were announced on May 12, 2024. For regular students, the overall pass percentage was 95.22%, while self-study candidates achieved 88.73%. Similar to Class 12, girl students outshone boys with a 2.10% higher pass percentage.

Students could access their results on bseh.org.in by entering their roll number and date of birth.

Upcoming Examinations: 2025 & 2026 Schedule

For the academic sessions 2024-25 and 2025-26, HBSE has already published the broad timelines for Class 10th and 12th board examinations.

Detailed subject-wise date sheets are typically released closer to the examination dates on the official website.

Registration for HBSE Exams 2026: The online registration for Class 10 and 12 exams for 2026 commenced in November 2025. Schools are responsible for accurately filling student details and submitting fees via bseh.org.in.

Revolutionizing Education: Curriculum Changes Aligned with NEP 2020

One of the most significant transformations initiated by the HBSE is the implementation of a fresh curriculum for Classes 9 to 12, effective from the academic session 2024-25. This overhaul is meticulously crafted in line with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020), emphasizing holistic development and skill-based learning.

1. NEP 2020 Integration
Over 100 subject experts contributed to developing this new curriculum, aiming to enhance the quality of education across the state. The focus is on enabling teachers to create lesson plans tailored to students' intellectual capacities.
2. Three-Language Formula and 'Best Six' Rule (Effective 2026)
A major change for Class 9 and 10 students starting from the 2026 academic year is the implementation of the three-language formula. Students will now study an additional compulsory language (Sanskrit, Urdu, or Punjabi) alongside Hindi and English, promoting multilingual education. Consequently, the existing 'best five' system for calculating results will be replaced by a 'best six' formula, requiring students to pass six subjects.

Stakeholders can access and download the new curriculum for Classes 9, 10, 11, and 12 from the official website, bseh.org.in.
