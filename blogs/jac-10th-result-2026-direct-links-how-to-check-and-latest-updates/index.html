JAC 10th Result 2026: Your Gateway to Future Endeavors!

The wait is almost over for lakhs of students who appeared for the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC) Class 10 Matriculation Examinations 2026!

Anticipation is at an all-time high as the Jharkhand Academic Council (JAC) prepares to unveil the much-awaited Class 10 results for 2026. Students across Jharkhand are eagerly checking official portals and news updates for the precise moment their academic futures will unfold. This year, approximately 4.23 lakh students participated in the matric exams, making the result declaration a significant event for countless families.

Expected Release Date & Key Exam Highlights

The JAC 10th examinations for 2026 were successfully conducted from February 3 to February 17, 2026. Following the theory papers, practical examinations for Class 10 students were held from February 24 to March 7, 2026. While an official confirmation is still pending, the JAC 10th Result 2026 is widely expected to be declared between April 15 and April 20, 2026. Some reports also suggest the result might be released in May 2026.

"Keep your admit cards ready! The moment you've been preparing for is just around the corner. Stay patient and follow the official channels for the most accurate updates."

How to Check Your JAC 10th Result 2026

Once declared, students can access their results through multiple convenient methods:

1. Online Through Official Websites

The primary and most common way to check your results is online. Follow these simple steps:

  1. Visit any of the official JAC results portals: jacresults.com, jac.jharkhand.gov.in, or jacexamportal.in.
  2. Look for the link titled "JAC Annual Secondary Examination Results 2026" or similar, usually found in the 'Latest Notification' or 'Results' section.
  3. Click on the link to open the result login page.
  4. Enter your Roll Code and Roll Number accurately as printed on your admit card.
  5. Click on the 'Submit' button.
  6. Your JAC Class 10 board examination result 2026 will be displayed on your screen.
  7. Download and print a provisional copy of your marksheet for future reference.
2. Via SMS

For quick access, especially during heavy website traffic, the SMS option is highly useful:

  • Type JHA10<space>Roll Number and send it to 5676750.
  • Alternatively, type RESULT<space>JAC10<space>RollCode + RollNumber<space>REGISTRATION NUMBER and send to 56263.
  • Your JAC 10th result 2026 will be sent as an SMS to your mobile number.
3. Through DigiLocker

DigiLocker is another secure way to access your digital marksheet:

  • Visit the DigiLocker website or open the DigiLocker app.
  • Sign up if you are a first-time user, or log in with your existing credentials.
  • From the dashboard, click on 'Import Documents'.
  • Select 'Jharkhand Academic Council, Ranchi' from the list.
  • Choose '2026' as the exam year and enter your roll number and other required details.
  • Your JAC 10th result 2026 mark sheet will be imported to your DigiLocker account, which you can then view and download.

Details Mentioned on Your Marksheet

Once you download your provisional marksheet, ensure to verify all the details carefully. It will typically include:

✅ Student's Roll Number and Roll Code

✅ Student's Name and Parent's Name (Father's Name, Mother's Name)

✅ School Name

✅ Subject-wise Marks Obtained

✅ Total Marks and Percentage

✅ Overall Grades and Division

✅ Result Status (Pass/Fail)

Passing Criteria for JAC 10th Exams 2026

To successfully clear the JAC 10th examinations, students must adhere to the following passing criteria:

✅ Secure at least 30% marks in theory examinations separately.

✅ Secure at least 30% marks in practical examinations separately.

✅ Achieve a minimum of 30% aggregate marks across all subjects.

What After the JAC 10th Result 2026?

The declaration of results opens up several avenues for students. Here's what you need to know about post-result procedures:

1. Re-evaluation / Scrutiny

If you are unsatisfied with your marks, you can apply for scrutiny or rechecking. The application forms are expected to be available online on jac.jharkhand.gov.in/jac/ in June 2026. A fee of Rs 450 per subject will be applicable. The revised results after scrutiny are anticipated in June 2026.

2. Compartmental Examinations

Students who fail in one or two subjects have the opportunity to appear for compartmental (supplementary) exams to save their academic year. These exams are expected to be conducted in July 2026, and students can apply through their respective schools. The results for the compartment exams are likely to be announced in August 2026.
