AP Inter Supplementary Hall Ticket 2026 Released: Your Gateway to Success!

The Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP), has officially released the highly anticipated AP Inter Supplementary Hall Tickets for the Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examinations (IPASE) May 2026. This is a crucial moment for all students who are set to appear for these exams, providing a golden opportunity to either clear subjects or improve their previous scores. The hall tickets were released today, May 16, 2026, on the official website.

Don't wait until the last minute! Download your AP Inter Supplementary Hall Ticket 2026 immediately to verify all details and prepare for your exams starting May 21, 2026.

Essential Details: AP Inter Supplementary Exams 2026

The supplementary examinations are a second chance for students who couldn't clear certain subjects in the regular Intermediate Public Examinations or wish to enhance their academic performance. This year, the AP Intermediate Public Examination results for Science, Arts, and Commerce streams were announced on April 15, 2026, with an overall pass percentage of 77% for first-year and 81% for second-year students.

✅ Hall Tickets Released: May 16, 2026

✅ Official Website: bie.ap.gov.in

✅ Theory Exam Dates: May 21 to June 4, 2026

✅ Practical Exam Dates: June 7 to June 11, 2026

✅ Results Expected: June or July 2026

How to Download Your AP Inter Supplementary Hall Ticket 2026

Accessing your admit card is a straightforward process. Follow these steps carefully to download your AP Inter Supplementary Hall Ticket 2026:

1. Visit the Official BIEAP Website
Navigate to the Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP) official website: bie.ap.gov.in.
2. Locate the Hall Ticket Link
On the homepage, look for the link that says "Download IPASE May 2026 Hall Tickets" or "I.P.A.S.E Theory Hall Tickets May 2026".
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will need to enter your previous roll number or Aadhaar number (for First Year students) or your previous hall ticket number (for Second Year students), along with your Date of Birth.
4. Download and Print
After submitting the required details, your AP Inter Supplementary Hall Ticket 2026 will appear on the screen. Download it and take a clear printout for the examination.

Alternatively, some sources indicate a WhatsApp download method. You can try sending "Hi" to 9552300009 and follow the prompts to receive your hall ticket as a PDF file.

AP Inter Supplementary Exam Timetable 2026 at a Glance

The examinations are scheduled to be held in two sessions daily. Here’s a quick overview of the timings:

Session Timings Applicable For
Morning Session 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM 1st Year Students
Afternoon Session 2:30 PM to 5:30 PM 2nd Year Students

Please refer to your individual hall ticket for subject-specific dates and detailed timings.

Crucial Instructions for Exam Day

To ensure a smooth examination experience, students must adhere to the following guidelines:

1. Verify All Details
Upon downloading, carefully check all information on your hall ticket, including your name, photograph, subjects, exam centre, and timings. In case of any discrepancy, contact your college principal immediately for correction.
2. Arrive Early
Candidates are advised to report to their respective examination centres at least 30 minutes before the commencement of the exams.
3. Strict Entry Timings
No student will be permitted to enter the examination hall after 9:00 AM for the morning session or after 2:30 PM for the afternoon session.
4. Mandatory Hall Ticket
The AP Inter Supplementary Hall Ticket is a mandatory document for entry into the examination hall. Without a valid hall ticket, you will not be allowed to appear for the exam.
5. Principal's Signature Not Required
The Board has clarified that the signature of the college principal is *not* required on the downloaded hall ticket for entry into the examination hall.

Final Words of Encouragement

The AP Inter Supplementary Exams are not just about passing; they are about improvement and a chance to demonstrate your true potential. Use these last few days effectively to revise, focus on challenging topics, and maintain a positive mindset. Get adequate sleep, eat well, and stay hydrated. You've prepared for this, and with proper planning, you can achieve success!

Good luck to all the students appearing for the AP Inter Supplementary Examinations 2026!
