MBOSE SSLC Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Higher Education!

The wait is finally over for thousands of students across Meghalaya! The Meghalaya Board of School Education (MBOSE) officially declared the Secondary School Leaving Certificate (SSLC) Class 10 Examination results for 2026 on April 7, 2026, at 11 AM. This crucial announcement marks a significant milestone in the academic journey of students, paving the way for their progression to higher secondary education.

MBOSE SSLC Result 2026: Key Highlights

The 2026 MBOSE SSLC examination saw impressive performance from students. Here are the crucial details:

✅ **Declaration Date:** April 7, 2026, at 11 AM.

✅ **Overall Pass Percentage:** A commendable 86.84% of students successfully cleared the exam.

✅ **Total Students:** Out of 48,623 students who appeared for the examination, 42,228 passed.

✅ **Exam Dates:** The examinations were conducted from January 30 to February 11, 2026.

✅ **Online Access Only:** Results are exclusively available online; no physical display at board offices.

How to Check Your MBOSE SSLC 2026 Result

Students can easily access their provisional mark sheets online. Follow these simple steps:

1. Visit Official Websites
Navigate to any of the official MBOSE result portals: mbose.in, megresults.nic.in, or mboseresults.in.
2. Locate Result Link
On the homepage, click on the prominent link that says "MBOSE SSLC Result 2026" or "Class 10 SSLC Result 2026."
3. Enter Credentials
You will be redirected to a new page where you need to enter your roll number exactly as it appears on your admit card. Some portals might also require a roll code or CAPTCHA verification.
4. View and Download
After submitting, your MBOSE SSLC 2026 result will be displayed on the screen. Review your marks carefully, then download and print a copy for your records. Remember, this online marksheet is provisional; original mark sheets will be issued by your respective schools later.

MBOSE SSLC Pass Percentage: A Five-Year Trend Analysis

The Meghalaya Board has shown a remarkable improvement in its pass percentages over the last few years, especially a significant jump in 2025 and sustained high performance in 2026. This positive trend reflects enhanced educational quality and support within the state.

Year Overall Pass Percentage
2021 52.90%

The significant increase from 55.80% in 2024 to 87.10% in 2025 and a consistent 86.84% in 2026 showcases a major leap in student performance and educational effectiveness. For 2026, boys outshone girls with a pass percentage of 93.97%.

What's Next for Students?

With the results out, students now need to consider their next steps:

1. Applying for Re-evaluation
Students who are not satisfied with their marks in any subject have the option to apply for re-evaluation. Application forms and details for this process will be available through the official MBOSE channels.
2. Class 11 Admissions
Passing the SSLC exam opens the doors to Class 11. Students should begin preparing for admissions by selecting a suitable stream—Science, Commerce, or Arts—based on their interests and career aspirations. Schools across Meghalaya are expected to commence the Class 11 admission process shortly after the results, so keep your documents ready.
3. Understanding Passing Criteria
To pass the MBOSE SSLC 2026 exam, students needed to secure a minimum of 30% in each subject and an aggregate of 33%. For an 80-mark theory paper, a minimum of 24 marks was required, and for internal assessments, 6 marks out of 20.

The declaration of the MBOSE SSLC Result 2026 is a moment of both relief and excitement. We congratulate all the successful candidates and wish them the very best for their future academic endeavors!
