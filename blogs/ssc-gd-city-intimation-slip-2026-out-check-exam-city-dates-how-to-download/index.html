SSC GD City Intimation Slip 2026 Released: Your Essential Guide to Exam Planning!

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has activated a crucial link for all aspiring candidates of the SSC GD Constable Examination 2026! The much-awaited SSC GD City Intimation Slip 2026 was officially released on April 19, 2026. This vital document is your first step towards planning for the upcoming Computer-Based Test (CBT), providing advance information about your allotted exam city, date, and shift timing.

With the SSC GD CBT exams scheduled to commence from April 27 and continue until May 30, 2026, candidates are strongly advised to download their city intimation slips without delay. This proactive measure ensures you have ample time to arrange your travel and accommodation, making your examination experience as smooth as possible.

Key Update: The SSC GD City Intimation Slip 2026 is OUT! Download it now from the official SSC website to check your exam city, date, and shift. The CBT exams are set to run from April 27 to May 30, 2026.

What is the SSC GD City Intimation Slip?

Many candidates often confuse the City Intimation Slip with the Admit Card. It's crucial to understand that these are two distinct documents. The SSC GD City Intimation Slip serves as a pre-admit card notification. Its primary purpose is to inform candidates in advance about their assigned exam city, the specific date of their examination, and their shift timing. This allows you to make necessary travel and accommodation arrangements well before your actual exam date, reducing last-minute stress.

The actual SSC GD Admit Card 2026, which is mandatory for entry into the examination hall, will be released separately, typically 2-3 days before your scheduled exam date. The admit card will contain comprehensive details, including the exact exam center venue address, your roll number, reporting time, photograph, signature, and detailed instructions for the exam day.

How to Download Your SSC GD City Intimation Slip 2026

Accessing your SSC GD City Intimation Slip is a straightforward process. Follow these detailed steps to download your slip from the official SSC website:

1. Visit the Official SSC Website
Open your web browser and go to the Staff Selection Commission's official website: www.ssc.gov.in.
2. Navigate to the Login Section
On the homepage, look for and click on the "Login/Register" button or the "Admit Card" section in the top navigation bar.
3. Log In to Your Candidate Dashboard
Enter your Registration Number (Username) and Password (or Date of Birth) as per your registration details. Complete the captcha code if prompted and click "Submit."
4. Find the City Intimation Slip Link
Once logged in, navigate to the "SSC GD" section or look for a link specifically stating "Information regarding City of Examination – Constable (GD) Examination 2026" or "Status/Download City Intimation for Constable (GD) 2026."
5. View and Download Your Slip
Click on the relevant link. Your SSC GD City Intimation Slip will be displayed on the screen, showing your allotted exam city, date, and shift. Review the details carefully. Finally, click on the save or download button to get your PDF copy. It is recommended to take a printout for future reference and keep a digital backup.

Details Mentioned on the City Intimation Slip

Your SSC GD City Intimation Slip 2026 will contain the following key information:

✅ Candidate's Name

✅ Registration Number

✅ Roll Number (if available)

✅ Exam Date

✅ Exam Shift Timing

✅ Allotted Exam City

✅ Application Status

Remember, this slip DOES NOT include the exact exam center venue address. That information will only be provided on your official Admit Card.

Important Reminders for Candidates

Is the City Intimation Slip the same as the Admit Card?
No, they are different. The city slip is for advance information about your exam city and date, while the admit card is the mandatory document for entry into the exam hall, containing full venue details.
Can I change my allotted exam city?
No, the allotted exam city is final, and there is no provision to change it.
What login details are required to download the slip?
You will need your Registration Number and Password (or Date of Birth) to log in.
When will the SSC GD Admit Card 2026 be released?
The admit card will be released approximately 2-3 days before your scheduled examination date. Keep checking the official SSC website, ssc.gov.in, regularly for updates.
