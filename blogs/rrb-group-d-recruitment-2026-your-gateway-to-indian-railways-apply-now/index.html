RRB Group D Recruitment 2026: Chart Your Course with Indian Railways!

The much-anticipated Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Group D Recruitment for 2026 (CEN 09/2025) has officially opened its doors, presenting a golden opportunity for countless aspirants dreaming of a career with Indian Railways. With thousands of Level-1 posts announced, this recruitment drive is a significant event for job seekers across the nation. The official notification, CEN 09/2025, was released on January 30th/31st, 2026, detailing all essential information for prospective candidates.

This comprehensive guide provides an in-depth look into the RRB Group D Recruitment 2026, covering everything from key dates and eligibility to the selection process and syllabus. Read on to prepare yourself for a rewarding career!

Key Highlights of RRB Group D Recruitment 2026

✅ Official Notification: Released on January 30/31, 2026 (CEN 09/2025).

✅ Total Vacancies: A massive 22,195 (some sources indicate 21,997) Level-1 posts across various railway zones.

✅ Application Dates: Online application commenced on January 31, 2026, with the deadline extended to March 9, 2026.

✅ Eligibility: Minimum 10th pass, ITI, or NAC certificate, with age between 18 and 33 years (with relaxations).

✅ Selection Process: Comprises a Computer-Based Test (CBT), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Document Verification, and Medical Examination.

Important Dates at a Glance

Staying informed about the critical dates is paramount for all applicants. Here’s a summary of the key timelines for RRB Group D Recruitment 2026 (CEN 09/2025):

Eligibility Criteria: Do You Qualify?

Before applying, candidates must ensure they meet the specific eligibility requirements set by the Railway Recruitment Board.

1. Educational Qualification
Candidates must have successfully passed Class 10th or possess an Industrial Training Institute (ITI) certificate or a National Apprenticeship Certificate (NAC) granted by NCVT. Those awaiting final results are not eligible to apply.
2. Age Limit
Applicants must be between 18 and 33 years of age as of January 1, 2026. Age relaxations are applicable for candidates belonging to reserved categories as per government norms.
3. Nationality/Citizenship
Candidates must be a citizen of India, Nepal, or Bhutan. Tibetan refugees who arrived before January 1, 1962, and persons of Indian origin who migrated from specific countries to settle permanently in India are also eligible.

The Selection Process: A Multi-Stage Journey

The RRB Group D selection process is rigorous and designed to assess candidates on multiple fronts. It typically involves four main stages:

1. Computer-Based Test (CBT)
This is the first and most crucial stage, where candidates' knowledge and aptitude are assessed through an online examination.
2. Physical Efficiency Test (PET)
Candidates who qualify the CBT are called for the PET, which evaluates their physical fitness. PwD candidates are generally exempted from PET.
3. Document Verification (DV)
Successful candidates from the PET stage undergo document verification to confirm the authenticity of their submitted credentials.
4. Medical Examination
The final stage involves a medical examination to ensure candidates meet the prescribed medical standards for railway service.

RRB Group D Exam Pattern 2026 (CBT)

Understanding the CBT exam pattern is vital for effective preparation. The Computer-Based Test consists of 100 objective-type multiple-choice questions across four sections.

Subject Number of Questions Maximum Marks Duration
General Science 25 25 90 Minutes (120 minutes for PwBD with scribe)
Mathematics 25 25
General Intelligence & Reasoning 30 30
General Awareness & Current Affairs 20 20
Total 100 100

✅ Negative Marking: 1/3rd of a mark will be deducted for each incorrect answer.

✅ Question Type: All questions will be objective multiple-choice.

Syllabus Overview for CBT

The syllabus for RRB Group D CBT covers a wide range of topics. A detailed understanding is crucial for focused preparation.

1. Mathematics
Includes Number System, BODMAS, Decimals, Fractions, LCM, HCF, Ratio & Proportion, Percentages, Mensuration, Time & Work, Time & Distance, Simple & Compound Interest, Profit & Loss, Algebra, Geometry & Trigonometry, Elementary Statistics, Square Root, Age Calculations, Calendar & Clock, Pipes & Cisterns.
2. General Intelligence & Reasoning
Topics such as Analogies, Alphabetical and Number Series, Coding and Decoding, Mathematical Operations, Relationships, Syllogism, Jumbling, Venn Diagram, Data Interpretation and Sufficiency, Conclusions and Decision Making, Analytical Reasoning, Classification, Statement-Argument & Assumptions.
3. General Science
Covers Physics, Chemistry, and Life Sciences up to the 10th standard level (NCERT). Key areas include Motion, Work & Energy, Sound, Light, Electricity, Magnetism, Units & Measurements (Physics); Matter, Atoms & Molecules, Acids, Bases & Salts, Metals & Non-Metals, Carbon & Compounds, Periodic Classification (Chemistry); Classification of Organisms, Cytology, Genetics, Human Body, Nutrition (Life Sciences).
4. General Awareness & Current Affairs
Focuses on Science & Technology, Sports, Culture, Personalities, Economics, Politics, Indian History, Geography, Indian Polity & Constitution, and current affairs of national and international importance (last 6-12 months).

Physical Efficiency Test (PET) Details

The PET requirements differ for male and female candidates:

For Male Candidates

• Lift and carry 35 kg of weight for a distance of 100 meters in 2 minutes without dropping the weight (one chance).

• Run a distance of 1000 meters in 4 minutes and 15 seconds (one chance).

For Female Candidates

• Lift and carry 20 kg of weight for a distance of 100 meters in 2 minutes without dropping the weight (one chance).

• Run a distance of 1000 meters in 5 minutes and 40 seconds (one chance).

How to Apply for RRB Group D 2026

The application process is entirely online. Candidates are advised to follow these steps:

1. Visit the Official RRB Application Portal
Go to rrbapply.gov.in or your respective regional RRB website.
2. Read the Notification Carefully
Thoroughly review the CEN 09/2025 notification PDF to understand all guidelines, eligibility, and post-specific details.
3. Complete the Online Application Form
Fill in your personal, educational, and other required details accurately.
4. Upload Documents & Pay Fee
Upload necessary documents as per specifications and pay the application fee online. General, OBC, and EWS categories typically pay Rs. 500, while SC, ST, PwD, and all women candidates pay Rs. 250 (with a refund for CBT attendance).
5. Submit & Save
Submit your application and download a copy for future reference.

The RRB Group D Recruitment 2026 is a significant opportunity for individuals seeking stable employment and a dynamic career with Indian Railways. By understanding the eligibility criteria, meticulously preparing for the CBT and PET, and adhering to the application deadlines, you can significantly enhance your chances of success. Stay updated with official RRB announcements and start your preparation today!
