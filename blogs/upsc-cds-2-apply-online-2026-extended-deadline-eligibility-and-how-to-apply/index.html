UPSC CDS 2 2026: Your Last Chance to Apply Online – Deadline Extended!

Aspiring to don the prestigious uniform and serve the nation? The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has opened its gates for the Combined Defence Services (CDS) Examination (II) 2026, presenting a golden opportunity for graduates to join the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA), and Officers’ Training Academy (OTA). With the application deadline extended, this is your crucial last call to apply!

The UPSC has extended the application deadline for CDS 2 2026 until **June 11, 2026 (6:00 PM)**. Ensure you complete your application today to avoid missing out on this significant opportunity!

Key Dates for UPSC CDS 2 2026

Stay organized with these important dates for the CDS 2 2026 examination:

✅ Notification Release Date: May 20, 2026

✅ Online Application Start Date: May 20, 2026

✅ Extended Last Date to Apply Online: June 11, 2026 (6:00 PM)

✅ Date of Written Examination: September 13, 2026 (Sunday)

A total of 451 vacancies have been announced across various academies. Here’s a detailed breakdown:

Eligibility Criteria for CDS 2 2026

Before applying, it's crucial to ensure you meet all the eligibility requirements set by UPSC.

1. Nationality
Candidates must be a citizen of India, a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, a Tibetan refugee who came to India before January 1, 1962, with the intention of permanently settling, or a person of Indian origin who has migrated from certain East African or Asian countries with the intention of permanently settling in India.
2. Age Limit (as of July 1, 2027)

The age limits vary for each academy and course:

  • IMA & INA: Born not earlier than July 2, 2003, and not later than July 1, 2008.
  • AFA: Born not earlier than July 2, 2002, and not later than July 1, 2006. (Age relaxation up to 26 years for candidates holding a valid and current Commercial Pilot Licence issued by DGCA (India)).
  • OTA (Men): Born not earlier than July 2, 2001, and not later than July 1, 2007.
  • OTA (Women): Unmarried women, issueless widows who have not remarried, and issueless divorcees (in possession of divorce documents) who have not remarried. Born not earlier than July 2, 2001, and not later than July 1, 2007.
3. Educational Qualification
  • For IMA & OTA: A Bachelor's degree from a recognized University or equivalent.
  • For INA: A Bachelor's degree in Engineering from a recognized University/Institution.
  • For AFA: A Bachelor's degree from a recognized University with Physics and Mathematics at the 10+2 level, or a Bachelor of Engineering degree.

Candidates in the final year of graduation are also eligible to apply, provided they submit proof of passing the degree examination at the time of commencement of the course.

4. Marital Status
Unmarried male candidates are eligible for IMA, INA, and AFA. For OTA, unmarried women, issueless widows who have not remarried, and issueless divorcees (in possession of divorce documents) who have not remarried are eligible.

How to Apply for UPSC CDS 2 2026 Online

The application process is entirely online and conducted through the UPSC's official website. Follow these steps carefully:

1. Visit the Official UPSC Website
Navigate to www.upsc.gov.in or upsconline.nic.in.
2. Complete One-Time Registration (OTR)
If you haven't already, complete the One-Time Registration (OTR) by providing your basic personal and contact details. This is a mandatory step.
3. Access the CDS 2026 Application Link
Look for the "Online Application for Various Examinations" section and click on the link for the CDS 2026 exam.
4. Fill Part I Registration
Enter your personal details, educational qualifications, and other requested information.
5. Complete Part II Registration
After Part I, you will receive a registration ID. Use this to proceed to Part II, where you will select your exam preferences, upload necessary documents, and pay the application fee.
6. Upload Required Documents
You will need to upload scanned copies of your recent passport-size photograph, signature, a valid photo identity card, matriculation certificate (for date of birth proof), and educational qualification proof. Ensure they meet the specified format and size requirements.
7. Pay the Application Fee
The application fee is ₹200 for General/OBC/EWS male candidates. Female candidates and those belonging to SC/ST categories are exempted from paying the fee. Payment can be made online through various modes.
8. Review and Submit
Carefully review all the details filled in the application form before final submission. UPSC provides an application correction window if needed, but it's best to be accurate initially.
9. Download Confirmation Page
After successful submission, download and save a copy of the confirmation page for your records.

Selection Process and Exam Pattern Highlights

The CDS 2 2026 selection process involves a multi-stage evaluation:

✅ Written Examination: Candidates will first appear for a written exam consisting of objective-type questions.

✅ SSB Interview/Personality Test: Those who qualify in the written exam will be called for an intelligence and personality test by the Service Selection Board (SSB).

✅ Document Verification: Verification of all submitted documents.

✅ Medical Examination: A thorough medical examination to ensure candidates meet the physical and medical standards.

Exam Pattern at a Glance
The written exam includes subjects like English, General Knowledge, and Elementary Mathematics. Candidates applying for the Officers Training Academy (OTA) only need to appear for English and General Knowledge papers, while those aspiring for IMA, INA, and AFA must attempt all three papers. Each paper is typically 200 marks and 2 hours in duration, with a negative marking of 1/3rd for incorrect answers.

Prepare diligently, verify your eligibility, and submit your application to embark on a journey of honor and service.
