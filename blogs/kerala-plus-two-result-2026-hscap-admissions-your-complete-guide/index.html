Kerala Plus Two Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to HSCAP Plus One Admissions!

The much-awaited Directorate of Higher Secondary Education (DHSE) Kerala Plus Two (Class 12) results for 2026 have officially been declared, marking a pivotal moment for over 4.5 lakh students across the state. Announced by Kerala General Education Minister N. Samsudheen in a press conference on May 26, 2026, at 3 PM, these results pave the way for higher education and future academic pursuits. If you're a student eager to check your scores or a parent guiding your child through the next steps, this comprehensive guide will walk you through everything from result checking to the crucial HSCAP Plus One admission process for 2026.

How to Check Your DHSE Kerala Plus Two Result 2026

Accessing your Kerala 12th result is now simpler than ever, with multiple official channels provided by the DHSE Kerala. Students can check their results using their roll number and date of birth, or even school-wise using a dedicated school code.

1. Online Portals
The primary and most common method is through the official websites. Ensure you have your registration number and date of birth ready.

Key Official Websites:

Website Address Purpose
keralaresults.nic.in Main result portal
dhsekerala.gov.in Official DHSE Kerala website
results.kite.kerala.gov.in KITE's dedicated result portal
results.hse.kerala.gov.in Higher Secondary Examination results
prd.kerala.gov.in Public Relations Department portal
nammudekeralam.kerala.gov.in Kerala Government services portal

To check online, simply visit any of these websites, click on the "Kerala Plus Two Result 2026" link, and enter your registration number and date of birth.

2. School-Wise Results
For a school-specific overview, you can check the school-wise results. This option is particularly useful for schools to track their overall performance.

Steps to Check School-Wise Results:

✅ Visit results.kite.kerala.gov.in or dhsekerala.gov.in.

✅ Click on the 'DHSE Kerala Plus Two Result 2026 School-Wise' or 'School wise Results' option.

✅ Enter your school code and district name (if prompted).

✅ Submit the details to view the list of students and their results for that school.

3. Mobile Applications & SMS
For added convenience, the results are also available through dedicated mobile apps and SMS services.

✅ DigiLocker: Log in with your Aadhaar-linked mobile number, then enter your registration number and date of birth.

✅ SAPHALAM App (Saphalam 2026): Download from Google Play Store, select "HSE/Plus Two Result 2026", and enter your credentials.

✅ PRD Live App & iExaMS-Kerala App: These applications also provide result access.

✅ SMS Service: Type KERALA12 [Your Registration Number/Roll Number] and send it to 56263.

Kerala Plus Two Result 2026: Performance Highlights

This year, approximately 4,52,437 students appeared for the Kerala Plus Two examinations, conducted between March 6 and March 28, 2026. The overall pass percentage for 2026 stands at a commendable 77.97%, showing a marginal improvement of 0.16 percentage points compared to the previous year.

Idukki district emerged as the top performer with an impressive pass percentage of 84.64%, while Kasaragod recorded the lowest at 71.72%.

While official stream-wise pass percentages for 2026 are awaited, looking at the trends from 2025 can provide insight. Last year, the Science stream led with an 83.25% pass percentage, followed by Commerce at 74.21%, and Humanities at 69.16%.

What's Next: HSCAP Kerala Plus One Admissions 2026

For students who have successfully cleared their Plus Two examinations, the next crucial step is securing admission to Plus One (Class 11) through the Higher Secondary Centralised Admission Process (HSCAP). This centralized single-window admission system, known as 'Ekajalakam', simplifies the application process for government and aided higher secondary schools.

Understanding HSCAP Kerala Plus One Admission
Instead of applying to individual schools, students submit a single online application via the official HSCAP portal, hscap.kerala.gov.in, selecting multiple preferred schools and streams. Allotments are then made based on marks and preferences, ensuring a fair and transparent process.

Important Dates for HSCAP Plus One Admission 2026

The application window for HSCAP Plus One admissions is typically concise, so staying informed about the deadlines is vital.

✅ Online Registration Started: May 25, 2026

✅ Last Date to Apply: June 3, 2026

✅ Trial Allotment Result: June 8, 2026

✅ First Main Allotment Result: June 15, 2026

✅ Second and Third Allotment Rounds: Expected by late June 2026

✅ Commencement of Plus One Classes: July 2, 2026

Documents Required for Plus One Admission

When reporting to the allotted school, ensure you have the following essential documents ready:

✅ Original SSLC Mark Sheet

✅ Transfer Certificate (TC)

✅ Passport-size photographs

✅ Any additional documents specifically requested by the allotted school

For those who may not have achieved the desired results, the DHSE Kerala will conduct SAY (Save A Year) exams from June 29 to July 3, 2026, offering another opportunity to improve scores.

Congratulations to all the students who have successfully passed their Kerala Plus Two examinations! We wish you the very best in your future academic endeavors, especially as you navigate the HSCAP Kerala Plus One admissions for 2026. Stay vigilant, check the official portals regularly, and ensure all deadlines are met for a smooth transition into your next academic chapter.
