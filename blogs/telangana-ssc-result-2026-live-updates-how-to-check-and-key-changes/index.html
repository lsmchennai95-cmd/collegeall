Telangana SSC Result 2026: Your Gateway to Future Endeavors is Here!

The wait is finally over! The Directorate of Government Examinations, Telangana (BSE Telangana) has officially announced the Class 10 (SSC) Public Examination results for 2026. This marks a pivotal moment for lakhs of students across the state, opening doors to their intermediate education and chosen career paths.

Result Declared: Date, Time & Key Officials

The much-anticipated Telangana SSC Results for 2026 have been officially released today, **April 29, 2026, at 2:00 PM (IST)**. The announcement took place at the prestigious Godavari Auditorium, SCERT Campus in Basheerbagh, Hyderabad.

K Keshava Rao, Advisor to the Government of Telangana, was present to officially declare the results, a moment of significant importance for the state's educational landscape. Dr. Yogita Rana, IAS, Principal Secretary to the Government, and Dr. E Naveen Nicolas, IAS, Director of School Education, also attended the declaration.

How to Check Your TS SSC Result 2026

Accessing your Class 10 marks memo is a straightforward process. Students will need their Hall Ticket Number to log in and view their results. The Board has ensured multiple platforms are available to handle the high traffic expected.

1. Official Websites
Visit any of the following official portals by the Board of Secondary Education, Telangana:
  • bse.telangana.gov.in
  • results.bse.telangana.gov.in
  • results.bsetelangana.org
  • schooledu.telangana.gov.in
  • results.shiksha
  • manabadi.co.in
Once on the website, click on the link for "TS SSC Result 2026" or "SSC Public Examination Result 2026". Enter your Hall Ticket Number and other required credentials, then submit. Your marks memo will appear on the screen.
2. Via SMS and WhatsApp
For quick access, students can also receive their results through mobile services:

✅ MeeSeva WhatsApp Service: Send "Hi" to 8096958096 and follow the instructions.

✅ SMS: Type TS10 Hall Ticket Number and send it to 56263. (Note: It's advisable to confirm the official SMS number from board notifications as it may vary.)

Remember, the online marks memo is provisional. You will need to collect your original certificates from your respective schools at a later date. Ensure all details on your digital marks memo are accurate and report any discrepancies to your school authorities immediately.

Important Changes: Marks-Based System for 2026

A significant update for the 2025-26 academic year is the shift from a grading system to a **marks-based evaluation**. The Telangana Board has discontinued the previous grading system (A1, B2, etc.), and students will now receive raw marks instead of grades.

Furthermore, the internal assessment component (which previously carried 20 marks) has been removed. The entire examination will now carry 100 marks, emphasizing 100% external evaluation. This change aims to streamline the evaluation process and aligns with the move towards online admissions in junior colleges.

Understanding Passing Criteria

To successfully qualify for the Telangana SSC exams, students must meet specific minimum passing marks in each subject.

✅ Students must secure at least 35% marks in each subject.

✅ For the Second Language paper, a minimum of 20% marks is required to pass.

✅ Earlier, each subject had 80 marks for theory and 20 for internal assessment, but for 2026, it's 100% external evaluation.

Key Dates for TS SSC 2026

For students planning their academic future, here's a quick overview of the key dates:

Event Date/Period
TS SSC Board Exams 2026 March 14 to April 16, 2026
Result Declaration April 29, 2026
Revaluation/Rechecking Result June 2026 (Expected)
Supplementary Exam June 2026 (Expected)
Supplementary Result July 2026 (Expected)

What's Next? Supplementary Exams and Academic Planning

For students who may not have cleared all subjects, the Telangana Board provides an opportunity to appear for supplementary examinations. The schedule for these exams will be announced by the board shortly after the main results are declared.

The TS SSC result is more than just a scorecard; it's a stepping stone. Students are advised to utilize this time to plan their next academic steps, whether it's enrolling in intermediate courses, vocational training, or other educational pursuits.

Historically, female students have consistently achieved higher pass percentages than male students in the TS SSC exams. In 2025, girls recorded a pass percentage of 88.53%, compared to 84.68% for boys, and a similar trend was observed in 2024. It remains to be seen if this trend continues in 2026.

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared their Telangana SSC Public Examinations 2026! Your hard work and dedication have paid off. For those awaiting supplementary exams, stay focused and prepare well. The future is bright!
