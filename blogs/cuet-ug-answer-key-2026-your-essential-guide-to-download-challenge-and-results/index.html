CUET UG Answer Key 2026: Your Ultimate Guide to Downloads, Challenges & Results

The Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) 2026 examinations have recently concluded, and the anticipation for the answer key is at an all-time high among lakhs of aspirants. The National Testing Agency (NTA), responsible for conducting this massive entrance exam, is all set to release the provisional answer key, a crucial step for candidates to estimate their scores and prepare for the next stages of university admissions. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about the CUET UG Answer Key 2026, from downloading it to challenging incorrect responses and understanding its impact on your final results.

Key Dates to Remember for CUET UG 2026

Keeping track of important dates is vital. Here’s a tentative timeline for the CUET UG 2026 answer key and result events:

Event Tentative Date (2026)
CUET UG 2026 Exam Dates May 11 - May 31
Provisional Answer Key Release Fourth week of June / Early June
Answer Key Challenge Window Fourth week of June
Final Answer Key Release June / First week of July
CUET UG 2026 Result Declaration July / Late June / Early July
How to Download the CUET UG 2026 Provisional Answer Key

Once released, candidates can easily access the provisional answer key from the official NTA CUET websites. Follow these simple steps:

1. Visit the Official NTA CUET Website
Navigate to exams.nta.ac.in or cuet.nta.nic.in. Some sources also mention cuet.samarth.ac.in.
2. Locate the Answer Key Link
On the homepage, look for the "CUET UG 2026 Answer Key" or "Provisional Answer Key of CUET (UG)" link.
3. Log In with Your Credentials
You will be prompted to log in using your CUET Application ID/Number and Date of Birth or Password.
4. View and Download the Answer Key
The answer key, often in PDF format, will be displayed on your screen. Download and save it for your reference.

Along with the provisional answer key, NTA will also release your individual response sheet. This sheet contains the answers you marked during the exam, allowing you to compare your responses with the official key to estimate your score.

Challenging the CUET UG 2026 Answer Key

If you identify any discrepancies or errors in the provisional answer key, NTA provides a window to challenge them. This is a crucial opportunity to ensure fairness in evaluation.

1. Access the Challenge Link
Go to the official CUET exam page on the NTA website and find the "Answer Key Challenge" link.
2. Log In and Select Questions
Log in using your application number and date of birth/password. You can then view the question papers and the official answer IDs. Select the question(s) you wish to challenge.
3. Upload Supporting Documents
Provide valid proof or documentation to support your challenge for each selected question.
4. Pay the Challenge Fee
A non-refundable fee of INR 200 per question must be paid for each challenge.
5. Submit and Print Receipt
Submit your challenge and remember to keep a printout of the confirmation receipt for your records.
Understanding the CUET UG 2026 Marking Scheme

To accurately calculate your estimated score using the provisional answer key and your response sheet, it's essential to know the marking scheme:

✅ For every correct answer, you will be awarded +5 marks.

✅ For every incorrect answer, 1 mark will be deducted (negative marking).

✅ Unattempted questions will receive 0 marks, with no deductions.

What Happens After the Provisional Answer Key?

After the challenge window closes, NTA reviews all submitted objections. Based on this review, a **final answer key** will be released. This final key is used for the preparation of the CUET UG 2026 results.

The CUET UG 2026 result is expected to be declared in July 2026, or in the last week of June/early July. You will be able to check your result on the official NTA CUET website by logging in with your application number and date of birth. There will be no re-checking or re-evaluation of results after the final scores are declared.

The CUET UG 2026 exam, conducted in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode from May 11 to May 31, 2026, saw widespread participation. Initial analyses indicated a moderate overall difficulty, with a strong emphasis on NCERT-based concepts across various domain subjects.

Preparing for Admissions

Once the results are out, participating universities will begin their admission processes, which typically involve preparing merit lists and conducting counselling sessions. Your CUET score will be a crucial factor in securing admission to your desired undergraduate program for the academic year 2026-2027.

Keep a close watch on the official NTA CUET website and the websites of your target universities for the latest announcements regarding the final answer key, results, and admission counselling schedules.
