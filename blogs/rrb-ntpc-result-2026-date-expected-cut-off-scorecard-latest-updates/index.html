RRB NTPC Result 2026 Date: Expected Cut Off & Scorecard – Your Complete Guide

The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) Non-Technical Popular Categories (NTPC) examination is one of the most sought-after recruitment drives in India, attracting millions of aspirants. With the Computer Based Test 1 (CBT 1) for Graduate Level posts (CEN 06/2025) having recently concluded between March 16 and March 27, 2026, the anticipation for the RRB NTPC Result 2026 is at an all-time high. Approximately 40 lakh candidates appeared for this examination, eagerly awaiting their scores and qualification status for the next stages.

RRB NTPC Result 2026 Date: What to Expect

Candidates are keen to know when they can expect their RRB NTPC CBT 1 results. The provisional answer key for the Graduate Level CBT 1 (CEN 06/2025) was released on April 6, 2026, and candidates had until April 12, 2026, to raise any objections. Following the evaluation of these objections and the release of the final answer key, the Railway Recruitment Board typically takes two to three months to announce the results.

✅ **Expected Result Release:** The RRB NTPC CBT 1 Result 2026 for Graduate Level posts (CEN 06/2025) is anticipated to be released in May or June 2026.

✅ **Official Websites:** Results will be published on the respective regional RRB websites in PDF format.

✅ **Scorecard Release:** Individual scorecards will be released concurrently with the results.

Understanding RRB NTPC Cut Off 2026

The cut-off marks are crucial as they determine a candidate's eligibility for the subsequent stages of the selection process. The RRB NTPC Cut Off 2026 will be released category-wise and zone-wise for both CBT 1 and CBT 2 exams.

Factors Influencing the Cut-Off

Several dynamic factors influence the final cut-off marks:

✅ Number of Vacancies: Higher vacancies can sometimes lead to lower cut-offs.

✅ Difficulty Level of the Exam: A tougher paper may result in a lower cut-off.

✅ Number of Candidates Appeared: Higher competition can push cut-offs higher.

✅ Normalization Process: Since the exam is conducted in multiple shifts, scores are normalized to ensure fairness, which can impact the final cut-off.

✅ Previous Year Trends: Historical cut-offs provide a reference point for expectations.

Minimum Qualifying Marks vs. Cut-Off

It's important to distinguish between minimum qualifying marks and the actual cut-off. The minimum qualifying marks are set by RRB as a baseline to be eligible for evaluation, but they do not guarantee selection. The actual cut-off will always be higher and depends on the factors mentioned above.

Category Minimum Qualifying Marks (CBT 1)
General (UR) / EWS 40%
OBC (Non-Creamy Layer) / SC 30%
ST 25%

Expected RRB NTPC 2026 Cut Off (CBT 1 - Graduate Level)

Based on initial difficulty levels observed in the March 2026 Graduate Level exams (CEN 06/2025) and historical trends, the expected cut-off ranges for CBT 1 (out of 100 marks) are as follows. These are estimated ranges, and actual cut-offs will be officially released with the results.

Category Expected Cut Off Range (Graduate Level CBT 1)
General (UR) 75 – 85
OBC/EWS 60 – 70
SC 50 – 60
ST 45 – 55

Note: These are expected ranges. High-competition zones may see cut-offs at the higher end, while others might be slightly lower. Target scores should ideally be higher than these ranges to ensure a comfortable buffer.

How to Check Your RRB NTPC Result 2026

Once released, candidates can easily access their results online. Follow these steps:

1. Visit Your Regional RRB Website
Go to the official website of the RRB region you applied for (e.g., rrbcdg.gov.in).
2. Locate the Result Link
Look for the notification or link titled "RRB NTPC CBT 1 Result 2026 (CEN 06/2025)" on the homepage.
3. Download the Result PDF
Click the link to open the result PDF. This PDF will contain the roll numbers of all shortlisted candidates.
4. Search Your Roll Number
Use the "Ctrl+F" function to search for your roll number within the PDF. If your roll number is present, you have qualified for the next stage.
5. Save the PDF
Download and save the result PDF for future reference.

Your RRB NTPC Scorecard 2026

The RRB NTPC scorecard is an essential document providing a detailed breakdown of your performance in the exam. It will be released along with the result for all candidates who appeared, regardless of whether they qualified or not.

What Your Scorecard Contains

Your RRB NTPC Scorecard will typically include:

Candidate Details
Name, Registration Number, Date of Birth, Post Name.
Exam Information
Exam Name, CEN Number, Exam Date, Shift Timing.
Performance Breakdown
Total number of questions, questions cancelled/ignored, attempted questions, correct answers, incorrect answers, raw marks.
Scores
Total Raw Score and Normalized Score (after the normalization process).
Status
Qualifying Status (Pass/Fail) and Shortlisting Status for the next stage.

How to Download Your Scorecard

1. Visit the Official RRB Portal
Go to your regional RRB website.
2. Click Scorecard Link
Look for the "RRB NTPC CBT 1/2 Scorecard" link.
3. Enter Login Credentials
Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth.
4. View and Download
Your scorecard will appear on the screen. Download and print it for your records.

What Happens After CBT 1 Results?

Candidates who successfully clear CBT 1 will be shortlisted for the second stage of the recruitment process, CBT 2. RRB typically shortlists candidates at 20 times the total post-wise vacancy in each zone for CBT 2. The entire selection process for RRB NTPC involves multiple stages: CBT 1, CBT 2, Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) / Typing Skill Test (TST) (as applicable per post), Document Verification (DV), and Medical Examination.

✅ **CBT 2:** The second computer-based test for shortlisted candidates.

✅ **CBAT/TST:** Aptitude or typing tests for specific positions.

✅ **Document Verification (DV):** Candidates attend DV in a ratio of 1.5x-2x of vacancies based on CBT 2 merit.

✅ **Medical Examination:** A mandatory medical fitness test.

✅ **Final Merit List:** Based on overall performance and stages cleared, leading to appointment letters.

The journey to an RRB NTPC post requires consistent effort and strategic preparation. Stay updated by regularly checking the official regional RRB websites for the latest announcements regarding results, cut-offs, and further stages of recruitment.
