TNPSC Group 4 Recruitment 2026: Your Gateway to Government Service in Tamil Nadu!

Are you aspiring for a prestigious government job in Tamil Nadu? The Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC) Group 4 Recruitment 2026 presents a golden opportunity to join various departments as a Village Administrative Officer (VAO), Junior Assistant, Typist, Steno-Typist, and other crucial roles. The official notification is highly anticipated, and with the exam date already announced, now is the perfect time to kickstart your preparation!

The TNPSC Group 4 Exam 2026 is scheduled for December 20, 2026, offering a clear target for dedicated aspirants. The detailed notification will be released on October 6, 2026.

Key Highlights of TNPSC Group 4 2026

✅ Official Notification Date: October 6, 2026

✅ Application Period: Expected from October 6, 2026, to November 5, 2026

✅ Exam Date: December 20, 2026

✅ Posts: VAO, Junior Assistant, Typist, Steno-Typist, and more

✅ Selection Process: Written Exam, Document Verification, Counselling (and Physical Test for specific posts)

✅ Exam Mode: Offline (OMR-based)

Important Dates to Remember

Staying informed about the critical dates is paramount for a smooth application and preparation journey. Here's a quick overview of the announced schedule for TNPSC Group 4 2026:

Eligibility Criteria: Do You Qualify?

Before applying, ensure you meet the essential eligibility requirements set by TNPSC. These typically cover nationality, age, and educational qualifications.

1. Nationality
Applicants must be an Indian citizen. Citizens of Nepal or Bhutan, and Sri Lankan repatriates with a Government of India consent letter, are also eligible.
2. Age Limit (as of July 1, 2026 - specific cut-off date to be confirmed in official notification)

For most Group 4 posts, the minimum age is 18 years, and the maximum is 32 years for General category candidates. However, for posts like Village Administrative Officer (VAO) and Forest posts, the minimum age is 21 years.

Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/MBC/BC/BCM/Destitute Widows) as per government norms. For instance, the maximum age limit for reserved categories can extend to 42 years for most posts, and 37 years for Forest posts.

3. Educational Qualification

The minimum educational qualification for most TNPSC Group 4 posts is a pass in the SSLC (10th Standard Public Examination) from a recognized board.

For specific posts like Typist and Steno-Typist, candidates must also possess valid typing/shorthand certificates (Higher/Lower Grade in English and/or Tamil) from recognized institutions.

Proficiency in the Tamil language is also a crucial requirement. Candidates must have studied Tamil as a subject in SSLC or equivalent, or pass the Second Class Language Test in Tamil conducted by TNPSC.

Exam Pattern & Syllabus: Your Roadmap to Success

The TNPSC Group 4 examination is a single-stage written test conducted in offline (OMR-based) mode. Understanding the exam pattern and syllabus is crucial for effective preparation.

Exam Pattern 2026

Exam Structure:

The exam is 3 hours long, consisting of 200 objective-type questions for a total of 300 marks. The minimum qualifying mark for all categories is 90 out of 300.

The paper is divided into two parts:

  • Part A: Tamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test (SSLC Standard) - 100 questions, 150 marks. Candidates must secure a minimum of 40% (60 marks) in Part A for their Part B paper to be evaluated.
  • Part B: General Studies (SSLC Standard) - 75 questions, 150 marks.
  • Part B: Aptitude & Mental Ability Test (SSLC Standard) - 25 questions.

Questions in Part A will be in Tamil only. Questions in General Studies and Aptitude & Mental Ability Test (Part B) will be available in both English and Tamil.

Syllabus 2026

The syllabus for TNPSC Group 4 is comprehensive, covering various subjects at the SSLC level.

General Tamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test:
This section assesses your proficiency in the Tamil language, including grammar, vocabulary, literature, comprehension, synonyms, antonyms, and works of renowned Tamil poets and authors.
General Studies:

Topics include General Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), History and Culture of India and Tamil Nadu, Indian Polity, Geography, Economics, Indian National Movement, Current Events, and Development Administration in Tamil Nadu.

Aptitude & Mental Ability Test:
This section evaluates your logical reasoning and problem-solving skills, with topics like Simplification, Percentage, HCF & LCM, Ratio and Proportion, Simple Interest, Compound Interest, Area, Volume, Time and Work, Logical Reasoning, Puzzles, and Number Series.

Selection Process: Your Journey to a Government Job

The TNPSC Group 4 selection process is straightforward and merit-based.

✅ Written Examination: The primary stage, where candidates are assessed on their knowledge and aptitude.

✅ Document Verification & Counselling: Candidates who qualify in the written exam will be called for document verification and counselling to assess their suitability for various posts.

✅ Physical Standards Verification & Endurance Test: Applicable only for specific posts like Forest Guard, Forest Guard with Driving License, Forest Watcher, and Forest Watcher.

How to Apply for TNPSC Group 4 2026

The application process for TNPSC Group 4 2026 will be conducted online through the official TNPSC website.

Step-by-Step Application Guide:
  1. One-Time Registration (OTR): If you haven't already, complete the One-Time Registration on the TNPSC official website (www.tnpsc.gov.in) by paying Rs. 150. This registration is valid for five years.
  2. Login: After OTR, log in using your credentials.
  3. Fill Application Form: Navigate to the "Apply Online" section, select "Group 4," and fill in all personal, educational, and contact details accurately.
  4. Upload Documents: Scan and upload required documents, including a passport-sized photograph and signature, as per the specified guidelines.
  5. Pay Application Fee: Pay the examination fee of Rs. 100 (if applicable, as some categories are exempted) through online mode.
  6. Submit and Print: Review all details carefully before submitting the application. Download and print a copy for your records.

The TNPSC Group 4 Recruitment 2026 is a significant opportunity for government job aspirants in Tamil Nadu. With the official notification and exam date approaching, a well-planned and consistent preparation strategy is key to achieving success. Stay tuned to the official TNPSC website for the latest updates and detailed notification. Good luck with your preparation!
