CGBSE Result 2026: Class 10th & 12th Scores Released Today! Your Complete Guide

Excitement fills the air as the Chhattisgarh Board of Secondary Education (CGBSE) announces the much-awaited Class 10th and 12th results for 2026.

The moment of truth has arrived for thousands of students across Chhattisgarh! The Chhattisgarh Board of Secondary Education (CGBSE) has officially declared the Class 10 (High School) and Class 12 (Higher Secondary) examination results today, April 29, 2026, at 2:30 PM. State Education Minister, Gajendra Yadav, confirmed the release, bringing an end to the anticipation for approximately 5.66 lakh students who appeared for the exams this year.

This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about checking your CGBSE Result 2026, understanding your scorecard, and planning your next steps.

Key Highlights of CGBSE Result 2026

✅ Result Declared Today: April 29, 2026

✅ Time: 2:30 PM (IST)

✅ Official Websites: cgbse.nic.in, results.cg.nic.in, cg.results.nic.in

✅ Alternative Access: DigiLocker, SMS, UMANG App

✅ Approx. 5.66 Lakh Students: Awaited results for Class 10th and 12th combined

How to Check Your CGBSE Result 2026 Online

The CGBSE results are primarily accessible through the official websites. Here's a step-by-step guide to help you retrieve your scorecard:

1. Visit Official Websites
Open your web browser and go to one of the official Chhattisgarh Board websites:
  • cgbse.nic.in
  • results.cg.nic.in
  • cg.results.nic.in
2. Navigate to the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the 'Students Corner' or 'Exam Results 2026' section. Click on the link relevant to your class: "CG Board Class 10th result 2026 link" or "CGBSE Class 12th Result 2026 link".
3. Enter Your Roll Number
You will be redirected to a login window. Carefully enter your examination roll number as it appears on your admit card. You might also need to enter a captcha code for verification.
4. Submit and View Result
After entering the details, click on the 'Submit' or 'Get Results' button. Your CGBSE result for 2026, including subject-wise marks and grades, will be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print Provisional Marksheet
It is highly recommended to download and save a copy of your provisional marksheet. Take a printout for immediate reference and use in college admissions, as this online copy is temporary.

Alternative Ways to Access Your Scores

In case of heavy website traffic, or if you prefer other methods, CGBSE also offers alternative ways to check your results:

✅ SMS Service: Type CG10 [space] [Roll Number] for Class 10th or CG12 [space] [Roll Number] for Class 12th and send it to 56263.

✅ DigiLocker: Your digital marksheet will be available on DigiLocker (digilocker.gov.in) shortly after the official declaration.

✅ UMANG App: You can also check your results via the UMANG mobile application.

Understanding Your CGBSE Marksheet and Passing Criteria

Once you download your provisional marksheet, ensure all the details are accurate. The marksheet will contain vital information:

✅ Candidate's Name

✅ Roll Number

✅ Father's & Mother's Name

✅ Centre Code and School Code

✅ Subjects and Marks Obtained (Theory and Practical)

✅ Grand Total and Division

Passing Criteria
To successfully pass the CGBSE examinations, students must secure a minimum of 33% marks in each subject (both theory and practical components where applicable) and also in the overall aggregate.
Re-evaluation and Supplementary Exams
If you are not satisfied with your marks, you can apply for re-evaluation or rechecking of your answer sheets. The application forms for this will be available on the official website, typically within 15 days of the result declaration. Students who fail in one or two subjects have the option to appear for supplementary examinations, usually scheduled for July 2026, to avoid losing an academic year.
Collecting Original Marksheets
Remember that the online marksheet is provisional. You will need to collect your original mark sheets, passing certificates, and migration certificates from your respective schools a few weeks after the results are declared. These original documents are crucial for further academic processes and college admissions.

This year, approximately 5.66 lakh candidates appeared for the CGBSE exams, with 3.21 lakh for Class 10th and 2.45 lakh for Class 12th. While the 2026 pass percentages will be announced today, we can look at the previous year's performance for context:

For Class 12 students, it's important to recall that the Hindi exam conducted on March 24, 2026, was declared null and void due to allegations of a paper leak. A re-examination for this subject was successfully held on April 10, 2026.
