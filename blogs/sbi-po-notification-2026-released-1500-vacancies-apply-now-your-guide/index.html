SBI PO Notification 2026 Released: 1500 Vacancies Announced – Your Comprehensive Guide!

The wait is finally over for aspiring banking professionals! The State Bank of India (SBI) has officially released the much-anticipated SBI PO Notification 2026 on June 18, 2026. This year, SBI aims to recruit 1500 Probationary Officers (POs) across its branches nationwide, offering a golden opportunity for graduates seeking a prestigious career in the banking sector. The application window for this highly sought-after position is now open, providing candidates a limited time to apply online.

Being an SBI PO is not just a job; it's a pathway to an attractive salary, excellent career growth, job security, and invaluable exposure to diverse banking operations. If you're ready to embark on a rewarding journey with India's largest public sector bank, read on for a detailed breakdown of everything you need to know about the SBI PO Recruitment 2026.

Key Highlights of SBI PO Notification 2026

✅ Official Notification Released on June 18, 2026.

✅ Total 1500 Vacancies Announced (1446 fresh & 54 backlog).

✅ Online Application Started: June 18, 2026.

✅ Last Date to Apply Online: July 8, 2026.

✅ Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline.

✅ Age Limit: 21-30 years as of April 1, 2026.

✅ Selection Process: Prelims, Mains, Psychometric Test, Group Exercise, and Interview.

SBI PO Vacancy 2026

This year, SBI has announced a total of 1500 vacancies for the Probationary Officer posts. This includes 1446 fresh vacancies and 54 backlog vacancies, distributed across various categories. This is a significant number, offering a strong chance for dedicated aspirants.

Eligibility Criteria for SBI PO 2026

Before applying, ensure you meet the essential eligibility requirements as specified in the official notification.

1. Nationality
Applicants must be Indian Citizens.
2. Educational Qualification (as on September 30, 2026)
Candidates must hold a Bachelor's Degree in any discipline from a recognized University or any equivalent qualification recognized by the Central Government. Those in their final year/semester of graduation are also eligible to apply provisionally, provided they can produce proof of passing their graduation on or before September 30, 2026.
3. Age Limit (as on April 1, 2026)
Candidates should not be below 21 years and not above 30 years of age. This means applicants should have been born between April 2, 1996, and April 1, 2005 (both dates inclusive). Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per government norms.

The Road to Becoming a PO: Selection Process

The SBI PO selection process is a rigorous three-phase examination designed to identify the best talent.

Phase I: Preliminary Examination
This is the initial screening stage. Candidates must qualify in the Prelims to be shortlisted for the Mains Examination based on category-wise merit.
Phase II: Main Examination
The Mains exam comprises both Objective and Descriptive Tests. Successful candidates from this phase proceed to the final selection stage.
Phase III: Psychometric Test, Group Exercise & Interview
Candidates shortlisted after the Mains examination undergo a Psychometric Assessment, a Group Exercise (20 marks), and a Personal Interview (30 marks).

What's New in SBI PO 2026?

The SBI PO 2026 notification brings a few significant changes that aspirants should be aware of:

Aadhaar Verification Made Mandatory
For the first time, Aadhaar verification has been made compulsory for all candidates.
Increased Number of Attempts
The maximum permissible attempts have been increased by 2 for several categories: General/EWS candidates can now avail 6 attempts (previously 4), and OBC/OBC (PwBD) candidates can avail 9 attempts (previously 7). SC/ST candidates continue to have no restriction on attempts.
Revised Descriptive Paper Marks
The marks allotted to the descriptive paper have been revised from 50 to 30. Consequently, the Mains Total Marks are now reduced to 230 (previously 250).
Enhanced Annual CTC
The Annual CTC at the initial scale for the Mumbai Centre has been significantly increased from Rs. 20.43 Lakhs to Rs. 21.97 Lakhs.
New Official Website
The official SBI website has been updated from sbi.co.in to sbi.bank.in.
Additional Exam Centres
Four new exam centres have been added for the SBI PO examination.

Salary and Career Growth

A Probationary Officer in SBI enjoys a highly competitive salary package. Presently, the starting basic pay is ₹48,480. Coupled with various allowances and benefits, the Annual CTC at the initial scale at the Mumbai Centre is now ₹21.97 Lakhs. Beyond the attractive remuneration, SBI POs have excellent opportunities for career advancement, with structured paths to higher managerial positions within the bank.

Don't miss out on this incredible opportunity to shape your banking career with SBI. The online application link is active, and the last date to apply is July 8, 2026. Start your preparation today!

For detailed information, candidates are strongly advised to download and thoroughly read the official SBI PO Notification 2026 PDF available on the SBI careers website, sbi.bank.in.
