Kerala SSLC Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Higher Education!

The much-awaited Kerala SSLC (Secondary School Leaving Certificate) Examination Results for 2026 have been officially declared by the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan! On May 15, 2026, at 3 PM, the results were announced, marking a pivotal moment for thousands of students across the state. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about accessing your results, checking school-wise performance, and important post-result procedures.

A total of 4,17,497 students appeared for the Kerala Class 10 exams this year, conducted from March 5 to March 30, 2026. Of these, an impressive 4,14,290 students successfully passed, making them eligible for higher education. The overall pass percentage for 2026 stands at 99.07%.

How to Check Your Kerala SSLC Result 2026 Online

Students can access their individual results easily through multiple official websites. To view your scorecard, you will typically need your Registration Number and Date of Birth.

Step-by-Step Guide to Checking Individual Results:
  1. Visit any of the official result websites listed below.
  2. Look for the 'Kerala SSLC Result 2026' or 'SSLC Result 2026' link on the homepage.
  3. Click on the link, and a new page will open.
  4. Enter your Registration Number and Date of Birth in the respective fields.
  5. Click on 'Submit' or 'Get Result'.
  6. Your Kerala SSLC 2026 Marksheet will be displayed on the screen.
  7. Download and save the provisional scorecard for future reference.

The Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan has made the results available on several platforms to ensure smooth access and reduce server load.

Kerala SSLC Result 2026 School Wise: How to Check

School authorities and students interested in checking results for an entire school can do so using the school code. The school-wise results provide a comprehensive overview of the performance of all students from a particular institution.

Checking School-Wise Results:
  1. Go to the official website: sslcexam.kerala.gov.in or results.kite.kerala.gov.in.
  2. Click on the 'SSLC Result 2026 School-wise' link.
  3. Enter your School Code in the designated field.
  4. Click on 'Submit' or 'Get Results'.
  5. A list of students' results for that school will appear. You may also need to enter a registration number and date of birth for specific details.
  6. Download and save the Saphalam school-wise result for future reference.

Other Ways to Access Your SSLC Results

Beyond the official websites, the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan facilitates result access through various digital platforms for convenience.

1. Via DigiLocker
Students can access their digital mark sheets securely through the DigiLocker platform (digilocker.gov.in or results.digilocker.kerala.gov.in). You'll need your login credentials to view your provisional mark sheet.
2. Mobile Applications: PRD Live & Saphalam KITE
The official mobile applications, "PRD Live" and "Saphalam KITE," are excellent resources. Download them from the Google Play Store or App Store, enter your registration number and date of birth, and view your results instantly.
3. Via SMS
Though less common for detailed results, some services might offer results via SMS. Check local announcements for specific SMS formats and numbers.
4. WhatsApp Service
This year, the board has introduced a provision to get results via WhatsApp. Students can save the number 9188619958 and send "RESULT" along with their Registration number and Date of Birth to receive their marksheet.

Key Statistics and Highlights of SSLC Result 2026

The 2026 SSLC results showcase several notable performances across the state.

  • Overall Pass Percentage: 99.07%
  • Students Appeared: 4,17,497
  • Students Passed: 4,14,290
  • Students with A+ in All Subjects: 30,514
  • Girls vs. Boys Performance: Girls outperformed boys with a pass percentage of 99.22% compared to boys' 98.93%. Additionally, 20,771 girl students secured A+ grades in all subjects, significantly higher than 9,743 boys.
  • Schools with 100% Pass Rate: 2,105 schools achieved a 100% pass rate this year, including 767 government schools.
  • Best Performing Revenue District: Pathanamthitta emerged as the best-performing revenue district with an impressive pass percentage of 99.72%.

✅ Roll number/Registration number

✅ School name and School code

✅ Subject-wise marks secured

✅ Total marks secured

✅ Qualifying status

✅ Grades obtained

What's Next? Re-evaluation, Scrutiny, and Photocopies

For students who are not satisfied with their Kerala SSLC Result 2026, the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan provides options for re-evaluation, scrutiny (re-totalling), and obtaining photocopies of answer sheets.

Application Process:
The application process for these services will be conducted online through the official portal of the Kerala Pareeksha Bhavan at sslcexam.kerala.gov.in. Students can typically apply within five days of the result declaration. The board will release a detailed notification with dates and fee structures shortly.

For re-evaluation, the application fee is Rs. 250 per subject.

The Kerala SSLC Result 2026 is a significant milestone, opening doors to higher secondary education and future career paths. Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared their examinations!
