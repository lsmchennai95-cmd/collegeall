ICSE Class 10 Result 2026: Date, Time & Direct Links – Your Comprehensive Guide

The wait is almost over for lakhs of students who appeared for the Indian Certificate of Secondary Education (ICSE) Class 10 examinations in 2026. The Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE) is gearing up to release the much-anticipated results, marking a significant milestone in every student's academic journey. While an official notification detailing the exact date and time is still awaited from CISCE, based on previous year trends and recent reports, students can expect the results very soon.

Breaking News: The ICSE Class 10 Result 2026 is widely expected to be declared by the end of April 2026 or in the first week of May 2026, with several reports pointing to a declaration around April 30, 2026.

Expected Date and Time of ICSE Class 10 Result 2026

Anticipation is at an all-time high, and students, parents, and educators are eager to know the precise moment the results will be live. While CISCE maintains a shroud of secrecy until the official announcement, past trends offer a strong indication.

✅ Expected Declaration Date: Last week of April 2026 or first week of May 2026. Many sources suggest around April 25-30, 2026, or by April 30, 2026.

✅ Expected Time: Historically, results are often declared around 11:00 AM.

✅ Official Confirmation: The exact date and time will be confirmed by CISCE a few days prior to the declaration. Students are advised to rely solely on official sources.

How to Check Your ICSE Class 10 Result 2026 Online

Once declared, the results will be accessible through the official CISCE websites. To avoid last-minute panic, familiarize yourself with the process:

1. Visit the Official CISCE Result Portal
Navigate to either results.cisce.org or cisce.org. These are the primary platforms for result declarations.
2. Select Your Course
On the homepage, you will find an option to select your course. Choose "ICSE" for Class 10 results.
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will need to input your unique Unique ID and your Index Number. These details are crucial and can be found on your ICSE Class 10 admit card.
4. Complete the Captcha
For security purposes, enter the captcha code displayed on the screen.
5. View and Download Your Result
After entering the details, click on "Submit" or "Show Result". Your provisional marksheet will be displayed. Download and take a printout for your records and future reference.

Alternative Ways to Access Your Scorecard

Heavy traffic on official websites can sometimes lead to slow loading times. CISCE provides alternative methods to check your results swiftly:

Via DigiLocker App
DigiLocker is a secure platform for accessing official documents.
  1. Open the DigiLocker website or mobile app.
  2. Log in using your registered number or Aadhaar-linked credentials. New users should complete sign-up and link their Aadhaar.
  3. Go to the "Issued Documents" section.
  4. Click "Search Documents" and select CISCE as the issuing authority.
  5. Choose "ICSE Class 10 Marksheet 2026".
  6. Enter your Unique ID and other required details.
  7. Click "Get Document" to view and save your digital marksheet.
Via SMS Service
You can receive your results directly on your mobile phone via SMS.

Type ICSE <Your Unique ID> and send it to 09248082883.

You will receive your result as an SMS shortly after.

What to Do After Receiving Your Results

Once you have your results in hand, it's essential to understand the next steps:

✅ Save Your Provisional Marksheet: Download and print a copy for immediate use. The original mark sheet will be issued by your school later.

✅ Consider Re-evaluation/Re-verification: If you are not satisfied with your scores, CISCE provides options for re-evaluation and re-verification of marks. Be aware of the application window and process.

✅ Improvement Exams: Students wishing to improve their performance in up to two subjects can appear for improvement exams.

✅ Minimum Passing Marks: Remember, students need to score at least 33% marks in each subject to pass the ICSE Class 10 examination.

Important Points to Remember

✅ Always check results from official CISCE websites or trusted sources like DigiLocker.

✅ Keep your admit card handy as it contains your Unique ID and Index Number, which are essential for checking results.

✅ Be wary of unverified messages or social media rumors regarding result dates. Always wait for an official announcement from CISCE.
