CUET UG City Intimation Slip 2026: Your Essential Guide to Exam Planning

The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially released the CUET UG 2026 City Intimation Slip, a crucial preliminary document designed to help candidates plan their logistics for the upcoming Common University Entrance Test (UG). If you're appearing for CUET UG 2026, this slip is your first step towards a well-organized exam experience.

The CUET UG 2026 examination is scheduled to be conducted between May 11 and May 31, 2026, in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode across various centers in India and abroad. The City Intimation Slip, released on April 29, 2026, provides advance information about your allotted examination city and subject-wise exam dates, allowing you to make necessary travel and accommodation arrangements well in time.

What is the CUET UG City Intimation Slip 2026?

The CUET UG City Intimation Slip 2026 is a preliminary document issued by the NTA, not to be confused with your admit card. Its primary purpose is to inform candidates about the city where their examination center will be located. This early notification is vital for candidates to plan their travel and accommodation, especially for those who need to travel to a different city for their examination.

✅ Helps in planning travel and accommodation in advance.

✅ Confirms that your application has been processed successfully.

✅ Provides subject-wise exam dates and shift timings.

✅ Reduces last-minute stress and rush.

Key Details on Your City Intimation Slip

Upon downloading your CUET UG 2026 City Intimation Slip, ensure you carefully check all the details mentioned. This slip contains crucial information that will aid your exam preparation and logistics.

1. Candidate Information
Your name, application number, father's name, gender, date of birth, category, and PwD status (if applicable) will be listed.
2. Exam Logistics
This includes your allotted exam city and state, the specific exam date, and the shift and timing for your subjects.
3. Subject Details
The subjects you have opted for, along with the medium of the examination, will be clearly indicated.
4. Important Instructions
The slip may also contain important instructions for exam day.

How to Download Your CUET UG City Intimation Slip 2026

Downloading your city intimation slip is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to access your document:

1. Visit the Official Website
Go to the official CUET UG website: cuet.nta.nic.in.
2. Locate the Link
Click on the tab or link that says 'CUET UG Advance City Slip 2026 Download' or 'CUET UG City Intimation Slip 2026'.
3. Enter Login Details
You will need to enter your application number and password. Some portals might also ask for your date of birth or a security pin/captcha.
4. View and Download
After logging in, your CUET advance city slip will be displayed on the screen. Check all the details carefully.
5. Print for Reference
Download the PDF and take a printout for your reference. While not mandatory for exam entry, it's useful for planning.

Important: In case you encounter any issues while downloading the slip, or if there are discrepancies in the details, contact the NTA helpline immediately.

CUET City Intimation Slip vs. Admit Card: What's the Difference?

It's crucial to understand that the City Intimation Slip is NOT the Admit Card. Many students confuse these two documents.

Feature CUET UG City Intimation Slip 2026 CUET UG Admit Card 2026
Purpose To inform candidates of their allotted exam city and subject-wise exam date in advance for planning travel and accommodation. The official hall ticket for entry into the examination center.
Content Exam City, Exam Date, Shift & Timing, Subject/Language Details. Candidate details, application/roll number, photo & signature, final exam centre name & full address, reporting time, gate closing time, exam-day instructions.
Release Date Released on April 29, 2026. Expected to be released by May 8, 2026 (first week of May 2026).
Entry Requirement Not valid for entry to the exam center. Mandatory document for entry to the exam center, along with a valid photo ID.

The CUET UG Admit Card 2026 is expected to be released in the first week of May 2026, likely around May 8, 2026. Make sure to download it once available and take a clear printout, as it is a mandatory document for entry to the examination hall.
