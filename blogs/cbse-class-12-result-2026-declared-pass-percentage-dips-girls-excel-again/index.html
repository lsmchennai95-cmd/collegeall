CBSE Class 12 Result 2026 Declared: Key Highlights and How to Check Your Scores Today!

The wait is finally over for millions of students as the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) officially declared the Class 12 results for 2026 today, May 13, 2026. This announcement brings an end to months of anticipation following examinations held earlier this year.

A Closer Look at the 2026 Results

The CBSE Class 12 examinations for the academic year 2026 were conducted from February 17 to April 10. This year, a total of 17,80,365 students registered for the examination, with 17,68,968 appearing, and 15,07,109 successfully clearing the exams. The board continued its trend of announcing results around mid-May, aligning with declaration dates from previous years.

Breaking News: The overall pass percentage for CBSE Class 12 in 2026 stands at 85.20%, marking a slight dip of 3.19 percentage points compared to the 88.39% recorded in 2025.

Gender and Regional Performance: Girls Continue to Shine

In a continuing trend, girls have once again outperformed boys in the CBSE Class 12 results. Girls achieved a pass percentage of 88.86%, significantly higher than the 82.13% secured by boys, creating a commendable gap of 6.73 percentage points. Moreover, transgender candidates achieved a 100% pass rate.

Regionally, Trivandrum has emerged as the top-performing region with an impressive pass percentage of 95.62%. Conversely, Prayagraj recorded the lowest pass percentage at 72.43%.

Pass Percentage Trends Over the Years

Understanding the historical context of pass percentages can provide valuable insights into this year's performance. Here's a quick look at the CBSE Class 12 pass percentages over the last few years:

Year Overall Pass Percentage
2026 85.20%
2025 88.39%
2024 87.98%
2023 87.33%

The data indicates a slight decrease in the overall pass percentage for 2026 after a consistent performance in previous years.

How to Check Your CBSE Class 12 Result 2026

Students can access their much-awaited results through various official platforms. It is crucial to have your roll number, school number, admit card ID, and date of birth ready for quick access.

✅ Official Websites: Visit results.cbse.nic.in, cbse.gov.in, or cbseresults.nic.in.

✅ Digital Platforms: Results are also available on DigiLocker (digilocker.gov.in) and the UMANG App.

✅ IVRS: Students can also utilize the Interactive Voice Response System (IVRS) for result access.

Step-by-Step Guide to Download Your Scorecard:

1. Navigate to an Official Website
Go to results.cbse.nic.in or cbse.gov.in.
2. Click on the Result Link
Look for and click on the "CBSE Class 12 Result 2026" link.
3. Enter Your Credentials
Input your roll number, school number, and admit card ID. Your date of birth might also be required.
4. Submit and View
Submit the details, and your provisional marksheet will be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print
Download your scorecard and take a printout for future reference. Remember, this is a provisional marksheet; original documents will be issued by your school.

Important Post-Result Information

For students who may not have achieved their desired results, or wish to improve their scores, CBSE has announced key details:

✅ No Merit List: For 2026, CBSE has decided not to release an official merit list.

✅ Supplementary Examinations: Supplementary exams are scheduled for July 15, 2026, as a single-day examination. This provides a crucial opportunity for eligible students to improve their performance or clear compartment subjects.

✅ Digital Evaluation: This year, the board implemented an On-Screen Marking system for digital evaluation of answer sheets, aiming to enhance accuracy and expedite the result compilation process.

The declaration of CBSE Class 12 results marks a significant milestone for students. While the pass percentage has seen a slight decline, the individual achievements and efforts of lakhs of students are commendable. We wish all students the very best for their future endeavors!
