12th Board Results 2026: Your Essential Guide to Dates, Checking, and Next Steps

The wait is finally over, or almost over, for lakhs of students across India who appeared for their 12th board examinations in 2026! This crucial milestone marks the culmination of years of hard work and opens doors to higher education and future career paths. As an expert content creator and frontend developer, we understand the anticipation and need for accurate, timely information. Here’s a comprehensive guide to the 12th board results for various states and national boards for the academic year 2026, along with important details on how to check your scores and what to do next.

With many results already declared and others eagerly awaited, ensure you have your roll number and other credentials ready to access your scores swiftly!

National Board Results 2026

1. CISCE ISC (Class 12) Results 2026 Declared
The Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE) announced the ISC (Class 12) results for 2026 on April 30, 2026, at 11:00 AM. Students can access their results on the official CISCE websites. The exams were conducted from February 12, 2026, to April 6, 2026.

Official Websites:
  • cisce.org
  • results.cisce.org
To download marksheets, students typically need to enter their course code, candidate UID, index number, and captcha code.
2. CBSE Class 12 Results 2026 - Expected Soon
The Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) Class 12 results for 2026 are highly anticipated and are expected to be declared soon. While an official date hasn't been confirmed, media reports suggest the results are likely to be announced between May 11 and May 15, 2026, or in the third week of May. The board usually releases results without prior notice, so students are advised to stay alert.

The CBSE Class 12 Board Exams 2026 were conducted from February 17 to April 10, 2026.

Official Websites:
  • results.cbse.nic.in
  • cbseresults.nic.in
  • cbse.gov.in
Students can also check their results via DigiLocker and the UMANG app. To check online, students will need their roll number, school number, and admit card ID.

State Board Results 2026

1. Bihar Board (BSEB) 12th Results 2026 Declared
The Bihar School Examination Board (BSEB) was one of the first to announce its Class 12 (Intermediate) results. The BSEB Inter Results 2026 were declared on March 23, 2026, at 1:30 PM. A total of 13,17,846 students appeared for the exams, which were conducted from February 2 to February 13, 2026.

Official Websites:
  • www.interbiharboard.com
  • www.bsebexam.com
  • results.biharboardonline.com
Students need their roll code and roll number to check their results.
2. UP Board (UPMSP) 12th Results 2026 Declared
The Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad (UPMSP) announced the UP Board 12th Result 2026 on April 23, 2026, at 4:00 PM. Over 24 lakh students appeared for the Intermediate examinations, which took place from February 18 to March 12, 2026.

Official Websites:
  • upmsp.edu.in
  • upresults.nic.in
Students can check their results using their roll number.
3. Karnataka 2nd PUC Results 2026 Declared
The Karnataka School Examination and Assessment Board (KSEAB) declared the 2nd PUC Results 2026 on April 9, 2026, at 3:00 PM. Around 7.10 lakh students appeared for the examinations conducted from February 28 to March 17, 2026.

Official Website:
  • karresults.nic.in
Results were also made available digitally via DigiLocker. Students need their registration number to check their scores.
4. Maharashtra HSC Results 2026 Declared
The Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE) announced the MAH HSC Result 2026 on May 2, 2026, at 12:00 PM (or 1 PM on some platforms). A total of 15,32,487 candidates registered for the HSC Exams 2026, which were held from February 10, 2026, to March 11, 2026.

Official Websites:
  • mahresult.nic.in
  • hscresult.mkcl.org
  • mahahsscboard.in
Students can access their results using their seat number and mother's first name. Digital marksheets are available on DigiLocker.
5. Tamil Nadu 12th Results 2026 - Expected Soon
The Directorate of Government Examinations (DGE), Tamil Nadu, is tentatively expected to release the Tamil Nadu 12th Result (TN HSE +2) for 2026 on May 8, 2026, likely between 9 AM and 11 AM. The public examinations were conducted from March 3 to March 26, 2026, with practicals earlier in February.

Official Websites:
  • tnresults.nic.in
  • dge.tn.gov.in
Students will need their registration number and date of birth to view their results.
6. West Bengal HS Results 2026 - Upcoming
The West Bengal Council of Higher Secondary Education (WBCHSE) will announce the WB Uchcha Madhyamik Class 12 result 2026 on May 14, 2026. The results will be declared at 10:30 AM via a press conference, with the result link activated from 11:00 AM. The exams were held from February 12 to 27, 2026.

Official Websites:
  • wbchse.wb.gov.in
  • wbresults.nic.in
  • result.wb.gov.in
Students can check their results using their roll number and date of birth.

What to Do After Your Results Are Out

✅ Download Provisional Marksheet: Immediately download and print your provisional marksheet for temporary use.

✅ Verify Details: Carefully check all details on your marksheet, including your name, roll number, subject-wise marks, and grades.

✅ Apply for Re-evaluation/Scrutiny: If you're dissatisfied with your marks, most boards offer options for mark verification (re-totalling) or re-evaluation of answer sheets. Apply within the stipulated timeframe.

✅ Prepare for Compartment Exams: If you haven't cleared one or more subjects, compartment/supplementary exams provide another opportunity. Dates for these are usually announced shortly after the main results.

✅ Plan for Higher Education: Based on your results, start researching colleges and universities and prepare for admission processes, entrance exams, or counselling sessions.

✅ Collect Original Documents: Original marksheets and passing certificates are typically distributed by schools a few weeks after the online declaration.

We wish all students the very best for their 12th board results 2026 and their future endeavors. Stay positive, stay informed, and remember that your results are just one step in your long and successful journey!
