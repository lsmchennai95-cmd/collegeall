AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026 Declared: All You Need to Know!

The wait is finally over for thousands of students across Andhra Pradesh! The Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP), has officially announced the AP Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examination (IPASE) Results 2026 for both 1st and 2nd-year students. The results were declared on June 18, 2026, around 12:00 PM to 12:30 PM, bringing much-anticipated news for those who appeared to clear backlogs or improve their scores.

This declaration is a crucial step for many students, paving the way for their higher education pursuits or career plans. Whether you appeared for the supplementary exams to pass a failed subject or to enhance your overall marks in the betterment scheme, you can now access your detailed scorecards online and through other convenient methods.

Key Highlights of AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026

✅ Results Declared: June 18, 2026, at approximately 12:00 PM - 12:30 PM.

✅ Examinations Conducted: Theory exams from May 21 to June 4, 2026, and practical exams from June 7 to June 11, 2026.

✅ Purpose: For students who failed in regular intermediate exams or wished to improve their marks (betterment).

✅ Passing Criteria: A minimum of 35% in each subject and 35% aggregate marks.

✅ Accessibility: Results are available on official websites, WhatsApp, and DigiLocker.

How to Check Your AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026 Online

Checking your results is a straightforward process. Follow these detailed steps to download your provisional marks memo:

1. Visit Official Websites
Navigate to any of the official BIEAP results portals:
  • resultsbie.ap.gov.in
  • bie.ap.gov.in
  • bieap.apcfss.in
You might also find links on educational news websites like Sakshi Education or Manabadi.
2. Locate the Results Link
On the homepage, look for the link titled "AP Inter 1st Year Advanced Supplementary Result 2026" or "AP Inter 2nd Year Advanced Supplementary Result 2026" or "IPASE Results 2026." Click on the relevant link for your year.
3. Enter Your Hall Ticket Number
A new login window will appear. Carefully enter your Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examination (IPASE) hall ticket number in the designated field. Some portals might also require your date of birth.
4. Submit and View Result
After entering the required details, click on the "Submit" or "Get Result" button. Your AP Inter Supplementary Result 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print
Review all the details on your provisional marks memo, including subject-wise marks, total marks, and pass/fail status. It's highly recommended to download the PDF and take a printout for future reference, as this provisional memo is essential for college admissions and further academic processes.

Alternative Ways to Check Results

BIEAP has also provided additional convenient options for students to check their results:

Via WhatsApp Service
Students can receive their results directly on WhatsApp.
  1. Send a "Hi" message to the Mana Mithra WhatsApp number: 9552300009.
  2. Follow the prompts and select the "Intermediate Results" option.
  3. Enter your hall ticket number and other required credentials to receive your digital marks memo.
Through DigiLocker
Candidates can also access their marks memos via the DigiLocker platform, which provides a secure way to store and access official documents.

Important Note: The marks memo available online is provisional. Original certificates will be distributed by your respective junior colleges at a later date. Keep your provisional marks memo safe for admission procedures.

What's Next After Checking Your Results?

Once you have checked your AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026, your next steps will depend on your outcome:

For Passed Candidates
Congratulations! You can now proceed with your plans for higher education, college admissions, or vocational courses. Ensure you have a printed copy of your marks memo for all application processes.
For Candidates Who Failed Again
If you unfortunately did not clear the supplementary examination, you will need to reappear for the board exams in 2027. Students who fail the supplementary exams cannot directly apply for the next year's supply exam. It is advisable to consult with your college authorities or academic counselors for guidance on your next steps.
Discrepancies in Marksheet
If you find any discrepancies in your marksheet (e.g., incorrect personal details, marks mismatch), immediately contact the Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh, for assistance and corrections.

The release of the AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026 marks a significant moment for many students. We wish all candidates the very best for their future endeavors!
