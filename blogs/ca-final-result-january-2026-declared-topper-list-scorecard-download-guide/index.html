CA Final Result January 2026: Topper List & Scorecard Download Released by ICAI!

The much-anticipated results for the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) CA Final Examination held in January 2026 have been officially declared on March 1, 2026. This announcement brings both relief and excitement for thousands of aspirants who appeared for one of the country's most challenging professional examinations. Candidates can now access their scorecards, view the coveted topper list, and get a detailed understanding of their performance.

"The CA Final January 2026 results are a pivotal moment for aspiring Chartered Accountants, marking the culmination of years of hard work and dedication. Congratulations to all who have successfully cleared this rigorous examination!"

How to Check Your CA Final Result January 2026

Accessing your CA Final January 2026 result is a straightforward process. The ICAI has made the results available online on its official examination portals.

1. Visit Official ICAI Websites
Navigate to any of the official ICAI result portals: icai.nic.in, caresults.icai.org, or icaiexam.icai.org.
2. Click on the Result Link
On the homepage, locate and click on the link that reads 'CA Final Result – January 2026' or similar.
3. Enter Login Credentials
You will be prompted to enter your Roll Number along with your Registration Number or PIN.
4. Complete CAPTCHA and Submit
Enter the CAPTCHA code displayed on the screen and click on the 'Submit' button.
5. View and Download Scorecard
Your CA Final January 2026 scorecard will be displayed on your screen. You can download and print it for future reference.

Download Your CA Final Scorecard 2026

The CA Final scorecard is an essential document that details your subject-wise marks, total marks, and qualification status. It's crucial to download and keep a copy of this for your records.

✅ Your scorecard will include your name, roll number, registration number, subject-wise marks, and total marks.

✅ It will also clearly state your qualification status (pass/fail) for each group and overall.

✅ In case you registered your email ID with ICAI before the result declaration, you would have also received your scorecard in your inbox.

CA Final January 2026 Topper List and Merit List

Along with the individual results, ICAI has also released the CA Final January 2026 Topper List and Merit List. This list showcases the top 50 candidates who have achieved All India Ranks.

Accessing the Topper List
The merit list is available on the official ICAI website, accessible using your registration or roll number. Rank certificates are awarded to candidates up to the 50th position, provided they cleared all exams in one attempt with at least 55% overall.

Important Details on Your Scorecard and Passing Criteria

The ICAI CA Final scorecard is a detailed document. To qualify for the CA Final exams, candidates must secure a minimum of 40% marks in each paper and an overall aggregate of 50% in each group.

✅ Candidate Name, Roll Number, and Registration Number.

✅ Subject-wise marks and total marks obtained.

✅ Group-wise and overall pass/fail status.

✅ Rank, if applicable (for top 50 rank holders).

CA Final Marks Verification Process 2026

If you have any doubts about your marks, the ICAI provides a facility for marks verification.

1. Access ICAI E-services
Log on to the official ICAI e-services website.
2. Login to Your Account
Enter your user ID, password, and the CAPTCHA code to log in.
3. Choose Verification Option
Select the specific CA paper(s) for which you wish to request marks verification.
4. Upload Handwritten Request
You will need to upload a scanned copy of a handwritten application requesting the verification.
5. Pay the Fee and Submit
Pay the requisite application fee online and then submit your form.
