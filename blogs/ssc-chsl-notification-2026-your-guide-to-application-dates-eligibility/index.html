SSC CHSL Notification 2026: Your Gateway to Central Government Jobs!

Are you a 12th-pass aspirant dreaming of a prestigious career in Central Government departments? The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL) Examination 2026 is your golden opportunity! The much-awaited notification for SSC CHSL 2026 is just around the corner, promising numerous vacancies for posts like Lower Division Clerk (LDC), Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Data Entry Operator (DEO), Postal Assistant, and Sorting Assistant. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from important dates and eligibility to the exam pattern and syllabus.

SSC CHSL 2026: Key Dates at a Glance

The Staff Selection Commission is gearing up to release the official notification, and candidates should mark their calendars for these crucial timelines:

Event Expected Date
SSC CHSL 2026 Notification Release May 2026
Online Application Start Date May 2026
Online Application Last Date June 2026 (Approximately)
Tier-I Exam Dates (CBT) July - September 2026
Tier-II Exam Dates (CBT + Skill Test) To be announced after Tier-I results

While the notification was initially anticipated by April 30, 2026, a slight administrative delay means aspirants can now expect the official release in May 2026. Keep an eye on the official SSC website (ssc.gov.in) for the confirmed dates!

Understanding SSC CHSL Eligibility Criteria 2026

Before you dive into the application process, it's crucial to ensure you meet the prescribed eligibility requirements. These include nationality, age limit, and educational qualifications.

✅ Nationality: Candidates must be an Indian Citizen, a subject of Nepal or Bhutan, or a Tibetan refugee/Person of Indian Origin (POI) migrated with the intention of permanent settlement in India.

✅ Educational Qualification: A 12th standard pass or its equivalent from a recognized Board or University is generally required. For Data Entry Operator (DEO) in the Office of the Comptroller and Auditor General of India (C&AG), a 12th pass in Science stream with Mathematics as a subject is mandatory. Candidates appearing for their 12th exams can also apply, provided they possess the essential qualification by the cut-off date specified in the official notification.

Age Limit for SSC CHSL 2026

As of the cut-off date mentioned in the notification, candidates must be between 18 and 27 years of age. However, the SSC provides age relaxations for various reserved categories as per government norms:

  • SC/ST: 5 years
  • OBC: 3 years
  • PwD (Unreserved): 10 years
  • Ex-Servicemen: As per government rules
  • Other categories: Refer to the official notification for specific relaxations.

Expected Vacancies & Posts

The SSC CHSL exam is a highly anticipated recruitment drive, and for the 2026 cycle, aspirants can expect over 3000 vacancies. These vacancies are for crucial roles within various Central Government departments and ministries, including:

✅ Lower Division Clerk (LDC)

✅ Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA)

✅ Data Entry Operator (DEO)

✅ Postal Assistant (PA)

✅ Sorting Assistant (SA)

The SSC CHSL Selection Process 2026

The selection process for SSC CHSL 2026 is a two-tiered Computer Based Examination (CBE), followed by a Skill Test/Typing Test for qualified candidates.

1. Tier-I: Computer Based Examination (CBE)

This is the first stage and is qualifying in nature. It consists of objective type, multiple-choice questions.

Subject No. of Questions Max Marks Duration
General Intelligence 25 50 60 minutes (80 minutes for PwD candidates)
English Language (Basic Knowledge) 25 50
Quantitative Aptitude (Basic Arithmetical Skill) 25 50
General Awareness 25 50
Total 100 200

Negative Marking: 0.50 marks will be deducted for each incorrect answer.

2. Tier-II: Computer Based Examination (CBE) & Skill Test/Typing Test

Candidates who qualify Tier-I proceed to Tier-II, which is also a Computer Based Examination but with a more comprehensive syllabus, followed by a Skill Test/Typing Test. Tier-II includes various modules testing Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning & General Intelligence, English Language & Comprehension, General Awareness, and Computer Proficiency.

The Skill Test/Typing Test is mandatory for specific posts and aims to assess the practical skills required for the job.

SSC CHSL Syllabus 2026: What to Study

A well-structured preparation strategy begins with a thorough understanding of the syllabus. Here’s a brief overview of what each tier covers:

Tier-I Syllabus Highlights
  • General Intelligence: Semantic Analogy, Symbolic operations, Trends, Figural Analogy, Space Orientation, Venn Diagrams, Series, Coding-Decoding, Problem Solving, Emotional Intelligence, Word Building.
  • English Language: Spot the Error, Fill in the Blanks, Synonyms/Antonyms, Spellings, Idioms & Phrases, One-word Substitution, Sentence Improvement, Active/Passive Voice, Direct/Indirect Narration, Cloze Passage, Comprehension Passage.
  • Quantitative Aptitude: Number Systems, Fundamental Arithmetical Operations (Percentages, Ratio & Proportion, Averages, Interest, Profit & Loss), Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Data Interpretation.
  • General Awareness: History, Culture, Geography, Economic Scene, General Policy & Scientific Research, Current Affairs.
Tier-II Syllabus Highlights

Tier-II delves deeper into the subjects, incorporating advanced topics and a computer proficiency module. Key areas include Mathematical Abilities, Reasoning and General Intelligence, English Language and Comprehension, General Awareness, and Computer Proficiency.

  • Mathematical Abilities: Number Systems, Algebra, Geometry, Mensuration, Trigonometry, Statistics & Probability.
  • Reasoning and General Intelligence: Advanced logical and analytical reasoning.
  • English Language and Comprehension: Enhanced focus on vocabulary, grammar, sentence structure, and comprehension.
  • General Awareness: Broader coverage of national and international events, scientific developments.
  • Computer Proficiency: MS Word, MS Excel, MS PowerPoint, basics of computer hardware, software, networking, and cyber security.

How to Apply for SSC CHSL 2026

The application process will be entirely online on the official SSC website, ssc.gov.in.

Application Process Steps
  1. One-Time Registration (OTR): If you haven't already, register on the SSC portal with your basic details, mobile number, and email ID.
  2. Fill the Online Application Form: Log in using your registration details and fill in your personal and educational information.
  3. Upload Documents: Upload scanned copies of your recent photograph and signature as per the specified dimensions and file size (e.g., JPG 10-20 KB for photograph and signature).
  4. Choose Exam Centers: Select your preferred examination centers.
  5. Pay Application Fee: The application fee is ₹100 for General/OBC male candidates. Women candidates and candidates belonging to SC/ST, Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), and Ex-servicemen are exempted from fee payment.
  6. Review and Submit: Carefully review all entered details before final submission.

The SSC CHSL 2026 notification is an eagerly awaited event for countless aspirants. Start your preparation early, stay updated with the official announcements, and give your best shot to secure a coveted position in the Central Government.
