PSEB 8th Class Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Class 9!

The much-anticipated Punjab School Education Board (PSEB) 8th Class Result for the academic session 2025-26 has officially been declared on April 10, 2026, bringing excitement and relief to lakhs of students across the state. Students can now access their provisional mark sheets online and celebrate their academic achievements.

This year, the PSEB 8th class examinations were conducted from February 17 to February 27, 2026, with practical exams held earlier from February 2 to February 12, 2026. The board has reported impressive statistics, reflecting the hard work of both students and educators.

Result Highlights and Key Statistics

The Punjab School Education Board proudly announced an overall pass percentage of 96.51% for the 8th class examinations in 2026. This figure showcases a consistent dedication to academic excellence within the state.

✅ Overall Pass Percentage: 96.51%

✅ Total Students Appeared: 2,62,966

✅ Total Students Passed: 2,53,789

In a remarkable achievement, girls once again outshone boys, securing a higher pass percentage.

Gender-wise Performance

Category Pass Percentage
Girls 97.74%
Boys 95.42%

District-wise, Pathankot emerged as the top-performing district with an outstanding pass percentage of 98.90%. Amritsar and Gurdaspur also showed excellent results, closely following the top spot.

Meet the Brightest Minds: PSEB Class 8 Toppers 2026

The 2026 examinations saw some truly exceptional performances. Tamanna, a student from Saraswati Senior Secondary School in Jaito, Faridkot, achieved a perfect score, securing the first position with 600 out of 600 marks (100%). Following closely were Japneet Kaur from Government Middle School, Arkwas (Sangrur), and Jashandeep Kaur from Sant Mohan Das Memorial Senior Secondary School, Kot Sukhia (Faridkot), who jointly secured the second and third positions with 598 marks each (99.67%).

How to Check Your PSEB 8th Class Result 2026

Accessing your result is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to view your provisional mark sheet:

1. Visit the Official Website
Go to the official website of the Punjab School Education Board: pseb.ac.in.
2. Navigate to the Results Section
On the homepage, look for and click on the "Results" link or a specific link titled "Middle System (Class 8th) Examination Result 2026."
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be prompted to enter your registered roll number or your name in the designated fields. Some platforms might also require a captcha code.
4. View and Download Your Result
Click on the "Submit" or "Find Results" button. Your result will be displayed on the screen. Download the provisional mark sheet and take a printout for your records.

You can also check your results via DigiLocker.

Understanding Your Marksheet and Grading System

Your online provisional mark sheet will contain crucial information about your performance. It will include your name, roll number, school name, subject-wise marks, total marks, and your overall qualifying status (Pass/Fail). Remember, this online result is provisional. Original mark sheets and certificates will be distributed by your respective schools at a later date.

PSEB 8th Class Grading System 2026

The PSEB employs a detailed grading system based on marks obtained:

Marks Range Grade
>90 to ≤100 A+
>80 to ≤90 A
>70 to ≤80 B+
>60 to ≤70 B
>50 to ≤60 C+
>40 to ≤50 C
≤40 D

To successfully clear the examination, students must secure a minimum of 33% marks in each subject.

What's Next? Supplementary Exams and Re-evaluation

For students who, unfortunately, could not clear one or two subjects, the Punjab Board provides a second opportunity through supplementary examinations. These exams are crucial for saving an academic year.

✅ Supplementary Exam Schedule: Expected to be held in May-June 2026.

✅ Supplementary Result Date: Anticipated in August 2026.

✅ Re-evaluation/Rechecking: Students dissatisfied with their marks can apply for re-evaluation through their schools, along with the prescribed fee.

Detailed schedules and application procedures for supplementary exams and re-evaluation will be communicated by the board through official channels and schools.

Congratulations to All!

The declaration of the PSEB 8th Class Result 2026 marks a significant milestone for all students. Congratulations to everyone who successfully cleared the examinations! For those who need another chance, remember that supplementary exams are a valuable opportunity to achieve your academic goals. We wish all students the very best for their future endeavors as they transition into Class 9 and continue their educational journey.
