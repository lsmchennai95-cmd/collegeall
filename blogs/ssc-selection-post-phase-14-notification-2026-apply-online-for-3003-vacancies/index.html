SSC Selection Post Phase 14 Notification 2026: Your Gateway to Central Government Jobs!

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially unveiled the much-awaited Selection Post Phase 14 Notification for 2026, opening doors to a massive recruitment drive across various Central Government departments. This is a golden opportunity for candidates with 10th, 12th, and Graduate qualifications to secure a prestigious position within the government sector. With 3003 vacancies announced, aspirants are encouraged to prepare thoroughly and apply before the deadline.

The online application window for SSC Selection Post Phase 14 Recruitment 2026 commenced on April 13, 2026, and will close on May 4, 2026, at 11:00 PM. Make sure to complete your application well in advance to avoid any last-minute technical glitches!

SSC Selection Post Phase 14: Key Highlights

✅ Notification Released: April 13, 2026

✅ Total Vacancies: 3003 posts

✅ Application Mode: Online via ssc.gov.in

✅ Eligibility: 10th Pass, 12th Pass, and Graduates

✅ Exam Date (Tentative): June 2026

✅ Selection Process: Computer-Based Examination (CBE), Skill Test (where applicable), and Document Verification

Important Dates to Remember

Staying informed about crucial dates is vital for a smooth application process. Here’s a quick overview:

Event Date
SSC Selection Post Phase 14 Notification Release April 13, 2026
Online Application Start Date April 13, 2026
Last Date to Apply Online May 4, 2026 (11:00 PM)
Last Date for Online Fee Payment May 5, 2026 (11:00 PM)
Application Form Correction Window May 11 - May 13, 2026 (11:00 PM)
Tentative Date for Computer-Based Examination (CBE) June 2026

Eligibility Criteria for Phase 14

Before applying, candidates must ensure they meet the SSC Selection Post Phase 14 eligibility requirements:

1. Nationality
Applicants must be a Citizen of India, or a subject of Nepal/Bhutan. Tibetan refugees who came to India before January 1, 1962, for permanent settlement, and persons of Indian origin who migrated from specific countries (Pakistan, Myanmar, Sri Lanka, East African countries like Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania, Zambia, Malawi, etc.) with the intention of permanently settling in India, are also eligible.
2. Educational Qualification
The required educational qualification varies depending on the specific post. Positions are available for Matriculation (10th Pass), Higher Secondary (12th Pass), and Graduate-level candidates. It is crucial that the qualification is from a recognized board or university and must be completed on or before May 4, 2026. Some posts may also require specific typing skills, diplomas, or technical qualifications.
3. Age Limit
The age limit varies significantly from post to post, generally ranging from 18-25, 18-27, 18-30, and for some specific posts, up to 42 years. The age will be calculated as per the date mentioned in the official notification, and the date of birth on the Matriculation certificate will be considered final. Age relaxation is applicable for reserved categories as per government rules.

How to Apply for SSC Selection Post Phase 14

The application process is entirely online. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. One-Time Registration (OTR)
If you haven't already, complete your One-Time Registration on the new SSC website (www.ssc.gov.in). OTR created on the old site (ssc.nic.in) is not valid. Once generated, your new OTR remains valid for all future SSC exams.
2. Log In and Apply
After successful OTR, log in using your credentials. Navigate to the "Apply" section and select "Phase-XIV/2026/Selection Posts".
3. Fill the Application Form
Carefully fill out the application form with accurate personal, educational, and contact details. You must submit separate applications for each category of post you wish to apply for.
4. Upload Documents
Upload a live photograph and your scanned signature in the prescribed format. Ensure the photograph is clear, without specs or a cap. No other documents are required to be uploaded at the time of online application.
5. Choose Exam Centres
Select up to three preferred examination centers.
6. Pay Application Fee
The application fee is ₹100/- for General/OBC candidates. Women candidates, and those belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), and Ex-Servicemen (ESM) are exempted from fee payment. The fee can be paid online via BHIM UPI, Net Banking, or Credit/Debit Cards. The last date for online fee payment is May 5, 2026.
7. Review and Submit
Review all entered details carefully before final submission. Save a printout of the submitted form for future reference. An application correction window will be available from May 11 to May 13, 2026, for any necessary edits.

Changes in Exam Pattern: Sectional Timing Introduced

Candidates should note an important change in the Computer-Based Examination (CBE) pattern for Phase 14. SSC has introduced a new sectional timer. As per the official notice, candidates will now be allocated 15 minutes for each section of the paper (General Intelligence, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, and English Language). Candidates eligible for a scribe will get 20 minutes per section. The syllabus, however, remains unchanged.

The minimum qualifying marks for the Computer Based Test are 30% for unreserved categories, 25% for OBC and EWS, and 20% for other categories.

Prepare for Success!

The SSC Selection Post Phase 14 Exam offers diverse opportunities for government job aspirants. With 3003 vacancies across various educational levels, meticulous preparation is key. Download the official notification PDF from the SSC website (ssc.gov.in) for detailed post-wise vacancies, syllabus, and exam patterns. Utilize previous year's papers and mock tests to enhance your preparation. Don't miss this chance to embark on a rewarding career in central government services!
