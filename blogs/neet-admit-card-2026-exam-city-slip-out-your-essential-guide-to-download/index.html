NEET Admit Card 2026 & Exam City Slip OUT – Your Essential Guide to Download

The National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (NEET UG) 2026 is just around the corner, scheduled to take place on May 3, 2026. As lakhs of medical aspirants prepare for this crucial examination, the National Testing Agency (NTA) has made significant announcements regarding the release of the NEET UG 2026 Exam City Intimation Slip and the eagerly awaited Admit Card. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about downloading these essential documents and preparing for exam day.

NEET UG 2026 Exam City Intimation Slip: What You Need to Know

Good news for all NEET UG 2026 candidates! The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially released the advance exam city intimation slip on April 13, 2026. This slip is a crucial document, but it's important to understand its purpose: **it is NOT your admit card.** Instead, it serves as an early notification about the city where your examination centre will be located.

✅ Helps candidates plan travel and accommodation in advance.

✅ Informs about the assigned exam city, not the exact centre address.

✅ Reduces last-minute stress and logistical challenges.

The exam city intimation slip includes basic candidate information, the name of the examination, and most importantly, your assigned exam city.

How to Download Your NEET UG 2026 Exam City Intimation Slip

1. Visit the Official NTA NEET Website
Navigate to the official NEET (UG) website: neet.nta.nic.in.
2. Locate the City Intimation Link
On the homepage, look for and click on the "Advance Intimation of Examination City for NEET (UG) 2026" link or similar.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your Application Number, Date of Birth (or Password), and the Security Pin as displayed.
4. View and Download
Submit the details, and your NEET UG 2026 Exam City Intimation Slip will appear on your screen. Download the document and take a printout for your reference.

NEET Admit Card 2026: Your Official Gateway

While the city slip is out, the NEET UG 2026 Admit Card is the mandatory document you must carry to the examination centre. The NTA is expected to release the NEET Admit Card in the last week of April 2026, likely around 3-4 days before the exam, which means approximately by April 30, 2026.

The admit card will contain crucial information including your name, roll number, photograph, signature, exact exam centre address, reporting time, and important exam day guidelines. Without a valid admit card and a valid photo ID, you will not be permitted to enter the examination hall.

Steps to Download Your NEET UG 2026 Admit Card

1. Access the Official NTA NEET Portal
Once released, visit the official website: neet.nta.nic.in.
2. Click on the Admit Card Link
Look for the "NEET UG 2026 Admit Card Download" link on the homepage and click it.
3. Enter Login Details
You will need your Application Number, Date of Birth, and the Security Pin to log in.
4. Verify and Print
Your admit card will be displayed. Carefully verify all the details. Download and take at least 3-4 printouts for future reference.

Crucial Instructions for NEET Admit Card 2026

✅ **Verify All Details:** Ensure your name, roll number, photograph, signature, exam centre, and other personal details are correct. Contact NTA immediately if you find any discrepancies.

✅ **Paste Photograph:** Paste a postcard-size photograph in the designated space on the admit card.

✅ **Parent's Signature:** Ensure your parent's signature is in the designated space on the admit card.

✅ **Carry Valid ID:** Along with your admit card, you must carry a valid photo identification proof (e.g., Aadhaar card, Passport, Driving License, PAN Card, Voter ID).

✅ **Read Guidelines:** Carefully read all instructions and guidelines mentioned on the admit card regarding reporting time, prohibited items, and exam day decorum.

Facing Issues? Contact NTA Helpline

If you encounter any difficulties in downloading your exam city intimation slip or admit card, or if you find any discrepancies in your details, do not panic. The NTA has provided dedicated helpline numbers and an email address for assistance.

📞 **Helpline Numbers:** 011-40759000 / 011-69227700

📧 **Email:** neetug2026@nta.ac.in

It is strongly recommended that candidates and their guardians regularly check the official NTA website (neet.nta.nic.in) for the latest updates and notifications regarding NEET UG 2026.
