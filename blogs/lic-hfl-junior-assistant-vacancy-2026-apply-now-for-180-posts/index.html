LIC HFL Junior Assistant Vacancy 2026: Your Gateway to a Promising Career!

Exciting news for job aspirants! LIC Housing Finance Ltd (LIC HFL) has officially released its notification for the recruitment of Junior Assistants in 2026. This is a fantastic opportunity for graduates looking to build a stable and rewarding career in the housing finance sector. With 180 vacancies announced across various cities and states, now is the time to prepare your application and seize this chance!

The online application process commenced on April 16, 2026, and the deadline for submissions is April 30, 2026. Mark your calendars, as the online examination is tentatively scheduled for the first week of June 2026!

Key Highlights of LIC HFL Junior Assistant Recruitment 2026

✅ Total Vacancies: 180 Junior Assistant posts available across India.

✅ Application Mode: Exclusively online through the official LIC HFL website.

✅ Selection Process: Comprises an Online Written Examination followed by an Interview.

✅ Attractive Salary: Starting basic pay of ₹20,000/- per month, with total emoluments ranging from ₹32,000 to ₹35,200/- per month, along with other benefits.

Important Dates to Remember

Staying updated with the crucial dates is vital for a smooth application process. Here's a quick overview:

Event Date
Online Application Start Date April 16, 2026
Online Application End Date April 30, 2026
Online Exam (Tentative) 1st Week of June 2026

Are You Eligible? Check the Criteria!

Before applying, ensure you meet the essential eligibility requirements set by LIC HFL:

1. Nationality
Applicants must be Indian Citizens.
2. Age Limit (as on April 1, 2026)
Candidates must be between 21 and 30 years of age. This means candidates should have been born not earlier than April 2, 1996, and not later than April 1, 2005 (both dates inclusive).
3. Educational Qualification (as on April 1, 2026)

You must hold a Graduate degree (minimum aggregate 55% marks) in any discipline from a university recognized by the Government of India. It's crucial to note that courses completed through correspondence, distance learning, or part-time modes are not eligible.

Additionally, candidates must possess operating and working knowledge of computer systems.

Application Process: Step-by-Step Guide

The application process is entirely online. Follow these steps to submit your application successfully:

1. Visit the Official Website
Go to the official website of LIC HFL: www.lichousing.com.
2. Navigate to Careers
On the homepage, click on the "Careers" section.
3. Find the Recruitment Link
Locate and click on the "Recruitment of Junior Assistants" link or the external link to the 2026 exam process.
4. Register and Fill the Form
Access the Online Application Form tab. Complete the registration, fill in all your personal and educational details accurately.
5. Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of your photograph (passport size, 4.5 cm × 3.5 cm), signature (in black ink, not in CAPITAL LETTERS), left thumb impression (on white paper with black/blue ink), and a handwritten declaration (on white paper with black ink). Ensure documents are as per specified format and size.
6. Pay the Application Fee
The application fee is ₹800/-, plus 18% GST, which is non-refundable. Pay this online using available payment methods.
7. Submit and Print
Review your application thoroughly before final submission. After submission, take a printout of the confirmation page for your records.

LIC HFL Junior Assistant Exam Pattern 2026

The online examination is a critical stage. Familiarize yourself with the exam pattern to prepare effectively:

Section No. of Questions Max Marks Duration
English Language 40 40 120 Minutes (2 Hours)
Logical Reasoning 40 40
General Awareness (with emphasis on Housing Finance) 40 40
Numerical Ability 40 40
Computer Skills 40 40
Total 200 200

Key points regarding the exam:

✅ The exam is objective-type, multiple-choice questions.

✅ It will be conducted in English only.

✅ There is a negative marking of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer.

✅ No penalty for unattempted questions.

✅ Candidates must qualify in each stage (online exam and interview) to be considered for final selection.

Salary and Benefits

Selected Junior Assistants will enjoy an attractive remuneration package. The starting basic pay is ₹20,000/- per month. Including various admissible allowances, the total monthly emoluments will be approximately in the range of ₹32,000 to ₹35,200/-, depending on the place of posting.

Additionally, employees receive other benefits such as Provident Fund, Mediclaim, Gratuity, LTC, Group Insurance Scheme, Housing Loan, Performance Linked Incentive, and other benefits as per rules. The annual CTC is expected to be between ₹4.64 lakh and ₹4.98 lakh.

Conclusion

The LIC HFL Junior Assistant recruitment 2026 presents a golden opportunity for eligible candidates to embark on a promising career with a reputable housing finance company. With 180 vacancies, a clear selection process, and attractive benefits, this is a chance not to be missed. Ensure you meet the eligibility criteria, complete your application before the deadline, and begin your preparation for the online examination. Good luck!
