NEET UG 2026: Navigating Re-Exam, Results, Cut-Offs, and Counselling

A comprehensive guide for aspiring medical students on the latest developments regarding NEET UG 2026.

The journey for aspiring medical students appearing for NEET UG 2026 has seen significant developments. The initial examination, held on May 3, 2026, was unfortunately cancelled due to allegations of a paper leak. In response, the National Testing Agency (NTA) swiftly announced a re-examination, ensuring fairness and transparency for over 22 lakh candidates. This blog post aims to provide a clear overview of the updated schedule, how to access results, understand cut-off scores, and navigate the crucial counselling process.

NEET UG 2026 Re-Examination: Key Details

The NTA has confirmed that the re-examination for NEET UG 2026 (Re-NEET UG 2026) will be conducted on June 21, 2026 (Sunday). Candidates who previously registered for the May 3rd exam do not need to re-register and are automatically eligible to appear.

✅ Re-Exam Date: June 21, 2026 (Sunday)

✅ Exam Timing: 2:00 PM to 5:15 PM (3 hours and 15 minutes, including 15 mins for OMR/attendance formalities)

✅ Eligibility: All previously registered candidates are automatically eligible.

✅ Fees: Application fees paid for the cancelled exam will be refunded.

✅ No Postponement: The Supreme Court has declined pleas for a CBT mode or postponement, indicating the June 21st exam is on track.

NEET UG Result 2026: When and How to Check

Following the re-examination, the NTA is expected to announce the Re-NEET 2026 results by July 15, 2026. The results will be made available online on the official NTA NEET website.

1. Official Website for Results
Visit the official NTA NEET website: neet.nta.nic.in.
2. How to Access Your Scorecard
On the homepage, look for the "View NEET-UG 2026 Result" link. You will need to enter your NEET UG 2026 application number, date of birth, and the security PIN displayed on the page.
3. What Your Scorecard Contains
Your NEET UG 2026 scorecard will be a crucial document containing essential details such as your personal information, qualifying marks, total marks obtained, subject-wise scores, All India Rank (AIR), category rank, and 15% All India Quota (AIQ) rank. It will also specify the minimum qualifying scores for different categories. Remember to download and print multiple copies for future reference and the admission process.

NEET UG Cut Off 2026: Understanding the Thresholds

The NEET UG cut-off marks are a critical aspect of the admission process. These are determined by several factors, including the difficulty level of the exam, the total number of test-takers, the total number of seats available, and the candidate's category.

It's important to distinguish between the 'qualifying cut-off' and the 'admission cut-off'. The qualifying cut-off is the minimum score required to be eligible for counselling, while the admission cut-off is the actual score needed to secure a seat in a specific medical college.

Qualifying Cut-Off Percentiles

The minimum percentile required to qualify for NEET UG 2026 remains consistent with previous years:

✅ General Category: 50th percentile

✅ SC/ST/OBC Categories: 40th percentile

✅ PwD Candidates: 45th percentile

Expected NEET UG 2026 Cut-Off Marks (Qualifying)

While the official cut-off marks will be released with the results, here are the expected qualifying ranges based on expert analysis and previous trends:

Category Expected Qualifying Marks (Range)
General (UR) 135 - 164 marks
SC/ST/OBC 107 - 143 marks
General-PH (UR-PwD) 120 - 158 marks
OBC-PH/SC-PH/ST-PH 107 - 136 marks

Expected Safe Scores for MBBS Government Colleges

Securing a seat in a government medical college requires a significantly higher score than the qualifying cut-off. These are highly competitive seats, and aspirants should aim for the following ranges for a good chance:

Category Expected Safe Score (MBBS Government Colleges)
General (UR) 610 - 650+ marks (650+ for top AIQ/AIIMS)
OBC 590 - 630+ marks
SC 500 - 550+ marks
ST 480 - 530+ marks

NEET UG Counselling 2026: Your Path to Admission

Qualifying NEET UG is just the first step. The NEET Counselling Process 2026 is where your All India Rank (AIR) translates into an actual medical college admission.

Types of Counselling

1. All India Quota (AIQ) - 15% Seats
Conducted by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC), this covers 15% of government college seats, 100% of deemed universities, and central institutes like AIIMS and JIPMER. It is open to students from all states.
2. State Quota - 85% Seats
Administered by respective state counselling authorities, these seats are for candidates meeting state domicile requirements in government and private colleges within that state.

Step-by-Step Counselling Journey

1. Online Registration and Fee Payment
Register on the MCC portal (for AIQ) or your state's portal using your NEET roll number and application number. Pay the required counselling registration fee and security deposit.
2. Choice Filling and Locking
This is a critical stage where you select and prioritize your preferred colleges and courses. Ensure to lock your choices before the deadline.
3. Seat Allotment Result
The counselling authority will release the seat allotment result online based on your NEET rank, category, choices, and seat availability.
4. Reporting to the Allotted College
If you are allotted a seat, download the allotment letter and report to the assigned college within the stipulated deadline for document verification and fee payment.

Mandatory Documents for Counselling

Keep the following documents ready in original and with photocopies:

✅ NEET UG 2026 Admit Card & Scorecard

✅ Class 10 Certificate (for Date of Birth proof)

✅ Class 12 Marksheet

✅ Photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, PAN Card)

✅ Category Certificate (if applicable)

✅ Domicile Certificate (for state quota seats)

✅ Passport-size Photographs

✅ Seat Allotment Letter

Important Dates at a Glance

Event Expected Date / Timeline
NEET UG 2026 Re-Exam Date June 21, 2026
NEET UG 2026 Result Release By July 15, 2026
NEET UG Counselling 2026 Registration Begins July – August 2026 (2-3 weeks after results)

The NEET UG 2026 examination has presented unique challenges, but with the re-examination date set and a clear roadmap for results and counselling, aspirants can focus on their preparation and subsequent steps. Staying informed through official NTA and MCC websites is paramount. Good luck to all the medical aspirants!
