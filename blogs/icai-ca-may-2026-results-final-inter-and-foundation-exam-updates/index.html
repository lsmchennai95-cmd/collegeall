ICAI CA May 2026 Results: Your Comprehensive Guide to Scores, Dates, and More

The wait is finally over for thousands of aspiring Chartered Accountants! The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) has begun declaring the results for the highly anticipated CA Final and Intermediate examinations held in May 2026, with Foundation results to follow soon. This guide provides you with all the essential information regarding the ICAI CA May 2026 results, including official release dates, how to check your scores, and what to expect on your scorecard.

CA Final May 2026 Results - Out Now!

The moment many have been eagerly awaiting has arrived! The ICAI CA Final May 2026 examination results were declared by the evening of June 18, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the exams, conducted from May 2 to May 12, 2026, can now access their scorecards online.

✅ Result Declared: June 18, 2026 (by evening)

✅ Exams Held: May 2 - May 12, 2026

✅ Access Via: Official ICAI result portals

CA Intermediate May 2026 Results - Mark Your Calendars!

For those who took the CA Intermediate exams, the results are just around the corner! The ICAI has announced that the CA Intermediate May 2026 results will be declared by the evening of June 24, 2026. The examinations for the Intermediate level were held between May 5 and May 15, 2026.

✅ Result Declaration: June 24, 2026 (by evening)

✅ Exams Held: May 5 - May 15, 2026

✅ Official Access: caresults.icai.org

CA Foundation May 2026 Results - Anticipation Builds!

Students who appeared for the CA Foundation examinations in May 2026 can expect their results in the first week of July 2026. The Foundation exams were conducted on May 14, 16, 18, and 20, 2026, under the new ICAI scheme. To pass, candidates need a minimum of 40% in each paper and an aggregate of 50%.

✅ Expected Result Date: First week of July 2026

✅ Exams Held: May 14, 16, 18, 20, 2026

✅ Passing Criteria: 40% in each paper, 50% aggregate

How to Check Your ICAI CA May 2026 Results Online

Checking your ICAI CA May 2026 results is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to access your scorecard:

1. Visit the Official ICAI Result Portals
Navigate to any of the official ICAI result websites:
  • icai.nic.in
  • icaiexam.icai.org
  • icai.org
  • caresults.icai.org
2. Select Your Examination Level
On the homepage, click on the relevant link for your examination: "CA Final May 2026 Result," "CA Intermediate May 2026 Result," or "CA Foundation May 2026 Result."
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be redirected to a new login window. Enter your Roll Number and Registration Number as accurately as they appear on your admit card. For Foundation candidates, a PIN can also be used.
4. Complete the Captcha and Submit
Enter the displayed captcha code for verification and click on the "Submit" button.
5. View and Download Your Scorecard
Your ICAI CA May 2026 result scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Carefully check all the details. It is highly recommended to download and save the result PDF for future reference and to take a printout.

Pro Tip: Keep your registration number and roll number handy to avoid delays during result checking. You can also register your email ID on icaiexam.icai.org to receive results directly in your inbox.

Important Information on Your Scorecard

Your ICAI CA May 2026 scorecard is a crucial document. It typically includes the following details:

Candidate's Personal Information
Your name, roll number, date of birth, gender, and category.
Examination Details
Course/exam name, examination session/year, and course group name.
Marks Obtained
Subject-wise marks and total marks secured in the examination.
Result Status
Your qualifying status (pass/fail).

Beyond the Scorecard: Pass Percentage & Merit List

Along with the individual results, ICAI is also expected to release the pass percentage, merit list, and details of All India Rank (AIR) holders for each examination level. These statistics provide valuable insights into the overall performance of candidates and highlight top achievers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

1. Can I check my ICAI CA result without my roll number?
No, it is not possible to check your CA exam result without your roll number. If you've lost your admit card, you can typically download it again from the ICAI examination portal by logging in with your registration ID and password.
2. What if I am not satisfied with my results?
If you are not satisfied with your CA Final May 2026 Result, you can apply for verification of marks. ICAI provides a facility for candidates to request verification of their answer books.
3. Is there an option to receive results via SMS?
Yes, ICAI generally provides a facility to check results via SMS. You need to send a specific message format (e.g., CAFINAL XXXXXX for Final, CAINTER XXXXXX for Inter) to 57575.
4. What are the next steps after clearing the CA Foundation exam?
Candidates who pass the CA Foundation Examination will be eligible to register for CA Intermediate, which is the next level of the CA course.
