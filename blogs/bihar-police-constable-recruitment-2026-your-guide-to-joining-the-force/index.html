Bihar Police Constable Recruitment 2026: Your Path to Serve Bihar!

The year 2026 brings forth a multitude of exciting opportunities for aspiring candidates looking to join the esteemed Bihar Police Force. The Central Selection Board of Constables (CSBC) is actively conducting and planning several recruitment drives for various Constable positions. Whether you're aiming for a general duty role, a specialized operator position, or a coveted spot in the Special Branch or Prohibition unit, 2026 holds promising prospects. This comprehensive guide will walk you through the latest announcements, eligibility criteria, application process, and key dates to help you prepare for a rewarding career in the Bihar Police.

Stay Alert! The Bihar Police Constable recruitment process is dynamic, with multiple notifications released throughout the year. Keep a close eye on the official CSBC website for the most accurate and up-to-date information.

Key Highlights of Bihar Police Recruitment 2026

✅ Multiple recruitment drives are active/expected for different Constable posts in 2026.

✅ Educational qualification typically requires a 10+2 (Intermediate) pass from a recognized board.

✅ Age limits generally range from 18 to 25 years, with relaxations for reserved categories.

✅ Selection usually involves a Written Exam, Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and Document Verification.

✅ Specific drives like the Special Branch Constable may not include a PET.

Eligibility Criteria for Bihar Police Constable 2026

Meeting the eligibility criteria is the first crucial step towards a successful application. While specific requirements might vary slightly between different constable roles, the general criteria are as follows:

1. Nationality
Applicants must be Indian citizens.
2. Educational Qualification

Candidates must have passed Intermediate (10+2) or an equivalent examination from a recognized board.

For Bihar Police Constable Operator, candidates generally need to have passed 10+2 with Science, including Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics, securing at least 50% marks (45% for SC/ST candidates).

Equivalent qualifications such as a Maulvi certificate from the Bihar State Government's Madrasa Board or a Shastri/Acharya certificate from the Bihar State Sanskrit Board are also accepted for some posts.

3. Age Limit (as on a specified cut-off date, usually August 1st of the recruitment year)
  • Minimum Age: 18 years
  • Maximum Age (General Category): 25 years

Age relaxations are provided for reserved categories as per Bihar Government rules. For instance, BC/EBC males may get up to 27 years, females up to 28 years, and SC/ST candidates up to 30 years.

4. Physical Standards

Candidates must meet prescribed physical standards for height, chest (for males), and weight (for females). These are verified during the Physical Standards Test (PST).

Bihar Police Constable Selection Process 2026

The selection process for Bihar Police Constable posts typically involves multiple stages designed to assess a candidate's academic aptitude, physical fitness, and document authenticity:

1. Written Examination

This is usually the first stage, often comprising 100 objective-type questions for 100 marks, with a duration of 2 hours. The question level is generally of intermediate standard.

For the Special Branch Constable, the written examination consists of two papers, each 90 minutes long, carrying 100 questions and 100 marks. There's also a negative marking of 0.25 for each incorrect answer. Candidates scoring below 30% in the written exam will not qualify for the next stage.

2. Physical Standards Test (PST) & Physical Efficiency Test (PET)

Candidates who qualify the written exam proceed to these physical tests. PST involves checking physical measurements (height, chest, weight). PET assesses physical abilities through tasks like running, shot put, and high jump.

Note: The Bihar Police Special Branch Constable recruitment (Advt. No. 01/2026) specifically mentions that there will be no Physical Efficiency Test (PET), only a Physical Measurement Test (PMT) after the written exam.

3. Document Verification (DV)
Candidates who clear the PST/PET stages must present their original documents (educational certificates, age proof, category certificates, etc.) for verification.
4. Medical Examination
A medical examination ensures candidates are fit for duty.
5. Final Merit List
A final merit list is prepared based on the performance in the written exam and/or PET, depending on the specific recruitment, leading to final selection.

How to Apply for Bihar Police Constable Recruitment 2026

The application process for Bihar Police Constable posts is conducted online through the official website of the Central Selection Board of Constables (CSBC), csbc.bihar.gov.in.

Step-by-Step Application Guide

1. Visit the Official Website: Go to csbc.bihar.gov.in.

2. Locate Recruitment Link: Find the relevant application link for the Bihar Police Constable Recruitment 2026 (e.g., "Bihar Police application form 2026 link").

3. Registration: Register yourself by providing basic details.

4. Fill Application Form: Complete the online application form with all required personal, educational, and contact details.

5. Upload Documents: Upload scanned copies of necessary documents such as photographs, signatures, and educational certificates as per the specified format and size.

6. Pay Application Fee: Pay the application fee through the available online payment modes.

7. Review and Submit: Carefully review all the entered information before final submission.

8. Print Confirmation: Take a printout of the submitted application form for future reference.
