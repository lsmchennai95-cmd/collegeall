RRB Technician Grade 3 Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to a Railway Career!

The wait is finally over for thousands of aspiring technicians! The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has officially released the RRB Technician Grade 3 Result 2026 for the CEN 02/2025 recruitment cycle. This marks a significant milestone for candidates who appeared in the Computer-Based Test (CBT) held in March 2026.

The Railway Recruitment Board announced the highly anticipated RRB Technician Grade 3 Result 2026 on May 29, 2026. Candidates who participated in the CBT examination conducted on March 6, 9, and 10, 2026, can now access their zone-wise merit lists, individual scorecards, and category-wise cut-off marks. This result is a crucial step towards filling 6,055 Grade 3 Technician vacancies across various technical streams in Indian Railways.

How to Check Your RRB Technician Grade 3 Result 2026

Checking your result and scorecard is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to know your qualifying status:

1. Visit Your Regional RRB Website
Navigate to the official website of the Railway Recruitment Board zone from which you applied. All regional RRBs have activated the result links.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage or in the "Results" section, click on the link titled "CEN 02/2025 Technician Grade 3 Result 2026" or "List of Candidates Shortlisted for Document Verification & Medical Examination."
3. Download the Merit List PDF
The result will typically be available as a PDF file. Download it to your device.
4. Search for Your Roll Number
Open the downloaded PDF and use the "Ctrl + F" function to search for your roll number. If your roll number is in the list, you have been shortlisted for the next stage.
5. Access Your Scorecard
A separate link for the RRB Technician Grade 3 Scorecard 2026 has also been activated. You will need your Registration Number and Date of Birth/Password to log in and download your individual scorecard from rrb.digialm.com. This facility is generally available for a limited period, so download it promptly.
Understanding Your Scorecard and Cut-Off Marks

The scorecard provides a detailed breakdown of your performance, offering valuable insights into your exam attempt.

✅ Details on Scorecard: Your scorecard will display section-wise marks, raw marks, normalized marks, overall score, and your qualifying status.

✅ Merit List vs. Scorecard: The merit list PDF contains the roll numbers of only the shortlisted candidates, while the scorecard shows individual marks and performance details for all who appeared in the CBT.

Along with the results, the RRB has also released the zone-wise and category-wise cut-off marks. These marks represent the minimum score required to qualify for Document Verification. The cut-off scores vary based on factors such as the difficulty level of the exam, the total number of vacancies, the number of candidates who appeared, and category-wise reservation criteria.

Minimum Qualifying Marks: To even be considered for the merit list, candidates had to secure minimum qualifying marks in the CBT. Generally, these are 40% for UR/EWS, 30% for OBC/SC, and 25% for ST candidates.

While specific zone-wise cut-offs are available on respective RRB websites, here's an example of expected cut-off ranges based on previous trends for a general understanding:

Category Expected Cut-Off Marks (Range)
UR 65 - 80 Marks
OBC 60 - 75 Marks
EWS 60 - 72 Marks
SC 50 - 65 Marks
ST 45 - 60 Marks
Next Steps: Document Verification (DV) & Medical Examination

Candidates whose roll numbers appear in the merit list have successfully cleared the CBT stage and are now shortlisted for the subsequent phases of the selection process:

1. Document Verification (DV)
Shortlisted candidates will be called for Document Verification. This stage requires candidates to present all original certificates and documents for verification. It is crucial to have all your educational, caste, and other relevant documents ready and in order.
2. Medical Examination
Following successful document verification, candidates will undergo a comprehensive medical examination at designated Railway Hospitals. This is to ensure that candidates meet the prescribed medical fitness standards for the Technician Grade 3 posts as outlined in the official notification (CEN 02/2025).

Final selection and posting will be based on merit, medical fitness, post preference, and the availability of vacancies.

Important Dates at a Glance (CEN 02/2025)

✅ CBT Exam Dates: March 6, 9, and 10, 2026

✅ Result Declaration Date: May 29, 2026

✅ Scorecard & Cut-Off Release: May 29, 2026

✅ Last Date to Download Scorecard: June 16, 2026

Congratulations to all the candidates who have successfully cleared the RRB Technician Grade 3 CBT! Prepare diligently for the upcoming Document Verification and Medical Examination. For those who did not make it this time, remember that new opportunities are on the horizon. The Railway Recruitment Board has already announced a new recruitment drive (CEN 02/2026) for 6,565 Technician Grade 1 Signal and Grade 3 posts, with applications commencing on June 30, 2026. Keep an eye on official RRB websites for future notifications and never stop striving for your goals.
