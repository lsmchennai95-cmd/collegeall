HBSE 12th Result 2026 Declared: All You Need to Know!

The wait is finally over for lakhs of students across Haryana! The Board of School Education Haryana (BSEH) has officially declared the much-anticipated HBSE Class 12 Result 2026 on May 12, 2026, at 4 PM. Students who appeared for the Senior Secondary Annual Examinations can now access their provisional marksheets and celebrate their hard-earned achievements.

This year, the overall pass percentage for regular candidates stands at an impressive 84.67%, marking it as the highest in the last five years, excluding the pandemic-affected session of 2021.

How to Check Your HBSE 12th Result 2026

Checking your HBSE 12th Result is a straightforward process. The Board has made the results available online on its official website. Here's a step-by-step guide to access your provisional marksheet:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official website of the Board of School Education Haryana: bseh.org.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, click on the link that says "HBSE 12th Result 2026" or "HBSE Senior Secondary Examination 2026 Result".
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your 10-digit Roll Number and Date of Birth. Private candidates may also need to enter their Father's Name/Mother's Name.
4. View and Download Your Result
After submitting the details, your provisional marksheet will appear on the screen. Download it and take a printout for future reference. Remember, this is a provisional marksheet, and the original will be distributed by your respective schools later.

Alternative Methods to Check Results

In case of heavy website traffic, students can also check their results through alternative platforms:

✅ DigiLocker: Access your results via the DigiLocker app or website using your Aadhaar number or registered mobile number.

✅ SMS Service: Type 'RESULTHB12 (Roll Number)' and send it to 56263.

This year's results showcase notable performances across various categories. A total of 2,95,478 students registered for the Class 12 examinations, with 2,42,856 appearing for the regular examinations. Out of these, 2,05,618 candidates successfully passed.

Meet the HBSE 12th Toppers 2026

This year, girls have truly shined, sweeping the top ranks in the HBSE Class 12 examinations. Deepika from Vivekanand Vidya Mandir, Chillar in Rewari, secured the overall state topper position with a remarkable score of 499 out of 500 marks in the Arts stream. She was closely followed by Udita, who achieved the second rank with 498 marks. Yogita and Muskan jointly shared the third position, both scoring 497 marks.

Other notable high achievers include Bhawna, Kamakshi (both with 493 marks), and Jyoti, Muskan, and Sia (all with 492 marks).

What's Next? Re-evaluation and Compartment Exams

For students who may not be satisfied with their results or have not cleared certain subjects, the Haryana Board provides opportunities for re-evaluation and compartment examinations.

1. Rechecking/Revaluation
Students can apply for rechecking or revaluation of their answer sheets online within 20 days of the result announcement. A prescribed fee, approximately ₹1,000 per subject, will be applicable. Discounts might be available for BPL cardholders.
2. Compartment (Supplementary) Exams
Candidates who fail to secure the minimum 33% passing marks in one or two subjects are eligible to appear for compartment examinations. These exams are tentatively scheduled for July 2026, with results expected by August 2026. This provides a crucial chance to clear the subjects and avoid losing an academic year.

The HBSE Class 12 Result 2026 marks a significant milestone in the academic journey of nearly three lakh students. We congratulate all the successful candidates and wish them the very best for their future endeavors. Keep an eye on the official HBSE website for further updates regarding original marksheets and detailed schedules for re-evaluation and compartment exams.
