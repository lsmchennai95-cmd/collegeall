CGBSE Class 12 Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Future Endeavors!

The moment countless students across Chhattisgarh have eagerly awaited is finally here! The Chhattisgarh Board of Secondary Education (CGBSE) officially declared the Class 12 results for 2026 on April 29, 2026, at 2:30 PM. This declaration marks a significant milestone for over 2.38 lakh students who appeared for the Higher Secondary examinations.

This year, the overall pass percentage for the CGBSE Class 12 examination stands impressively at 83.04%, showcasing the diligent efforts of students and educators alike. The results, available for Science, Commerce, and Arts streams, are now accessible online, paving the way for students' next academic and professional journeys.

How to Check Your CGBSE Class 12 Result 2026

Checking your results is a straightforward process. Ensure you have your roll number handy to access your provisional marksheet. Here's a step-by-step guide:

1. Visit the Official Websites
Navigate to one of the official CGBSE result portals:
  • cgbse.nic.in
  • results.cg.nic.in
These are the primary and most reliable platforms for result access.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for a link related to "CGBSE 12th Result 2026" or "Higher Secondary Certificate (Class 12) Examination Result 2026". Click on it.
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your unique Roll Number and the displayed Captcha code.
4. View and Download Your Result
After submitting the details, your CGBSE Class 12 scorecard will appear on the screen. Download and print a copy for your immediate reference. Remember, this online marksheet is provisional.

Pro Tip: Due to heavy traffic immediately after the announcement, websites might experience slowdowns. If you face issues, try again after a few minutes or use alternative methods.

Alternative Ways to Check Results

✅ Via SMS: For CG Board 12th result, type CG12(Roll Number) and send it to 56263. You will receive your result on the same mobile number.

✅ Via DigiLocker: Students can also securely download their digital marksheets via the DigiLocker app/website.

✅ Through Third-Party Portals: Websites like ndtv.com/education and indiaresults.com often host the results as well.

Key Statistics and Highlights of CGBSE 12th Result 2026

The 2026 results bring forth several notable observations:

Overall Performance
The pass percentage of 83.04% for Class 12 this year reflects a strong academic showing. This is an improvement compared to the previous year's (2025) Class 12 pass percentage of 81.87%.
Girls Outshine Boys
Continuing a trend from previous years, girls have once again outperformed boys in the Class 12 examinations. This highlights the consistent dedication and hard work of female students across the state.
Meet the Topper
This year, Jigyasu Verma from Balodha Bazar has topped the CGBSE 12th exams with an impressive 493 marks, scoring 98.60%. Congratulations to Jigyasu and all the other meritorious students!

CGBSE Class 12 Pass Criteria

To successfully clear the CGBSE Class 12 examinations, students must meet specific passing criteria:

✅ A minimum of 33% marks in each subject (both theory and practical, where applicable).

✅ An overall aggregate of at least 33% across all subjects.

Students who secure less than 33% in any subject or overall will be considered for supplementary examinations.

What's Next? Post-Result Steps for CGBSE Class 12 Students

1. Collect Original Documents
While the online marksheet is provisional, students must collect their original marksheets and certificates from their respective schools a few weeks after the result declaration. These original documents are crucial for college admissions and other academic processes.
2. Supplementary Examinations
For students who could not clear one or two subjects, the CGBSE will conduct supplementary exams. These exams are tentatively scheduled for June or July 2026, with results expected in August 2026. Students can appear for a maximum of two subjects to improve their scores. Application details will be available on the official CGBSE website.
3. Revaluation and Re-totaling
If you believe there's a discrepancy in your marks, you can apply for revaluation or re-totaling of your answer scripts. The application window is expected to open in May 2026, and the revaluation results are anticipated in June 2026. A fee will be applicable per subject. Stay tuned to the official website for detailed announcements.

Looking Ahead

The declaration of the CGBSE Class 12 results marks the culmination of years of hard work and opens doors to new opportunities. Whether you plan to pursue higher education, vocational training, or embark on a career, these results are a stepping stone. We wish all the students the very best for their future endeavors. Remember to celebrate your achievements and approach any challenges with determination!
