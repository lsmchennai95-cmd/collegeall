Charting Your Course: Indian Navy SSR Recruitment 2026 Under the Agniveer Scheme

Are you ready to embark on an exhilarating career path that combines adventure, service to the nation, and unparalleled growth? The Indian Navy's Agniveer Senior Secondary Recruit (SSR) scheme offers an incredible opportunity for young, aspiring individuals to contribute to national security while forging a strong future. The recruitment drive for the 01/2027 and 02/2027 batches, with its selection process commencing in 2026, has already generated significant interest. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every essential aspect of the Indian Navy SSR Recruitment 2026, ensuring you're well-prepared to seize this prestigious opportunity.

Key Recruitment Highlights

✅ Application Window: Applications for Agniveer SSR were open from March 14 to April 6, 2026.

✅ Exam Conducted: The Stage 1 Indian Navy Entrance Test (INET) 2026 was tentatively scheduled for May 2026, with results expected in May and June 2026.

✅ Batches: Recruitment is for Agniveer SSR 01/2027 and 02/2027 batches.

✅ Gender Inclusivity: Unmarried male and female candidates are eligible for Agniveer SSR posts.

✅ Training Commencement: Training for the 01/2027 batch is expected to commence in December 2026, and for the 02/2027 batch in May 2027, at INS Chilka, Odisha.

Eligibility Criteria: Are You Ready to Serve?

To be considered for the Indian Navy Agniveer SSR recruitment, candidates must meet specific eligibility requirements. Ensure you fulfill all conditions before applying.

1. Nationality
Applicants must be Indian citizens or Gorkhas from Nepal.
2. Age Limit

The age requirements vary based on the batch you are applying for:

  • Agniveer 01/2027 Batch: Candidates must be born between December 1, 2004, and May 31, 2009 (both dates inclusive).
  • Agniveer 02/2027 Batch: Candidates must be born between May 1, 2005, and October 31, 2009 (both dates inclusive).
3. Educational Qualification

Candidates must have passed Class 12 with Mathematics and Physics, and at least one of the following subjects: Chemistry, Biology, or Computer Science, from a Board of School Education recognized by MHRD, Govt. of India. A minimum of 50% aggregate marks is generally required.

Alternatively, candidates with a three-year diploma in Engineering with a minimum of 50% marks are also eligible.

4. Marital Status
Only unmarried male and female candidates are eligible. Marriage during the four-year Agniveer tenure is strictly prohibited and will lead to dismissal from service.
The Selection Process: Your Journey to the Navy

The selection process for Agniveer SSR is rigorous and comprises multiple stages designed to assess your aptitude, physical fitness, and medical suitability.

1. Stage I: Indian Navy Entrance Test (INET) 2026 (Computer-Based Test)

This is the initial screening stage, a computer-based examination that covers key subjects.

Exam Details:

  • Subjects: English, Science, Mathematics, and General Awareness.
  • Number of Questions: 100 objective-type questions.
  • Duration: 1 hour.
  • Language: Papers are available in English and Hindi.
  • Negative Marking: A penalty of 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer.
2. Stage II: Physical Fitness Test (PFT) & Stage II Written Examination

Candidates shortlisted from Stage I (based on state-wise merit) proceed to Stage II.

  • Physical Fitness Test (PFT): This is a mandatory qualifying stage.
    Activity Male Candidates Female Candidates
    1.6 Km Run 6 minutes 30 seconds 8 minutes
    Squats (Uthak Baithak) 20 15
    Push-ups 10 Not Applicable
    Sit-ups Not Applicable 10
    *Separate standards apply for male and female candidates.
  • Stage II Written Examination: This further evaluates candidates' reasoning, numerical aptitude, and problem-solving skills.
3. Document Verification & Recruitment Medical Examination

Successful candidates from Stage II undergo thorough document verification and a comprehensive medical examination. Standards include a minimum height of 157cm and specific criteria for physical and mental health.

Note for NCC 'C' Certificate Holders: Candidates holding an NCC 'C' certificate and uploading verified documents during application may be exempted from the INET (Stage I) and called directly for Stage II.

Life as an Agniveer SSR: A Path of Growth and Opportunity

Joining the Indian Navy as an Agniveer SSR is not just a job; it's a transformative experience under the Agnipath scheme, offering a unique blend of service, training, and financial security.

✅ Service Tenure: Agniveers serve for a fixed period of four years under the Navy Act 1957.

✅ Monthly Package: Agniveers receive a monthly package of ₹30,000 in the first year, incrementally rising over the four-year tenure.

✅ Seva Nidhi: Upon exit, Agniveers receive a tax-free 'Seva Nidhi' payout of approximately ₹11.71 lakh (which includes their contribution plus government matching contribution and interest).

✅ Insurance Coverage: All enrolled Agniveers are covered under a non-contributory life insurance cover of ₹48 lakh.

✅ Skill Development: Agniveers undergo comprehensive training at INS Chilka, acquiring valuable skills for both naval service and future civilian careers.

✅ Opportunities for Permanent Cadre: Up to 25% of each Agniveer batch may be offered permanent enrolment as regular Sailors based on performance and organizational requirements.

How to Apply (Past Application Window)

The application window for the Agniveer SSR 01/2027 and 02/2027 batches ran from March 14 to April 6, 2026. For future reference and upcoming recruitments, the process typically involves these steps:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the Indian Navy's official recruitment portal: joinindiannavy.gov.in.
2. Register and Apply Online
Register with your email ID and proceed to fill out the application form with accurate personal, educational, and contact details.
3. Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of your photograph, signature, and other required documents in the specified format.
4. Pay Application Fee
An application fee of Rs. 550 plus 18% GST was applicable, payable online.
5. Review and Submit
Carefully review all entered information before submitting the application form and take a printout for future reference.

Your Future Awaits in the Indian Navy!

The Indian Navy Agniveer SSR recruitment offers a unique chance to build a dynamic career while serving your country. Stay updated with the official Indian Navy website for future recruitment notifications and seize your opportunity to join this esteemed force!
