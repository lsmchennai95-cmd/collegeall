UGC NET June 2026 Admit Card: Your Gateway to the Examination Hall

The National Testing Agency (NTA) is all set to conduct the University Grants Commission – National Eligibility Test (UGC NET) for the June 2026 session, a crucial examination for aspiring Assistant Professors and Junior Research Fellows across Indian universities and colleges. As the exam dates draw near, candidates are eagerly awaiting the release of their Admit Cards. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about the UGC NET June 2026 Admit Card, from its release date and download process to essential exam day instructions.

Important Dates at a Glance

Keeping track of important dates is crucial for every aspirant. The NTA has released a detailed schedule for the UGC NET June 2026 examination. Make sure you're aware of these key deadlines:

Event Date (2026)
Official Notification Release April 29
Application Period April 29 - May 24 (Extended)
Application Correction Window May 25 - May 28
City Intimation Slip Release By June 10
Admit Card Release By June 15
UGC NET June 2026 Exam Dates June 22 - June 30

The UGC NET June 2026 examination will be conducted in Computer-Based Test (CBT) mode from June 22 to June 30, 2026, across various centres in India. The exams will be held in two shifts daily: 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM and 3:00 PM to 6:00 PM.

How to Download Your UGC NET June 2026 Admit Card

The Admit Card is a mandatory document for entry into the examination hall. It will be released online on the official NTA UGC NET website. Follow these simple steps to download yours:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official UGC NET website: ugcnet.nta.nic.in.
2. Locate the Admit Card Link
On the homepage, look for the section titled "Latest News" or "Candidate Activity" and click on the link that reads "UGC NET June 2026: Click Here to Download Admit Card."
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you need to enter your Application Number and Date of Birth (or Password) that you generated during the application process.
4. Input Security Pin
Carefully enter the Security Pin (Captcha Code) displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print
After successful login, your UGC NET June 2026 Admit Card will be displayed on the screen. Verify all the details thoroughly. Download the PDF and take multiple printouts for future reference. It is advisable to take a color printout for clarity.

Crucial Details to Verify on Your Admit Card

It is absolutely essential to check all the particulars mentioned on your admit card for any discrepancies. In case of any errors, immediately contact the NTA helpline.

What to Check:
  • Your Name, Father's Name, Mother's Name
  • Your Roll Number and Application Number
  • Date of Birth, Gender, and Category
  • Date and Time of Examination (Shift and reporting time)
  • Subject Opted for the examination
  • Exam Centre Name and Complete Address
  • Your Photograph and Signature
  • Important Instructions for Exam Day

Essential Documents to Carry to the Exam Hall

Ensure you have the following documents ready before heading to your exam center:

✅ A printed copy of your UGC NET June 2026 Admit Card.

✅ One original, valid government-issued photo identification proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID).

✅ PwBD certificate, if applicable.

✅ A simple transparent ballpoint pen (as per NTA guidelines).

✅ A transparent water bottle (optional).

Key Instructions for Exam Day

To ensure a hassle-free examination experience, keep the following points in mind:

✅ Reach the examination center well before the reporting time mentioned on your admit card to complete frisking and registration formalities.

✅ Admit Cards will NOT be sent by post; they must be downloaded online.

✅ No candidate will be allowed to enter the examination centre without a valid Admit Card and Photo ID proof.

✅ Electronic devices, study material, and any unauthorized items are strictly prohibited inside the exam hall.

✅ Follow all COVID-19 safety protocols and guidelines issued by the NTA and the Government of India.

✅ Do not tamper with the admit card or present a forged one, as it will lead to disqualification.

New Additions to UGC NET June 2026

The NTA has introduced some notable changes for the June 2026 session:

New Subjects Introduced
This year, two new subjects, **Statistics** and **Forestry**, have been added to the UGC NET subject list, bringing the total to 87 subjects.
Enhanced Accessibility in North-East
To improve accessibility for candidates from the North-Eastern region, the NTA has added four new examination centres.

With your Admit Card in hand and a thorough understanding of the guidelines, you're well-prepared for the UGC NET June 2026 examination. Remember to stay calm, focused, and give your best shot. Good luck!
