MP Board Result 2026 Declared: Check Your Scores & What’s Next!

The wait is finally over! The Madhya Pradesh Board of Secondary Education (MPBSE) has officially announced the Class 10 (High School Certificate) and Class 12 (Higher Secondary School Certificate) results for 2026. Students can now access their highly anticipated scorecards online.

This year, the results for both classes were declared on April 15, 2026, at 11:00 AM, a significant step towards faster evaluation and processing compared to previous years. Chief Minister Mohan Yadav made the announcement, marking a crucial moment for lakhs of students across the state.

MP Board Result 2026: Key Highlights

✅ Declaration Date: April 15, 2026

✅ Declaration Time: 11:00 AM

✅ Official Websites: mpresults.nic.in, mpbse.nic.in, mpbse.mponline.gov.in

✅ Alternative Methods: DigiLocker, MPBSE Mobile App, SMS

✅ Required Credentials: Roll Number and Application Number

How to Check Your MP Board Result 2026 Online

Checking your MP Board Class 10th and 12th results is a straightforward process. Follow these step-by-step instructions to access your scorecard quickly:

1. Visit the Official Website
Open your web browser and navigate to one of the official MPBSE result portals: mpresults.nic.in, mpbse.nic.in, or mpbse.mponline.gov.in.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the specific link for "MPBSE – HSC (Class 10th) Main Examination Results 2026" or "MPBSE – HSSC (Class 12th) Main Examination Results 2026." Click on the relevant link.
3. Enter Your Login Details
A new page will appear where you need to enter your Roll Number and Application Number as mentioned on your admit card. Some portals might also ask for your date of birth or a captcha code for verification.
4. Submit and View Result
After entering the required details, click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button. Your MP Board Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print
Carefully check all the details on your provisional marksheet. Download a copy and take a printout for future reference. This provisional marksheet will be essential until you receive your original certificates from your school.

Direct Links to Check MP Board Result 2026

To ensure a smooth experience, especially during high traffic, students can use the following official portals:

✅ mpbse.nic.in

✅ mpbse.mponline.gov.in

✅ DigiLocker

Checking Results via SMS and Mobile App

In addition to the official websites, MPBSE provides alternative methods to check results:

Via SMS

For Class 10: Type MPBSE10<Roll Number> and send it to 56263.

For Class 12: Type MPBSE12<Roll Number> and send it to 56263.

Your result will be sent to your mobile phone.

Using MPBSE Mobile App / MP Mobile App

Download the official MPBSE Mobile App or MP Mobile App from the Google Play Store. Select the 'Know Your Result' option, enter your Roll Number and Application Number, then submit to view your result.

MP Board Result 2026 Statistics & Previous Year Trends

This year, the overall pass percentage for Class 10 stands at 73.42%, while for Class 12, it is 76.01%. Girls have once again outperformed boys in both examinations.

The 2026 results were declared significantly earlier than previous years, reflecting faster evaluation processes. For instance, in 2025, the results were announced on May 6th, while in 2024, they were released on April 24th.

What to Do After MP Board Result 2026

Congratulations to all the successful candidates! For those who didn't achieve their desired results, remember that one exam doesn't define your future. Here's what comes next:

Original Marksheet Collection
The online marksheet is provisional. You must collect your original official marksheet and certificates from your respective schools a few weeks after the online declaration.
Re-evaluation/Re-checking
If you are unsatisfied with your marks, you can apply for re-evaluation or re-checking of your answer sheets. The application window for this is generally open for about 15 days after the result declaration, and a nominal fee is usually charged per subject.
Supplementary/Second Chance Examinations
Students who fail in one or two subjects have the opportunity to appear for supplementary (now often called "second chance") examinations to save an academic year. The datesheet for these exams is usually announced shortly after the main results. For Class 10 and 12, the second chance examinations are scheduled to begin from May 7, 2026.
Career Planning and Higher Education
Start planning your next steps, whether it's applying for admission to higher classes (11th for Class 10 pass-outs) or various undergraduate courses (for Class 12 pass-outs). Explore different streams and career options.

The MPBSE has worked efficiently to release the 2026 results promptly, allowing students to proceed with their academic plans without delay. Always rely on official sources for any further updates.
