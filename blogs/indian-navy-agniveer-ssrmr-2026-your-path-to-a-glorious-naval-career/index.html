Chart Your Course: Indian Navy Agniveer SSR/MR 2026 Recruitment Unveiled!

Are you ready to embark on a journey of honor, adventure, and service to the nation? The Indian Navy Agniveer SSR/MR 2026 recruitment drive offers an unparalleled opportunity for aspiring young individuals to join the prestigious Indian Navy under the transformative Agnipath scheme. The official notification for the 01/2027 and 02/2027 training batches was released on March 10, 2026, inviting both male and female candidates to apply for Senior Secondary Recruit (SSR) and Matric Recruit (MR) posts, with specific provisions for SSR Medical Assistant roles.

This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know – from eligibility criteria and application procedures to the selection process, exam patterns, and crucial updates regarding the Agnipath scheme.

Key Dates at a Glance

Mark your calendars! The application window for the Indian Navy Agniveer SSR/MR 2026 recruitment was open for a limited period.

✅ **Notification Release:** March 10, 2026

✅ **Application Start Date:** March 14, 2026

✅ **Application End Date:** April 6, 2026

✅ **INET 2026 (Computer-Based Test):** Tentatively scheduled for May 2026 (SSR: May 13, MR: May 14 & 15)

All applications were to be submitted online through the official Indian Navy recruitment portal: joinindiannavy.gov.in.

Eligibility Criteria: Are You Ready to Serve?

Meeting the eligibility requirements is the first step towards realizing your dream. Here's a breakdown of the criteria for Agniveer SSR and MR posts for the 2026 recruitment cycle.

1. Marital Status & Gender

Both unmarried male and female candidates are eligible for Agniveer SSR and MR posts. However, the Agniveer SSR Medical Assistant post is exclusively for unmarried male candidates.

2. Age Limit

Candidates generally need to be between 17 and 21 years of age. Specifically, for the 01/2027 batch, candidates must have been born between December 1, 2004, and May 31, 2009. For the 02/2027 batch (and SSR Medical Assistant 02/2027), the birth date range is May 1, 2005, to October 31, 2009.

3. Educational Qualification
  • **Agniveer SSR:** Candidates must have passed Class 12 with Mathematics and Physics, and at least one of Chemistry, Biology, or Computer Science, from a recognized board with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate. A three-year engineering diploma or a two-year vocational course with Physics and Mathematics (with 50% aggregate) is also accepted.
  • **Agniveer MR:** A Class 10 pass from a recognized board with a minimum of 50% aggregate marks is required.
  • **Agniveer SSR (Medical Assistant):** Applicants must have cleared Class 10 and 12 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, securing at least 50% aggregate and a minimum of 40% in each of the three subjects.
4. Physical Standards

Candidates must meet specific physical and medical standards. The minimum height requirement is 157 cm, with weight proportionate to height. A minimum chest expansion of 5 cm is also mandatory. Candidates must be in good physical and mental health, free from any defects that could hinder their duties.

The Physical Fitness Test (PFT) includes a 1.6 Km run, which must be completed within 6 minutes 30 seconds for male candidates and 8 minutes for female candidates.

The Selection Process: Your Path to Becoming an Agniveer

The selection process for Indian Navy Agniveer SSR/MR is rigorous and conducted in two main stages.

Stage 1: Indian Navy Entrance Test (INET) 2026

This is a computer-based online examination, tentatively scheduled for May 2026. The SSR exam was held on May 13, 2026, while the MR exam took place on May 14 and 15, 2026. Qualified candidates are shortlisted state-wise for Stage 2.

Stage 2: Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Written Exam & Medical Examination

Candidates who clear Stage 1 proceed to Stage 2. This involves a Physical Fitness Test, followed by another written examination and a recruitment medical examination. It's important to note that NCC 'C' certificate holders and sponsor certificate holders, who upload verified documents during the application, are exempted from INET and are directly called for Stage 2.

Exam Pattern and Syllabus

A thorough understanding of the exam pattern and syllabus is crucial for effective preparation. The Indian Navy Agniveer syllabus is divided based on the entry type (MR and SSR).

Agniveer SSR Exam Pattern & Syllabus:

✅ **Duration:** 60 minutes

✅ **Total Questions:** 100

✅ **Negative Marking:** 0.25 marks for each incorrect answer

✅ **Subjects:** English, Science, Mathematics, and General Awareness

The Science syllabus for SSR is based on the Class 12 level and includes topics from Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science. Mathematics covers topics like Relations and Functions, Trigonometry, Algebra, Calculus, and Probability. General Awareness includes Culture, Geography, History, Current Affairs, and important national facts.

Agniveer MR Exam Pattern & Syllabus:

✅ **Exam Type:** Objective type, 10th level

✅ **Subjects:** Science & Mathematics, and General Awareness

The MR syllabus covers basic concepts of Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Geography, History, Culture, Sports, Defence, and Current Affairs. Both SSR and MR question papers are available in both English and Hindi.

The Agnipath Scheme: A Modern Approach to Defence

The Agniveer recruitment falls under the Agnipath scheme, introduced in June 2022, designed to create a younger and more agile armed force.

Service Duration and Training

Agniveers are inducted for a fixed tenure of four years, including six months of intensive training at INS Chilka, Odisha. The 01/2027 batch is expected to commence training in December 2026, and the 02/2027 batch in May 2027.

Salary and Benefits

Agniveers receive a competitive monthly package, ranging from INR 30,000 to INR 40,000. Upon completion of the four-year tenure, those not opting for permanent service receive a tax-free 'Seva Nidhi' package of approximately Rs 11.71 lakh.

Retention Rates: A Significant Development

While the initial scheme stipulated that 25% of Agniveers would be absorbed into permanent service after four years, significant developments are underway. As the first batch of Agniveers completes its term in October 2026, the Department of Military Affairs is actively reviewing proposals to increase retention rates.

Notably, the Indian Navy is reportedly pushing for a retention rate of up to 70-75% for its Agniveers, citing the need for experienced personnel in technical branches due to high training costs. The Army and Air Force are also seeking an increase to approximately 50%. These proposed changes aim to strike a balance between a youthful fighting force and maintaining operational continuity and specialized expertise.

How to Apply (Past Reference for Future Aspirants)

For those looking ahead to future recruitment drives, the application process for Indian Navy Agniveer posts typically involves these steps:

Step 1: Visit the Official Website
Navigate to joinindiannavy.gov.in.
Step 2: Register/Login
Complete the registration process with necessary details or log in if already registered.
Step 3: Fill Application Form
Carefully fill out the online application form with personal, educational, and contact details.
Step 4: Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of all required original documents (certificates, mark sheets, domicile, NCC certificate) as per specified formats.
Step 5: Pay Application Fee
Pay the application fee of Rs 550 + 18% GST online.
Step 6: Submit and Print
Submit the form and take a printout of the final application for future reference.

The Indian Navy Agniveer SSR/MR 2026 recruitment represents a golden opportunity for young Indians to serve their country with pride. With detailed eligibility criteria, a structured selection process, and ongoing enhancements to the Agnipath scheme, a fulfilling career awaits those with dedication and zeal. Prepare diligently, stay updated with official announcements, and take the leap towards a distinguished service in the Indian Navy!
