TN 12th Result 2026 Declared: A Comprehensive Guide for Students

The much-awaited Tamil Nadu 12th Result 2026 has been officially declared by the Directorate of Government Examinations (TNDGE) today, May 8, 2026, at 9:30 AM. This marks a pivotal moment for over 8 lakh students who appeared for the Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) examinations held between March 2 and March 26, 2026. Students can now access their scores and plan their future academic endeavours.

Key Highlights of TN 12th Result 2026

This year's results bring good news with an impressive overall pass percentage and remarkable achievements across the state.

✅ Overall Pass Percentage: The state recorded a commendable 95.20% overall pass percentage in the Class 12 board examinations for the 2025-2026 academic session.

✅ Girls Outperform Boys: Girls have once again outshone boys, securing a pass percentage of 97% compared to boys' 93.19%.

✅ Erode District Tops: Erode district emerged as the top-performing district with an outstanding pass percentage of 98.97%.

✅ Increased Pass Rate: The current pass percentage shows a slight increase from last year's 95.03% overall pass percentage.

✅ Schools with 100% Pass: Out of 7,536 schools, an impressive 2,639 schools achieved a 100% pass percentage.

How to Check Your TN 12th Result 2026

Students can check their results through various convenient methods. Make sure to keep your registration number and date of birth (in dd/mm/yyyy format) handy.

1. Via Official Websites
The Directorate of Government Examinations (TNDGE) has activated multiple official portals to ensure smooth access to results.
  • Visit tnresults.nic.in
  • Alternatively, you can visit dge.tn.gov.in
  • Other backup portals include dge1.tn.nic.in, dge2.tn.nic.in, and apply1.tndge.org.
  • Click on the "TN 12th Result 2026" or "HSC (+2) Result 2026" link on the homepage.
  • Enter your registration number and date of birth.
  • Submit the details, and your provisional marksheet will be displayed.
  • Download and save a copy for future reference.
2. Through DigiLocker
The results are also available on DigiLocker, providing a secure and convenient way to access your marksheet.
  • Go to results.digilocker.gov.in.
  • Select the board name and your class (Class 12).
  • Provide the requested login details.
  • Submit to view and download your result.
3. Via SMS and WhatsApp
For quick access, students may also receive results via SMS or check through WhatsApp. While specific 2026 details for SMS were not explicitly stated, in previous years, students could send an SMS in the format "TNBOARD12REGNO., DATE OF BIRTH" to designated numbers like 09282232585 or +91 9282232585. Keep an eye on official announcements for 2026 SMS details. WhatsApp is also mentioned as an available channel.

The consistent rise, especially notable in recent years, highlights the growing academic success in the state.

Important Details for Students

Minimum Passing Marks: To clear the TN 12th exams, students must secure a minimum of 35 marks in each theory subject out of 100. For subjects with practical components, the criteria are 70 marks in theory, 20 in practicals, and 10 in internal assessment.

The provisional marksheet downloaded online is crucial for immediate academic procedures like college admissions. Original marksheets will be distributed by schools a few days after the online declaration.

TN 12th Supplementary Exams 2026: Your Second Chance

For students who may not have cleared all subjects, the Directorate of Government Examinations offers a valuable opportunity through supplementary exams.

Eligibility and Application
Candidates who failed in less than three subjects in the main board exams are eligible to appear for the supplementary examinations. The application process will begin through respective schools after the revaluation process.
Exam Schedule and Timings
The TN 12th supplementary exams for 2026 are scheduled to be conducted from June to July 2026. The detailed timetable will be released in the first or second week of May 2026. The examinations typically run from 10:00 AM to 1:15 PM daily, including 10 minutes for reading the question paper and 5 minutes for verifying particulars.
Supplementary Results
The results for the supplementary exams are tentatively expected to be released in July 2026. These exams are designed to provide students with a crucial second chance to improve their scores and continue their academic journey without losing a year.

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared the TN 12th examinations! Your hard work and dedication have paid off. For those appearing for supplementary exams, remember that this is another opportunity to achieve your goals. We wish all students the very best in their future endeavours.
