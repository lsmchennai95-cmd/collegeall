UP Board Result 2026 Date – Official Update & Direct Link

The wait is almost over for over 52 lakh students who appeared for the Uttar Pradesh Board High School (Class 10th) and Intermediate (Class 12th) examinations in 2026. The Uttar Pradesh Madhyamik Shiksha Parishad (UPMSP) is gearing up to announce the much-anticipated UP Board Result 2026, with official updates pointing towards a late April declaration. While an exact date and time are still awaited, students are advised to stay tuned to official channels for the latest information. This comprehensive guide will walk you through the expected result date, how to check your scores, direct links, and other crucial details.

Official Update on UP Board Result 2026 Date

The UP Board Class 10 and 12 examinations for the academic year 2026 were successfully conducted between February 18 and March 12, 2026. The answer sheet evaluation process, which commenced on March 18, 2026, faced a slight delay due to public holidays like Eid-ul-Fitr and Ram Navami. Consequently, the deadline for evaluation was extended to April 4, 2026, with examiners instructed to submit final award lists by April 5, 2026.

Following the completion of evaluation and subsequent compilation, the UPMSP is expected to announce the results in the third or fourth week of April 2026. Based on previous trends, the board typically declares results within 15-20 days after the evaluation process concludes. For instance, in 2025, the results were released on April 25th at 12:30 PM. An official notification confirming the precise date and time is expected to be released at least a day prior to the declaration.

✅ Official date announcement to be made shortly.

How to Check Your UP Board Result 2026 Online

Once declared, students can easily access their UP Board Class 10th and 12th results through the official websites. Here’s a step-by-step guide:

1. Visit the Official Websites
Navigate to either upmsp.edu.in or upresults.nic.in. These are the primary portals for result declarations.
2. Click on the Result Link
On the homepage, locate and click on the link relevant to your class: "UP Board High School (Class 10) Result 2026" or "UP Board Intermediate (Class 12) Result 2026".
3. Enter Your Credentials
A new page will open. You will need to enter your Roll Number. For Class 12, you might also need your School Code. Ensure these details match your admit card.
4. Submit and View Result
After entering the required information (and often a captcha code), click on the "Submit" or "View Result" button. Your UP Board Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen.
5. Download and Print
It is highly recommended to download your provisional mark sheet and take a printout for temporary use and future reference. The original mark sheets will be issued by your respective schools later.

Checking Results via SMS and DigiLocker/UMANG App

In addition to the official websites, students can also access their results through alternative methods:

Via SMS:

For Class 10: Type UP10Roll_Number and send it to 56263.

For Class 12: Type UP12Roll_Number and send it to 56263.

Your result will be sent to your mobile number.

Via DigiLocker and UMANG App:
The results will also be made available on the DigiLocker and UMANG mobile applications. Students can log in using their credentials to download their digital mark sheets. This is particularly useful if the official websites face heavy traffic.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Q1: When will the official UP Board Result 2026 date be announced?
The official date and time are yet to be announced by UPMSP. However, results are widely expected in the third or fourth week of April 2026. An official notification will precede the declaration.
Q2: What details are required to check the UP Board Result 2026?
Students will need their Roll Number and, for Class 12, potentially their School Code, as mentioned on their admit card.
Q3: Can I get my UP Board Result 2026 offline?
While results are primarily declared online, you can receive your provisional mark sheet via SMS. Original mark sheets must be collected from your respective schools.
Q4: What if I am not satisfied with my results?
Students dissatisfied with their scores can apply for scrutiny (re-evaluation) or compartment (supplementary) exams, details for which will be announced after the main results.
Q5: Is the online mark sheet valid for admissions?
Yes, the provisional mark sheet downloaded online is generally accepted for initial admission procedures. The original mark sheet will be provided by the school later.
