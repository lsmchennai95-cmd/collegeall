SSB Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2026: Your Gateway to a Rewarding Career!

The Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) has announced a significant recruitment drive for Constable Tradesman positions in 2026, offering a golden opportunity for eligible candidates to join one of India's esteemed paramilitary forces. With online applications currently underway, aspiring individuals are encouraged to seize this chance for a stable and respectable government job. This comprehensive guide provides all the essential details you need to navigate the application process successfully, from eligibility criteria to the selection stages and salary prospects.

Key Highlights of SSB Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2026

✅ Total Vacancies: 827 posts for various Constable Tradesman and Driver roles.

✅ Application Mode: Entirely online through the official SSB website.

✅ Application Dates: Applications commenced on March 21, 2026, with the deadline on April 20, 2026.

✅ Official Website: ssb.gov.in

✅ Eligibility: Primarily for 10th-pass candidates with relevant trade experience or qualifications.

Important Dates to Remember

Mark your calendars with these crucial dates for the SSB Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2026:

Event Date

Candidates are strongly advised to apply well before the closing date to avoid any last-minute technical glitches.

Eligibility Criteria

Before applying, candidates must ensure they meet the SSB Constable Tradesman eligibility criteria. Failure to do so will lead to disqualification.

1. Nationality/Citizenship

For Constable (Washerman, Barber, Safaiwala, Tailor, Gardener, Cobbler, Cook & Water Carrier): A citizen of India only.

For Constable (Driver, Veterinary, Carpenter, Blacksmith and Painter): A citizen of India, or a subject of Nepal, or a subject of Bhutan.

2. Educational Qualification

Generally, candidates must have passed Matriculation (10th pass) from a recognized board.

Specific posts may require additional qualifications, experience, or certifications:

  • Constable (Driver): Matriculation or equivalent with a valid heavy vehicle driving license.
  • Constable (Veterinary): Matriculation with Science as a main subject, desirable one year experience in the treatment of different species of animals.
  • Other Tradesmen (e.g., Cook, Washerman, Barber): Matriculation with two years of work experience in the respective trade, or one year certificate course from a recognized ITI/Vocational Institute with at least one year experience, or two years diploma from a recognized ITI in the respective or similar trade. Must also qualify a trade test.
3. Age Limit

The minimum age to apply is 18 years. The upper age limit varies by post:

  • Constable (Driver): 21 to 27 years.
  • Constable (Veterinary, Carpenter, Blacksmith, Painter): 18 to 25 years.
  • Constable (Washerman, Barber, Safai Karmchari, Tailor, Gardener, Cobbler, Cook & Water Carrier): 18 to 23 years.

Age relaxations are applicable for reserved categories (SC/ST/OBC), ex-servicemen, and departmental candidates as per government rules.

Application Process: Your Step-by-Step Guide

The application for SSB Constable Tradesman Recruitment 2026 is an online process. Follow these steps carefully to ensure a successful submission:

1. Visit the Official Website

Go to the official Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB) recruitment website: www.ssb.gov.in.

2. Find the Recruitment Link

Look for the "Apply Online" link for Constable (Tradesman/Driver) Recruitment 2026 on the homepage and click it.

3. Register and Create Login

Click on the 'Register' button and fill in your basic details, including a valid email ID and mobile number. You will receive a Registration ID and password.

4. Complete the Application Form

Log in using your credentials and fill out the detailed application form with personal, educational, and trade-specific information. Ensure all details are accurate.

5. Upload Documents

Upload scanned copies of your passport-size photograph and signature as per the prescribed format and size.

6. Pay Application Fee

If applicable, pay the application fee online. The fee is Rs. 100 for General, OBC, and EWS male candidates. SC/ST, female candidates, and Ex-servicemen are exempted from the fee.

7. Review and Submit

Carefully review all the entered information before final submission. After submission, download and print the confirmation page for your records.

Selection Process: Your Path to Joining SSB

The SSB Constable Tradesman selection process is multi-staged and designed to assess candidates comprehensively. Candidates must qualify each stage to proceed further.

1. Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standard Test (PST)

This is the initial stage, qualifying in nature, testing physical fitness and measurements like height and chest. PET includes a race (e.g., 4.8 km in 24 minutes for males).

2. Written Examination

Successful candidates from PET/PST will appear for a written exam, which is typically an objective-type test with 100 questions for 100 marks. There might be negative marking (e.g., one-fourth). The exam duration is usually 120 minutes.

3. Skill Test / Trade Test

This qualifying stage assesses practical skills related to the chosen trade (e.g., cooking for Cook, driving for Driver). It carries a maximum of 50 marks, with a minimum qualifying score of 60% for all categories.

4. Document Verification

Candidates will undergo thorough verification of their original educational, professional, and other relevant documents.

5. Detailed Medical Examination (DME) & Review Medical Examination (RME)

The final stage involves a detailed medical examination to ensure candidates meet the prescribed medical standards. A review medical examination is conducted for those declared unfit in DME.

Salary Structure & Perks

Candidates selected as SSB Constable Tradesman will receive an attractive salary package as per the 7th Central Pay Commission.

✅ Pay Level: Level 3

✅ Pay Scale: Rs. 21,700 - Rs. 69,100 per month.

✅ In-Hand Salary: Approximately Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 35,000 per month, depending on posting location and allowances.

✅ Allowances: In addition to the basic pay, personnel receive various allowances such as Dearness Allowance (DA), House Rent Allowance (HRA), Ration Money Allowance, and other benefits as admissible under government rules.

Ensure you meet all eligibility criteria, gather your documents, and apply online before the deadline. Stay updated with the official SSB website for any further announcements regarding the recruitment process. Good luck!
