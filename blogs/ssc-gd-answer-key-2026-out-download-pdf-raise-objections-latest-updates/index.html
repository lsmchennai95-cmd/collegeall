SSC GD Answer Key 2026 Released: Your Essential Guide to Download and Objections

The moment many aspiring candidates have been eagerly waiting for is finally here! The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has officially released the provisional Answer Key for the Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination 2026. This is a crucial step for all candidates who appeared for the Computer-Based Examination (CBE) as it allows them to verify their responses, estimate their scores, and prepare for the next stages of the selection process.

The SSC GD Constable provisional Answer Key 2026 was released on June 15, 2026, on the official website ssc.gov.in. The objection window is active until June 20, 2026.

SSC GD 2026 Examination at a Glance

The SSC GD Constable 2026 examination was a significant recruitment drive aimed at filling 25,487 vacancies across various paramilitary forces. The Computer-Based Test (CBT) was conducted from April 27 to May 30, 2026, spread across multiple shifts and four distinct phases. The examination serves as the initial screening stage, leading to Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standards Test (PST), Detailed Medical Examination (DME), and Document Verification for shortlisted candidates.

How to Download Your SSC GD Answer Key 2026 PDF

Accessing your SSC GD Answer Key and response sheet is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to download your provisional answer key and evaluate your performance:

1. Visit the Official SSC Website
Navigate to the Staff Selection Commission's official website: www.ssc.gov.in.
2. Locate the Answer Key Section
On the homepage, look for and click on the "Answer Key" section.
3. Find the Relevant Link
Search for the notification titled "Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs) and SSF, and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2026: Uploading of Tentative Answer Keys along with Question Paper(s) cum Response Sheet(s)" and click on it.
4. Log In with Your Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password (as provided in your admission certificate).
5. Download Your Answer Key and Response Sheet
After successful login, your individual response sheet along with the provisional answer key will be displayed on the screen. Download the PDF file for your reference.

Raise Objections: Challenging the Provisional Answer Key

The released answer key is provisional, meaning candidates have the opportunity to challenge any answer they believe is incorrect. The objection window for the SSC GD Answer Key 2026 is open from June 15 to June 20, 2026, until 6:00 PM. If you find any discrepancy, you can raise an objection by submitting valid supporting documents and paying a fee of ₹50 per question.

✅ Review your response sheet carefully.

✅ Identify questions where you believe the official answer is incorrect.

✅ Gather valid documentary evidence to support your objection.

✅ Submit your objection through the official portal before June 20, 2026 (6 PM).

✅ Pay the prescribed fee of ₹50 per question challenged.

The SSC will review all submitted objections. If an objection is found valid, necessary corrections will be made, and a final answer key will be released before the declaration of results.

Understanding the SSC GD Cut-Off 2026 and Expected Results

The cut-off marks are the minimum scores required to qualify for the subsequent stages of the selection process. These are determined by several factors, including the number of vacancies, the overall difficulty level of the examination, and the total number of candidates who appeared. There is no sectional cut-off in the SSC GD Constable 2026 exam. Candidates must score at least 35% (General/Ex-servicemen) or 33% (SC/ST/OBC) to meet the minimum qualifying criteria.

Based on previous trends, here's an expected range for the SSC GD Cut-Off 2026-27 (out of 160 marks):

Category Expected Cut-Off Range (Marks out of 160)
General 140–150
OBC 135–145
EWS 138–148
SC 125–135
ST 120–130

It's important to note that these are expected ranges and the actual cut-off may vary. Candidates targeting 145+ marks generally fall into a safer zone for selection.

The SSC GD Result 2026 is anticipated to be released in July-August 2026. The result will be published in PDF format on the official website, containing the roll numbers and names of candidates shortlisted for the next stages of recruitment.

What's Next? The SSC GD Selection Process

For those who successfully clear the CBT by achieving the cut-off marks, the journey continues with the following stages:

Physical Efficiency Test (PET) & Physical Standards Test (PST)
These tests assess candidates' physical fitness and measurements as per the prescribed standards.
Detailed Medical Examination (DME)
Candidates undergo a thorough medical examination to ensure they meet the stringent medical requirements for the Constable (GD) post.
Document Verification (DV)
This final stage involves verifying all original documents, including educational certificates, identity proof, and caste/category certificates.

Stay tuned to the official SSC website for the latest updates regarding the final answer key, result declaration, and the schedule for PET/PST. Good luck to all candidates!
