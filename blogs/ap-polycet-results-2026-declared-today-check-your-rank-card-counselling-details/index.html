AP POLYCET Results 2026 Declared Today! Your Gateway to Polytechnic Admissions

The wait is over! Andhra Pradesh Polytechnic Common Entrance Test (AP POLYCET) 2026 results are officially announced.

Breaking News: AP POLYCET 2026 Results are OUT!

The State Board of Technical Education and Training (SBTET), Andhra Pradesh, has officially declared the much-anticipated AP POLYCET 2026 results today, May 5, 2026. Candidates who appeared for the examination on April 25, 2026, can now access their rank cards online. This announcement marks a crucial step for thousands of students aspiring to secure admission into various diploma-level polytechnic courses across engineering and non-engineering branches in government and private polytechnic colleges in Andhra Pradesh.

✅ Result Declared: May 5, 2026

✅ Exam Date: April 25, 2026

✅ Official Websites: polycetap.nic.in, polycetap.ap.gov.in

✅ Rank Card Available: Download using your Hall Ticket Number

How to Check Your AP POLYCET 2026 Rank Card

Accessing your AP POLYCET 2026 rank card is a straightforward process. Follow these simple steps to download your results:

1. Visit the Official Portal
Navigate to the official AP POLYCET website: polycetap.nic.in or polycetap.ap.gov.in. Some educational portals like manabadi.co.in may also provide direct links.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for a prominent link such as "AP POLYCET 2026 Results," "POLYCET Rank Card," or "Download Rank Card." Click on this link.
3. Enter Your Credentials
You will be prompted to enter your AP POLYCET 2026 Hall Ticket Number. Ensure you enter it correctly to avoid any issues. In some cases, your Date of Birth might also be required.
4. View and Download Your Rank Card
After submitting your details, your AP POLYCET 2026 Rank Card will be displayed on the screen. It will contain crucial information such as your total marks, subject-wise scores, and the rank secured.
5. Print for Future Reference
Download the rank card and take a clear printout. This document is mandatory for the upcoming counselling process and admission procedures. Remember, hard copies will not be sent by post.
Important: To qualify in AP POLYCET 2026, general category candidates must secure a minimum of 30% marks (36 out of 120). For SC/ST candidates, there are no minimum qualifying marks; their rank is allotted based on merit.
What's Next? Understanding the Counselling Process

Once you have your rank card, the next critical step is the AP POLYCET 2026 counselling process. This is where qualified candidates will register, verify documents, fill their preferred choices of colleges and courses, and ultimately secure their seats. The counselling will be conducted online by SBTET, AP.

1. Online Registration & Fee Payment
Qualified candidates must register online for counselling and pay a processing fee. The fee for OC and BC category candidates was INR 700 in the previous year, while for SC and ST candidates, it was INR 250. Please refer to the official website for the exact 2026 fee structure and payment methods.
2. Certificate Verification
After fee payment, candidates must visit designated helpline centres for physical document verification. It is essential to carry all original documents along with photocopies. A 'Receipt of Certificates' will be issued upon successful verification.
3. Option Entry (Choice Filling)
This is a crucial stage where candidates fill in their preferred courses and colleges in order of preference. The choices made here will significantly influence seat allotment.
4. Seat Allotment
Based on the candidate's rank, category, choices filled, and the availability of seats, the AP POLYCET 2026 seat allotment results will be announced online.
5. Self-Reporting & Reporting to Allotted Institute
Candidates who are allotted a seat must first self-report online and then physically report to their respective allotted institute to confirm admission by paying the required fees. Failure to do so by the stipulated deadline may result in the cancellation of the allotted seat.
