NEET UG 2026 Admit Card Released: Your Gateway to the May 3rd Exam!

The wait is over for over 22 lakh medical aspirants! The National Testing Agency (NTA) has officially released the NEET UG 2026 admit cards, paving the way for the highly anticipated examination scheduled for May 3, 2026. This is a crucial step for all registered candidates as the admit card is your mandatory entry pass to the examination hall. Here’s everything you need to know about downloading your admit card, important dates, and essential exam day guidelines.

Breaking News: The NEET UG 2026 admit card was officially released on April 26, 2026, by the National Testing Agency (NTA). The examination is slated for May 3, 2026, in a single shift.

How to Download Your NEET UG 2026 Admit Card

Accessing your NEET UG 2026 admit card is a straightforward process. Follow these steps carefully to ensure a smooth download:

1. Visit the Official NTA NEET Website
Navigate to the official NEET website: neet.nta.nic.in.
2. Locate the Admit Card Link
On the homepage, look for the link specifically marked "Admit Card for NEET (UG) 2026" or "NEET UG 2026 Admit Card Download Link" under the candidate activity section.
3. Enter Your Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Here, you need to enter your NEET Application Number and your Date of Birth or Password, followed by the security pin displayed on the screen.
4. Download and Print
After successfully entering your details, click on 'Submit'. Your NEET UG 2026 admit card will appear on the screen. Download the admit card in PDF format and take multiple printouts, preferably in color, on an A4 sheet. It is advised to keep several copies for future reference and the entire admission process.

Key Details to Verify on Your Admit Card

Once you have downloaded your admit card, it is absolutely critical to meticulously check all the details mentioned. Any discrepancy should be reported to the NTA immediately.

✅ Candidate's Name, Roll Number, and Application Number

✅ Father's Name and Mother's Name

✅ Date of Birth, Gender, and Category (if applicable)

✅ Photograph and Signature

✅ Examination Centre Name and Address

✅ Reporting Time and Gate Closing Time

✅ Test Centre Code

✅ Medium of Examination

✅ Important Instructions for the Examination Day

Essential Exam Day Guidelines

The NTA has issued important advisories for candidates appearing for the NEET UG 2026 examination. Adhering to these guidelines is crucial for a hassle-free experience.

1. Mandatory Documents
Candidates must carry a printed copy of their NEET UG 2026 admit card, a valid photo ID proof (Aadhaar Card, Voter ID, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License), and a passport-size photograph. Without these, entry to the examination hall will be denied.
2. Reporting Time
Arrive at your allotted examination centre well before the reporting time mentioned on your admit card. The gate closing time will be strictly followed.
3. Dress Code and Prohibited Items
Follow the prescribed dress code and be aware of the list of prohibited items inside the examination hall, which typically includes electronic devices, personal items like wallets and belts, and certain stationery.
4. Biometric Verification and Surveillance
Be prepared for biometric verification and enhanced surveillance measures, including CCTV cameras, at the examination centers.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is the NEET UG Admit Card 2026 released?
Yes, the NEET UG 2026 Admit Card has been officially released on April 26, 2026.
Where can I download the NEET UG Admit Card 2026?
You can download it from the official NTA NEET website: neet.nta.nic.in.
What details are required to download the admit card?
You will need your Application Number and Date of Birth or Password, along with the security pin.
What is the NEET UG 2026 exam date?
The NEET UG 2026 examination is scheduled for May 3, 2026, from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM.
Will the NTA send the admit card by post?
No, the admit card will only be available online for download. NTA will not send physical admit cards via post.
