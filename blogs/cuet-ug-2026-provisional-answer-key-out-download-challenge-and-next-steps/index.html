CUET UG 2026 Provisional Answer Key Released: Your Guide to Checking, Challenging & Next Steps!

The much-anticipated provisional answer key for the Common University Entrance Test (CUET UG) 2026 has been officially released by the National Testing Agency (NTA)! This crucial update arrived on June 9, 2026, bringing relief and a new phase of activity for over 15.68 lakh candidates who appeared for the examination nationwide and internationally. If you're one of these aspirants, it's time to check your responses, calculate your probable scores, and prepare for the next steps in your admission journey.

The CUET UG 2026 examination, conducted between May 11 and May 31, 2026, with rescheduled papers on June 6 and 7, 2026, saw a massive participation, paving the way for admissions into undergraduate programs across 230+ universities in India.

How to Download Your CUET UG 2026 Answer Key & Response Sheet

Accessing your provisional answer key and recorded response sheet is straightforward. The NTA has made these documents available on the official CUET portal. Follow these steps to get yours:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official CUET NTA website: cuet.nta.nic.in.
2. Locate the Answer Key Link
On the homepage, look for the "CUET (UG) 2026 Answer Key" link.
3. Log In
You will need to log in using your application number and password or your date of birth.
4. View and Download
Your recorded responses and the provisional answer key will appear on the screen. Select your paper and download the answer key PDF for your records.

Challenging the Provisional Answer Key

The NTA provides a crucial window for candidates to challenge any discrepancies they find in the provisional answer key. If you believe an answer provided by NTA is incorrect, you can raise an objection.

✅ The online window to challenge answers and submit objections is open from June 9 to June 11, 2026.

✅ The deadline for submitting challenges is 10:00 PM on June 11, 2026.

✅ The last date to pay the objection fee is 11:50 PM on June 11, 2026.

✅ A non-refundable processing fee of Rs. 200 per question challenged is required.

✅ Payment can be made via credit card, debit card, net banking, or UPI.

✅ No challenge will be considered without successful payment of the prescribed fee.

After the challenge window closes, a panel of subject experts will review all submitted objections. If a challenge is found to be valid, the answer key will be revised accordingly, and this revision will apply uniformly to all candidates' responses. The NTA will not individually inform candidates about the acceptance or rejection of their objections. The expert-verified final answer key will be the basis for preparing the CUET UG 2026 results.

Calculating Your Probable Score

Once you have your response sheet and the provisional answer key, you can estimate your score using the official marking scheme:

Correct Answer
For every correct answer, you will be awarded 5 marks.
Incorrect Answer
For every incorrect answer, 1 mark will be deducted.
Unattempted Questions
There is no negative marking for unattempted questions.

This calculation will give you a good indication of your performance, though your final score will be based on the revised answer key after all challenges are addressed.

What Comes Next? Important Dates

The release of the provisional answer key marks a critical juncture. Here's a look at the anticipated timeline for the subsequent phases:

Event Date (2026)
CUET UG 2026 Provisional Answer Key Released June 9
Answer Key Challenge Window Open June 9 - June 11 (10 PM)
Last Date for Objection Fee Payment June 11 (11:50 PM)
CUET UG 2026 Final Answer Key Expected End of June / First week of July
CUET UG 2026 Result Expected First week of July

The NTA is expected to release the final answer key by the end of June or the first week of July 2026. Following this, the CUET UG 2026 results are anticipated in the first week of July.

Stay tuned to the official CUET NTA website for all official announcements and further updates regarding the final answer key, results, and subsequent counselling processes. Wishing all candidates the best of luck with their results!
