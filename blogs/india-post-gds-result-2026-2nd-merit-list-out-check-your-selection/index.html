India Post GDS Result 2026: 2nd Merit List Released – Your Gateway to a Postal Career!

The anticipation for lakhs of aspirants has come to an end as India Post has officially declared the Gramin Dak Sevak (GDS) Result 2026, including the highly awaited 2nd Merit List! This recruitment drive is a significant opportunity for individuals seeking to join the Indian postal system as Branch Postmasters (BPM), Assistant Branch Postmasters (ABPM), and Dak Sevaks across various postal circles. If you've applied for these prestigious positions, it's time to check your selection status and prepare for the next crucial step: document verification. This blog post brings you all the essential details regarding the GDS 2026 results, how to check them, and what lies ahead.

Key Highlights of GDS Result 2026

✅ The 1st Merit List for the India Post GDS 2026 (January cycle) was released on March 6, 2026.

✅ The 2nd Merit List has been officially declared on April 10, 2026.

✅ A total of 28,636 to 28,740 vacancies are being filled for BPM, ABPM, and Dak Sevak posts.

✅ Selection is purely merit-based on Class 10th marks; no written exam or interview is conducted.

✅ Shortlisted candidates must proceed with mandatory document verification.

The India Post GDS recruitment process is renowned for its transparency, relying solely on the academic performance of candidates in their Class 10th examination. This merit-based approach ensures a fair selection for aspiring Gramin Dak Sevaks across the nation.

How to Check Your India Post GDS Result 2026

Checking your GDS result is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to download the merit list PDF for your respective circle:

1. Visit the Official GDS Portal
Navigate to the official India Post GDS online engagement portal: indiapostgdsonline.gov.in.
2. Locate the "Shortlisted Candidates" Section
On the homepage, look for the "Shortlisted Candidates" section or "Result / Merit List" link. You might find links titled "GDS Online Engagement Schedule-I, January-2026 Shortlisted Candidates".
3. Select Your Postal Circle/State
Click on your specific postal circle (state) for which you applied. This will lead you to the respective result link.
4. Download the Merit List PDF
A PDF file containing the merit list for your selected circle will either open directly or download automatically.
5. Search for Your Name/Registration Number
Open the downloaded PDF and use the 'Ctrl+F' (Find) function to quickly search for your registration number or name to check your selection status.

GDS Selection Process: Merit-Based and Transparent

It's important to understand that the India Post GDS recruitment does not involve any written examination or interview. The selection is solely based on the marks obtained by candidates in their Class 10th (Matriculation) examination. Here's a breakdown:

Merit List Preparation
The merit list is prepared automatically by the system, considering the Class 10th marks of candidates. Marks are calculated up to four decimal places for accuracy. Higher academic qualifications do not hold any additional weightage in the selection process.
Tie-Breaking Criteria
While not explicitly detailed in all search results for 2026, typically in case of a tie in marks, older candidates are preferred, followed by factors like ST female, SC female, OBC female, UR female, ST male, SC male, OBC male, UR male.
Multiple Merit Lists
To fill all vacant positions, India Post releases multiple merit lists. After the 1st and 2nd lists, a 3rd merit list is expected to be released around May-June 2026 to fill any remaining vacancies after the document verification process of previous lists.

Important Dates for GDS Recruitment 2026

Keep track of these key dates for the India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 (Schedule-I, January 2026 cycle):

Event Date
Application Begin January 31, 2026
Last Date for Apply Online February 14, 2026 (up to 5 PM)
Last Date for Fee Payment February 16, 2026
Correction Window February 18-19, 2026
1st Merit List / Result Release March 6, 2026
Last Date for Document Verification (1st List) March 23, 2026
2nd Merit List / Result Release April 10, 2026
3rd Merit List (Expected) May - June 2026

What's Next? Document Verification (DV)

If your name appears in the merit list, congratulations! The next mandatory step is Document Verification (DV). This process is crucial, and failure to complete it within the specified timeline or with incomplete documents will lead to disqualification.

Gather Original Documents
Candidates must carry all original documents, including educational certificates (Class 10th marksheet and passing certificate), identity proof (Aadhar Card, PAN Card), caste certificate (if applicable), disability certificate (if applicable), and other required documents as mentioned in the official notification.
Attend DV at Designated Office
You will be required to appear for document verification at the Divisional Head's office or the allotted post office/divisional office within the given timeframe. The result PDF usually contains details regarding the DV dates and venue.

Stay vigilant and regularly check the official India Post GDS website for any further announcements or supplementary lists. Best of luck to all the shortlisted candidates for a successful document verification and a fulfilling career with India Post!
