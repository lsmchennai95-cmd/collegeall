TNPSC Group 4 Apply Online 2026: Your Gateway to a Rewarding Government Career

Aspiring for a stable and prestigious career in the Tamil Nadu government? The Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission (TNPSC) Group 4 examination is your golden opportunity! With the official notification for 2026 on the horizon, now is the perfect time to understand the application process, eligibility, and what it takes to succeed. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about applying for TNPSC Group 4 online in 2026.

Key Dates for TNPSC Group 4 2026

Mark your calendars! The TNPSC has released its annual planner, detailing crucial dates for the Group 4 examination.

Event Date
Official Notification Release October 6, 2026
Online Application Start Date Expected after notification release
Online Application Last Date Expected around 30 days from application start
Exam Date December 20, 2026

Stay vigilant for the official notification on October 6, 2026, to get precise application start and end dates!

Understanding TNPSC Group 4

The TNPSC Group 4 examination is a combined civil services examination conducted annually to recruit candidates for various non-interview posts in different Tamil Nadu government departments. These roles include Village Administrative Officer (VAO), Junior Assistant, Typist, Steno-Typist, and other similar administrative positions.

Who Can Apply? Eligibility Criteria

Before you embark on your application journey, ensure you meet the essential eligibility criteria specified by TNPSC.

✅ Nationality: Applicant must be an Indian citizen.

✅ Age Limit (as of the notification date): Generally, candidates must be between 18 and 32 years. Age relaxations are applicable for Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Most Backward Classes (MBC), Backward Classes (BC), Backward Classes (Muslim), and Destitute Widows.

✅ Educational Qualification: A minimum of an SSLC (10th standard) pass or its equivalent is generally required for most posts. Specific posts like Typist or Steno-Typist may require additional technical qualifications such as typing skills (Higher/Senior Grade in English and/or Tamil) and a Certificate Course in Computer Office Automation.

✅ Language Proficiency: Adequate knowledge of the Tamil language is mandatory for all Group 4 posts. This can be demonstrated by having studied Tamil as a subject in SSLC or higher, or by passing the Second Class Language Test (Full Test) in Tamil conducted by TNPSC.

The Application Journey: Step-by-Step Online Process

The TNPSC Group 4 application process is entirely online. Follow these steps carefully to ensure a smooth application.

1. One-Time Registration (OTR)
If you haven't already, the first crucial step is to complete your One-Time Registration (OTR) on the official TNPSC website (tnpsc.gov.in or apply.tnpscexams.in). This involves providing basic details and paying a one-time registration fee of ₹150, which is valid for five years. You will receive a unique OTR ID and password.
2. Log In and Select the Post
Once registered, log in to the TNPSC application portal using your OTR ID and password. Navigate to the "Apply Online" section and select the "TNPSC Group 4 Apply Now" link corresponding to the latest notification (for 2026).
3. Fill the Application Form
The application form will appear, with many of your personal details auto-filled from your OTR. Carefully fill in all remaining personal, academic, and other required information.
4. Upload Required Documents
Scan and upload your passport-sized photograph and signature in the prescribed format and size. Typically, a photograph should be between 20 KB to 50 KB, and a signature between 10 KB to 20 KB. Ensure they meet the specified dimensions and quality standards.
5. Pay the Application Fee
Pay the examination fee of ₹100 through online modes such as Net Banking, Credit Card, Debit Card, or UPI. Ensure the payment is successful and keep the transaction receipt for future reference. Exemptions from the examination fee are available for certain reserved categories.
6. Review and Submit
Before final submission, meticulously review all the details entered in the application form to avoid any errors. Once satisfied, submit the application. Take a printout of the submitted form and the fee receipt for your records.

Application Fees and Exemptions

Understanding the fee structure is crucial for a complete application:

✅ One-Time Registration Fee: ₹150 (valid for 5 years, required only once).

✅ Examination Fee: ₹100 (applicable for each Group 4 exam).

✅ Exemptions: Candidates belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Caste (Arunthathiyars), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Persons with Benchmark Disability, and Destitute Widows are exempted from paying the examination fee. Most Backward Classes/Denotified Communities, Backward Classes (Other than Muslim), and Backward Classes (Muslim) are entitled to 3 free chances, while Ex-Servicemen get 2 free chances.

Exam Pattern and Syllabus at a Glance

The TNPSC Group 4 examination consists of a single written test, conducted in offline mode (OMR-based).

Exam Structure:
  • Duration: 3 hours
  • Total Questions: 200 (Multiple Choice Questions - MCQs)
  • Total Marks: 300 (Each question carries 1.5 marks)
  • Negative Marking: No negative marking.
  • Minimum Qualifying Marks: 90 marks (for all communities).
Syllabus Breakdown:
The syllabus is primarily based on the SSLC (10th standard) level and generally covers three main sections:
  • General Studies (75 questions): Includes topics such as General Science, Current Events, Geography, History and Culture of India and Tamil Nadu, Indian Polity, Indian Economy, Indian National Movement, and Development Administration in Tamil Nadu.
  • Aptitude & Mental Ability Test (25 questions): Focuses on problem-solving, reasoning, and basic mathematical skills.
  • Tamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test (100 questions): This section assesses your proficiency in the Tamil language and also contributes to your overall score. (Note: Previous patterns sometimes included General English/General Tamil as an optional section, but the latest updates emphasize the Tamil Eligibility-cum-Scoring Test).

Important Documents for Application

Keep the following documents ready before starting your online application:

✅ Passport-sized Photograph (scanned)

✅ Signature (scanned)

✅ SSLC/10th Standard Certificate

✅ Higher Secondary/Degree Certificates (if applicable for specific posts)

✅ Community Certificate (for reserved categories)

✅ Technical Qualification Certificates (e.g., Typewriting, Computer Automation, if applicable)

✅ Aadhaar Card (for OTR)

Conclusion

The TNPSC Group 4 examination in 2026 offers a significant opportunity for individuals seeking to join the Tamil Nadu government services. By meticulously following the application process, understanding the eligibility criteria, and preparing diligently according to the prescribed syllabus and exam pattern, you can significantly enhance your chances of success. Stay updated with the official TNPSC website for the latest announcements and start your preparation today!
