SSC CGL Final Result 2025-26 OUT: Your Gateway to Government Service!

The wait is over! The Staff Selection Commission has announced the much-anticipated Combined Graduate Level (CGL) Examination 2025-26 Tier 2 Final Result. Congratulations to all the successful candidates embarking on their journey in central government departments.

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) officially released the SSC CGL Final Result 2025-26 for the Tier 2 examination on April 8, 2026. This highly anticipated announcement brings good news for thousands of aspirants eyeing Group "B" and "C" posts across various central government departments.

Key Highlights of SSC CGL 2025-26 Final Result

✅ **Result Declaration Date:** The SSC CGL Tier 2 Final Result 2025-26 was declared on April 8, 2026.

✅ **Examination Dates:** The Tier 2 examination was conducted on January 18th and 19th, 2026.

✅ **Total Vacancies:** A total of 15,118 vacancies were advertised for Group "B" and "C" posts.

✅ **Candidates Recommended:** 15,118 candidates have been provisionally recommended for recruitment against these vacancies.

✅ **Official Website:** Candidates can access their results on the official SSC website, ssc.gov.in.

How to Check Your SSC CGL Final Result 2025-26

Checking your result is a straightforward process. Follow these simple steps to download the merit list PDF and ascertain your selection status:

1. Visit the Official SSC Website
Navigate to the Staff Selection Commission's official website: ssc.gov.in.
2. Access the Results Section
On the homepage, locate and click on the "Result" section, usually found on the left side or top right portion of the screen.
3. Select the CGL Tab
Within the results section, click on the "CGL" tab.
4. Download the Result PDF
Look for the "Combined Graduate Level Examination (Tier-2) 2025: Candidates provisionally shortlisted for DV" or "SSC CGL Final Result 2025-26" links and click on them. Separate PDFs are often released for different posts.
5. Search for Your Details
Open the downloaded PDF file. Use the "Ctrl + F" shortcut to search for your Name or Roll Number in the list of qualified candidates.
6. Save for Future Reference
If your name/roll number appears, congratulations! Download and save the PDF for your records and take a printout if needed.

Here’s a quick overview of the crucial dates related to the SSC CGL 2025-26 examination cycle:

Understanding the SSC CGL Sliding Mechanism

The Staff Selection Commission has introduced the SSC Sliding Mechanism for the CGL 2025-26 selection process. It's crucial for provisionally selected candidates to understand and participate in this process.

What is the Sliding Mechanism?
The Sliding Mechanism allows candidates who have been provisionally allocated a post to potentially "slide" into a more preferred post if vacancies become available later due to other candidates opting out or being disqualified. It aims to optimize the allocation process.
Participation is Mandatory
Only candidates whose names appear in the final SSC CGL Result 2025-26 are eligible to take part in the Sliding Process. Failure to attend the Sliding Process will result in exclusion from the final allocation.
Key Dates for Sliding Process
Candidates must select their venue, date, and slot for the Sliding Process between April 8th to 10th, 2026. The physical appearance at the concerned Regional Office will commence from April 13th, 2026 onwards.

What's Next for Provisionally Selected Candidates?

Congratulations once again on clearing one of India's most competitive examinations! Here's what you need to do next:

✅ **Participate in the Sliding Process:** This is a critical step for all provisionally shortlisted candidates. Ensure you select your preferred slot and appear as per the schedule.

✅ **Document Verification (DV):** Keep all your original documents ready for the document verification round, which will be conducted by the respective user departments.

✅ **Monitor Official Website:** Regularly check ssc.gov.in for further updates, including details on scorecards, final answer keys, and specific instructions from your allocated department.

✅ **Appointment Letters:** Final appointment letters will be issued by your respective User Departments after the successful completion of all formalities.

The journey to a fulfilling career in government service is now within reach for the successful candidates of SSC CGL 2025-26. We wish you all the best in your future endeavors!
