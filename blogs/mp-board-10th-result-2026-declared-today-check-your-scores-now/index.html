MP Board 10th Result 2026 DECLARED: Direct Links, How to Check, and Key Details!

The wait is officially over! The Madhya Pradesh Board of Secondary Education (MPBSE) has announced the Class 10 (High School Certificate) results for 2026 today, April 15, at 11:00 AM. Lakhs of students who appeared for the examinations in February and March can now check their eagerly awaited scorecards across multiple official platforms.

The announcement, made by Chief Minister Dr. Mohan Yadav, brings an end to the anticipation for over 9 lakh students across the state. This year's results mark a significant moment as the board continues its efforts to streamline the evaluation and declaration process. The examinations for Class 10 were conducted from February 11/13 to March 2/6, 2026, across various centers in Madhya Pradesh.

Breaking News: The MP Board Class 10th and 12th Results for 2026 are officially OUT as of April 15, 2026, at 11:00 AM! Get your results instantly via official websites, DigiLocker, and mobile apps.

How to Check Your MP Board 10th Result 2026

Accessing your MP Board 10th Result 2026 is a straightforward process. The MPBSE has enabled several platforms to ensure a smooth experience for all students. Keep your admit card handy, as you'll need your Roll Number and Application Number.

1. Visit Official Websites
Navigate to any of the following official MPBSE result portals:
  • mpresults.nic.in
  • mpbse.nic.in
  • mpbse.mponline.gov.in
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for and click on the link titled "MPBSE – HSC (Class 10th) Main Examination Results 2026" or similar.
3. Enter Your Credentials
A login window will appear. Carefully enter your 9-digit Roll Number and your Application Number, as printed on your admit card. You may also need to enter a Captcha code.
4. View and Download Your Result
Click the "Submit" or "View Result" button. Your MP Board 10th Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen. It is highly recommended to download the scorecard and take a printout for future reference.

Alternative Methods to Access Your Scorecard

In anticipation of high traffic, the MPBSE has ensured alternative ways to check results, including mobile applications and digital platforms.

✅ DigiLocker: Students can access certified digital copies of their marksheets through the DigiLocker platform.

✅ MPBSE Mobile App / MP Mobile App: Download these apps from the Google Play Store, select "Know Your Result," and enter your credentials. This is particularly useful during peak hours.

✅ SMS Service: In some instances, results might also be accessible via SMS. For Class 10th, type MPBSE10 <Roll Number> and send it to 56263.

Details Mentioned in Your Scorecard

Your provisional marksheet will contain crucial information. Please verify all details carefully:

✅ Student's Name

✅ Roll Number and Application Number

✅ Father's Name and Mother's Name

✅ School Name and Examination Centre

✅ Subject-wise Marks (Theory and Practical)

✅ Total Marks Obtained

✅ Division/Grade

✅ Qualifying Status (Pass/Fail)

What's Next? Re-evaluation and Second Examination

To pass the MP Board 10th examination, students must secure a minimum of 33% marks in each subject, including both theory and practical components. If you are not satisfied with your results or could not clear one or more subjects, the MPBSE offers options:

1. Re-evaluation/Rechecking
Students who believe there might have been an error in their marks can apply for re-evaluation or rechecking. The application forms for these processes will be made available on the board's official website shortly after the result declaration.
2. Second Examination (Compartment/Supplementary)
The MPBSE has replaced the traditional supplementary examination system with a "second examination" for both passed and failed students to improve their scores. The timetable for the second examination 2026 has already been released by MPBSE, with exams scheduled from May 7 to May 19, 2026.
