MHT CET 2026 Results: PCB First Attempt Declared, PCM Soon!

The much-anticipated MHT CET 2026 results are making headlines! The Maharashtra State Common Entrance Test Cell has officially declared the results for the PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) group's first attempt. Aspirants for the PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) group are on the edge of their seats as their results are expected to follow shortly. This blog post brings you the latest updates, important dates, and a step-by-step guide to accessing your MHT CET 2026 scorecard.

MHT CET 2026 Exam: A Dual-Phase Assessment

The MHT CET 2026 examination was conducted in two phases, allowing candidates to appear for their preferred group in different slots. This year's examination cycle spanned across April and May, culminating in intense anticipation for the results.

Key Exam Dates for MHT CET 2026

For those who appeared for the MHT CET 2026, here’s a quick recap of the examination schedule:

Group Phase Exam Dates
PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) Phase 1 April 11 to 20, 2026 (excluding April 12)
PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) Phase 1 April 21 to 26, 2026
PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology) Phase 2 May 10 & 11, 2026
PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics) Phase 2 May 12 to 21, 2026 (excluding May 16 & 17)

MHT CET 2026 Result: The Current Scenario

The Maharashtra State CET Cell has been swift in releasing the first set of results.

✅ PCB Group (1st Attempt) Results OUT: The MHT CET 2026 results for the PCB group's first attempt were officially declared on June 8, 2026.

✅ PCM Group (1st Attempt) Results Expected Soon: Candidates who appeared for the PCM group's first attempt can anticipate their results very shortly, with some reports suggesting a release by June 10 or June 12, 2026.

✅ Official Website: All results are exclusively released online on the official website, cetcell.mahacet.org.

Unlike previous years, the CET Cell has not released specific result declaration dates in advance. Instead, scorecards are being directly published, and candidates are being notified via SMS on their registered mobile numbers.

How to Check Your MHT CET 2026 Result

Accessing your MHT CET 2026 scorecard is a straightforward process. Follow these steps to download your result:

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the Maharashtra State CET Cell's official website: cetcell.mahacet.org.
2. Locate the Result Link
On the homepage, look for the link related to "MHT CET Result 2026" or "Result declared for MHT- CET 2026- PCB Group (First Attempt). Score card available in candidate's login."
3. Enter Login Credentials
You will be redirected to a login page. Enter your registered email ID and password.
4. View and Download Scorecard
Once logged in, your MHT CET 2026 scorecard will be displayed on the screen. Download it and take a printout for future reference and the upcoming admission process.

What Your Scorecard Contains

Your MHT CET 2026 scorecard is a crucial document that will include important details about your performance in the examination. Expect to find the following information:

✅ Candidate's Name and Application Number

✅ MHT CET Roll Number

✅ Equated CET Score

✅ Category

✅ All India Rank

✅ Subject-wise Percentile Score

✅ Total Percentile Score

Next Steps: Counselling and Admissions

The declaration of MHT CET 2026 results marks the beginning of the Centralised Admission Process (CAP). Qualified candidates will be eligible to participate in the counselling rounds for admission to various professional undergraduate courses, including B.E./B.Tech, B.Pharm, and others, offered by universities and institutes across Maharashtra.

The CET Cell will release detailed schedules for counselling, merit lists, and seat allotment rounds following the result declarations. It is imperative for candidates to keep a close watch on the official website for these crucial updates.

We wish all the MHT CET 2026 candidates the very best for their results and future academic endeavors!
