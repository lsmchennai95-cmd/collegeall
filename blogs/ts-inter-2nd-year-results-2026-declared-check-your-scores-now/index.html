Telangana TS Inter 2nd Year Results 2026 Declared: Your Guide to Checking Scores and Next Steps

The wait is over! The Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education (TSBIE) has officially announced the TS Inter 2nd Year Results for 2026.

A wave of anticipation and excitement has swept across Telangana as lakhs of intermediate students finally have access to their long-awaited 2nd Year Public Examination results. The Telangana State Board of Intermediate Education (TSBIE) officially declared the TS Inter 2nd Year Results 2026 on April 12, 2026, at 11:00 AM. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know, from checking your scores to understanding the grading system and planning your next academic steps.

How to Check Your TS Inter 2nd Year Results 2026

Accessing your results is a straightforward process. Ensure you have your Hall Ticket Number ready. Here’s a step-by-step guide to help you retrieve your marks memo online:

1. Visit Official Websites
Navigate to any of the official TSBIE result portals. The primary websites are:
  • tgbie.cgg.gov.in
  • results.cgg.gov.in
  • tgbienew.cgg.gov.in
In case of heavy traffic, alternative trusted educational portals such as Manabadi and Eenadu also provide direct links.
2. Select Your Result Link
On the homepage, click on the link specifically for "TS Inter 2nd Year Results 2026" or "IPE/IPASE Marks Memos" for the second year.
3. Enter Your Hall Ticket Number
A login window will appear. Carefully enter your unique Hall Ticket Number in the designated field. Some portals might also require your date of birth.
4. View and Download Your Result
After submitting the details, your TS Inter 2nd Year marks memo will be displayed on the screen. Review all the information carefully.
5. Save for Future Reference
Download and save a copy of your provisional marks memo. It is advisable to take a printout for your records, as it will be crucial for college admissions and other academic processes until you receive your original certificate.
Important Dates at a Glance

✅ **Exam Period:** February 26 to March 18, 2026

✅ **Result Declaration Date:** April 12, 2026

✅ **Result Declaration Time:** 11:00 AM

✅ **Re-evaluation/Re-counting Application Window:** Expected May 2026

✅ **Practical Examinations:** May 22 to May 25, 2026 (for upcoming sessions)

✅ **Supplementary Examinations:** Expected May-June 2026

Understanding the TS Inter Grading System 2026

The TSBIE employs a robust grading system to evaluate student performance. Your grades are based on the aggregate marks from both 1st and 2nd year, out of a total of 1000 marks across subjects.

Passing Marks Criteria
To pass the Telangana Intermediate examinations, students must secure a minimum of 35% marks in each subject. For students with visual impairment or hearing impairment, the passing mark is 25% in each subject. It's also important to note that specific subjects like Mathematics and Geography are for 75 marks, while Physics, Chemistry, Botany, and Zoology are for 60 marks, with others like English, Commerce, History, etc., being for 100 marks.

Here’s a breakdown of the TS Inter Grading System for 2026:

Marks Range Grade Description
75% and above (750+ marks) A Outstanding / Distinction
60% to 74% B Excellent / Very Good
50% to 59% C Good
35% to 49% D Pass
Below 35% Fail Unsuccessful

A minimum of 350 marks out of 1000 is required to be declared 'Pass' in the overall intermediate examination.

Key Statistics and Highlights for TS Inter 2nd Year Results 2026

The 2026 results reflect positive trends in student performance. Here are some key highlights:

✅ **Overall 2nd Year Pass Percentage:** 70.58%

✅ **Girls' Pass Percentage (2nd Year):** 78.65%

✅ **Boys' Pass Percentage (2nd Year):** 62.50%

✅ **Total Students Appeared (Both 1st & 2nd Year):** Approximately 9.9 lakh

✅ **Vocational Stream 2nd Year Pass Percentage:** 70.58% (overall with general stream)

Notably, female students have once again demonstrated exceptional performance, outshining their male counterparts in the 2nd-year examinations.

What to Do After Your Results

Once you have checked your results, here are the essential next steps:

Verify Your Marks Memo
Carefully check all details on your provisional marks memo, including your name, hall ticket number, subject-wise marks, total marks, grade, and qualifying status. Report any discrepancies immediately to your college authorities.
Re-evaluation and Re-counting
If you are dissatisfied with your scores, TSBIE provides an option for re-evaluation or re-counting of your answer scripts. The application window for this is expected to open in May 2026. Keep an eye on the official website for detailed instructions and deadlines.
Supplementary Examinations
Students who did not pass in one or two subjects have the opportunity to appear for supplementary examinations. These exams are typically held a few weeks after the main results, allowing students another chance to clear their papers. Information regarding supplementary exam schedules will be released soon.
Original Marks Memo
While the online marks memo is provisional, the original marks certificates will be distributed later through your respective junior colleges. You will need the provisional marks memo for immediate admission procedures.
Troubleshooting: Official Website Not Working?

Due to the high volume of traffic immediately after results declaration, official websites may sometimes experience temporary slowdowns or crashes. Don't panic! Here are alternative ways to check your results:

✅ **Alternative Websites:** Besides the main portals, you can try results.gov.in, manabadi.com, eenadu.net, and schools9.com.

✅ **DigiLocker:** The TS Inter Results 2026 are also available on DigiLocker. You can sign up or log in to your DigiLocker account, navigate to the education section, select TSBIE, and enter your hall ticket number and date of birth to access your marksheet.

✅ **WhatsApp Service:** For the first time, students can check their TS Inter Results 2026 via WhatsApp. Send a "Hi" to 9552300009, then select "TS Inter Results 2026" and enter your hall ticket number to receive your results.

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared their TS Inter 2nd Year examinations! For those who need to appear for supplementary exams or opt for re-evaluation, stay positive and focus on your preparation. Your hard work and dedication will surely pave the way for a bright future.
