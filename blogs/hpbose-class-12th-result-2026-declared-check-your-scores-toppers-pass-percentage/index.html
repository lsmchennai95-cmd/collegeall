HPBOSE Class 12th Result 2026 Declared: A New Benchmark for Excellence!

The Himachal Pradesh Board of School Education (HPBOSE) has officially declared the much-awaited Class 12th (Plus Two) examination results for 2026. Bringing an end to the anticipation of thousands of students, the results were announced on May 4, 2026, at 11 AM, marking a significant moment in the academic journey of many. This year, the board has set a new benchmark with an impressive overall pass percentage, reflecting the hard work and dedication of both students and educators.

The overall pass percentage for the HPBOSE Class 12th examinations in 2026 stands at a remarkable 92.02%, demonstrating a significant improvement over previous years.

Key Highlights of HPBOSE 12th Result 2026

✅ **Declaration Date:** May 4, 2026, at 11 AM.

✅ **Overall Pass Percentage:** 92.02%, a notable increase from the previous year.

✅ **Streams Covered:** Results for Science, Commerce, and Arts streams declared simultaneously.

✅ **Accessibility:** Results available online, via SMS, and DigiLocker.

✅ **Toppers:** Anshit Kumar leads the overall merit list, with exceptional performances across all streams.

✅ **Government Schools Shine:** Government schools have secured top positions in the merit list this year.

How to Check Your HPBOSE Class 12th Result 2026

Students can easily access their results through various online and offline methods. Ensure you have your roll number ready to view your provisional marksheet.

1. Via Official Website (hpbose.org)

The primary and most reliable method is through the official HPBOSE website.

  • Visit the official website: hpbose.org.
  • Click on the 'Results' tab located in the main menu.
  • Look for the link that says "HPBOSE 12th Exam Result 2026" or "Class 12 Regular Examination Result 2026" and click on it.
  • A login page will appear. Enter your roll number in the designated field.
  • Click on the 'Submit' or 'Search' button. Your HPBOSE 12th Result 2026 will be displayed on the screen.
  • Download and take a printout or screenshot of your provisional marksheet for future reference.
2. Through SMS Facility

For quick access, students can also check their results via SMS.

  • Open your SMS application.
  • Type: 
    HP12 [Your Roll Number]
    (e.g., HP12 206151051).
  • Send this message to 5676750.
  • You will receive your result on your mobile number.
3. Via DigiLocker and Other Portals

The results are also made available on platforms like DigiLocker and UMANG app. Additionally, some third-party websites like India Results may offer a name-wise search option, though cross-verification with official sources is always recommended.

HPBOSE Class 12th Toppers 2026

This year's results celebrate the outstanding achievements of several students. Anshit Kumar from PM SHRI Government Senior Secondary School, Bhawarna in Kangra district, has topped the Class 12 board exams with an impressive 496 marks out of 500, securing 99.2%.

Other notable toppers include:

  • Science Stream: Shayla Kashyap topped with 495 marks (99%), followed by Sanchita Dhiman and Kashi Sharma (98.6% each).
  • Commerce Stream: Yashvani Chauhan and Krishika jointly topped with 491 marks (98.2%), both from Una district.
  • Arts Stream: Anshit Kumar (overall topper) led with 496 marks (99.2%), followed by Arushi (495 marks) and Shamya Barjatya (494 marks).

Pass Percentage Analysis: A Look at the Trend

The 2026 HPBOSE Class 12th results show a consistent upward trend in performance, with a significant jump in the overall pass percentage. This year's 92.02% is a testament to improved academic standards and dedicated efforts.

The data clearly indicates a positive trajectory in student performance, with the 2026 results surpassing previous years by a significant margin.

What's Next? Re-evaluation, Compartment Exams, and Original Marksheets

1. Minimum Passing Marks

To successfully clear the Class 12 examination, students must secure a minimum of 33% marks in each subject, including internal assessments and practicals. An overall aggregate of 33% is also required.

2. Re-checking and Re-evaluation

Students who are not satisfied with their marks have the option to apply for re-checking or re-evaluation of their answer sheets. Details and application forms will be available on the official HPBOSE website.

3. Compartment/Supplementary Exams

For students who have failed in a maximum of two subjects, the board provides an opportunity to appear for compartment/supplementary examinations to save their academic year. Information regarding these exams will be released by HPBOSE in due course.

4. Collection of Original Marksheets

While the online result is provisional, students must collect their original marksheets and certificates from their respective schools after a few days of the online declaration. These documents are crucial for further academic admissions.

Congratulations to all the students who successfully cleared the HPBOSE Class 12th examinations! For those planning their next steps, remember to stay updated with official announcements from HPBOSE for further procedures regarding admissions, re-evaluation, or compartment exams.
