CGBSE 10th Result 2026 Declared: Your Gateway to Future Endeavors!

The moment of anticipation has culminated for lakhs of students across Chhattisgarh as the Chhattisgarh Board of Secondary Education (CGBSE) officially declared the Class 10 High School Certificate Examination results for 2026. State Education Minister Gajendra Yadav made the significant announcement on April 29, 2026, at 2:30 PM, marking a crucial milestone in the academic journey of countless students.

This year's results bring forth a mix of celebrations and opportunities for further growth. For those eager to check their performance and plan their next steps, all essential details, including pass percentages, topper lists, and how to access your digital mark sheet, are available here.

CGBSE 10th Result 2026: Key Highlights

✅ Overall Pass Percentage: A commendable 77.15% of students successfully cleared the Class 10 examinations.

✅ Girls Outshine Boys: Continuing a noticeable trend, girls once again performed exceptionally well with a pass percentage of 81.03%, significantly higher than boys at 72.27%.

✅ Student Participation: A total of 3,21,677 candidates registered, with 3,16,730 appearing for the exams. Out of these, 2,43,016 candidates passed.

✅ Top Performer: Sandhya Nayak emerged as the Class 10 topper, achieving an outstanding 99% (594 marks).

✅ Competitive Scores: This year saw close competition, with three students securing the Rank 1 position.

The examinations for the CGBSE Class 10 were conducted between February 21, 2026, and March 13, 2026, across various centers in the state.

How to Check Your CGBSE 10th Result 2026

Accessing your results is a straightforward process. Students can check their scores through the official websites and other authorized platforms. Remember to keep your roll number handy!

1. Via Official Websites

The primary platforms for checking your results are the official CGBSE portals.

✅ Visit any of these websites: www.cgbse.nic.in, results.cg.nic.in, or cg.results.nic.in.

✅ Look for the link titled "CGBSE Class 10 Result 2026" or "High School (10th) Examination Results 2026" on the homepage and click on it.

✅ You will be prompted to enter your Roll Number and a Captcha Code.

✅ After entering the details, click on 'Submit'.

✅ Your detailed result will be displayed on the screen. Download and print a copy for future reference.

2. Through SMS Service

In case of heavy website traffic, the SMS option provides a quick alternative to get your result.

✅ Type CG10<space>Roll Number.

✅ Send this message to 56263.

✅ Your CGBSE 10th Result 2026 will be sent to your registered mobile number.

3. DigiLocker and Other Portals

Students can also access their digital mark sheets via DigiLocker by logging in with their Aadhaar or mobile number. Additionally, third-party portals like the Times of India (TOI) result portal and indiaresults.com (which sometimes offers a name-wise search feature) can also provide access to results.

Important Post-Result Information

✅ Provisional Marksheet: The online mark sheet is provisional. Students must collect their original certificates and mark sheets from their respective schools later for official use and Class 11 admissions.

✅ Verify Details: Carefully check all details on your provisional mark sheet, including your name, roll number, subject-wise marks, and overall result status, to avoid any discrepancies.

✅ Revaluation: If you are not satisfied with your results, the CGBSE provides an option to apply for revaluation. Details regarding the revaluation process will be available on the official websites soon after the results.

✅ Supplementary Exams: For students who couldn't clear certain subjects, supplementary exams offer a second chance to pass without repeating the entire academic year. The schedule for these exams will be announced later. (Based on 2024 trends, supplementary exams typically occur in July-August with results in September).
