AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026 Declared: Direct Links, How to Check, and Next Steps!

The wait is finally over for thousands of students across Andhra Pradesh! The Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh (BIEAP) has officially declared the AP Inter Supplementary (IPASE) Results 2026 for both first and second-year students today, June 18, 2026, at 12 PM. This announcement brings relief and clarity for students who appeared for these crucial examinations, aiming to clear subjects or improve their scores.

These supplementary examinations, also known as IPASE (Intermediate Public Advanced Supplementary Examination), provide a vital second chance for students to achieve their academic goals and proceed with their higher education aspirations. The results are now available online through various platforms, ensuring easy access for all candidates.

Key Dates and Overview

Event Date(s)
AP Inter Supplementary Theory Exams 2026 May 21 - June 4, 2026
AP Inter Supplementary Practical Exams 2026 June 7 - June 11, 2026
AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026 Release June 18, 2026 (12 PM)

How to Check Your AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026

Checking your results is a straightforward process. Ensure you have your 10-digit hall ticket number and Date of Birth ready. Here’s a detailed guide on how to access your scores:

1. Via Official BIEAP Websites

The primary method to check your results is through the official websites of the Board of Intermediate Education, Andhra Pradesh.

  • Visit resultsbie.ap.gov.in or bie.ap.gov.in.
  • Look for the link "AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026" (or similar for 1st Year/2nd Year).
  • Click on the relevant link for your year (1st Year or 2nd Year / General or Vocational).
  • Enter your 10-digit hall ticket number.
  • Enter your Date of Birth in DD/MM/YYYY format.
  • Click on "Submit" or "Get Result" to view your marks memo.
  • Download and print your provisional marks memo for future reference.
2. Through Third-Party Portals & DigiLocker

Due to anticipated high traffic on official websites, results may also be available on other trusted platforms.

  • Manabadi: Visit manabadi.co.in and follow the links for AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026.
  • DigiLocker: Students can also access their digital marks memo through the DigiLocker platform.
3. WhatsApp Service

For added convenience, BIEAP also provides a WhatsApp service to receive scores directly on your mobile.

  • Send "Hi" to the BIEAP WhatsApp service number: 9552300009.
4. SMS Service (Alternative Method)

In case of website issues, you can also get your scores via SMS.

  • For General Stream: Type APGEN2 <space> Registration Number and send to 56263.
  • For Vocational Stream: Type APVOC2 <space> Registration Number and send to 56263.

Passing Criteria

To successfully clear the AP Inter Supplementary Exams 2026, students must meet the following criteria:

✅ Secure a minimum of 35 marks out of 100 in each subject.

The provisional marks memo available online will indicate your subject-wise marks, grades, and overall qualifying status.

What's Next After Checking Your Results?

Congratulations to all the successful candidates! For those who have cleared their exams, here are the essential next steps:

1. Download and Verify Marks Memo

Immediately download and print multiple copies of your provisional marks memo. This document is crucial for college admissions and scholarship applications. Carefully verify all details, including your personal information, subject-wise marks, and pass/fail status.

2. Re-verification and Recounting

If you are not satisfied with your marks or believe there has been an error in evaluation, you have the option to apply for re-verification or recounting.

  • Applications usually need to be submitted within 10-15 days of result declaration.
  • Visit the official BIEAP website (bie.ap.gov.in or bieap.apcfss.in) for the application form and detailed instructions.
  • Fees typically apply: approximately ₹260 per subject for recounting and ₹1,300 per subject for re-verification (which includes a scanned copy of your answer script).
3. Pursue Higher Education Admissions

Successful candidates are now eligible for higher education. Start applying to degree colleges, prepare for entrance examinations like EAMCET, JEE, NEET, or other competitive exams relevant to your chosen stream.

4. Collect Original Mark Sheets

The online result is provisional. Students must collect their original mark sheets and other official certificates from their respective junior colleges once they are issued by BIEAP. Stay in touch with your college administration for collection dates.

Regular Exam Performance (For Context)

To provide context, the regular AP Inter 2nd Year results for 2026 were declared on April 15, 2026, with an overall pass percentage of 81%. Girls demonstrated a strong performance, outperforming boys in the general stream. A total of over 10.57 lakh students appeared for the regular intermediate examinations across both years.

Don't panic if your results aren't as expected. The option for re-verification and recounting exists, and academic counselors can provide guidance for your future path.

The declaration of AP Inter Supplementary Results 2026 marks a significant milestone for many students. We wish all candidates the very best for their future academic and career endeavors!
