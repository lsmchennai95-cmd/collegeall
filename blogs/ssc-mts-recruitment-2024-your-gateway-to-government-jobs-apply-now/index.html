SSC MTS Recruitment 2024: Your Ultimate Guide to Landing a Government Job!

Are you aspiring for a stable career in government service after completing your 10th grade? The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) Multi-Tasking (Non-Technical) Staff and Havaldar (CBIC & CBN) Examination 2024 presents a golden opportunity! The highly anticipated notification for SSC MTS Recruitment 2024 has been released, inviting eligible candidates to apply for a significant number of vacancies across various ministries, departments, and offices of the Government of India. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know to successfully navigate the application process and prepare for this promising exam.

The Staff Selection Commission released the official SSC MTS Notification 2024 on June 27, 2024, opening doors for thousands of hopefuls. This year, the number of vacancies has seen a remarkable increase, making it an even more attractive opportunity!

Key Highlights of SSC MTS 2024 Recruitment

✅ Official Notification: Released on June 27, 2024.

✅ Application Window: June 27, 2024, to August 3, 2024 (extended).

✅ Revised Vacancies: A massive 11,518 posts for MTS and Havaldar!

✅ Educational Qualification: Minimum 10th Pass.

✅ Exam Type: Computer-Based Examination (CBE).

Important Dates to Remember

Mark your calendars with these crucial dates for the SSC MTS & Havaldar Examination 2024:

Activity Date
Official Notification Release Date June 27, 2024
Online Application Start Date June 27, 2024
Last Date to Submit Online Application August 3, 2024 (Extended)
Last Date for Online Fee Payment August 4, 2024
Application Form Correction Window August 16 - 17, 2024
Computer Based Examination (Paper I) September 30 - November 14, 2024

SSC MTS Vacancy 2024: A Significant Increase!

The Staff Selection Commission has significantly increased the number of vacancies for the 2024 recruitment cycle. Initially, 8,326 vacancies were announced, which were later revised to 9,583. The latest update confirms a total of 11,518 vacancies! This includes 8,079 positions for Multi-Tasking Staff (MTS) and 3,439 for Havaldar in CBIC & CBN. This increase provides a greater chance for aspiring candidates to secure a coveted government job.

Eligibility Criteria: Do You Qualify?

Before applying, it's crucial to ensure you meet the eligibility requirements set by the SSC:

1. Educational Qualification
Candidates must have passed the Matriculation Examination (10th grade) or its equivalent from a recognized board by the specified cut-off date. Even candidates appearing for their 10th-grade exams can apply, provided they produce the necessary certificates during document verification.
2. Age Limit (as of a specified date, usually August 1, 2024)
  • For most MTS and Havaldar in CBN posts: 18 to 25 years.
  • For Havaldar in CBIC and certain MTS posts: 18 to 27 years.
Age relaxation is applicable for candidates belonging to reserved categories (SC/ST, OBC, PwBD, Ex-servicemen, etc.) as per government rules. For instance, OBC candidates receive a 3-year relaxation, and SC/ST candidates receive a 5-year relaxation.

SSC MTS Selection Process 2024

The selection process for SSC MTS and Havaldar positions involves the following stages:

✅ Computer-Based Examination (Paper I)

✅ Physical Efficiency Test (PET) / Physical Standard Test (PST) (Only for Havaldar posts)

✅ Document Verification

SSC MTS Exam Pattern 2024 (Paper I)

The Computer-Based Examination (Paper I) has been revised and will be conducted in two sessions. Both sessions are mandatory. There will be no negative marking in Session 1, but Session 2 will have a penalty for incorrect answers.

Session Subject No. of Questions Max. Marks Duration Negative Marking
Session 1 Numerical and Mathematical Ability 20 60 45 minutes No
Reasoning Ability and Problem-Solving 20 60
Session 2 General Awareness 25 75 45 minutes Yes (1 mark for each wrong answer)
English Language and Comprehension 25 75
Total 90 270 90 minutes (45 per session)
Key Points on Exam Pattern

• The exam will consist of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs) and will be conducted online.

• Questions will be set in English, Hindi, and 13 regional languages for Session-I and the General Awareness section of Session-II.

• Only candidates who qualify Paper 1 (CBT) will proceed to the next stage (PET/PST for Havaldar).

• Minimum qualifying marks in both Session-I and Session-II apply: General (UR): 30%; OBC/EWS: 25%; All other categories: 20%.

Application Fee

The application fee for SSC MTS 2024 is ₹100. However, female candidates and those belonging to Scheduled Castes (SC), Scheduled Tribes (ST), Persons with Benchmark Disabilities (PwBD), and Ex-servicemen are exempted from paying the fee. Payment can be made online through Net-Banking, Credit/Debit cards, BHIM UPI, etc.

How to Apply for SSC MTS Recruitment 2024

To apply for the SSC MTS 2024 examination, follow these steps:

1. Visit the Official Website
Go to the official website of the Staff Selection Commission (www.ssc.gov.in).
2. Register (if new user) or Log In
If you are a new user, register on the portal. Existing users can log in with their credentials.
3. Fill the Application Form
Locate the SSC MTS 2024 application link and fill in all the required details accurately.
4. Upload Documents
Upload scanned copies of your photograph and signature as per the specifications. SSC has also introduced a new feature of live photograph capture.
5. Pay Application Fee
Pay the application fee online, if applicable, before the deadline.
6. Submit and Print
Review your application, submit it, and take a printout for your records.

The SSC MTS Recruitment 2024 offers a significant opportunity for individuals seeking government employment. With revised vacancies and a clear examination structure, now is the time to prepare diligently. Keep an eye on the official SSC website for any further updates and detailed instructions. Good luck!
