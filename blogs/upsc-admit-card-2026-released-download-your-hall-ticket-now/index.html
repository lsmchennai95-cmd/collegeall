UPSC Admit Card 2026 Released: Your Essential Guide to Downloading and Exam Day Success

The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has officially released the e-admit cards for the highly anticipated Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2026! Aspirants who have diligently prepared for one of the nation's toughest competitive exams can now download their hall tickets. The Prelims examination is scheduled for May 24, 2026, and securing your admit card well in advance is a critical step towards your civil service dream. This comprehensive guide will walk you through everything you need to know about downloading your UPSC Admit Card 2026, important dates, details to verify, and crucial exam day instructions.

UPSC Prelims Admit Card 2026: Key Dates at a Glance

Staying informed about important dates is paramount for every UPSC aspirant. The UPSC Prelims Admit Card 2026 has been released, marking a significant milestone for candidates.

Event Date
UPSC CSE Prelims Admit Card 2026 Release Date May 15, 2026
UPSC Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination 2026 Date May 24, 2026
UPSC Prelims Exam Timings (Shift 1) 09:30 AM to 11:30 AM (General Studies)
UPSC Prelims Exam Timings (Shift 2) 02:30 PM to 04:30 PM (CSAT)

The admit card download link will remain active until the day of the examination.

How to Download Your UPSC Admit Card 2026 (Step-by-Step)

The process of downloading your UPSC Hall Ticket 2026 is straightforward. Follow these detailed steps to secure your admit card from the official UPSC websites.

1. Visit the Official UPSC Websites
Navigate to any of the official UPSC online portals:
  • upsc.gov.in
  • upsconline.nic.in
  • upsconline.gov.in
2. Locate the Admit Card Link
On the homepage, look for the "Admit Cards" section or the "What's New" section. Click on the link that states "e-Admit Card: Civil Services (Preliminary) Examination, 2026" or similar.
3. Choose Your Login Method
You will be redirected to a login page where you can choose to log in using either your:
  • Registration ID
  • Roll Number (if already generated)
  • Some portals may also offer login via Mobile Number/URN/Email.
4. Enter Your Credentials
Enter the required details such as your Registration ID/Roll Number, Date of Birth, and the Captcha Code (security code) as prompted.
5. Read and Accept Instructions
Carefully read all the important exam-related instructions and guidelines displayed on the screen. After reviewing, click on the "I Agree" or "Submit" button to proceed.
6. Download and Print Your Admit Card
Your UPSC Prelims Admit Card 2026 will appear on the screen. Download it in PDF format and take 2-3 printouts for future reference and exam day use. Remember, only a hard copy of the admit card will be accepted at the examination center.

Forgot Your Registration ID?

If you've misplaced or forgotten your Registration ID, don't panic. The UPSC portal provides an option to retrieve it. You will typically need to click on the "Forgot RID" option and provide details like your full name, date of birth, and registered email ID or mobile number.

Crucial Details to Verify on Your Admit Card

Once you have downloaded your UPSC Admit Card 2026, it is imperative to thoroughly check all the details mentioned on it. Any discrepancies should be reported to the UPSC authorities immediately.

✅ Candidate's Name

✅ Roll Number

✅ Registration Number

✅ Father's Name and Mother's Name

✅ Photograph and Signature

✅ Examination Centre Address and Code

✅ Exam Date and Time (Shift timings for GS and CSAT)

✅ Reporting Time

✅ Exam Day Instructions and Guidelines

If you find any errors or discrepancies on your admit card, contact UPSC officials without delay to get them corrected.

Important Exam Day Instructions and Reminders

The UPSC Civil Services Examination has strict guidelines to ensure fair conduct. Adhering to these instructions is crucial for a smooth examination experience.

✅ **Carry a Hard Copy:** A printed copy of your e-Admit Card is mandatory. Soft copies on mobile phones or other devices will NOT be accepted.

✅ **Valid Photo ID Proof:** Along with your admit card, carry a valid government-issued photo identity proof (e.g., Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID) in original.

✅ **Reach On Time:** Arrive at the examination center well before the reporting time to avoid last-minute rush and complete the mandatory face authentication/identity verification process.

✅ **Prohibited Items:** Electronic gadgets, mobile phones, smartwatches, Bluetooth devices, and any other prohibited items are strictly not allowed inside the exam hall.

✅ **Read Instructions Carefully:** Before the examination, thoroughly read all the instructions mentioned on the admit card and the OMR sheet.

The UPSC Civil Services Preliminary Examination is a significant step towards a rewarding career in public service. With your admit card in hand and a clear understanding of the exam day protocols, you are one step closer to achieving your aspirations. Good luck to all the candidates!
