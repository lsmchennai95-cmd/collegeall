Why CTET is Crucial for Your Teaching Career

The CTET exam is not just another test; it's a gateway to a rewarding career in teaching. Successfully clearing CTET provides you with the eligibility to pursue teaching roles for classes 1 to 8 across various central government and affiliated schools in India. The CTET qualifying certificate now holds lifetime validity, making it an invaluable asset for your professional journey.

Eligibility Criteria: Are You Qualified?

Before you hit that 'Apply Now' button, ensure you meet the CTET eligibility criteria. The requirements vary for Paper 1 (Primary Stage: Classes 1 to 5) and Paper 2 (Elementary Stage: Classes 6 to 8).

1. Age Limit

Candidates must be at least 17 years old. There is no upper age limit to apply for CTET 2026.

2. Nationality

Only Indian citizens are eligible to apply for the CTET.

3. Educational Qualification for Paper 1 (Classes I-V)

Candidates must have one of the following qualifications:

  • Senior Secondary (or equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed).
  • Senior Secondary with at least 45% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (as per NCTE Regulations 2002).
  • Senior Secondary with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed).
  • Senior Secondary with at least 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Special Education.
  • Graduation with at least 50% marks and a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education.
  • Post-graduation (any discipline) with a minimum of 55% marks or equivalent grade along with a 3-year integrated B.Ed-M.Ed (new criterion).
4. Educational Qualification for Paper 2 (Classes VI-VIII)

Candidates must have one of the following qualifications:

  • Graduation with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 2-year Diploma in Elementary Education (D.El.Ed).
  • Graduation with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in a 1-year Bachelor in Education (B.Ed).
  • Graduation with at least 45% marks and a 1-year Bachelor in Education (B.Ed), in accordance with the NCTE Regulations.
  • Senior Secondary (or equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 4-year Bachelor of Elementary Education (B.El.Ed).
  • Senior Secondary (or equivalent) with at least 50% marks and passed or appearing in the final year of a 4-year B.A/B.Sc.Ed or B.A.Ed/B.Sc.Ed.
  • Graduation with at least 50% marks and a 1-year B.Ed. (Special Education).
  • Any candidate having a qualified B.Ed Programme recognized by the NCTE is eligible to appear in TET/CTET.
  • Post-graduation (any discipline) with a minimum of 55% marks or equivalent grade along with a 3-year integrated B.Ed-M.Ed (new criterion).

How to Apply for CTET 2026 Online: A Step-by-Step Guide

The application process is entirely online. Follow these steps carefully to ensure your application is submitted successfully before the deadline.

1. Visit the Official Website
Navigate to the official CTET website: ctet.nic.in.
2. Click on 'Apply for CTET 2026'
On the homepage, locate and click the link that says "Apply for CTET September 2026".
3. New Registration
If you are a new user, click on 'New Registration'. Carefully read through the instructions and prospectus, then proceed.
4. Fill in Details & Create Login
Complete the registration with your basic details, including a valid Email ID and Mobile Number. This will generate your login credentials (Application Number and Password). Keep these safe.
5. Complete the Application Form
Log in using your new credentials. Fill out the online application form with personal information, contact details, and educational qualifications accurately.
6. Upload Documents
Upload scanned images of your recent photograph and signature as per the specifications mentioned in the official notification.
7. Pay the Application Fee
Pay the required application fee online through Debit Card / Credit Card / Net Banking / UPI. The fee structure is provided below.
8. Submit and Print Confirmation
After reviewing all details, submit the form and download the confirmation page. It is highly recommended to take a printout for future reference.

Important Points to Remember

✅ Deadline is Today: The application window closes tonight, June 10, 2026, at 11:59 PM. There is no indication of an extension.

✅ Online Only: Applications will only be accepted online through the official website.

✅ No City Choice: Examination city will be allotted randomly by CBSE, and no requests for changes will be entertained.

✅ Correction Window: A correction window will be available from June 15 to June 18, 2026, to rectify any errors in your submitted form.

✅ No Negative Marking: The CTET exam does not have negative marking, encouraging candidates to attempt all questions.

✅ Offline Exam: The exam will be conducted in offline mode using OMR sheets.
